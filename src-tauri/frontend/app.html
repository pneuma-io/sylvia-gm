    Sylvia saves campaign history on this computer. Messages and relevant campaign content, including source material used by the Builder, are sent to your selected model service. Remote services receive this data outside your computer and handle it under their own privacy and retention policies. Saving locally does not prevent this transmission.

    Sylvia connects to model services you already run or subscribe to. It does not download or start models. Saved API keys and private headers use Windows protection tied to your user account. They are sent to your selected service for authentication and are not included in chats or campaign backups. If Windows cannot unlock them, re-enter them in the connection settings.

    This protects a copied settings file outside your Windows protection context. It does not protect against software running as your Windows user or erase older plaintext copies of your settings.
