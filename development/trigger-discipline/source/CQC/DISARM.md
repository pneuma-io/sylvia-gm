# DISARM

**At a glance:** CQC maneuver that removes one declared held item from the target's control without automatically giving it to the attacker.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - Declare the held item before resolving the maneuver.
> - On success, the item leaves the target's control and becomes a ground object at the target's position by default, adjusted only when the physical maneuver clearly sends it to another nearby valid location.
> - DISARM does not automatically transfer the item to the attacker. Picking it up or seizing it is another physical interaction unless an explicit ability changes that sequence.
> - Retention devices, straps, mounts, two-handed holds, unusual size, or other physical constraints can modify difficulty or make an ordinary DISARM inappropriate.
> - DISARM does not automatically deal DAMAGE, move the target, or make them PRONE.
> - Exact AP cost and retention/equipment modifiers are balance parameters.
