# CQC DEFENSE

**At a glance:** Derived DIFFICULTY an attacker rolls against for CQC rather than defaulting to an opposed-roll exchange.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - CQC DEFENSE is built from a base defense, a CQC contribution, a relevant defensive APTITUDE contribution, and situational modifiers.
> - CQC expertise contributes more heavily than raw APTITUDE. High FORCE alone does not make someone a close-quarters expert.
> - Against SHOVE, THROW, and BODY CONTROL, FORCE is the ordinary benchmark defensive APTITUDE because leverage and body position are central.
> - Against TRIP, DISARM, and WEAPON CONTROL, PRECISION is the ordinary benchmark defensive APTITUDE because timing and limb/weapon positioning are central.
> - Circumstances can justify another defensive APTITUDE under the normal relevant-APTITUDE rule.
> - CQC BRACE and other explicit effects can modify CQC DEFENSE.
> - Exact base value, CQC coefficient, APTITUDE coefficient, and situational modifiers are balance parameters.
