# THROW

**At a glance:** Stronger CQC repositioning maneuver that moves the target and leaves them PRONE.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - Declare a physically plausible nearby destination before resolving the maneuver.
> - On success, reposition the target to that destination and make them PRONE.
> - THROW is a stronger combined result than either SHOVE or TRIP and may therefore cost more AP or face greater difficulty.
> - The destination must be physically reachable by the throw. Solid geometry, size, leverage, footing, barriers, and elevation can modify the maneuver or make it impossible.
> - THROW does not automatically DISARM the target or deal melee DAMAGE. A real environmental impact or hazard can create additional consequences through its own rules.
> - Exact AP cost, displacement distance, and leverage/difficulty modifiers are balance parameters.
