# CONTROL

**At a glance:** Persistent CQC hold that physically denies one defined form of freedom without becoming a generic stun state.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - On success, establish physical CONTROL and choose the hold's focus: BODY CONTROL or WEAPON CONTROL.
> - **BODY CONTROL:** the target cannot voluntarily leave the controller's physical contact without first ending CONTROL. The controller may move while bringing the target with them where geometry permits, using a reduced/burdened movement rate.
> - **WEAPON CONTROL:** choose one held weapon or controlled limb. While CONTROL persists, actions requiring freedom of that weapon or limb cannot be performed.
> - CONTROL never means the target is globally unable to act. Speech, other limbs, other equipment, and other actions remain available where physically possible.
> - CONTROL persists without a maintenance roll until the controller releases it, the target successfully ESCAPES, required physical contact is broken, the controller is incapacitated, forced movement separates them, or the controller loses the limb/body position required to maintain the hold.
> - The controller must keep whatever hand/body commitment the hold physically requires.
> - A controlled person's body can obstruct a projectile if actual geometry places them between shooter and controller. Resolve the projectile against that person's body first rather than treating them as an abstract COVER-HP object.
> - Size, leverage, footing, free hands, and geometry can modify the initial CONTROL attempt or make a proposed hold impossible.
> - Exact AP cost, movement penalty while moving a controlled target, and situational modifiers are balance parameters.
