# CQC BRACE

**At a glance:** PREP ACTION that improves resistance to the first incoming CQC maneuver while the braced posture is maintained.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - Spend AP during your TEAM TURN to deliberately brace against incoming physical contact.
> - Until the start of your next TEAM TURN, the first CQC maneuver made against you faces increased CQC DEFENSE.
> - CQC BRACE ends after that maneuver resolves, whether it succeeds or fails.
> - It also ends if you voluntarily move, lose the physical posture supporting the brace, or perform an action incompatible with maintaining it.
> - CQC BRACE does not attack someone for approaching and is not a universal opportunity-action rule.
> - Exact setup AP and CQC DEFENSE increase are balance parameters.
