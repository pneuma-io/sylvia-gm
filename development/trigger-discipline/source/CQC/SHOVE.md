# SHOVE

**At a glance:** CQC maneuver for short forced repositioning rather than raw melee damage.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - Declare a physically plausible direction and destination before resolving the maneuver.
> - On success, force the target a short distance in that direction.
> - The target normally keeps their current STANCE.
> - SHOVE does not automatically deal DAMAGE, knock the target PRONE, or DISARM them.
> - Forced movement cannot pass through solid geometry or end in an invalid location.
> - Size, leverage, footing, bracing, and obstacles can modify the maneuver or make a proposed SHOVE impossible.
> - If the forced movement places the target into a real environmental hazard, resolve that hazard normally.
> - Exact AP cost, base forced distance, and leverage modifiers are balance parameters.
