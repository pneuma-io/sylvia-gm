# TRIP

**At a glance:** CQC maneuver whose primary result is putting the target PRONE without automatically adding displacement or damage.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - On success, the target becomes PRONE in their current position.
> - TRIP does not automatically move the target, deal DAMAGE, or DISARM them.
> - If the fiction requires displacement as part of the intended result, use THROW instead.
> - Exact AP cost and situational difficulty modifiers are balance parameters.
