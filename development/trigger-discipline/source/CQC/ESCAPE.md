# ESCAPE

**At a glance:** CQC action used by a CONTROLLED character to break the current physical hold.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - A CONTROLLED character may spend AP on ESCAPE.
> - Roll CQC against the controller's CQC DEFENSE using the relevant APTITUDE for the hold and circumstances.
> - On success, CONTROL ends.
> - On failure, the AP is spent and the hold remains.
> - ESCAPE does not automatically reverse CONTROL, SHOVE, THROW, DISARM, or damage the controller.
> - If the character wants to escape and then perform another maneuver, they must spend AP on that second CQC ACTION separately.
> - Exact AP cost and situational modifiers are balance parameters.
