# HP RECOVERY

**At a glance:** Slow, resource-intensive recovery from actual bodily injury; speed depends strongly on Campaign Setting medicine.

> [!note]- Full rules
> A conventional 2026/27 setting can require days or weeks for serious trauma; advanced settings can shorten recovery without changing what HP means. Explicit INJURIES may need their own treatment.
