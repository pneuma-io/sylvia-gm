# RES RECOVERY

**At a glance:** Generous between genuinely secured encounters; with a few safe minutes and ordinary support, full RES restoration can often be normal.

> [!note]- Full rules
> The adventure pressure is whether the squad actually has enough time and safety. RES does not passively regenerate during a firefight by default.
