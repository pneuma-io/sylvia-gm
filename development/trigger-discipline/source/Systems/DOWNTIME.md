# DOWNTIME

**At a glance:** Activity-based time between operations: heal, train, investigate, repair, socialize, shop, gather intel, prepare, and similar goals rather than a daily AP minigame.

> [!note]- Full rules
> The DM judges what reasonably fits the available time and rolls only when uncertainty matters. Rest does not reload the world: enemies reposition, networks get patched, credentials rotate, evidence moves, and reinforcements can arrive.
