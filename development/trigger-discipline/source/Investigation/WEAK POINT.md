# WEAK POINT

**At a glance:** A real physical/system vulnerability that can be discovered through RECON, INVESTIGATION, hacking, observation, or other plausible means.

> [!note]- Full rules
> Once discovered, a WEAK POINT becomes TEAM KNOWLEDGE rather than forcing every character to reroll whether they know it. CALLED SHOTS can exploit a known weak point at the appropriate DIFFICULTY.
