# TEAM KNOWLEDGE

**At a glance:** Information successfully shared with the squad becomes common operational knowledge; it is not re-rolled separately by every character.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - Examples include discovered WEAK POINTS, enemy positions transmitted by a drone, credentials shared between hackers, verified investigative facts, and TARGET KNOWLEDGE communicated between observers.
> - A recipient cannot receive more precision than the source actually had. APPROXIMATE information does not become CONFIRMED merely because it was radioed to the team.
> - Shared positional information becomes stale when the underlying observation becomes stale unless another observer or sensor maintains better current information.
> - Successful communication shares what is known; it does not create telepathic squad vision or automatically share facts that were never transmitted.
