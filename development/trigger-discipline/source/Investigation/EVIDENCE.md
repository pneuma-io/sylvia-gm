# EVIDENCE

**At a glance:** Information or material whose source, authenticity, completeness, and provenance can matter independently of whether someone personally believes it.

> [!note]- Full rules
> Finding a file is not automatically the same as proving a claim. Essential adventure information should not disappear behind one failed CHECK. Evidence can support SOCIAL FACTS, LEVERAGE, weak-point discovery, legal claims, and investigation conclusions.
