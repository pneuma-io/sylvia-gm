# STABILIZE

**At a glance:** MEDICINE action that controls one specific ACTIVE LIFE-THREATENING CONDITION by applying an intervention capable of stopping its lethal mechanism.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - Declare the ACTIVE [[LIFE-THREATENING CONDITION]] being treated and the intervention being attempted.
> - The acting character needs sufficient diagnosis/information, physical access, and whatever equipment or method the condition actually requires.
> - Spend the required AP/time and make a MEDICINE CHECK when success is uncertain.
> - Success changes that condition from **ACTIVE → STABILIZED**.
> - Failure spends the attempted time/resources but does not itself invent deterioration; the condition worsens only when its own DETERIORATION TRIGGER occurs.
> - Stabilizing one condition does not stabilize unrelated conditions.
> - STABILIZE does not automatically restore HP, RES, consciousness, erase an INJURY, or end DOWNED.
> - Any character may attempt STABILIZE when skill, equipment, access, and fiction support the intervention. CORPSMAN progression improves the action rather than granting permission to use it.
> - Exact AP, DIFFICULTY, equipment, and treatment-effect values are balance parameters.
