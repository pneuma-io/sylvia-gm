# CASUALTY MOVEMENT

**At a glance:** Moving an impaired or DOWNED character is a physical GRID interaction with distinct DRAG and CARRY modes, occupied hands, real paths, and movement cost.

> [!note]- Full rules
> **DRAG**
> - Spend the required AP/time to take hold of the casualty.
> - The casualty remains PRONE in an adjacent trailing GRID space. Both the mover's space and the casualty's path must remain physically passable.
> - A one-handed DRAG is slower but leaves one hand available for compatible one-handed equipment. A two-handed DRAG is faster but leaves no hand available.
> - DRAG cannot cross a vault, climb, or other route the trailing body cannot physically negotiate unless equipment, assistance, or an explicit ability provides a valid method.
> - Releasing a dragged casualty costs no additional AP because the casualty is already on the ground.
>
> **CARRY**
> - Spend the required AP/time to lift the casualty. CARRY normally occupies both hands.
> - The casualty moves with the carrier in the carrier's GRID space, allowing routes such as a narrow doorway or ordinary stairs when the carrier can physically negotiate them.
> - CARRY is faster than DRAG once established but may require a FORCE CHECK when the load, equipment, injury, terrain, or carrier makes success genuinely uncertain. Do not roll every movement step.
> - Safely lowering a carried casualty costs time/AP. Dropping them immediately can be faster, but creates a consequence only when the actual fall, terrain, INJURY, or LIFE-THREATENING CONDITION supports one.
>
> A casualty's movement does not create generic drag/carry damage. Movement, exertion, lost treatment, or impact worsens a condition only when that condition defines the event as a DETERIORATION TRIGGER or the new event causes real additional injury.
>
> Exact take-hold, lift/lower, and movement-rate values are balance parameters. GUARDIAN, SHIELD BEARER, CORPSMAN, equipment, or assistance may improve the procedure only through an explicit mechanical delta; ordinary characters may still drag or carry casualties.
