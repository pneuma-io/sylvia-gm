# MOVEMENT

**At a glance:** Movement is AP spent on actual GRID distance and may be split freely around other actions.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - No once-per-turn MOVE ACTION.
> - Ordinary combat movement assumes urgency; no default WALK/JOG/SPRINT taxonomy.
> - Terrain, stance, burden, CLASS MOBILITY, and circumstances can modify distance:AP.
> - CROUCHED movement is slower than upright movement; PRONE movement is much slower.
> - Current base costs are 1 AP per metre STANDING, 2 AP per metre CROUCHED and 4 AP per metre PRONE. Apply the current route's actual distance and round the total cost up to whole AP, as for other movement routes.
> - Meaningful stance changes, vaulting, climbing, and similar transitions cost time/AP.
> - Triggered actions such as OVERWATCH can pause movement at the actual point of trigger.
> - No universal cover-to-cover defense bonus or opportunity-action rule.
> - Players need not predeclare an entire turn.
> - Dragging or carrying another character uses the physical modes and occupied-space rules in [[CASUALTY MOVEMENT]].
> - In theater-of-the-mind play, the GM tracks an approximate grid-equivalent position and explains inferred distance, exposure, and COVER before commitment where those consequences are not obvious.
