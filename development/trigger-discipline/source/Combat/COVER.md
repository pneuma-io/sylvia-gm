# COVER

**At a glance:** Directional physical geometry separate from RES. Protection depends on source height, STANCE, angle, material, and the amount of the target silhouette actually exposed.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - COVER is resolved from the GRID and actual LINE OF SIGHT, not from a universal "in cover" tag.
> - A low source can fully conceal a PRONE character but normally leaves part of a CROUCHED or STANDING character exposed.
> - A chest-high source can fully conceal a CROUCHED or PRONE character but leaves a STANDING character partially exposed.
> - A full wall or equivalent obstruction provides full COVER from its protected angle without requiring a lower STANCE.
> - When authoring new map geometry, use these editable physical-height presets as starting values: Quarter = 0.6 m, Half = 1.2 m, and Full = 2.4 m. These short authoring labels replace Low, Chest-high and Full-wall; they do not promise a fraction of protection.
> - These presets are authoring defaults, not protection categories. Actual source height and size, target STANCE and silhouette, and the firing angle determine the resulting protection.
> - Editing a preset changes newly authored geometry that uses it; it does not resize existing map geometry.
> - Full COVER means there is no valid direct path to the protected character from that angle. Partial COVER means an exposed silhouette remains.
> - No separate HUNKER action or state exists. If current geometry and STANCE already block the direct firing line, the character receives that protection immediately without spending AP to activate it.
> - Acting through COVER normally exposes the body or equipment needed for the action.
> - Moving between sources grants no automatic protection while crossing open space.
> - For a partially exposed target, compare the ACTUAL DIFFICULTY created by the exposed silhouette with the OPEN DIFFICULTY the same firing solution would have against the fully exposed target. A roll that fails ACTUAL DIFFICULTY but would meet OPEN DIFFICULTY is a COVER INTERCEPT: the blocking cover, not the target, is struck. A roll that fails even OPEN DIFFICULTY is a wide miss and does not automatically hit the cover.
> - Full COVER cannot be bypassed by an ordinary direct attack. A shooter must attack the cover, use a physically plausible through-material firing solution, SUPPRESS the position, or obtain another angle.
> - Height and destructibility are independent. A source may have finite COVER HP or be effectively indestructible.
> - Changes to a source normally update protection immediately, including during an unresolved sequential multi-round firing string.
> - Exception: one explosive detonation uses the pre-detonation geometry snapshot defined by BLAST GEOMETRY for all simultaneous components of that same detonation. Cover destroyed by the detonation can still protect against that detonation before the geometry updates afterward.
> - Shields and deployable barriers use the same directional framework.
