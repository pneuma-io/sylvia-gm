# PREP ACTION

**At a glance:** Spend AP during your TEAM TURN to establish a prepared posture or declared response that may resolve during the enemy TEAM TURN when its trigger occurs.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - PREP ACTIONS are the reaction model: prepared responses rather than universal free reactions.
> - AP is spent up front. There is no hidden generic reaction-AP pool.
> - The user declares the relevant posture, zone, target condition, or trigger when preparing the action.
> - Each PREP ACTION defines its own persistence, trigger, resolution, and break conditions.
> - Losing a required posture, LINE OF SIGHT, equipment state, or other prerequisite ends the prepared response when that action says it does.
> - When one event satisfies multiple prepared or other triggered effects, resolve them through TRIGGER ORDERING rather than inventing an ad hoc interrupt sequence.
> - The core example is OVERWATCH.
