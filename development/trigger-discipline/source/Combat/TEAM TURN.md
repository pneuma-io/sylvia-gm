# TEAM TURN

**At a glance:** One side acts as a team, with each character spending their own AP in any interleaved order; when that side is finished, the other side takes its TEAM TURN.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - TEAM INITIATIVE decides which side receives the opening TEAM TURN.
> - A → B → A → C is valid while those characters still have AP.
> - Each character retains their own AP pool; the team does not share one pooled currency.
> - The active side may stop using one character, act with another, and return to the first while AP remains.
> - Prepared responses such as OVERWATCH may resolve during the opposing TEAM TURN when their declared triggers occur.
> - After the active side has finished spending AP, play passes to the other side. The side order persists rather than being rerolled every round.
