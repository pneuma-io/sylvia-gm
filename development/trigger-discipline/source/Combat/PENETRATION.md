# PENETRATION

**At a glance:** Target PENETRATION is the portion of a damaging hit that bypasses the target's RES and reaches HP directly; external walls, barriers, and COVER use the separate MATERIAL PENETRATION process.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - Once a projectile or damaging effect has reached the target, PENETRATION determines how much of that damaging hit bypasses RES and reaches HP directly. The remainder attacks RES normally.
> - Once RES is gone, ordinary DAMAGE goes to HP normally.
> - High-DAMAGE/low-PEN attacks excel at stripping RES; lower-DAMAGE/high-PEN attacks can make permanent HP progress through RES.
> - PENETRATION against a target and MATERIAL PENETRATION through external objects are distinct quantities. A shot can be good at defeating personal protection while poor at punching through a wall, or vice versa.
> - HIT QUALITY improvements to effective PENETRATION apply to this target-resolution PENETRATION unless an explicit effect specifically says it improves MATERIAL PENETRATION.
> - Good placement or explicit WEAK POINTS can improve effective target penetration when physically sensible.
> - Ammunition or weapons may modify target PENETRATION, MATERIAL PENETRATION, or both, but one does not automatically imply the other.
> - Exact target-PEN formula and final display term remain open.
