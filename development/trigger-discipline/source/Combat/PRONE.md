# PRONE

**At a glance:** Very low, slow stance with strong firearm stability and a small profile, but sometimes poor firing geometry.

> [!note]- Full rules
> PRONE in the open is not COVER. It may reduce explosive exposure and improve stability, but bullets can still reach the character. Behind the wrong object, PRONE may leave no valid firing angle.
