# FRIENDLY FIRE

**At a glance:** Real when the physical action plausibly endangers allies; not a random comedy result on ordinary missed shots.

> [!note]- Full rules
> Explosives, dangerous automatic fire, and shooting through allies can create real risk. A missed pistol shot does not randomly choose a teammate because the die was low.
