# INJURY

**At a glance:** Explicit lasting harm created by a specific event, not an automatic penalty every time HP falls.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - Called shots, critical hits, explosions, and fictionally catastrophic events can create explicit INJURIES. Stabilization does not erase them. There is no universal wound table or generic HP-based death spiral.
> - An INJURY may be painful or disabling without being immediately life-threatening.
> - When an INJURY creates a mechanism that can kill the casualty, it also creates one or more [[LIFE-THREATENING CONDITION|LIFE-THREATENING CONDITIONS]]. The condition represents the lethal process; the INJURY remains the underlying damage.
> - Medical treatment may control a lethal condition without repairing the underlying INJURY. Definitive trauma care may later repair or improve the INJURY itself.
