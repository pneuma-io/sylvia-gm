# AP

**At a glance:** Each character has an AP pool representing usable combat time; AP is spent granularly on actions and movement.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - AP refreshes for eligible characters at the start of their TEAM TURN.
> - Allied AP spending can interleave freely.
> - Ordinary unused AP disappears when the TEAM TURN ends.
> - Exact pool size and action costs are intentionally open for testing.
