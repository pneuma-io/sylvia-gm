# TEAM INITIATIVE

**At a glance:** Clear fictional advantage decides the opening side; only genuinely contested openings require a TEAM INITIATIVE roll. It selects the first TEAM TURN rather than an individual order.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - A genuine successful AMBUSH automatically gets the opening TEAM TURN.
> - If both sides know a conflict is imminent and neither clearly owns the opening, roll TEAM INITIATIVE.
> - Initiative is rolled once, not every round.
> - One character should probably lead the team roll; TACTICS is expected to be highly relevant, but the exact formula is open.
> - After the opening side is established, sides alternate TEAM TURNS under that order unless an explicit rule changes it.
