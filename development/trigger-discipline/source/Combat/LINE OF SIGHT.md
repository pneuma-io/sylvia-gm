# LINE OF SIGHT

**At a glance:** Whether a character or system can physically perceive or target through current GRID geometry; it is evaluated from actual positions, stance, and obstruction.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - LINE OF SIGHT is directional and recalculated when positions, STANCE, or COVER geometry changes.
> - A descriptive lane is never a substitute for checking the actual map geometry.
> - Theater-of-the-mind play uses the GM's maintained grid-equivalent state.
