# BREACHING

**At a glance:** Defeat a physical barrier with an actual capable weapon, tool, charge, or method; the affected barrier section then changes to its authored destroyed geometry.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - There is no universal BREACH CHECK. Use the real method and its ordinary AP, ammunition, resource, DAMAGE, MATERIAL PENETRATION, setup, access, and equipment requirements.
> - Do not require a CHECK to hit a large stationary barrier at a range and angle where missing is not meaningfully possible. Targeting a small lock, hinge, frame, mounting point, or other precise component uses the appropriate CHECK and actual geometry.
> - A method incapable of defeating the material cannot succeed through repeated rolls. A capable damaging method resolves COVER damage and MATERIAL PENETRATION normally.
> - Barriers are divided into authored physical sections where the scene needs section-level destruction. Each section defines its protection and what geometry replaces it at 0 HP: removed, reduced, passable, impassable rubble, an aperture, difficult terrain, or another physically supported state.
> - A destroyed door or panel does not universally produce the same opening. The encounter/map data owns the exact resulting passability, LINE OF SIGHT, remaining COVER, and debris state.
> - Minor debris may leave a passable but movement-impeding opening until cleared. Major collapse requires an actual clearing method and is not converted into minor debris by declaration.
> - A wider breach attacks an adjacent section and still pays the method's marginal execution cost and resources. Skipping repeated setup does not grant free damage or make an incapable method capable.
>
> Exact damage conversion, debris movement/clearing costs, breaching-method profiles, and setup/resource values are balance/reference parameters.
