# HIT QUALITY

**At a glance:** Success margin produces one of four compact quality tiers: HIT, CLEAN HIT, DECISIVE HIT, or CRITICAL HIT. The tiers describe how well the firing solution connected without changing the underlying projectile into something physically stronger.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - HIT QUALITY is determined from attack success margin. Exact margin thresholds are balance parameters.
> - The four locked tiers are **HIT → CLEAN HIT → DECISIVE HIT → CRITICAL HIT**.
> - A NATURAL 20 on a legitimate attack is a CRITICAL HIT regardless of ordinary margin.
> - Better quality represents placement, timing, exposure, and stability rather than magically increasing ammunition energy.
>
> **Single-projectile attacks**
> - **HIT:** resolve the weapon's normal DAMAGE and target PENETRATION.
> - **CLEAN HIT:** resolve normal DAMAGE and improve effective **target PENETRATION** by one quality increment, representing better placement against the target's personal protection/anatomy. This does not improve MATERIAL PENETRATION through external walls, COVER, or barriers unless an explicit effect says so. Exact increment is a balance parameter.
> - **DECISIVE HIT:** gain the CLEAN HIT target-PEN improvement and improve effective DAMAGE by one quality increment. Exact increment is a balance parameter.
> - **CRITICAL HIT:** counts as a DECISIVE HIT, then resolves exactly one applicable CRITICAL EFFECT through [[CRITICAL HIT]].
> - Small target exposure raises the DIFFICULTY to connect but does not impose an arbitrary maximum HIT QUALITY on a single projectile. If an exceptional shot legitimately connects with the exposed portion, it can still be CRITICAL.
>
> **CALLED SHOTS**
> - CALLED SHOTS continue to raise DIFFICULTY in exchange for a specified physically plausible outcome.
> - A normal successful CALLED SHOT already achieves the declared targeted result; higher HIT QUALITY improves the severity/placement of the same hit rather than retroactively choosing a different target feature.
>
> **Multi-round fire**
> - BURST FIRE and FULL AUTO generally use one ATTACK ROLL. The firing mode, rounds committed, RECOIL, and HIT QUALITY together determine **CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES**.
> - HIT provides the baseline connection opportunities for that firing action; CLEAN HIT and DECISIVE HIT provide progressively more; exact conversions are balance parameters.
> - CRITICAL HIT provides high connection quality and allows exactly one connected projectile in that firing action to resolve as a CRITICAL HIT. A NATURAL 20 does not make every connected round critical.
> - Connection opportunities can never exceed rounds fired.
> - Target exposure can cap how many rounds meaningfully connect in multi-round fire. Opportunities blocked by protective geometry can become COVER impacts under [[PROJECTILE]] routing.
> - Rounds that were never connection opportunities do not automatically become hits on COVER or other objects unless another rule such as SUPPRESSION, FRIENDLY FIRE, or a swept firing area gives them a relevant physical interaction.

## Balance parameters
- HIT QUALITY margin thresholds
- CLEAN HIT target-PEN increment
- DECISIVE HIT effective-DAMAGE increment
- firing-mode baseline connection opportunities
- additional connection opportunities by HIT QUALITY
- target-exposure connection caps
