# AMBUSH

**At a glance:** A genuinely hidden side that chooses the moment of violence gets the opening TEAM TURN; the advantage comes mainly from real preparation, position, and preserved positional uncertainty rather than a giant SURPRISED debuff.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - Pre-combat AIM, weapon state, posture, and prepared positions remain real when the fiction supports them.
> - An ambush can fail before shots are fired if the target detects and CONFIRMS the attackers.
> - Individual awareness may reduce how badly someone is caught without automatically stealing TEAM INITIATIVE.
> - UNAWARE and SUSPICIOUS opponents can plainly still be ambushed when the attackers' positions remain hidden.
> - APPROXIMATE or LAST KNOWN information can still permit an ambush from an actual attack position the defender has not CONFIRMED.
> - CONFIRMED knowledge generally prevents that attacker from counting as genuinely hidden for the ambush while the confirmation remains valid.
> - The DM does not retroactively invent readiness or information that was not present.
