# OVERWATCH

**At a glance:** Establish an OW ZONE. If an enemy crosses that zone in valid line of sight during their turn, the watcher immediately makes a normal ATTACK ROLL; after resolution, the enemy turn continues.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - OVERWATCH is a triggered normal attack, not a saving throw or universal interrupt framework.
> - The watcher declares an arc or area on the GRID rather than watching the entire battlefield.
> - Movement is interrupted at the point the OW trigger occurs so the attack resolves against the real position at that instant.
> - If the target remains capable of continuing afterward, their turn proceeds.
> - SUPPRESSION does **not** automatically cancel OVERWATCH or force the watcher into protection. If the watcher remains exposed inside active SUPPRESSION, relevant exposure events resolve normally and may injure, displace, incapacitate, or otherwise remove the prerequisites needed to keep OVERWATCH. Choosing to take protection can also break the LINE OF SIGHT or posture required by the prepared shot.
> - Multiple same-moment OVERWATCH, SUPPRESSION, protective PREP, or other triggers resolve through TRIGGER ORDERING. Exact arc representation and shot allowance remain balance/implementation parameters.
