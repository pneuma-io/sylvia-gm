# STANCE

**At a glance:** Persistent physical posture: STANDING, CROUCHED, or PRONE.

> [!note]- Full rules
> Lower stances generally improve firearm stability and change silhouette/cover interaction. Stance transitions cost time/AP. A stance never lets a character shoot through geometry that physically blocks the weapon.

## Current map defaults

Editable body heights are 1.8 m STANDING, 1.15 m CROUCHED and 0.45 m PRONE. Changing
stance updates the body's map height and firing position as well as its posture.
Authored character size and movement adjustments are retained proportionally between
these base profiles; stance changes do not reset a character to the default build.

**Provisional:** any voluntary change costs 1 AP, including standing up from PRONE.
Selecting the current stance does not spend AP. A forced TRIP or THROW applies PRONE
as part of that action, without a separate voluntary stance cost. The 1-AP transition
cost remains open for later balance review.
