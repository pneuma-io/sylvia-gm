# RES

**At a glance:** RESILIENCE is the outer survivability pool: armor, grazing, bracing, defensive skill, evasiveness, and similar easier-to-recover protection.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - Ordinary DAMAGE hits RES before HP.
> - Cover and external barriers are separate.
> - Body armor contributes to RES rather than creating another personal armor-health bar.
> - No passive in-combat RES regeneration by default, though explicit abilities/equipment may provide it.
> - When genuinely safe between encounters, full RES restoration can often be normal.
> - RES may scale more strongly with LEVEL than HP.
