# RESUSCITATION

**At a glance:** MEDICINE action for a reversible FAILED LIFE-THREATENING CONDITION when a real intervention can restore the failed vital function.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - RESUSCITATION is not a generic action used merely because a character is DOWNED. A reversible FAILED [[LIFE-THREATENING CONDITION]] must actually exist.
> - The acting character needs sufficient diagnosis/information, physical access, appropriate equipment/method, and a still-valid reversible path supported by the Campaign Setting's medical capability.
> - Spend the required AP/time and make a MEDICINE CHECK when success is uncertain.
> - Success normally changes the targeted condition from **FAILED → STABILIZED**, representing restoration of the failed vital function into a medically controlled state.
> - Success does not automatically restore lost HP, remove the underlying INJURY, restore RES, or end DOWNED.
> - Failure spends the attempted time/resources. The actual condition and reversible window determine whether further attempts remain possible.
> - Unsurvivable destruction, missing anatomy required for life, conditions beyond available technology, or a genuinely passed reversible window cannot be overridden by a successful roll.
> - Any character may attempt RESUSCITATION when training, equipment, access, and fiction support it. RESUSCITATOR progression improves speed, reliability, and achievable outcomes rather than granting permission.
> - Exact AP, DIFFICULTY, equipment, reversible-window, and treatment-effect values are balance/reference-data parameters.
