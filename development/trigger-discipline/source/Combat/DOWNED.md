# DOWNED

**At a glance:** Reaching 0 HP removes normal combat effectiveness without automatically causing death, unconsciousness, or a universal death countdown.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - At 0 HP a character becomes DOWNED and remains at 0 HP until an effect capable of restoring HP raises them above it.
> - DOWNED removes the character's normal full AP turn unless an explicit effect says otherwise.
> - Consciousness, speech, crawling, self-aid, comms, and similar limited activity depend on the actual INJURY and physical condition rather than a universal DOWNED script.
> - DOWNED by itself does not mean the casualty is actively dying. Life-threatening deterioration is represented by one or more [[LIFE-THREATENING CONDITION|LIFE-THREATENING CONDITIONS]] created by actual injuries or failures.
> - A DOWNED casualty with at least one ACTIVE or FAILED life-threatening condition is on [[DEATH'S DOOR]]. DEATH'S DOOR has no independent countdown or HP pool.
> - [[STABILIZE]] controls a specific immediate life threat; it does not automatically restore HP, erase the underlying INJURY, restore RES, or return the casualty to combat.
> - [[RESUSCITATION]] applies only when a reversible vital failure actually exists and the fiction/technology support an intervention.
> - DOWNED normally ends only when all immediate life threats are sufficiently controlled, HP has been restored above 0 by an appropriate effect or treatment, and no INJURY explicitly prevents normal participation. Explicit abilities may create narrower exceptions.
> - Further harm resolves normally. It may create or worsen INJURIES, create new life-threatening conditions, cause a condition to fail, or kill immediately when the resulting physical damage is genuinely unsurvivable. There is no special EXECUTE action or helpless-target damage multiplier.
> - DOWNED characters can be captured, dragged, carried, rescued, treated, or become a medical-evacuation objective through [[CASUALTY MOVEMENT]] and the actual medical rules.
