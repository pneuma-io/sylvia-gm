# DESTRUCTIBLE COVER

**At a glance:** A COVER source may have finite HP or be effectively indestructible. Real projectile impacts can wear away weak protection, and geometry updates when the source actually changes.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - Destructibility is a configurable property of each COVER source. It is not determined by height alone.
> - Material and construction inform whether a source is indestructible for the scene or receives a COVER HP value.
> - A metal wall may be effectively indestructible while a wooden pallet of similar height may have limited HP.
> - Any projectile that physically strikes damageable cover may damage it. This includes deliberate attacks on the cover, COVER INTERCEPTS from attacks against partially exposed targets, SUPPRESSION fire striking the defended position, blocked connection opportunities from multi-round fire, and penetrating rounds passing through.
> - A failed ATTACK ROLL does not automatically damage nearby cover. Only a projectile whose resolved path actually intersects the cover creates an impact.
> - MATERIAL PENETRATION can allow an effect to continue through the source after resolving its interaction with the material.
> - At 0 COVER HP, the source is destroyed or altered enough that its current protection is removed or reduced.
> - Where a source is a barrier or access point, its authored section state determines the passability, debris, aperture, and other changed geometry under [[BREACHING]].
> - Sequential projectile resolution updates geometry immediately: in multi-round fire, later unresolved projectiles use the new geometry after an earlier projectile destroys or deforms the cover.
> - A single explosive detonation is different. BLAST GEOMETRY snapshots the pre-detonation geometry for all simultaneous components of that same detonation; cover destroyed by the blast can still protect against that blast, then its changed geometry applies afterward.
> - Exact COVER HP, projectile-to-cover damage conversion, material resistance, and residual projectile values remain balance/reference parameters.
