# CALLED SHOT

**At a glance:** Deliberately target a specific smaller or harder feature, raising DIFFICULTY in exchange for the logical targeted outcome.

> [!note]- Full rules
> CALLED SHOTS are generally available. They do not automatically grant bonus DAMAGE. They can target weapons, hands, legs, devices, armor gaps, or known WEAK POINTS when physically plausible.
