# DEATH'S DOOR

**At a glance:** Umbrella critical state for a DOWNED casualty who has at least one ACTIVE or FAILED [[LIFE-THREATENING CONDITION]].

> [!note]- Full rules
> - DEATH'S DOOR does not have its own HP, save track, countdown, or universal worsening rule.
> - The underlying LIFE-THREATENING CONDITION determines what is killing the casualty, what makes it worsen, what can stabilize it, and whether a reversible path to survival exists.
> - Different casualties on DEATH'S DOOR can therefore have radically different urgency and treatment requirements.
> - A casualty leaves DEATH'S DOOR when no life-threatening condition remains ACTIVE or FAILED. They may still be DOWNED, seriously injured, unconscious, or otherwise unable to fight.
> - A FAILED condition is not automatically irreversible death. [[RESUSCITATION]] may restore a reversible failed vital function when the cause, timing, equipment, and Campaign Setting medical capability make that physically possible.
> - Unsurvivable destruction, missing anatomy required for life, conditions beyond available technology, or a genuinely passed reversible window can result in death without a generic death-save sequence.
