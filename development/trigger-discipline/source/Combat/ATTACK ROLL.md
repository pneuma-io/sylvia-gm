# ATTACK ROLL

**At a glance:** The attacker rolls to determine whether and how well a projectile or other attack connects; there is no universal defender dodge roll against bullets.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - Core firearm pipeline: ATTACK ROLL → HIT QUALITY → PROJECTILE routing → DAMAGE / target PENETRATION → RES / HP. External material encountered during PROJECTILE routing uses MATERIAL PENETRATION before the target step.
> - Target situation, exposure, geometry, RANGE, AIM, RECOIL, and other relevant states affect the firing solution.
> - NATURAL 1 is an automatic miss. NATURAL 20 is an automatic CRITICAL HIT when the attack was legitimately possible.
> - Against partial COVER, resolve both OPEN DIFFICULTY and ACTUAL DIFFICULTY. Meeting ACTUAL DIFFICULTY hits the exposed target; failing ACTUAL but meeting OPEN creates a COVER INTERCEPT against the blocking geometry; failing OPEN is a wide miss unless another explicit procedure determines a physical intersection.
> - A failed attack does not automatically deal chip DAMAGE. A miss can still damage physical cover only when projectile routing establishes that the cover was actually struck.
> - Full COVER with no valid direct path cannot be targeted by an ordinary direct attack. Through-material fire requires a plausible location/firing solution and resolves MATERIAL PENETRATION before any target PENETRATION / RES / HP consequence.
