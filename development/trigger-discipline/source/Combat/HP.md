# HP

**At a glance:** Actual bodily injury and deeper harm; harder and slower to recover than RES.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - HP does not automatically reset between encounters.
> - Losing HP does not automatically create a stacking action-penalty death spiral.
> - Explicit INJURIES arise from specific effects such as called shots, criticals, explosions, or catastrophic events.
> - Recovery speed depends partly on Campaign Setting medical capability.
> - At 0 HP a character becomes DOWNED rather than automatically dead.
