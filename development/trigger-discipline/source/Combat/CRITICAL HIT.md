# CRITICAL HIT

**At a glance:** A CRITICAL HIT is the highest HIT QUALITY: it includes DECISIVE HIT benefits, then adds exactly one physically supported critical effect rather than merely multiplying damage.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - A NATURAL 20 on a legitimate attack guarantees CRITICAL HIT quality.
> - A CRITICAL HIT first resolves all DECISIVE HIT benefits from [[HIT QUALITY]], then resolves exactly one CRITICAL EFFECT.
> - Choose one applicable effect:
>   - **DEEP HIT:** further improve effective **target PENETRATION**.
>   - **DEVASTATING HIT:** further improve effective DAMAGE.
>   - **CONTEXT EFFECT:** resolve one explicit physically applicable secondary consequence supported by the thing actually struck.
> - DEEP HIT does not improve MATERIAL PENETRATION through external walls, COVER, or barriers unless an explicit weapon/ammunition tag or ability specifically says it does.
> - CONTEXT EFFECT is not permission to invent an unrelated effect after seeing the roll. It must arise from an already-established source such as a CALLED SHOT, known WEAK POINT, weapon CRITICAL TAG, target/component CRITICAL TAG, or obvious physical object interaction.
> - Examples of possible tagged/contextual consequences include KNOCKDOWN, SHATTER, DISABLE, escalation of a specified limb/weapon/device CALLED SHOT, or catastrophic damage to an exposed component. The relevant attack/target must actually support the consequence.
> - If no CONTEXT EFFECT applies, DEEP HIT or DEVASTATING HIT always remains available.
> - A CRITICAL does not retroactively redirect a CALLED SHOT to another feature. It may escalate the severity of the feature that was actually targeted and hit.
> - Explicit INJURIES may result when the critical effect, called location, weapon, and fiction support one; there is no universal critical wound table.
> - During BURST FIRE or FULL AUTO, CRITICAL quality allows exactly one connected projectile in the firing action to resolve as a CRITICAL HIT unless an explicit ability says otherwise. Other connected rounds resolve at their ordinary quality.

## Balance parameters
- DEEP HIT target-PEN increment
- DEVASTATING HIT effective-DAMAGE increment
- exact tagged/contextual effect magnitudes
