# CLASS MOBILITY

**At a glance:** DISTANCE:AP can differ by CLASS from Level 1 because battlefield roles demand different amounts of traversal.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - GHOST is unusually mobile because flanking requires crossing meaningful distance.
> - BULWARK is deliberately slow because the archetype is heavy and positional.
> - SHARPSHOOTER uses ordinary mobility; the role tends to stay established because its tactics reward it, not because of a special short-hop mechanic.
> - Progression may deepen these identities.
