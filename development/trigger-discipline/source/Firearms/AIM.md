# AIM

**At a glance:** Spend AP/time improving a specific firing solution against a target or position.

> [!note]- Full rules
> AIM is not a permanent banked bonus and cannot erase a weapon's physical RANGE/HANDLING limits. It can support automatic fire, though accumulating RECOIL progressively degrades the firing solution. Legitimate pre-combat AIM persists into an ambush when the shooter has actually maintained the solution.
