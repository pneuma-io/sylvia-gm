# FIREARM MANIPULATION

**At a glance:** A firearm manipulation uses the operation's real physical steps. Any character may use a physically possible one-handed workaround, but doing so costs extra AP unless an ability reduces that surcharge.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - A firearm manipulation uses the ordinary AP cost, required equipment, and physical steps of the operation being performed. It does not erase magazine, chamber, mechanism, inventory, or interruption state.
> - Any character may perform the manipulation one-handed when the firearm, equipment, body position, and surrounding geometry provide a real one-handed method.
> - A one-handed method adds an AP surcharge to the operation. Abilities may reduce that surcharge, but the final cost cannot be lower than the ordinary two-handed cost.
> - If an operation literally requires two hands and no physical workaround exists, it cannot be performed one-handed. An ability that improves one-handed manipulation does not create missing equipment, a free hand, or an impossible method.
