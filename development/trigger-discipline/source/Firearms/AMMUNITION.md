# AMMUNITION

**At a glance:** Real consumable rounds; magazine contents matter in combat while loose identical rounds may be pooled outside magazines.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - Different ammunition types remain separate resources.
> - Ammunition construction may alter target PENETRATION, MATERIAL PENETRATION, or both. Armor-piercing ammunition should primarily change one or both penetration behaviors rather than simply adding generic DAMAGE.
> - Improving target PENETRATION does not automatically improve passage through external COVER, and improving MATERIAL PENETRATION does not automatically increase the amount of a hit that bypasses RES.
> - Special ammunition details are for later design or Campaign Settings.
