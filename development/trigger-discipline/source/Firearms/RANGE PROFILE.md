# RANGE PROFILE

**At a glance:** Each firearm has its own useful-range behavior rather than sharing one universal far-equals-bad curve.

> [!note]- Full rules
> Pistols/SMGs are naturally strong close; long precision rifles can be awkward at very close range; shotgun concentration declines with range; rifle DAMAGE does not automatically fall at moderate longer ranges. Exact representation remains open.
