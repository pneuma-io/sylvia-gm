# BURST FIRE

**At a glance:** A mechanically controlled packet of rounds that is more time-efficient than equivalent fully deliberate semi-auto shots and more predictable/easier to control than manually feathering FULL AUTO to the same count.

> [!note]- Full rules
> One continuous burst generally uses one ATTACK ROLL. Rounds fired remain real and cap possible connected hits. HIT QUALITY helps determine connections while RECOIL limits returns. Exact connection, RECOIL, AP/time, and exposure-cap values are balance parameters.
