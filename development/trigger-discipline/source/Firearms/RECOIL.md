# RECOIL

**At a glance:** Temporary attack penalty/state accumulated by firing faster than control recovers.

> [!note]- Full rules
> Time/AP passage lets RECOIL recover naturally. Stance, bracing, weapon design, and expertise can improve control. RECOIL persists between immediate firing actions rather than resetting after every ATTACK ROLL.
