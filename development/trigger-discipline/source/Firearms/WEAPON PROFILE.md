# WEAPON PROFILE

**At a glance:** Locked ruleset-owned schema describing how a firearm physically behaves; Campaign Settings supply the specific named weapons and values.

> [!note]- Full rules
> Every firearm profile uses the following core fields:
> - **DAMAGE** — harmful output carried by a connected projectile before target protection resolves.
> - **TARGET PENETRATION** — how much of a damaging hit can bypass target RES once the projectile reaches the target.
> - **MATERIAL PENETRATION** — how effectively the projectile defeats external walls, COVER, barriers, shields, and other intervening material during PROJECTILE routing.
> - **RANGE PROFILE** — weapon-specific useful-range behavior rather than one universal distance penalty curve.
> - **RECOIL** — control cost generated by firing tempo/volume and recovered through time, AP, posture, weapon design, and expertise.
> - **CYCLIC RATE** — physically available firing tempo where relevant.
> - **CAPACITY / FEED** — ammunition capacity and feed system, including magazine, belt, tube, cylinder, or other mechanism.
> - **FIRE MODES** — mechanically available firing modes such as semi-auto, burst, or full auto.
> - **MECHANISM** — operating system relevant to chambering/cycling and weapon state.
> - **RELOAD / CYCLE** — physical reload and cycling behavior, including steps that can be interrupted.
> - **HANDLING** — how quick or awkward the weapon is to bring to bear, transition, manipulate, and use in constrained/contact-range situations.
>
> Optional fields are used only when the weapon actually needs them:
> - **SIGNATURE** — normalized noise, flash, heat, electronic emission, or similar detectable output where the Campaign Setting needs explicit values/tags.
> - **CRITICAL TAGS** — defined physically supported CONTEXT EFFECTS available on a CRITICAL HIT. A weapon without a relevant tag still uses DEEP HIT or DEVASTATING HIT.
> - **RELIABILITY / MALFUNCTION TAGS** — explicit poor-quality, damaged, contaminated, incompatible, or otherwise unusual reliability behavior. Maintained quality firearms do not receive a universal random jam roll.
> - **SPECIAL AMMUNITION COMPATIBILITY** — restrictions or interactions for ammunition types whose target PENETRATION, MATERIAL PENETRATION, DAMAGE, SIGNATURE, or other physical behavior differs.
>
> The schema does **not** include a generic weapon QUALITY tier or universal `+X` equipment bonus. Better or different equipment changes the physical fields that actually differ.
>
> Exact field values, numeric scales, and named Campaign Setting weapons are balance/reference data rather than part of this schema lock.
