# MAGAZINE

**At a glance:** A persistent physical object that retains its remaining ordered ammunition when removed from a weapon.

> [!note]- Full rules
> Partial magazines remain partial, dropped magazines remain in the environment, and mixed ammunition order can matter. Safe downtime may repack magazines as a convenience operation.
