# SHOTGUN

**At a glance:** Resolve pellet spread through projectile concentration rather than individual attack rolls for every pellet.

> [!note]- Full rules
> Close range can concentrate many pellets into one target; increasing RANGE spreads the pattern and reduces concentration. A spread can physically encompass multiple things without simulating every pellet trajectory.
