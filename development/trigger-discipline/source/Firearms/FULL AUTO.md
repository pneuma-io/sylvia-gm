# FULL AUTO

**At a glance:** Flexible continuous fire resolved with one ATTACK ROLL; HIT QUALITY creates a limited number of connection opportunities that then route through actual exposure and geometry.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - One continuous firing action generally uses one ATTACK ROLL, not one d20 per bullet.
> - More rounds increase possible connections but generate increasing RECOIL and diminishing returns.
> - HIT QUALITY helps determine a number of **CONNECTION OPPORTUNITIES** for the firing string; this can never exceed rounds actually fired.
> - Target exposure can cap how many connection opportunities can physically connect with the target's exposed silhouette.
> - Connection opportunities blocked by protective geometry can become real COVER impacts when projectile routing places those rounds into the blocking source.
> - Rounds that were not connection opportunities do not automatically become cover hits or hits on nearby characters. They remain stray fire unless SUPPRESSION, FRIENDLY FIRE, a sweep, or another explicit procedure gives them a relevant physical interaction.
> - Resolve relevant projectiles in firing order. If an earlier round destroys or deforms COVER, later unresolved projectiles in the same firing action use the updated geometry.
> - FULL AUTO may sweep multiple targets/positions by dividing firing time/rounds across the sweep.
> - There should be no universally solved optimal round count.
> - Exact HIT QUALITY-to-connection conversion, exposure caps, RECOIL, AP/time, and ammunition-efficiency values remain balance parameters.
