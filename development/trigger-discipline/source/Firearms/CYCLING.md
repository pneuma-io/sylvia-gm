# CYCLING

**At a glance:** Physically move the firearm action/feed so the next round state changes according to the weapon mechanism.

> [!note]- Full rules
> Bolt, pump, and lever cycling can be manual AP operations; automatic cycling is a real advantage of self-loading firearms. Exact AP costs are open.
