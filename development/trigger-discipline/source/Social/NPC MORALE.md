# NPC MORALE

**At a glance:** NPCs can surrender, hesitate, retreat, negotiate, or break when actual circumstances support it; there is no morale HP bar.

> [!note]- Full rules
> Leadership loss, betrayal, encirclement, bad odds, and similar events can change surrender/INFLUENCE difficulty. PCs retain agency; a generic morale roll does not force a player character to flee.
