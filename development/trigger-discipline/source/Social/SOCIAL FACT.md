# SOCIAL FACT

**At a glance:** Persistent information about a person, relationship, desire, fear, institution, or circumstance that may create LEVERAGE or inform a social approach.

> [!note]- Full rules
> SOCIAL FACTS can be wrong, outdated, incomplete, or planted as disinformation. LEVERAGE is only as good as the underlying truth and relevance.
