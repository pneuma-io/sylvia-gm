# EVADER

**At a glance:** GHOST specialty that breaks contact, makes enemy information stale, and re-enters the fight from a new uncertain position.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - EVADER does not grant invisibility or permission to disengage. It improves the real sequence break LOS → re-enter uncertainty → reposition → re-engage.
> - Exact STEALTH, AP, movement, and attack values remain balance parameters.
>
> **ENTRY — BREAK CONTACT**
> - After movement or geometry causes every enemy currently observing the character's exact position to lose LOS, the next STEALTH attempt to re-enter an uncertain/hidden information state costs 1 AP instead of the ordinary 2 AP and applies -2 DIFFICULTY. Every exact observer must truly lose LOS. If even one still directly sees the character, contact is not broken and the STEALTH attempt cannot use this Entry.
>
> **TIER 1 — Lose the eyes**
> - **VANISHING MOVE:** Immediately after breaking LOS, the first movement toward COVER or CONCEALMENT that supports BREAK CONTACT costs less AP.
> - **CUT THE CORNER:** When solid geometry rather than temporary concealment breaks LOS, the BREAK CONTACT attempt gains additional effectiveness. The benefit comes from leaving no visible transition to follow.
> - **FALSE TRAIL:** After a successful BREAK CONTACT, attempts to determine the new route from indirect evidence become harder. Plausible alternative paths must actually exist.
>
> **TIER 2 — Put distance behind the disappearance**
> - **EXTENDED REPOSITION:** After a successful BREAK CONTACT, the first movement while remaining hidden covers more distance for the AP spent. Moving into direct observation still reveals the character normally.
> - **SLIP THE FIRE:** When movement triggers a SUPPRESSION ATTACK, sacrifice part of the movement distance gained from that AP to increase the attack's DIFFICULTY through deliberate evasive movement. This does not help while remaining exposed or performing another exposed action.
> - **HARD TO REACQUIRE:** Until the EVADER attacks or is directly observed again, enemy attempts specifically to determine the exact current position after BREAK CONTACT suffer increased difficulty. Enemies may still know the EVADER is somewhere nearby.
>
> **TIER 3 — Turn escape back into offense**
> - **AMBUSH AGAIN:** The first attack after successfully using BREAK CONTACT and reaching a new hidden position gains improved attack effectiveness. It does not automatically CRITICAL or ignore COVER.
> - **EVASIVE BREAK:** Gain a PREP ACTION that reserves limited movement. When a declared enemy enters LOS to the EVADER's position, use that prepared movement to attempt to break LOS before that enemy takes a subsequent action. It does not retroactively interrupt an attack already resolved.
> - **BREAK PURSUIT:** After a successful BREAK CONTACT, changing direction or crossing into another concealed route while pursued gains improved STEALTH effectiveness. The benefit ends once directly reacquired.
>
> **TIER 4 — Make the old position dangerous to trust**
> - **RAPID REENTRY:** After making an attack from a hidden position, if the EVADER subsequently breaks LOS during the same TEAM TURN, the next BREAK CONTACT attempt costs less AP. Real geometry capable of breaking LOS is still required.
> - **NOWHERE THERE:** If an enemy spends an attack, SUPPRESSION action, or other meaningful effort against the EVADER's stale last-known position and fails to reacquire them, that enemy's next attempt to locate the exact position becomes harder.
> - **GHOST WALK:** After a successful BREAK CONTACT, DISTANCE:AP while moving covertly improves until the EVADER attacks or is directly observed again. This does not permit visible open-ground sprinting to remain unnoticed.
>
> **TIER 5 KEYSTONE — CAN'T PIN ME**
> - The full loop becomes attack → break LOS → BREAK CONTACT → reposition → attack again. After attacking from an uncertain/hidden position, movement specifically used to break LOS is more efficient; a successful BREAK CONTACT restores GHOST WALK; and the next attack from the new position can benefit from AMBUSH AGAIN. The loop still requires actual geometry/concealment, AP, successful STEALTH where needed, a route, and a firing solution. Surrounding, maintained LOS, sealed exits, or correct prediction stops it.
