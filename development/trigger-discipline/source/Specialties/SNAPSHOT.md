# SNAPSHOT

**At a glance:** SHARPSHOOTER specialty that compresses the time between seeing a distant opportunity and building a credible long-range firing solution.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - SNAPSHOT remains a long-range precision specialty, not close-range run-and-gun.
> - Exact AIM, RANGE, AP, HIT QUALITY, and target PENETRATION values remain balance parameters.
>
> **ENTRY — QUICK SOLUTION**
> - The first AIM performed against a target the SNAPSHOT specialist has not yet attacked during the current TEAM TURN costs less AP. Changing targets can therefore be faster than for an ordinary shooter.
>
> **TIER 1 — Get onto the shot**
> - **SNAP BRACE:** When moving into a physically valid supported firing stance, combine the final stance/bracing transition with beginning AIM for reduced total AP. The position must still actually be reached.
> - **HASTY ZERO:** The first long-range attack after QUICK SOLUTION suffers less difficulty from RANGE than an ordinary hastily aimed shot. The firearm's actual ballistic capability is unchanged.
> - **OPENING WINDOW:** If a target's COVER is destroyed, reduced, or physically moved away from them earlier in the current TEAM TURN, the first QUICK-SOLUTION attack against them gains improved attack effectiveness. Their new COVER state still applies normally.
>
> **TIER 2 — Change solutions quickly**
> - **TARGET SWITCH:** After firing at one target, the first AIM against a different visible long-range target costs less AP.
> - **REACQUIRE:** If a previously engaged target breaks LOS and then reappears near its previous position, the first AIM after reacquiring them costs less AP. A completely new position requires a new solution.
> - **QUICK CALLED SHOT:** When making a CALLED SHOT after QUICK SOLUTION, reduce part of the additional DIFFICULTY imposed by the called target. The selected area must still be visible.
>
> **TIER 3 — Exploit the brief window**
> - **SECOND SIGHT PICTURE:** If a QUICK-SOLUTION attack misses and the target remains visible, the next AIM against that same target costs less AP. The shooter is correcting an existing solution rather than rebuilding it from nothing.
> - **SHOOT AND SHIFT:** After resolving a long-range attack that benefited from QUICK SOLUTION, the first movement toward another firing position costs less AP. This is positional repositioning, not firing while moving.
> - **NEXT TARGET:** If a QUICK-SOLUTION attack DOWNS or otherwise removes the target from the immediate fight, the first AIM against a different visible target costs even less AP.
>
> **TIER 4 — Make hasty fire dangerous**
> - **HASTY FULL SOLUTION:** QUICK SOLUTION provides a larger portion of the attack benefit normally associated with spending more time on AIM. The benefit applies only to the first shot against that target.
> - **SNAP PENETRATION:** The first attack after QUICK SOLUTION gains additional **target PENETRATION** on a successful hit. It does not improve MATERIAL PENETRATION through external COVER or walls; those still use the projectile's material capability unless another explicit effect applies.
> - **DECISIVE WINDOW:** The first successful QUICK-SOLUTION attack against a newly exposed target gains improved HIT QUALITY. A target already sitting exposed all turn does not count as a newly opened opportunity.
>
> **TIER 5 KEYSTONE — INSTANT SOLUTION**
> - Once per TEAM TURN, when acquiring a new valid long-range firing solution, reach the effectiveness of a substantially more developed ordinary AIM while spending only a fraction of its normal preparation time. It applies to one shot. Continued fire requires normal AIM, DEEP AIM remains SNIPER territory, sustained OVERWATCH remains SENTINEL territory, and movement, COVER, RANGE, ammunition, and firearm state still matter.
