# SHOCK

**At a glance:** BREACHER specialty that breaks an organized defensive plan through disruption, forced movement, close SUPPRESSION, grenades, and immediate exploitation.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - SHOCK does not own grenades or SUPPRESSION; it gains stronger conversion from real disruption into movement and follow-up pressure.
> - SUPPRESSION is active fire against a position, not a SUPPRESSED character state.
> - Exact AP, DAMAGE, HIT QUALITY, SUPPRESSION, and movement values remain balance parameters.
>
> **ENTRY — ASSAULT MOMENTUM**
> - When one of the SHOCK specialist's actions actually causes an enemy to leave COVER, be forcibly repositioned, or places the enemy's currently occupied position under the specialist's active SUPPRESSION, the specialist's next movement toward that enemy or the position they are being pressured out of costs less AP. The benefit must be used as immediate exploitation and cannot be banked.
>
> **TIER 1 — Make the opening hurt**
> - **CLOSE SUPPRESSION:** At close engagement distances, establishing SUPPRESSION requires less ammunition/time. A suitable firearm and real firing line are still required.
> - **EXPLOIT EXPOSURE:** When the specialist's action moves an enemy from better COVER to worse COVER or exposes them from a previously protected angle, the specialist's next attack against that enemy gains improved attack effectiveness. New geometry still applies normally.
> - **SHOVE THROUGH:** After successfully SHOVING an enemy out of a position, moving into the space they vacated costs less AP. The destination must be physically occupiable.
>
> **TIER 2 — Turn disruption into opportunity**
> - **ASSAULT WINDOW:** When ASSAULT MOMENTUM triggers, choose one nearby ally. Their next movement toward the same disrupted position also costs less AP.
> - **FOLLOWING FIRE:** If an enemy leaves the specialist's SUPPRESSION and moves to another nearby position that remains within the firing solution, shifting the SUPPRESSION to threaten the new position requires less additional ammunition/time than establishing it from scratch. LOS and weapon orientation still matter.
> - **GRENADE FOLLOW-UP:** If one of the specialist's grenades actually makes an enemy move, change STANCE, or lose useful COVER, the next firearm or CQC action against one affected target costs less AP. A grenade that produces no qualifying disruption grants nothing.
>
> **TIER 3 — Keep the defense unstable**
> - **COMBINED PRESSURE:** After establishing SUPPRESSION against a position, a subsequent grenade action against that same defended position costs less AP, or vice versa. Both actions still consume their normal ammunition/equipment.
> - **MOMENTUM CHAIN:** If ASSAULT MOMENTUM is used to advance and the next action from the new position disrupts another enemy position, gain another momentum movement discount. Failure to create a new disruption ends the chain.
> - **DISLOCATE:** Successful SHOVE and THROW effects produce greater forced displacement than normal. The CQC action must still succeed and the destination geometry must be valid.
>
> **TIER 4 — Prevent the reset**
> - **BREAK THE RHYTHM:** An enemy personally displaced by the specialist, or currently occupying a position under active SUPPRESSION established by the specialist, requires additional AP/time to establish their next PREP ACTION while the specialist continues maintaining relevant pressure. The effect expires after the enemy's next TEAM TURN or when the relevant pressure/firing solution is lost.
> - **ROLLING ASSAULT:** ASSAULT WINDOW may affect additional nearby allies, and a successful MOMENTUM CHAIN can refresh their movement benefit toward the new disrupted position. Everyone still spends their own AP.
> - **HARD PUSH:** After moving using ASSAULT MOMENTUM, the specialist's next close-range firearm attack against an enemy responsible for that momentum gains improved HIT QUALITY. No qualifying disruption means no benefit.
>
> **TIER 5 KEYSTONE — BREAK THE LINE**
> - ASSAULT MOMENTUM becomes a cascading assault sequence. Each time the specialist creates a genuine disruption, advances into that opening, and successfully disrupts a new enemy or position, the efficiency on the next advance and disruption refreshes. The chain ends on failure, lack of AP/resources, blocked geometry, or no new position to collapse; it cannot be farmed indefinitely against one target.
