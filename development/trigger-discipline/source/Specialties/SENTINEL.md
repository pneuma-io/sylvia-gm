# SENTINEL

**At a glance:** SHARPSHOOTER specialty that establishes a long-range firing position and controls a sightline through prepared fire.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - SENTINEL does not own OVERWATCH or AIM. It combines preparation, long sightlines, and repeated valid triggers more efficiently.
> - SUPPRESSION is active fire and does not automatically cancel OVERWATCH; SENTINEL nodes must add a real delta beyond that baseline.
> - Exact AP, sector size, trigger count, attack, and SUPPRESSION values remain balance parameters.
>
> **ENTRY — PREPARED SIGHTLINE**
> - When stationary, combine establishing OVERWATCH with one ordinary AIM step for less total AP than performing the two actions separately. OVERWATCH still requires a declared zone and valid LOS.
>
> **TIER 1 — Establish the position**
> - **BROAD SECTOR:** The OVERWATCH ZONE may cover a larger connected area than normal. Walls and other blocking geometry still divide the sector.
> - **STABLE NEST:** When firing OVERWATCH from a physically supported position, the attack gains improved effectiveness. Leaving the support removes the benefit.
> - **FAST SETUP:** Establishing OVERWATCH from a stationary position costs less AP when the SENTINEL has not moved since beginning the current activation.
>
> **TIER 2 — Track the lane**
> - **TRACKING SECTOR:** Shift an existing OVERWATCH ZONE into an adjacent connected area for less AP than completely rebuilding it. LOS into the new sector is still required.
> - **CROSSING SHOT:** OVERWATCH attacks triggered specifically by movement through the watched zone gain improved attack effectiveness.
> - **SECOND TRIGGER:** After resolving an OVERWATCH shot, the preparation may remain active for another valid trigger. Exact trigger count is a balance parameter; every shot still consumes ammunition and generates RECOIL normally.
>
> **TIER 3 — Control what happens there**
> - **COUNTER-PREP:** OVERWATCH may also be configured to trigger when an enemy in the watched area exposes themselves to establish AIM, OVERWATCH, or another firing PREP ACTION. It does not trigger on actions performed entirely behind full COVER.
> - **SUPPRESSIVE WATCH:** After resolving an OVERWATCH shot, commit additional ammunition to establish SUPPRESSION along the relevant watched route. Geometry and firearm must support the suppression.
> - **LINKED SECTOR:** When the SENTINEL already has OVERWATCH established, one ally setting up an adjacent or overlapping OVERWATCH ZONE pays less AP to establish theirs. The ally still uses their own weapon, LOS, AP, and PREP ACTION.
>
> **TIER 4 — Refuse the sightline**
> - **HOLD UNDER FIRE:** When the same enemy firing action both triggers your OVERWATCH and would establish or resolve SUPPRESSION against you, your prepared shot resolves first. If the result removes the enemy's ability to establish the fire, it never begins; otherwise SUPPRESSION resolves normally.
> - **DEEP SECTOR:** The watched area can extend farther along a continuous sightline than normal. This improves depth, not the ability to see around corners.
> - **CONTINUOUS WATCH:** Retained OVERWATCH can resolve additional triggers before the preparation ends. Every shot still deals with ammunition, magazine state, RECOIL, and actual weapon cycling.
>
> **TIER 5 KEYSTONE — KILL ZONE**
> - Establish a developed OVERWATCH position covering multiple connected watched areas within one physically plausible field of fire. The position includes prepared AIM, supports multiple valid triggers, and can shift between connected parts of the watched sector more efficiently. It does **not** gain a special immunity to SUPPRESSION; active fire still resolves normally against any exposure. The SENTINEL still has one gun, one physical position, and cannot answer simultaneous incompatible threats or shoot through blocking geometry.
