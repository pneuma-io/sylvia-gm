# SNIPER

**At a glance:** SHARPSHOOTER specialty that invests additional time to solve increasingly difficult long-range shots.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - SNIPER does not own AIM, CALLED SHOTS, optics, or long-range weapons. It deepens preparation and converts it into better long-range solutions.
> - Exact AIM, RANGE, HIT QUALITY, DAMAGE, target PENETRATION, MATERIAL PENETRATION, and AP values remain balance parameters.
>
> **ENTRY — DEEP AIM**
> - After performing the normal AIM process, spend 1 additional AP to enter DEEP AIM against that target from the current firing position. DEEP AIM applies -2 DIFFICULTY to the prepared attack. The solution is bound to that target and firing position; material movement by the shooter or target, a target change, or loss of the firing solution breaks it.
>
> **TIER 1 — Build the solution**
> - **BRACED SOLUTION:** When physically supported by a bipod, rest, barricade, prone firing position, or similar stable support, entering DEEP AIM costs less AP.
> - **RANGE DOPE:** DEEP AIM offsets more of the attack difficulty caused by extreme range. It does not extend a projectile beyond physically plausible range.
> - **FINE POINT:** When making a CALLED SHOT from DEEP AIM, reduce the additional DIFFICULTY caused by selecting the specific target area. The selected area must still be visible.
>
> **TIER 2 — Exploit the solution**
> - **FIRST-ROUND HIT:** The first attack from DEEP AIM gains improved HIT QUALITY on a successful hit. Subsequent shots require rebuilding the firing solution normally.
> - **THREAD THE GAP:** DEEP AIM reduces the attack penalty caused by a target presenting only a small exposed silhouette through partial COVER. Full COVER still provides no direct shot.
> - **FOLLOW THROUGH:** After firing from DEEP AIM, if the shooter remains in position and continues engaging the same visible target, rebuilding DEEP AIM costs less AP. Movement or a lost solution removes the benefit.
>
> **TIER 3 — Hard shots**
> - **EXTREME RANGE:** DEEP AIM extends the weapon's useful RANGE PROFILE beyond what an ordinary shooter could practically exploit. Damage and projectile behavior still follow the weapon.
> - **MATERIAL WINDOW:** When a target is behind penetrable COVER and a plausible weak section can be identified, DEEP AIM improves MATERIAL PENETRATION against that specific section of COVER. It does not generically raise target PENETRATION after the projectile reaches the target, and the projectile must still defeat the material.
> - **PATIENT REACQUIRE:** If a DEEP-AIMED target briefly breaks LOS and then reappears in the same local area without the shooter changing position, rebuilding DEEP AIM costs less AP. A genuinely relocated target must be solved again.
>
> **TIER 4 — Terminal solution**
> - **PENETRATING SHOT:** Before firing from DEEP AIM, sacrifice part of its attack benefit to gain additional **target PENETRATION** on a successful hit. Declare before the ATTACK ROLL. This does not improve MATERIAL PENETRATION through external COVER unless another explicit effect applies.
> - **TERMINAL SHOT:** Before firing from DEEP AIM, sacrifice part of its attack benefit to gain additional DAMAGE on a successful hit. Declare before the ATTACK ROLL.
> - **EXACT RELEASE:** Instead of converting DEEP AIM into target PENETRATION or DAMAGE, retain the full firing solution to gain a larger HIT QUALITY improvement on a successful shot.
>
> **TIER 5 KEYSTONE — PERFECT SOLUTION**
> - Invest one further stage of preparation beyond DEEP AIM, then allocate the resulting solution to exactly one benefit: **EXTREME RANGE DIFFICULTY**, **EXPOSED AREA DIFFICULTY**, **CALLED SHOT DIFFICULTY**, **HIT QUALITY**, **TARGET PENETRATION**, or **MATERIAL PENETRATION**. The MATERIAL PENETRATION choice is available only when MATERIAL WINDOW genuinely applies to the shot. The choices do not stack; the selected benefit's magnitude and the preparation AP are balance parameters. Moving, losing the firing solution, or being forced to abandon the shot wastes the investment.
