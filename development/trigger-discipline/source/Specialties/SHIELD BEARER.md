# SHIELD BEARER

**At a glance:** BULWARK specialty built around trained combat use of a full-size ballistic or riot shield as physical, directional COVER.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - ENTRY ABILITY: SHIELD TRAINING grants trained tactical use of a full-size ballistic or riot shield.
> - The entry establishes competent combat handling while preserving ordinary physical behavior: facing, protected geometry, material, durability, carried weight, and exposure still matter.
> - A non-SHIELD BEARER may still physically carry or manipulate a shield when the equipment rules allow. The specialty represents trained tactical use rather than exclusive permission to touch the object.
> - Allies behind a shield already benefit from whatever COVER its geometry physically provides. Progression must add concrete techniques or rule interactions rather than permission for baseline physics to work.
> - The specialty remains deliberately slow. It makes a slow advance valuable rather than becoming a mobility build.
> - The progression below is **tertiary**: approved as the current working tree, but still revisable during later class passes and implementation pressure-testing.
> - Exact shield dimensions, AP values, untrained handling consequences, compatible weapons, and other numeric balance remain open.

## Tertiary progression

### Entry — SHIELD TRAINING
You are trained to deploy, carry, orient, and fight around a full-size riot or ballistic shield while using compatible one-handed equipment. While the shield is deployed, its effective BURDEN and HANDLING are reduced for your movement and compatible one-handed actions. Its real weight, size, directional geometry, durability, flankability, and MATERIAL PENETRATION interaction do not change; nonspecialists use its full ratings.

### Tier 1 — Shield handling
- **BRACE SHIELD:** While stationary with the shield deployed, brace it. Attempts to SHOVE or forcibly displace you must overcome the brace before changing your position or shield facing. Voluntary movement breaks the brace.
- **SHIELD CHECK:** Make a SHOVE using the deployed shield without first lowering or stowing it. The shield remains deployed unless the action itself changes your position or facing.
- **INDEXED RELOAD:** Reload or cycle a compatible one-handed firearm without fully stowing the shield. The physical reload sequence and ammunition still matter, and your protected geometry may narrow while the weapon hand is occupied.

Taking any two Tier-1 abilities unlocks Tier 2.

### Tier 2 — Movement under shield
- **CORNER GUARD:** While adjacent to a solid corner, establish a shield-facing PREP ACTION against one declared direction around it. When a threat first enters a real line from that direction, rotate the shield toward it before the threat's next action resolves. This is paid preparation, not free awareness or protection from unseen directions.
- **CONTROLLED RETREAT:** When an impact or forced-movement effect would break your position, you may give ground while keeping the shield toward the source if a physically valid retreat space exists. You are still displaced; the technique prevents displacement from automatically becoming loss of facing and posture.
- **STEP TOGETHER:** When you and an adjacent ally each spend movement AP to advance together behind the shield, the ally may make one physically valid STANCE change as part of that movement rather than paying its separate AP. Both characters move at the slower applicable rate and occupy real GRID spaces.

Taking any two Tier-2 abilities unlocks Tier 3.

### Tier 3 — Protecting the advance
- **CROSSING SCREEN:** When an adjacent ally crosses exposed ground, you may spend your own movement/AP to move with them and keep the shield between them and one declared threat direction. The screen ends if you cannot physically keep pace or maintain the angle.
- **SHIELDED RECOVERY:** Drag or assist an impaired, prone, or DOWNED ally while keeping the shield deployed toward one direction. The burden constrains movement and occupies the relevant hands; this is extraction under cover, not medical treatment.
- **IMPACT CONTROL:** When an impact against the shield is strong enough to alter your defensive geometry, choose whether the resulting failure primarily changes your position or your facing when the fiction permits. The impact still has real consequences.

Taking any two Tier-3 abilities unlocks Tier 4.

### Tier 4 — Breaking dangerous ground
- **BREACH SCREEN:** Lead allies through an already usable doorway, opening, or similarly narrow exposed transition while maintaining shield-first geometry through the threshold. This does not breach obstacles or grant BREACHER capabilities.
- **DRAW THE BURST:** When a SUPPRESSION ATTACK physically intercepts the deployed shield, it consumes additional finite exposure capacity from that SUPPRESSION. The shield still receives the real impact and COVER damage.
- **SEAL THE GAP:** When nearby COVER is destroyed or an opening suddenly appears, a prepared shield response may let you occupy the newly dangerous line if it is physically reachable. You become the temporary cover source; you do not rebuild the destroyed object.

Taking any two Tier-4 abilities unlocks Tier 5.

### Tier 5 — WALKING BASTION
Maintain a reduced form of BRACED SHIELD while advancing. Controlled facing changes or giving ground no longer automatically collapse that moving brace. Allied protection continues to come from actual shield geometry; any future coordinated-ally movement upgrade depends on replacing the unresolved Tier-2 movement slot rather than being assumed for free. You remain slow, flankable, and dependent on a physical shield that can be damaged, penetrated, or destroyed.
