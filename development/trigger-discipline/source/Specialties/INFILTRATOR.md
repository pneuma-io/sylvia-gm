# INFILTRATOR

**At a glance:** GHOST specialty that penetrates guarded spaces unseen before contact by preparing and exploiting a known APPROACH.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - INFILTRATOR does not grant permission to use STEALTH. It improves tactical infiltration against observed guards, patrols, sensors, and access problems.
> - Exact STEALTH, AP, movement, and difficulty values remain balance parameters.
>
> **ENTRY — APPROACH PLAN**
> - After observing a guarded route, designate an APPROACH and the guards, sensors, patrol patterns, or other threats actually observed. STEALTH checks made while following that APPROACH gain improved effectiveness against those known threats. Material changes to the route/security remove the benefit against the changed element until reassessed.
>
> **TIER 1 — Get through the first layer**
> - **TIMED CROSSING:** When crossing a briefly exposed gap against an intermittent observed patrol or sensor, reduce the STEALTH difficulty created by that exposure. Continuous observation still sees the character; a real timing window must exist.
> - **QUIET HANDS:** Reduce the additional detection/noise penalty caused by manipulating ordinary physical objects while infiltrating, such as doors, windows, latches, loose equipment, or movable obstacles. Relevant skills/tools remain required where appropriate.
> - **GEAR CONTROL:** Choose one bulky or awkward mission item being carried. Its burden imposes less penalty on STEALTH and infiltration movement. Weight and occupied hands remain real.
>
> **TIER 2 — Penetrate the guarded space**
> - **CLOSE PASS:** When passing close to an unaware observer while remaining outside direct LOS, reduce the STEALTH penalty caused by proximity. Direct sight, contact, or an impossible concealment situation still reveals the infiltrator.
> - **DISTRACTION WINDOW:** When a real distraction materially shifts an observer's attention away from the APPROACH, the next STEALTH movement against that observer gains improved effectiveness.
> - **CLEAN ENTRY:** When quietly defeating a lock, access mechanism, window, fence, or similar non-destructive obstacle using appropriate skill/equipment, the operation requires less tactical time. It does not improve the underlying HACKING, ENGINEERING, or other check.
>
> **TIER 3 — Leave less behind**
> - **SYNCHRONIZED PATTERNS:** When the APPROACH must account for multiple observed patrols or sensors whose timing overlaps, reduce the additional difficulty created by coordinating those patterns together. Unobserved threats remain unaccounted for.
> - **LEAVE IT CLEAN:** After using or bypassing an access point, container, device, or similar object, restoring it to an apparently ordinary state requires less time. Later observers receive less evidence from that object; physically obvious damage cannot be hidden.
> - **SILENT REMOVAL:** After successfully incapacitating or CONTROLLING an unaware and isolated target using an appropriately quiet method, the action produces less noise and moving the target out of immediate view costs less AP. The underlying CQC roll is not improved and loud weapons remain loud.
>
> **TIER 4 — Deep infiltration**
> - **GUIDE THROUGH:** One nearby ally following the APPROACH gains part of its reduction to situational STEALTH difficulty against the observed patrol/sensor pattern. The ally still uses their own STEALTH and can compromise the infiltration.
> - **ADAPTIVE PLAN:** When one part of the APPROACH changes, reassessing and redirecting around that change takes substantially less observation time. A completely unknown security layer still requires actual reconnaissance.
> - **WORK IN THE SHADOWS:** While remaining concealed at an infiltration position, reduce the STEALTH/exposure penalty caused by performing another stationary task such as LOCAL HACKING, ENGINEERING, surveillance, placing equipment, or similar objective work. The task itself gains no bonus.
>
> **TIER 5 KEYSTONE — PHANTOM APPROACH**
> - While following a prepared APPROACH, if a STEALTH check fails before an observer has directly confirmed the infiltrator's position, the infiltrator may abort the attempted crossing and fall back to the last physically reachable concealed position. The committed time/AP is still spent and defenders gain any suspicion or indirect information the failure produced. Direct confirmation cannot be rewound.
