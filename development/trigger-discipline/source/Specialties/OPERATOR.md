# OPERATOR

**At a glance:** RIFLEMAN direction focused on weapon manipulation: reloads, cycling, chambering, magazine handling, and transitions while preserving the firearm's actual physical state.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - OPERATOR never invents a separate ready/unready weapon state. The gun is simply in whatever physical state it is actually in.
> - If the magazine is out, it cannot fire from that magazine. If the chamber is empty, it is empty. If the action requires cycling, it still requires cycling.
> - Progression improves the time/AP needed to perform real firearm operations and may allow physically compatible operations to overlap at high investment.
> - The progression below is **tertiary**: approved as the current working tree, but still revisable during the all-class review and implementation pressure-testing.
> - Exact AP values, manipulation timings, and other numeric balance remain open unless a linked canonical procedure states otherwise.

## Tertiary progression

### Entry — WEAPON MANIPULATION
The AP costs of ordinary firearm manipulation are reduced for you. This applies to operations such as RELOAD steps, CYCLING, chambering, magazine handling, and transitioning between carried firearms. It never skips a physical step the firearm actually requires.

### Tier 1 — Fundamentals
- **RETENTION RELOAD:** Retaining a partially loaded magazine during a reload is faster than normal. The magazine still has to be physically placed somewhere accessible rather than disappearing into inventory.
- **QUICK CYCLE:** Manual cycling actions such as bolt, pump, lever, or charging-handle operations cost less AP. If the mechanism requires cycling, you still perform the cycle.
- **TRANSITION DRILL:** Transitioning between a slung or holstered firearm and another accessible firearm costs less AP. The other weapon must actually be carried somewhere reachable.

Taking any two Tier-1 abilities unlocks Tier 2.

### Tier 2 — Manipulate under pressure
- **ONE-HANDED MANIPULATION:** Reduce the extra AP surcharge for a physically possible one-handed FIREARM MANIPULATION. The final cost cannot be lower than the ordinary two-handed cost. This does not create permission that other characters lack, and it cannot perform an operation that literally requires two hands with no physical workaround.
- **SHOULDER SWITCH:** Changing which shoulder a long gun is fired from costs less AP, making opposite sides of cover and changing firing angles easier. The character and firearm still physically change position.
- **SELECTIVE LOAD:** Changing to a different magazine or ammunition type is faster than a normal reload intended purely to refill the gun. The ammunition must already be carried and compatible.

Taking any two Tier-2 abilities unlocks Tier 3.

### Tier 3 — Continuity
- **CYCLE ON THE RETURN:** With a manually cycled firearm, begin the required cycling operation as the weapon returns from recoil rather than treating firing and cycling as fully isolated movements. The chamber state still changes normally; this reduces the AP needed for fire → cycle → fire.
- **EMPTY? SWITCH:** If the current firearm becomes unable to fire because it is empty, damaged, or otherwise unusable, transitioning to another accessible firearm costs less AP than an ordinary deliberate weapon change. The replacement weapon has whatever actual state it already had.
- **MALFUNCTION DRILL:** When an explicit MALFUNCTION occurs, clear it substantially faster than normal. This does not introduce random jams; it only matters when a real malfunction already exists because of damage, contamination, bad ammunition, poor equipment, or another explicit cause.

Taking any two Tier-3 abilities unlocks Tier 4.

### Tier 4 — No wasted motion
- **COMPOUND RELOAD:** During a complete reload, physically compatible steps may overlap rather than always being paid as fully separate sequential actions; for example, releasing the old magazine while the other hand is already reaching for the replacement. Firearm state still updates step by step, and interruption leaves the gun in the state actually reached.
- **FIGHTING HANDS:** Moving between your firearm and another immediately accessible hand-held object such as a grenade, radio, tool, medical item, or magazine costs less AP. This improves handling only; it does not grant another class's specialty abilities.
- **MANUAL-ACTION MASTER:** Bolt-, pump-, lever-, and other manually operated firearms become substantially less time-expensive for you to run. Their actual mechanisms and ammunition states remain unchanged.

Taking any two Tier-4 abilities unlocks Tier 5.

### Tier 5 — NO WASTED MOTION
When firearm-manipulation steps can physically occur at the same time, overlap them instead of paying for each as a fully sequential block of time: release a magazine while reaching for the next one, let a slung weapon fall while drawing a sidearm, or cycle an action while bringing the sights back onto the target. Operations that physically cannot happen simultaneously still cannot overlap, and the firearm's real state is tracked throughout the sequence.
