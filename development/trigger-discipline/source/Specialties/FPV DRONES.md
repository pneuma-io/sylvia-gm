# FPV DRONES

**At a glance:** TECH specialty that directly pilots a small unmanned system to put sensors, payloads, or attacks into dangerous or awkward positions.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - FPV DRONES trades the operator's own attention/AP for exceptional manual control of one drone.
> - Signal, terrain, battery, payload, sensor, movement, and drone durability remain physical constraints.
> - Exact AP, signal, movement, attack, and defense values remain balance parameters.
>
> **ENTRY — MANUAL PILOT**
> - When directly controlling an FPV drone, its movement is substantially more AP-efficient and precise than when operated through ordinary remote commands. The operator still spends their own AP to pilot it.
>
> **TIER 1 — Fly it where it matters**
> - **NAP-OF-THE-EARTH:** When real nearby terrain already provides partial masking, precise close-terrain flight reduces the drone's exposed silhouette by one exposure band. It cannot convert partial exposure into full COVER and provides no benefit in clear LOS.
> - **PRECISION FLIGHT:** Reduce the movement/time cost of passing through narrow openings, interior spaces, dense obstacles, or similarly demanding flight paths. The path must physically fit the drone.
> - **RAPID LAUNCH:** Launching and taking manual control of a combat-accessible FPV drone costs less AP. The drone still has to be physically deployed.
>
> **TIER 2 — Remote eyes**
> - **FORWARD OBSERVER:** While the drone has valid sensor LOS to a target or area, designated nearby allies gain improved effectiveness when acting against targets whose position the drone is actively providing. This is information support, not shooting through walls; loss of signal or observation ends it.
> - **SNAP PEEK:** Combine moving the drone from protection to the first valid sensor position, making one observation, and returning along the same route for less AP than paying for both movement legs separately. OVERWATCH, signal loss, and other events still resolve at the drone's actual positions, and the operator gains no awareness beyond the drone's sensors.
> - **MARK AND PASS:** After positively identifying a target through the drone, transmitting that target information to the squad costs less AP and preserves the identification for a limited period after personal observation stops. Unseen relocation makes the information stale normally.
>
> **TIER 3 — Deliver the payload**
> - **PRECISION DELIVERY:** When using a drone to place or release a carried payload at a specific reachable point, improve placement accuracy and reduce scatter/error compared with ordinary remote delivery. The payload follows its normal rules.
> - **ATTACK RUN:** If the drone carries a weapon or one-way attack payload, manually piloting the final approach improves its attack effectiveness. Enemy COVER and interception depend on the real attack path.
> - **MASKED APPROACH:** If the drone reaches its final attack or observation position while physically masked from the target for most of the approach, its first exposure is harder to react to or target. Once exposed, it can be engaged normally.
>
> **TIER 4 — Fly through opposition**
> - **EVASIVE PILOTING:** When an enemy attacks a drone currently under manual control, improve the drone's defense by spending additional piloting attention/AP or reducing its available movement. This is active evasion, not passive armor.
> - **SIGNAL DISCIPLINE:** A manually controlled drone suffers less degradation from weak, obstructed, or contested signal conditions. A completely severed link still breaks control.
> - **LAST-SECOND CORRECTION:** After a manually controlled drone's attack or payload delivery would narrowly fail because of final positioning or placement error, spend additional piloting AP to correct the approach before resolution. It cannot fix an impossible path, destroyed drone, or completely lost signal.
>
> **TIER 5 KEYSTONE — THROUGH THE EYE OF THE NEEDLE**
> - While manually piloting one drone, combine high-efficiency movement → terrain masking → precision approach → precision payload/attack without paying the full normal handling overhead between each stage. Only one drone is directly controlled at a time, the operator still spends attention/AP, and the drone remains vulnerable to physical destruction, jamming, range, battery, and signal limits.
