# GUARDIAN

**At a glance:** Ally-facing BULWARK specialty built around actively placing the GUARDIAN between nearby teammates and danger.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - ENTRY ABILITY: INTERPOSE.
> - INTERPOSE is a real positional interaction. It does not redirect attacks through walls, ignore distance, or protect allies from threats the GUARDIAN cannot physically reach or screen.
> - GUARDIAN protects teammates through movement, geometry, prepared responses, extraction, and taking dangerous positions rather than an abstract defense aura.
> - It is distinct from SHIELD BEARER's movable COVER and ANCHOR's established STRONGPOINT.
> - SUPPRESSION is active fire, not a SUPPRESSED character state, and does not automatically cancel PREP ACTIONS whose real prerequisites remain intact.
> - The progression below is **tertiary**: approved as the current working tree, but still revisable during later class passes and implementation pressure-testing.
> - Exact AP, PREP/trigger procedure, movement allowance, valid threat lines, and other numeric balance remain open.

## Tertiary progression

### Entry — INTERPOSE
Establish INTERPOSE as a protective PREP ACTION on a nearby ally. When a valid threat opens against that ally, and you can physically place yourself into the relevant line or space, you may do so before the threat resolves. Distance, walls, reach, movement, facing, and actual geometry still matter.

### Tier 1 — Personal protection
- **BODY COVER:** While physically screening an adjacent protected ally from one declared direction, reduce that ally's exposed silhouette by one band where your body can actually provide the additional screening. This cannot create full COVER. If an attack is intercepted by you, it resolves against you normally.
- **PULL BEHIND:** When INTERPOSE triggers and suitable protection is immediately available, move the consenting protected ally into an adjacent safer position instead of moving yourself into the line. This cannot move them through obstacles or beyond the physically permitted distance.
- **GUARDED STEP:** While INTERPOSE is established, spend your own movement/AP during the protected ally's movement to remain in protective position beside them. If you cannot stay with them, INTERPOSE breaks normally.

Taking any two Tier-1 abilities unlocks Tier 2.

### Tier 2 — Close protection
- **DEFENSIVE EXCHANGE:** When INTERPOSE triggers, exchange positions with the protected ally when both positions are physically reachable.
- **CLOSE PROTECTION:** INTERPOSE may also prepare against an enemy entering contact range to SHOVE, CONTROL, DISARM, or otherwise physically attack the protected ally. You still must be capable of getting between them.
- **PROTECT THE OPERATOR:** Establishing INTERPOSE costs less AP when protecting an adjacent ally who commits substantial time to a stationary operational task such as MEDICINE, hacking, ENGINEERING, AIM, or drone control. Minimum final setup cost remains 1 AP and all ordinary positioning requirements still apply.

Taking any two Tier-2 abilities unlocks Tier 3.

### Tier 3 — Rescue
- **EMERGENCY INTERCEPT:** INTERPOSE may respond when the protected ally suddenly loses COVER or is forcibly displaced into danger, not only when an attack is first declared. A reachable physical route is still required.
- **STAND OVER THEM:** Establish INTERPOSE on a prone, incapacitated, or DOWNED ally and use your own position as part of their physical protection. This provides defense, not stabilization or recovery.
- **EXTRACT:** While protecting an impaired ally, combine guarded movement with dragging, carrying, or assisting them. The physical burden still reduces movement and limits what else you can do.

Taking any two Tier-3 abilities unlocks Tier 4.

### Tier 4 — Refusing the opening
- **NO OPENING:** After INTERPOSE resolves, it may remain prepared if you still satisfy its positioning and protection requirements. This does not grant unlimited movement or unlimited responses; the preparation simply does not automatically end after its first successful use.
- **LAST GUARD:** If an attack intercepted through INTERPOSE leaves you DOWNED, the protected ally may immediately move a short distance into an adjacent physically legal safer position. This movement still resolves real OVERWATCH, SUPPRESSION, hazards, and geometry.
- **COVERING WITHDRAWAL:** Establish INTERPOSE around a nearby withdrawal route rather than one stationary position. A protected ally may retreat through your reachable protective area while you spend your own movement/AP to keep screening one dangerous direction.

Taking any two Tier-4 abilities unlocks Tier 5.

### Tier 5 — NONE LEFT EXPOSED
INTERPOSE no longer has to name one specific ally when established. Instead, establish a protective posture over your immediate physical reach. When a nearby ally becomes threatened, you may apply INTERPOSE to that ally if you can actually reach the necessary line or position. You can still only be in one place at a time and cannot answer simultaneous threats from incompatible directions by declaration alone.
