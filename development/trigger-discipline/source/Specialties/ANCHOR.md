# ANCHOR

**At a glance:** BULWARK specialty that establishes and locks down one durable STRONGPOINT the squad can operate from.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - ENTRY ABILITY: ESTABLISH STRONGPOINT.
> - The entry lets the ANCHOR commit to a suitable position and place it into the persistent STRONGPOINT state that later ANCHOR progression modifies.
> - A STRONGPOINT does not create imaginary COVER. Protection still comes from actual terrain, STANCE, material, angle, and physical equipment.
> - ANCHOR is the established-position specialty rather than the movable-cover specialty.
> - Its value comes from making one chosen position difficult to dislodge and unusually useful to nearby allies.
> - The progression below is **tertiary**: approved as the current working tree, but still revisable during later class passes and implementation pressure-testing.
> - HUNKER no longer exists as an action or state; ANCHOR must rely on actual COVER, STANCE, STRONGPOINT, and PREP rules.
> - Exact setup AP, qualifying terrain, area, persistence, break conditions, and other numeric balance remain open.

## Tertiary progression

### Entry — ESTABLISH STRONGPOINT
At a suitable position, spend the required time/AP to establish a STRONGPOINT. The state is attached to you and the actual position rather than generating cover of its own. Leaving, abandoning, or losing the physical position can end it.

### Tier 1 — Taking the position
- **SETTLE IN:** As part of ESTABLISH STRONGPOINT, adopt one physically valid STANCE and defensive facing appropriate to the cover you occupy instead of requiring a separate preparatory setup for that posture. This cannot place you into a stance or protected geometry the terrain does not support.
- **BRACED POSITION:** While inside the STRONGPOINT, attempts to forcibly move you out of it must overcome your established brace. If you are displaced beyond the position, the protection is lost.
- **FIRING EDGE:** When establishing the STRONGPOINT, designate one actual edge, opening, or firing position and fold one ordinary AIM step into setup for one declared firing solution through it. The AIM persists only while you maintain that edge and solution; changing position, edge, or target area loses it.

Taking any two Tier-1 abilities unlocks Tier 2.

### Tier 2 — Making it useful to the squad
- **RALLY POINT:** Designate protected positions within the STRONGPOINT. An ally who physically reaches one may adopt the STANCE and facing appropriate to that position as part of settling into it rather than rebuilding a separate defensive setup. The ally still has to reach real cover through normal movement.
- **HOLD UNDER FIRE:** When taking full protection inside the STRONGPOINT temporarily removes LOS, one defensive PREP ACTION may remain dormant instead of ending. It cannot trigger without LOS and must resume from the same position and firing edge before your next TEAM TURN.
- **CONTROLLED APPROACH:** When establishing the STRONGPOINT, declare one physically meaningful approach such as a doorway, corridor, stairwell, or gap between cover. Defensive preparations may use entry through that approach as a specific trigger; unseen or unthreatened paths are unaffected.

Taking any two Tier-2 abilities unlocks Tier 3.

### Tier 3 — Keeping the position alive
- **FALLBACK POSITION:** Designate nearby suitable secondary cover when establishing the STRONGPOINT. If the primary position becomes untenable, physically reaching the fallback can transfer the STRONGPOINT rather than requiring the entire setup to begin again.
- **ROTATE POSITIONS:** Allies occupying connected cover within the STRONGPOINT may exchange or rotate positions using their own movement/AP without automatically dismantling the defensive setup. Every move must remain physically possible.
- **PREPARED GARRISON:** When allies rotate between valid positions inside the STRONGPOINT, one ally may transfer an existing defensive PREP ACTION to the new position for less AP than rebuilding it. The original zone and all physical prerequisites must remain valid.

Taking any two Tier-3 abilities unlocks Tier 4.

### Tier 4 — Defense in depth
- **COUNTER-BREACH:** Establish a prepared close-defense response against an enemy physically entering the STRONGPOINT. The trigger is crossing into defended space, not an abstract threat aura.
- **SECOND LINE:** Maintain both the primary position and the designated FALLBACK POSITION as parts of the same defensive setup. Losing one does not automatically erase the other.
- **REFUSE COLLAPSE:** Destruction of one COVER source inside the STRONGPOINT does not automatically end the STRONGPOINT if you still occupy another physically viable defensive position within it. The battlefield geometry changes immediately even though the organizational state survives.

Taking any two Tier-4 abilities unlocks Tier 5.

### Tier 5 — FORTRESS
The STRONGPOINT becomes a developed defensive position rather than a single prepared location. When establishing it, organize a primary position, a fallback, a controlled approach, and protected positions for allies, all of which must correspond to real battlefield geometry. Ordinary internal movement or loss of one cover element does not by itself erase the STRONGPOINT; it ends when the position is genuinely bypassed, destroyed, abandoned, or taken.
