# GUNNER

**At a glance:** RIFLEMAN direction focused on volume, sustained fire, and using real damaging rounds to control enemy exposure through SUPPRESSION.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - GUNNER does not make SUPPRESSION exclusive; any suitable firearm can suppress.
> - SUPPRESSION is active fire, not a SUPPRESSED character state. GUNNER abilities must change ammunition/time, geometry, exposure resolution, RECOIL, or another real quantity rather than granting recovery from or permission around an undefined condition.
> - The specialty is defined by firing behavior rather than a weapon category. LMGs are an obvious fit, but the tree may work with any firearm whose actual fire mode and ammunition support the technique.
> - The progression below is **tertiary**: approved as the current working tree, but still revisable during the all-class review and implementation pressure-testing.
> - Exact RECOIL, ammunition, SUPPRESSION area, PREP timing, and AP values remain open.

## Tertiary progression

### Entry — SUSTAINED FIRE DISCIPLINE
When you make consecutive firing actions with the same firearm before accumulated RECOIL has fully recovered, each new firing action adds less additional RECOIL than it would for an ordinary shooter. Existing RECOIL still matters, every round is still consumed, and switching weapons does not transfer the benefit to the new firearm.

### Tier 1 — Control the gun
- **WEAPON REST:** When you brace or rest a firearm against physically suitable support such as a bipod, wall, barricade, vehicle edge, or ground, automatic fire gains additional RECOIL control. Moving away from the support ends the benefit.
- **SUPPRESSION DISCIPLINE:** Establish SUPPRESSION with less ammunition or firing time than normal by placing real rounds more efficiently into the threatened area. This does not make targets more frightened or remove their agency.
- **RECOIL RHYTHM:** If you stop firing but remain in the same firing posture with the same weapon, recover RECOIL more efficiently. Moving, switching weapons, or breaking the firing posture can interrupt that recovery.

Taking any two Tier-1 abilities unlocks Tier 2.

### Tier 2 — Sustain the pressure
- **SHIFT FIRE:** If SUPPRESSION is already established, move its threatened area into an adjacent or overlapping area with less firing time/ammunition than establishing completely new suppression. You need a valid firing line into the new area and cannot jump the zone across blocking geometry.
- **WALKING FIRE:** Move between sustained firing actions without completely losing the benefit of SUSTAINED FIRE DISCIPLINE. Movement still costs AP and RECOIL does not disappear; this is deliberate repositioning, not a mobility build.
- **LONG STRING:** Push a continuous automatic-fire action farther before additional rounds reach the normal point of severe diminishing returns from RECOIL. You still cannot connect more rounds than you fire, and excessive fire remains increasingly difficult to control.

Taking any two Tier-2 abilities unlocks Tier 3.

### Tier 3 — Control the area
- **SUPPRESSIVE SWEEP:** A single SUPPRESSION action may cover two physically connected adjacent areas within one continuous firing arc. You must commit enough ammunition to threaten both, and solid geometry can split or block the sweep.
- **COUNTERFIRE:** When establishing SUPPRESSION, also PREP a response against an enemy who exposes themselves to deliberately return fire from within the suppressed area. If triggered, make a normal ATTACK ROLL and expend the required ammunition. This is an additional prepared response beyond the baseline SUPPRESSION ATTACK and must obey the eventual trigger-ordering rule.
- **KEEP THE GUN UP:** When an effect would make you abandon a supported firing posture without actually displacing, incapacitating, or physically preventing you from holding the weapon, retain the posture. Forced movement, loss of support, losing the weapon, or being knocked PRONE can still break it.

Taking any two Tier-3 abilities unlocks Tier 4.

### Tier 4 — Fire superiority
- **DOMINATING FIRE:** Establish SUPPRESSION across a larger connected area than normal. Ammunition commitment scales with the threatened area, and walls, corners, and other geometry still break the firing solution normally.
- **FIRE THROUGH THE OPENING:** When SUPPRESSION is concentrated on one narrow opening, SUPPRESSION ATTACKS through that opening gain improved effectiveness. The smaller zone still consumes normal ammunition and cannot simultaneously cover a wider area.
- **RELENTLESS:** After resolving a sustained firing action, recover enough control to continue firing sooner than an ordinary shooter rather than needing a full break in firing tempo. This improves the fire → control → fire cycle without resetting RECOIL to zero.

Taking any two Tier-4 abilities unlocks Tier 5.

### Tier 5 — FIRE SUPERIORITY
At the start of your next TEAM TURN, spend reduced maintenance AP and fresh ammunition to continue an existing SUPPRESSION without a gap, refresh its finite exposure capacity, and optionally shift it into one adjacent valid area. The firing line, weapon state, and ammunition commitment must remain real; RECOIL continues accumulating and blocking geometry still limits the zone.
