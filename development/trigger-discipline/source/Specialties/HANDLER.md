# HANDLER

**At a glance:** INFLUENCER specialty for contacts, informants, favors, introductions, human intelligence, and maintaining useful relationship networks.

> [!note]- Full rules
> HANDLER turns relationships into access, information, introductions, assistance, and plausible favors. CONTACTS remain real people with motives, limits, risks, loyalties, and costs. HANDLER does not replace INVESTIGATION, RECON, or HACKING when the needed information must actually be discovered through those systems.
>
> Detailed progression below is **tertiary/current-working** for later all-class review. Exact contact limits, favor values, availability, timing, and similar numbers remain balance parameters.
>
> ## ENTRY — WORK A CONTACT
> When asking an established CONTACT for plausible information, access, an introduction, or a favor, the request costs less time and existing relationship LEVERAGE has greater effect. The contact still has their own interests, limits, and things they simply cannot provide.
>
> ## TIER 1 — Know Your Sources
> - **VET THE SOURCE:** After meaningful interaction with a CONTACT or informant, SOCIAL READ reveals more information about their likely reliability, what they want, what they fear, and where their knowledge probably ends. It does not tell you whether every individual statement is true.
> - **QUICK ASK:** Obtaining a narrow piece of information the CONTACT already knows requires less social setup/time. Research they have not done remains research they have not done.
> - **DISCREET CHANNEL:** Communication with a CONTACT can be arranged with reduced risk of exposing the relationship or origin of the request. The actual communication method still matters.
>
> ## TIER 2 — Reach Beyond Your Own Contacts
> - **INTRODUCTION:** A willing CONTACT can connect you to someone one relationship step beyond them with substantially less access difficulty than approaching that person cold. Your CONTACT cannot introduce you to someone they do not actually have access to.
> - **FAVOR LEDGER:** When you provide a meaningful service, resource, protection, introduction, or other benefit to a CONTACT, the resulting relationship LEVERAGE is stronger or persists longer before another substantial favor is required. The favor has to actually matter.
> - **CUTOUT:** You may route a request through an intermediary CONTACT so the final recipient does not automatically know the request originated with you. This increases secrecy but may increase time or distortion.
>
> ## TIER 3 — Build HUMINT
> - **CORROBORATE:** When two genuinely independent human sources support the same factual claim, combine them more efficiently to improve the confidence state of that information, including LEAD → SUPPORTED → VERIFIED when the evidence actually justifies it. Two people repeating the same rumor from one source are not independent corroboration.
> - **RUMOR MAP:** After collecting HUMINT from several relevant people in the same social environment, infer additional information about who is connected to whom, who is influencing whom, and where information appears to originate. The inference is bounded by what your sources collectively reveal.
> - **PROTECT THE SOURCE:** When a CONTACT is threatened specifically because of their relationship with you, extracting them, severing the exposed communication chain, or relocating future contact through another channel costs less time. If that action succeeds, one additional CONTACT directly connected through the exposed relationship avoids becoming exposed solely through that chain. It does not erase existing evidence, protect an unrelated contact, or stop exposure through another surviving chain.
>
> ## TIER 4 — Mobilize the Network
> - **PARALLEL ASK:** Send one clearly defined information request through several relevant CONTACTS at once for substantially less total time than working each source sequentially. Each CONTACT still returns only what they can actually learn.
> - **BORROWED ACCESS:** A willing CONTACT who genuinely has standing with a person, organization, or location can sponsor you or an ally. Their introduction provides stronger LEVERAGE toward gaining that access than an ordinary recommendation. It cannot bypass security they themselves lack authority to bypass.
> - **SAFE HAVEN:** When a CONTACT network plausibly has access to shelter, transport, storage, medical help, documents, equipment, or similar support, locating and arranging an available option requires substantially less time. The network still has to contain somebody capable of providing it.
>
> ## TIER 5 KEYSTONE — HUMAN NETWORK
> Your CONTACTS function as a connected human network rather than isolated NPC relationships. Requests can move through introductions and established relationships without rebuilding access at every step; you can efficiently route questions through several sources, obtain introductions beyond your immediate circle, corroborate HUMINT, mobilize favors, and shift communication away from compromised relationships. Contacts remain people who can refuse, lie, misunderstand, disappear, betray you, or simply not know anyone useful.
