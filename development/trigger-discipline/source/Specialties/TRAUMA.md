# TRAUMA

**At a glance:** CORPSMAN specialty that treats serious HP damage and INJURIES once the immediate life-threatening crisis is controlled.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - TRAUMA does not gate ordinary MEDICINE. It improves diagnosis, serious wound treatment, definitive field care, and recovery outcomes.
> - CORPSMAN's CLINICAL TEMPO baseline already improves treatment speed/effectiveness generally; TRAUMA nodes below are intended to improve serious-injury outcomes beyond that class baseline.
> - Anatomy and irreversible physical destruction remain real limits.
> - Exact HP restoration, recovery, AP, and treatment values remain balance parameters.
>
> **ENTRY — TRAUMA CARE**
> - When treating serious physical injury with appropriate equipment and working conditions, successful MEDICINE treatment restores more HP or produces a stronger injury-recovery result than baseline CORPSMAN treatment. The injury must actually be medically treatable.
>
> **TIER 1 — Assess the damage**
> - **TRAUMA ASSESSMENT:** A thorough examination reveals more information about observable/internal injury severity and identifies treatment priorities that can be determined using available diagnostic tools. The examination cannot reveal what its method cannot detect.
> - **HEMORRHAGE CONTROL:** Treatments specifically intended to control significant bleeding are more effective and consume less time than baseline CORPSMAN treatment. The source must be physically controllable.
> - **WOUND MANAGEMENT:** When cleaning, dressing, closing, or otherwise properly managing a treatable wound, gain improved MEDICINE effectiveness and reduce the likelihood that the wound worsens because of inadequate treatment.
>
> **TIER 2 — Repair function**
> - **FRACTURE MANAGEMENT:** Treating fractures, dislocations, and comparable structural injuries produces a stronger reduction in their ongoing functional penalties. The bone/joint remains injured until properly healed.
> - **DEBRIDEMENT:** With proper tools, treating contaminated, burned, damaged, or otherwise compromised tissue removes more of the ongoing complication from the INJURY. This requires real treatment time.
> - **EFFICIENT CLOSURE:** Once bleeding and contamination are controlled, closing a suitable wound requires less treatment time and improves HP recovery from that treatment.
>
> **TIER 3 — Serious trauma**
> - **DEEP WOUND CARE:** Restore more HP from severe but survivable tissue damage before further recovery requires extended rest or surgery. This does not allow infinite repeated healing of the same wound.
> - **LIMB PRESERVATION:** When a badly injured limb is still medically salvageable, treatment improves the effectiveness of preserving its function and reduces the severity of the resulting persistent INJURY. Destroyed or irretrievably severed structures remain destroyed.
> - **SHOCK MANAGEMENT:** Treating a casualty whose systemic condition is worsening from blood loss, trauma, or physiological shock more effectively arrests deterioration and improves subsequent recovery.
>
> **TIER 4 — Definitive field care**
> - **FIELD SURGERY:** With appropriate tools, supplies, and working conditions, perform limited invasive treatment that ordinarily requires evacuation to more developed medical care. This expands what can be treated in the field but environmental conditions still matter.
> - **SALVAGE:** When treating a severe INJURY that would normally leave a lasting functional penalty, successful definitive care can reduce the eventual persistent consequence by one severity step defined by that specific INJURY. Apply SALVAGE at most once to the same INJURY; repeated treatment cannot stack the reduction. The improvement is an eventual recovery outcome, not immediate HP restoration or restored combat function. Irreparable destruction cannot be reversed, and an INJURY without a medically achievable lower severity state receives no invented benefit.
> - **RECOVERY PLAN:** After completing definitive treatment, establish a recovery regimen. Subsequent natural/downtime HP and INJURY recovery is faster or more effective while the regimen is followed.
>
> **TIER 5 KEYSTONE — DEFINITIVE CARE**
> - With sufficient equipment, time, and a medically viable patient, resolve multiple parts of a serious trauma case as one coordinated treatment: diagnosis → hemorrhage control → repair/closure → complication management → recovery plan, with substantially greater efficiency and outcome quality. The keystone expands what serious injury can be properly dealt with before evacuation without violating anatomy.
