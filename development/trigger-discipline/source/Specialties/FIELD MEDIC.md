# FIELD MEDIC

**At a glance:** CORPSMAN specialty that provides fast immediate treatment and restores short-term combat function during an active fight.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - FIELD MEDIC does not gate MEDICINE, STABILIZE, or ordinary first aid. It improves treatment speed and immediate battlefield outcomes.
> - It does not erase serious HP loss or major INJURIES that require TRAUMA care.
> - Exact AP, RES restoration, penalty reduction, and supply values remain balance parameters.
>
> **ENTRY — COMBAT MEDICINE:** The first successful battlefield treatment addressing each distinct injury or condition also restores 1 RES when restoring immediate combat function is medically plausible. `INJURY_REPAIRABLE_FOREARM_TRAUMA_V1` is the reference eligible injury: its first successful battlefield treatment may restore that 1 RES once. The treatment restores no HP, does not remove the injury or destroyed anatomy, and cannot be repeated to farm RES.
>
> **TIER 1 — Get them treated fast**
> - **RAPID STABILIZATION:** STABILIZE requires less AP/time than it does for a baseline CORPSMAN. Stabilization follows the actual injury and does not restore HP by itself.
> - **KIT AT HAND:** A dedicated medical kit may be configured as QUICK ACCESS with reduced handling cost when beginning treatment. The kit still occupies inventory and consumes supplies.
> - **TRIAGE:** A brief examination identifies which observable ACTIVE injury or LIFE-THREATENING CONDITION is currently the greatest immediate threat and what available treatment would address it. Hidden/internal problems still require appropriate examination/equipment.
>
> **TIER 2 — Restore immediate function**
> - **PATCH AND MOVE:** When an injury permits continued movement after basic treatment, successful battlefield treatment reduces the injury's movement penalty for the rest of the encounter or until the treatment fails/is disrupted. The underlying INJURY remains.
> - **PAIN CONTROL:** Appropriate medication or treatment temporarily reduces an injury penalty affecting actions. Medication remains a physical resource and may have duration, dosage, and side-effect limits.
> - **GET THEM UP:** Assisting a conscious PRONE, impaired, or recently stabilized casualty back into a viable STANCE costs less AP as part of the treatment sequence. It cannot make a medically incapable casualty stand.
>
> **TIER 3 — Work in the fight**
> - **CONTINUE CARE:** If treatment is interrupted so you can move the casualty, take protection, or briefly respond to danger, completed treatment setup/progress is retained so care can resume rather than restarting. Lost supplies, lost access to the casualty, or a materially changed medical condition can still invalidate that progress.
> - **DRAG AND TREAT:** When moving an injured casualty a short distance into safer geometry, combine part of that movement with beginning immediate treatment for reduced total AP. Drag/carry burden remains physical.
> - **BATTLEFIELD RECOVERY:** When supplies and the injury permit it, immediate treatment restores more RES or equivalent short-term combat survivability than ordinary CORPSMAN battlefield treatment. It does not repair serious HP damage or remove major injuries.
>
> **TIER 4 — Keep the squad operational**
> - **MULTIPLE CASUALTIES:** After completing an immediate treatment, beginning a different battlefield treatment on another nearby casualty costs less AP. Each casualty is still treated separately and supplies are consumed normally.
> - **FUNCTIONAL SPLINT:** When treating an injury that impairs a limb or movement but does not make use physically impossible, reduce the functional penalty more strongly for the remainder of the operation. The wound remains and still needs real trauma care.
> - **SECOND WIND:** After successful treatment, a conscious casualty who is physically capable gains a small amount of immediate temporary AP for movement, changing STANCE, taking COVER, or readying equipment. It cannot be used to attack and cannot make a medically incapable casualty act. Each casualty can benefit only once per encounter.
>
> **TIER 5 KEYSTONE — KEEP THEM FIGHTING**
> - When the injury permits it, one battlefield-treatment sequence may combine assess → stabilize immediate threat → restore limited function → reposition or return casualty to action without paying the full ordinary time for every step separately. Serious HP loss, major INJURIES, destroyed tissue, and critical conditions remain.
