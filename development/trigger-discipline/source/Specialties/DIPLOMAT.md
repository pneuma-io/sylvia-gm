# DIPLOMAT

**At a glance:** INFLUENCER specialty for negotiation, de-escalation, surrender, bargains, and building agreements other people can plausibly accept.

> [!note]- Full rules
> DIPLOMAT is strongest when different people or groups have conflicting goals but some workable overlap exists. It improves the ability to identify acceptable terms, frame concessions, preserve face, reduce hostility, and turn LEVERAGE into negotiated outcomes. It does not create consent where no plausible agreement exists and does not function as social mind control.
>
> INFLUENCER's WORK THE ANGLE baseline already makes relevant SOCIAL FACTS faster/easier to convert into stronger LEVERAGE. A DIPLOMAT Entry must therefore add a negotiation-specific delta beyond simply strengthening SOCIAL FACT → LEVERAGE again.
>
> Detailed progression below is **tertiary/current-working** for later all-class review. Exact check modifiers, time requirements, leverage values, and similar numbers remain balance parameters.
>
> ## ENTRY — NEGOTIATING POSITION
> When a plausible concrete offer is rejected, keep negotiations open for one materially changed counteroffer rather than having rejection alone end the exchange. This cannot stop someone who has no acceptable deal, lacks authority, is already committed to violence, or has another real reason to leave.
>
> ## TIER 1 — Find the Deal
> - **BOTTOM LINE:** After successfully reading a negotiating counterpart, gain additional information about which observable demand or priority is flexible and which is currently non-negotiable. This does not reveal motives you had no way to detect.
> - **COUNTEROFFER:** After an offer is rejected, presenting a materially changed offer costs less time and gains improved effectiveness. Repeating the exact same demand louder does not qualify.
> - **FACE-SAVING EXIT:** When proposing withdrawal, surrender, apology, compromise, or another outcome that requires the other side to visibly back down, terms that preserve their dignity, status, safety, or reputation gain additional LEVERAGE. They still need a plausible reason to accept.
>
> ## TIER 2 — Control the Negotiation
> - **CEASEFIRE:** You may negotiate a temporary CEASEFIRE as a concrete agreement. On success, establish who is covered, what actions are prohibited, and what ends the ceasefire. This does not magically prevent betrayal; it creates an actual agreed pause without requiring the entire dispute to be resolved first.
> - **TERMS ON PAPER:** When you successfully secure an agreement, explicitly recorded terms become stronger LEVERAGE in later disputes about what was agreed. Written contracts, recordings, witnesses, or other evidence must actually exist.
> - **MEDIATOR:** When negotiating between two other parties with conflicting interests, relevant SOCIAL FACTS about either side can provide LEVERAGE toward constructing the shared agreement. You are still dealing with two independent decision-makers.
>
> ## TIER 3 — Get Better Outcomes
> - **SURRENDER TERMS:** When successfully negotiating surrender or withdrawal, you may secure one additional favorable condition without the normal increase in bargaining difficulty. The extra condition still has to be something the other side could plausibly accept.
> - **TRADE THE ISSUE:** When several issues are being negotiated together, a concession on one issue produces stronger LEVERAGE when asking for a concession on another. The concession must have actual value to the counterpart.
> - **PIVOT:** When a counterpart clearly rejects one line of LEVERAGE, shifting to a different known SOCIAL FACT or interest costs less negotiation time and gains improved effectiveness. You need a real second angle.
>
> ## TIER 4 — Keep Talks Alive
> - **MULTI-PARTY ACCORD:** When several parties genuinely share the same negotiation, you can resolve common terms through a coordinated negotiation rather than separately rebuilding the entire argument with every participant. Individual parties can still reject terms that specifically disadvantage them.
> - **LAST OFF-RAMP:** When negotiations are about to collapse into departure, violence, or another immediate escalation, you gain one final opportunity to propose a materially different compromise before that escalation resolves. The offer must address a real reason the talks failed; this is not a free reroll of the same demand.
> - **GUARANTEED TERMS:** When you attach credible collateral to an agreement—money, hostages, reputation, escrow, third-party guarantee, verifiable action, or similar—the guarantee produces more LEVERAGE than it would for an ordinary negotiator. The guarantee must be real and capable of being lost.
>
> ## TIER 5 KEYSTONE — GRAND BARGAIN
> You can structure a single negotiation around multiple linked interests and concessions. Resolve the first term normally; each additional materially linked term costs a reduced amount of negotiation setup AP instead of repeating the full setup. A concession on one issue can create LEVERAGE on another, and different parties can receive different terms inside the same agreement. Every affected party still accepts or rejects the terms that apply to them, and nothing forces agreement.
