# ARSENAL

**At a glance:** RIFLEMAN direction focused on carrying and efficiently transitioning among a deliberately broad set of firearms so the right weapon can be brought to bear for the current engagement problem.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - ARSENAL does not grant generic proficiency; skills remain independent of class.
> - The specialty makes a multi-firearm loadout practically usable through better carriage, access, transitions, and handling rather than by ignoring bulk, ammunition, slings, holsters, or inventory.
> - Carrying a shotgun does not grant BREACHER identity, and carrying a precision-oriented firearm does not grant SHARPSHOOTER identity.
> - The progression below is **tertiary**: approved as the current working tree, but still revisable during the all-class review and implementation pressure-testing.
> - Exact burden, accessible-weapon limits, transition AP, cache capacity, and other numeric balance remain open.

## Tertiary progression

### Entry — FIGHTING LOAD
Configure one additional firearm as combat-accessible rather than ordinary packed baggage. Slings, holsters, retention systems, physical placement, and burden still matter; the weapon must actually be carried.

### Tier 1 — Access
- **FAST TRANSITION:** Transitioning between firearms in your FIGHTING LOAD costs less AP because the loadout has been deliberately arranged for combat access rather than improvised carriage.
- **SIDEARM FIRST:** Releasing a long gun onto its sling and drawing an accessible sidearm is especially fast. The long gun remains physically hanging from the sling.
- **PICKUP DRILL:** When you pick up a conventional firearm whose basic mechanism you understand, identify its immediately visible/usable state and bring it into action faster than normal. Hidden modifications, ammunition quality, and internal damage remain unknown unless discovered normally.

Taking any two Tier-1 abilities unlocks Tier 2.

### Tier 2 — Expand the loadout
- **SECOND PRIMARY:** Configure another long gun or similarly bulky firearm as combat-accessible rather than STOWED. The increased burden is real and can still reduce mobility.
- **MATCHED AMMO:** Each firearm in your FIGHTING LOAD may have its own designated QUICK ACCESS ammunition locations, reducing sorting/handling delay when you change weapons. You still carry every magazine or round physically.
- **HANDOFF:** Giving an accessible firearm to an adjacent ally or receiving one from them costs less AP. The weapon retains its exact magazine, chamber, ammunition, and other physical state.

Taking any two Tier-2 abilities unlocks Tier 3.

### Tier 3 — Always have another answer
- **BACKUP PLAN:** When the firearm in your hands becomes empty, damaged, inappropriate to the current engagement, or otherwise unusable, transition to another firearm in your FIGHTING LOAD faster than an ordinary voluntary transition. The ability does not decide whether switching is tactically wise.
- **STAGED CACHE:** With preparation, place additional firearms and ammunition around a specific position so they count as immediately accessible while you remain there. They are real battlefield objects that can be moved, stolen, damaged, or left behind.
- **FAMILIAR CONTROLS:** After a brief physical inspection of an unfamiliar but conventional firearm, identify its obvious safety, feed system, selector, charging/cycling method, and controls without a prolonged familiarization process. Truly exotic or unfamiliar technology can remain outside this ability.

Taking any two Tier-3 abilities unlocks Tier 4.

### Tier 4 — Right gun, right problem
- **RAPID CHANGEOVER:** After transitioning to another firearm in your FIGHTING LOAD, the first normal manipulation actually required to bring that weapon into action costs less AP. If it needs cycling, loading, or another real step, that step still occurs; if it is already capable of firing, no fictional preparation is added.
- **ROLE PACKAGE:** When arranging a FIGHTING LOAD before an operation, assign genuinely different firearms to distinct tactical jobs such as range, suppression, penetration, close handling, or special ammunition. Choose one package benefit for that operation: **ACCESS** reduces the AP cost of changing from one assigned role to another, or **BURDEN** reduces the effective BURDEN of one assigned firearm for movement. BURDEN cannot reduce that firearm below effective BURDEN 0 and never removes its physical size, carry position, interference, or equipment requirement. Redundant copies do not qualify merely because they are given different labels.
- **ARMORY POSITION:** A STAGED CACHE can contain a larger set of firearms and ammunition and may be arranged so nearby allies can access clearly designated weapons without digging through packed inventory.

Taking any two Tier-4 abilities unlocks Tier 5.

### Tier 5 — WALKING ARMORY
Every firearm physically configured into your FIGHTING LOAD through a valid sling, holster, retention system, or equivalent combat carry position counts as combat-accessible. Transitions among those weapons become dramatically faster and can form a normal part of your firing sequence rather than behaving like unpacking baggage. You still must own and carry the firearms; ammunition, mounting space, long-gun interference, dropped weapons, and excessive burden remain real limits.
