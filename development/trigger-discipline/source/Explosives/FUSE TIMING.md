# FUSE TIMING

**At a glance:** Grenades have real fuse time. Remaining warning time determines whether threatened characters can attempt a defensive response and how much they can physically do before detonation.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - A grenade's FUSE is measured in tactical time. Arming the grenade starts or continues that fuse according to the device.
> - `remaining warning time = fuse duration - time already spent armed`.
> - A thrower may deliberately COOK an armed grenade by holding it before release. Cooking does not require a separate roll; it simply consumes real fuse time.
> - If the fuse expires while the grenade is still being held, it detonates at the holder's current position.
> - Remaining warning time controls whether an EXPLOSIVE SAVE exists and how complex/far the declared defensive response may be.
> - Long warning may permit meaningful movement behind nearby COVER. Short warning may permit only an instinctive dive or stance change. Effectively no warning may permit no save.
> - Fuse timing never grants impossible movement. The threatened character still has to complete a physically reachable response.

## Balance parameters
- fuse duration by grenade family
- AP/time cost of arming and throwing
- allowed cooking increments / interface granularity
- warning thresholds used by EXPLOSIVE SAVE
