# MONITORING

**At a glance:** Ability of a system or defender to notice suspicious activity; separate from how hard the system is to enter.

> [!note]- Full rules
> Different actions can have different visibility. Detection is not automatically instant failure: defenders may notice something and begin responding while the intrusion continues.
>
> MONITORING is attached to real NODES, LINKS, accounts, services, SEGMENTS, or defensive systems. It observes only activity within its authored coverage. When notice is uncertain, HACKING VISIBILITY modifies the separate STAY QUIET difficulty. Do not add a detection roll to automatic authorized routing or every harmless network step.
