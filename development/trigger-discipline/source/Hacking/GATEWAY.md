# GATEWAY

**At a glance:** Strategically important NODE connecting NETWORKS or SEGMENTS, such as a router, firewall, bridge, VPN endpoint, or internet gateway.

> [!note]- Full rules
> A NETWORKER may route through a GATEWAY if access and connectivity permit. Taking a GATEWAY offline can disconnect large portions of a system for attackers and defenders alike.
