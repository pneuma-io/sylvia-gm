# NETWORK SEGMENT

**At a glance:** A bounded portion of a NETWORK whose connectivity can be separated from other devices or systems.

> [!note]- Full rules
> SEGMENTATION is real protection. Compromising an office workstation does not automatically expose security, payroll, executive systems, or safety controllers. Defenders may deliberately isolate a segment to contain an intrusion, sacrificing capability to deny access.
