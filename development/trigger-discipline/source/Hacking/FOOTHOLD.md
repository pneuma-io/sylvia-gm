# FOOTHOLD

**At a glance:** Persistent useful access that lets an attacker return to or operate from a compromised position without starting from zero.

> [!note]- Full rules
> FOOTHOLDS can be created remotely or through LOCAL HACKER modification. Defenders may need to hunt, restore, or physically inspect compromised systems rather than merely changing one password.
>
> Ordinary sessions and temporary PRIVILEGE end when their route/session ends. A FOOTHOLD is the explicit exception that supplies persistent re-entry. Its authored method determines what remediation removes it.
