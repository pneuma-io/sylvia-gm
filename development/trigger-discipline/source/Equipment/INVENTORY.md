# INVENTORY

**At a glance:** Slot/location-based carry model built around where equipment physically lives, not a kilogram spreadsheet.

> [!note]- Full rules
> Pockets, belts, holsters, vests, slings, bags, and backpacks provide locations. Storage location affects retrieval AP. Dropping is faster than carefully stowing. FORCE matters only for obviously heavy/awkward loads. Significant combat consumables such as magazines and grenades are tracked individually.
