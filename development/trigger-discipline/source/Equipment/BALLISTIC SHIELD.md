# BALLISTIC SHIELD

**At a glance:** Mobile directional COVER with material-resistance / MATERIAL PENETRATION behavior; not bonus RES.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - Shields occupy hands, constrain weapon use, protect only relevant angles, and can be flanked or defeated according to MATERIAL PENETRATION and the shield's material profile.
> - Target PENETRATION through RES is a separate step used only if a projectile actually continues through or around the shield and reaches the protected character.
> - Exact durability and HANDLING rules remain open balance/reference parameters.
