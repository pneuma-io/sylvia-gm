# REFERENCE EQUIPMENT

**At a glance:** Small standardized equipment set for balance testing. These fixtures provide representative physical behaviors for simulations; they are not a final Campaign Setting catalog. The equipment families/schema are locked here; their initial numeric tuning values live in [[REFERENCE EQUIPMENT VALUES]].

> [!note]- Full rules
> - Reference equipment exists so classes, specialties, firing modes, medical actions, drones, armor, and scenarios can be compared against stable representative gear.
> - The fixtures must use the real rules objects they represent: firearms use [[WEAPON PROFILE]], armor contributes to [[RES]], shields use [[COVER]] / [[MATERIAL PENETRATION]], grenades use the locked grenade procedure, and drones remain physical systems.
> - No reference item receives a generic quality bonus. Differences must be expressed through actual fields such as DAMAGE, target PENETRATION, MATERIAL PENETRATION, RANGE PROFILE, RECOIL, HANDLING, capacity/feed, durability, burden, sensor capability, signal, medical capability, or another established physical/system quantity.
> - Starting numeric values are deliberately rough and are recorded in [[REFERENCE EQUIPMENT VALUES]]. [[BALANCE CONTRACT]] and [[BALANCE PARAMETERS]] define exactly which declared numeric parameters Codex may tune and the bounds/constraints on that tuning while forbidding conceptual rule changes.
> - The reference set is intentionally small. Its purpose is coverage of important behavior families, not catalog completeness.
>

> ## Numeric layer
> [[REFERENCE EQUIPMENT VALUES]] defines the v0.1 starting values and test-only interpretations for:
> - firearm DAMAGE / target PENETRATION / MATERIAL PENETRATION;
> - RANGE PROFILE breakpoints;
> - RECOIL / HANDLING ratings;
> - capacities, feed systems, reload/cycle AP;
> - armor RES contribution and BURDEN;
> - shield durability/material resistance;
> - grenade radii, component strengths, fuse timing, and smoke behavior;
> - medical supplies and setup/handling;
> - drone movement, durability, battery, sensors, signal, payload, autonomy/control costs;
> - the first engineering deployable's setup, durability, power, coverage, and relay behavior.
>
> Those values are controlled balance parameters rather than immutable conceptual canon. Codex may tune only values admitted by [[BALANCE PARAMETERS]] and must satisfy [[BALANCE CONTRACT]] / [[BALANCE SCENARIOS]]; it does not get to invent missing equipment mechanics or replace the locked physical behavior.
