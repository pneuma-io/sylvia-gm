# RIFLEMAN

**At a glance:** Sustained conventional firearm mastery, firing tempo, weapon manipulation, and suppression. The mechanical role is locked; the display name is provisional.

> [!note]- Full rules
> **Class baseline — FIRE CONTROL**
> - At Level 1, RIFLEMAN recovers RECOIL through time/AP more efficiently than an ordinary shooter.
> - Exact recovery rate is a balance parameter. This does not erase RECOIL or make sustained fire free; it means the class returns to a controlled firing state faster between strings.
>
> **Specialty directions**
> - RIFLEMAN baseline owns controlled conventional gunfighting tempo: maintaining useful fire through RECOIL, exposure, ordinary movement, and firearm state without turning that baseline competence into a specialty of its own.
> - The three locked specialty directions are GUNNER, OPERATOR, and ARSENAL.
> - **GUNNER:** keep firing — volume, sustained fire, RECOIL management, and SUPPRESSION.
> - **OPERATOR:** keep the gun running — reloads, cycling, chambering, magazine handling, and weapon transitions without deleting physical firearm state.
> - **ARSENAL:** keep the right gun available — carry and efficiently transition among a deliberately broad set of firearms while respecting burden, slings, holsters, ammunition, and inventory.
> - ARSENAL does not grant the class identities of BREACHER or SHARPSHOOTER merely because the character carries a shotgun, SMG, or precision-oriented firearm.
> - The specialty identities are locked. Their detailed Entry-through-Tier-5 progression is currently **tertiary**: approved as the working progression, but still open to later all-class review and implementation pressure-testing.
> - Exact AP, RECOIL, ammunition, load/burden, and other numeric balance values remain open.
