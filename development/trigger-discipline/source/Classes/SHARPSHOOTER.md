# SHARPSHOOTER

**At a glance:** Long-range precision class built around AIM, RANGE, deliberate firing solutions, and OVERWATCH. Mobility is ordinary rather than specially efficient; the class benefits from staying established when circumstances allow.

> [!note]- Full rules
> **Class baseline — FIRING SOLUTION**
> - At Level 1, ordinary AIM offsets more RANGE-derived attack difficulty for SHARPSHOOTER than for an ordinary shooter.
> - Exact AIM benefit and RANGE thresholds are balance parameters. AIM still costs time/AP and does not extend a weapon beyond physically plausible ballistic performance.
>
> **Specialty directions**
> - All three specialty directions preserve SHARPSHOOTER as the long-range precision class.
> - **SNIPER:** deepest investment in deliberate AIM and difficult long-range firing solutions.
> - **SENTINEL:** established-position specialist focused on OVERWATCH and controlling long sightlines.
> - **SNAPSHOT:** rapidly acquires a viable long-range firing solution when an opportunity is brief.
> - SNAPSHOT is not a close-range or run-and-gun branch and does not erase RANGE/HANDLING limits.
