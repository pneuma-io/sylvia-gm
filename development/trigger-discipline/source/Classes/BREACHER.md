# BREACHER

**At a glance:** Taking defended close positions through close gunplay, CQC, entry, and grenades.

> [!note]- Full rules
> **Class baseline — CLOSE ASSAULT**
> - At Level 1, movement that genuinely closes distance toward a known enemy into the close-engagement envelope is more DISTANCE:AP efficient than ordinary movement.
> - Exact efficiency and qualifying close-engagement distance are balance parameters.
> - The benefit does not apply to retreat, unrelated lateral movement, or movement that does not actually close toward the selected threat.
>
> **Specialty directions**
> The three locked major specialty directions are BREAKER, SHOCK, and ENFORCER.
>
> - **BREAKER:** creates and exploits entry points. The breach itself may use any appropriate method; the specialty is not defined by a breaching shotgun. Shotguns fit naturally because a newly created opening often produces a sudden close-range firefight immediately after entry.
> - **SHOCK:** disrupts and collapses organized defense through grenades, close suppression, forced movement, sudden violence, and momentum. Its strength is preventing defenders from executing or re-establishing their plan.
> - **ENFORCER:** dominates contact-range fighting through CQC, weapon retention, shoves, throws, CONTROL, DISARM, and close gunplay. Shotguns fit naturally because the specialty lives at the ranges where CQC and close firearm use overlap.
>
> BREAKER changes the entry problem, SHOCK breaks the defensive plan, and ENFORCER wins the physical close fight. Equipment remains available by fiction and skill rather than being class-exclusive.
