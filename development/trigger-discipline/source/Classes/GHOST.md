# GHOST

**At a glance:** Stealth, infiltration, flanking, and asymmetric positioning. GHOST has unusually strong baseline battlefield mobility because the role needs to cross meaningful distance to create flanks.

> [!note]- Full rules
> **Class baseline — LIGHT FOOTPRINT**
> - At Level 1, GHOST has better baseline DISTANCE:AP mobility than an ordinary combatant.
> - Exact movement efficiency is a balance parameter. The advantage is visible from Level 1 and does not require later specialty investment before the class feels mobile.
>
> **Specialty directions**
> - The three locked specialty directions are INFILTRATOR, FLANKER, and EVADER.
> - **INFILTRATOR:** specializes in penetrating guarded spaces unseen through stealth, concealment, observation, route choice, and patrol timing. Baseline GHOST can already infiltrate; this specialty goes deeper.
> - **FLANKER:** converts mobility and asymmetric positioning into combat advantage, exploiting directional COVER and relocating when enemies reorient.
> - **EVADER:** specializes in breaking contact, crossing sightlines, confusing pursuit, and making enemy information stale again after being detected.
> - Being seen once does not permanently end stealth play. EVADER works through the existing stateful-information model rather than a separate stealth minigame.
> - Sabotage, MEDICINE, hacking, and other objective work remain available through skills and equipment rather than being reserved for a GHOST specialty.
