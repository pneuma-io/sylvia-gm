# TECH

**At a glance:** Deploys, configures, repairs, and operates physical technology for battlefield control. TECH is the systems-operator / field-engineer class; hacking belongs to HACKER.

> [!note]- Full rules
> **Class baseline — SYSTEMS HANDLING**
> - At Level 1, intended operation and deployment of physical technical equipment costs less AP/time for TECH than for an ordinary competent user.
> - Exact efficiency is a balance parameter. The benefit does not grant unauthorized access, bypass security, or make the equipment capable of functions it does not physically possess.
>
> **Specialty directions and boundaries**
> - TECH progression enhances the intended tactical use of physical systems: drones, sensors, relays, deployables, communications equipment, barriers, tools, and other field technology supported by the Campaign Setting.
> - TECH does not gain privileged access to a system merely because it can operate or repair that system. Bypassing security, exploiting unfamiliar internals, stealing privilege, or subverting networked systems belongs to HACKER.
> - DRONE OPERATIONS covers piloting, tasking, and coordinating unmanned systems. ENGINEERING covers building, repairing, modifying, and physically configuring systems.
> - Current locked specialty directions are FPV DRONES, AUTODRONES, and FIELD ENGINEER. Additional TECH specialties may be added only when they create a genuinely distinct operational fantasy.
> - A mundane 2026/27 Campaign Setting must fully support TECH using contemporary drones, sensors, radios, tools, and deployable equipment.
