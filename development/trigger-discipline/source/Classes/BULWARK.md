# BULWARK

**At a glance:** Protection, defensive positioning, RES, and holding ground. Physically heavy and deliberately less mobile than most classes.

> [!note]- Full rules
> **Class baseline — HARD TO SHIFT**
> - At Level 1, BULWARK has higher baseline RES than an ordinary combatant and worse DISTANCE:AP mobility.
> - Exact RES and movement values are balance parameters, but the tradeoff is part of the class identity: more survivable in a position, less efficient at rapidly traversing the battlefield.
>
> **Specialty directions**
> - The class is about occupying space rather than rapidly traversing it.
> - Its locked specialty directions are SHIELD BEARER, ANCHOR, and GUARDIAN.
> - SHIELD BEARER uses a full-size ballistic or riot shield as trained movable directional COVER.
> - ANCHOR establishes a durable STRONGPOINT that the squad can operate from.
> - GUARDIAN actively protects nearby allies through INTERPOSE, physical positioning, screening, and extraction rather than medical recovery.
> - The specialty identities and ENTRY ABILITIES are locked. Their detailed Tier-1 through Tier-5 progression is currently **tertiary**: approved as the working progression but still open to later class-pass and implementation revision.
