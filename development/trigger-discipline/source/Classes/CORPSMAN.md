# CORPSMAN

**At a glance:** Battlefield medicine, RES recovery, HP treatment, stabilization, resuscitation, and getting DOWNED allies back under control.

> [!note]- Full rules
> **Class baseline — CLINICAL TEMPO**
> - At Level 1, MEDICINE actions that diagnose or treat a casualty under operational conditions cost less AP/time and receive a stronger treatment/check benefit than the same action performed by an ordinary competent character.
> - Exact AP reduction and effectiveness benefit are balance parameters. The class does not gain permission to perform treatment that the injury, equipment, skill, or medical technology cannot support.
>
> **Specialty directions and boundaries**
> - CORPSMAN does not own MEDICINE. A party without a CORPSMAN can still stabilize, resuscitate, treat wounds, and perform other medical actions when the fiction, equipment, and skill allow.
> - CORPSMAN progression makes those actions much faster, more reliable, more capable under pressure, and able to achieve stronger recovery outcomes.
> - The three locked specialty directions are FIELD MEDIC, TRAUMA, and RESUSCITATOR.
> - FIELD MEDIC specializes in restoring immediate combat effectiveness and performing treatment during an active fight.
> - TRAUMA specializes in HP damage, serious wounds, and INJURY treatment.
> - RESUSCITATOR specializes in DOWNED characters and LIFE-THREATENING CONDITIONS, including STABILIZE, RESUSCITATION, and preventing critical deterioration toward death.
> - All CORPSMEN remain capable across all three medical problem types; specialties improve one area rather than granting permission to perform it.
> - The structural procedures for STABILIZE, LIFE-THREATENING CONDITION states, DEATH'S DOOR, and RESUSCITATION are locked. Exact AP costs, DIFFICULTIES, treatment magnitudes, medical equipment requirements, deterioration intervals, and reversible windows remain balance/reference parameters.
> - Specialty Entry abilities must add a mechanical delta beyond CLINICAL TEMPO rather than merely repeating that CORPSMAN performs MEDICINE faster or more effectively.
