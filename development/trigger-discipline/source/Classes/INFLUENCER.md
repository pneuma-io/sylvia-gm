# INFLUENCER

**At a glance:** Leverage, influence, negotiation, social advantage, reputation, and human intelligence.

> [!note]- Full rules
> **Class baseline — WORK THE ANGLE**
> - At Level 1, once INFLUENCER identifies a relevant SOCIAL FACT, converting that fact into usable LEVERAGE in the same social situation requires less setup/time and produces a stronger leverage benefit than it would for an ordinary competent character.
> - Exact timing and leverage strength are balance parameters. The SOCIAL FACT must still be true or credibly believed, relevant to the target, and capable of affecting the requested outcome.
>
> **Specialty directions and boundaries**
> - INFLUENCER does not own general battlefield command or TACTICS. Tactical judgment remains an independent trained competence available to any class.
> - INFLUENCER progression makes social leverage unusually powerful, flexible, and actionable when the fiction provides a plausible social route.
> - The three locked specialty directions are DIPLOMAT, HANDLER, and ICON.
> - DIPLOMAT specializes in negotiation, de-escalation, surrender, bargains, and constructing agreements other people can plausibly accept.
> - HANDLER specializes in contacts, informants, favors, human intelligence, introductions, and maintaining useful relationship networks.
> - ICON specializes in reputation, recognition, public image, audience, social proof, and turning personal visibility into leverage. ICON does not require worldwide celebrity at low level; reputation can begin within a narrow scene, profession, faction, or community and grow with progression.
> - All INFLUENCERS remain capable across all three social problem types; specialties improve one direction rather than granting permission to attempt it.
> - INFLUENCER may influence morale, rally, negotiate surrender, or direct people through social leverage when the fiction supports it, but it does not replace ordinary player choice or create social mind control.
> - Exact bonuses, reach, reputation thresholds, contact availability, and progression benefits remain open for balance implementation.
