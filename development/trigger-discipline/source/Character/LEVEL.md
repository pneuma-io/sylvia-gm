# LEVEL

**At a glance:** Trigger Discipline uses Levels 1–20; advancement adds capability and options far more than raw physical inflation.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - Level 1 is already competent and includes a fully functional CLASS identity plus the character's first SPECIALTY progression choice.
> - Milestone advancement is the default; a Campaign Setting or Plan may use numerical XP.
> - Every level provides a meaningful SPECIALTY progression choice.
> - SPECIALTY progression is not a locked subclass path: a character may deepen one specialty direction or invest across several directions when prerequisites allow.
> - Deeper and more transformative specialty nodes require prior investment rather than being available as shallow dips.
> - SKILLS improve regularly and APTITUDES improve rarely as separate progression layers; these do not replace the level's SPECIALTY choice.
> - Mundane high-level humans remain physically human unless a Campaign Setting provides explicit augmentation.
> - RES may scale substantially more than HP.
> - No hard multiclass system initially.
