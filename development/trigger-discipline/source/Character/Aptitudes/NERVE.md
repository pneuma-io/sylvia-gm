# NERVE

**At a glance:** Functioning under stress and maintaining composure.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - Detailed NERVE applications remain open.
> - NERVE may matter when fear, shock, pain, interrogation pressure, or another genuinely stressful circumstance warrants a CHECK.
> - SUPPRESSION itself does **not** call for a NERVE check, create a fear/pinned state, decide whether a character is allowed to act, or make bullets less dangerous. Exposure into active SUPPRESSION is resolved through the physical SUPPRESSION / projectile procedure.
> - A future morale, panic, or psychological-pressure rule may use NERVE, but it must remain distinct from the ballistic mechanics of SUPPRESSION.
