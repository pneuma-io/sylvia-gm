# DRONE OPERATIONS

**At a glance:** Trained operation of unmanned systems: manual piloting, autonomous tasking, coordination, and demanding control under real battlefield conditions.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - DRONE OPERATIONS governs operating drones, not constructing or repairing them.
> - Manual precision flying may pair with PRECISION; interpreting a difficult sensor picture may pair with ACUITY; sophisticated tasking or coordination may pair with REASON. There is no permanent APTITUDE binding.
> - ENGINEERING covers building, repairing, modifying, and physically configuring the machine.
> - LOCAL HACKING and NETWORKING cover subverting a drone or its connected systems rather than operating it normally.
> - VEHICLES remains the broad skill for demanding operation of manned vehicles and ordinary travel machinery.
> - Routine, consequence-free drone use does not require a CHECK merely because the device is a drone.
