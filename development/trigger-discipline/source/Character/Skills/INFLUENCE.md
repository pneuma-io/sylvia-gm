# INFLUENCE

**At a glance:** Persuasion, pressure, deception, command, and attempts to change behavior without mind control.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - The desired outcome must be psychologically plausible.
> - DECEPTION uses INFLUENCE rather than a separate lying skill.
> - Known SOCIAL FACTS and LEVERAGE can make an attempt more reliable.
> - Communication requires a channel, not necessarily line of sight.
