# MEDICINE

**At a glance:** Treatment, stabilization, resuscitation, and medical care. CORPSMAN makes medical actions exceptionally effective rather than owning them.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - Medical capability is not class-gated. A party without a CORPSMAN can still attempt [[STABILIZE]], [[RESUSCITATION]], wound treatment, diagnosis, and other medical care when the fiction, equipment, and training support it.
> - A medical action must address an actual physical problem with an intervention capable of affecting it. Skill does not substitute for missing anatomy, unavailable equipment, or nonexistent medical capability.
> - [[STABILIZE]] targets a specific ACTIVE [[LIFE-THREATENING CONDITION]] and attempts to change it to STABILIZED by controlling its real lethal mechanism.
> - [[RESUSCITATION]] targets a reversible FAILED life-threatening condition and attempts to restore the failed vital function to a medically controlled state.
> - Failure on a MEDICINE CHECK does not by itself invent catastrophic deterioration. Time, continued injury, lost treatment, and the casualty's actual condition determine worsening.
> - CORPSMAN progression improves speed, reliability, performance under pressure, and the strength of achievable recovery outcomes.
> - Exact AP costs, DIFFICULTIES, equipment consumption, recovery amounts, deterioration intervals, and similar magnitudes are balance parameters.
