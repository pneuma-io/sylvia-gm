# STEALTH

**At a glance:** Avoiding detection and managing enemy TARGET KNOWLEDGE in a stateful information model; failure reveals only what circumstances could actually reveal.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - STEALTH does not make a character invisible and is not a binary hidden/unhidden toggle.
> - Roll STEALTH only when an action creates a meaningful uncertain chance for an observer to gain or preserve information.
> - Examples include crossing a sightline, passing close to an observer, manipulating a noisy object, moving through poor concealment, changing position while enemies search a LAST KNOWN area, or performing another task while concealed.
> - On success, the action does not give the observer more TARGET KNOWLEDGE than circumstances inevitably reveal.
> - On failure, grant the strongest TARGET KNOWLEDGE the observer could physically have obtained from that failed action. Failure does not automatically mean exact detection.
> - A failed quiet interaction may create SUSPICIOUS information; localized sound or movement may create APPROXIMATE information; unavoidable direct observation can create CONFIRMED information and may require no roll at all.
> - Breaking LINE OF SIGHT automatically removes the observation supporting CONFIRMED knowledge and leaves LAST KNOWN information. STEALTH may then be used to prevent enemies from following the new route or position and to make that information stale.
> - Attacking does not automatically reveal an exact position; the attack's visible, audible, sensor, and other physical signatures determine what information it creates.
> - Enemies may know an intruder exists without knowing the exact position, and information can become stale without the enemy forgetting that an intrusion occurred.
> - Exact DIFFICULTIES, search timing, and benchmark staleness distances remain balance/reference parameters.
