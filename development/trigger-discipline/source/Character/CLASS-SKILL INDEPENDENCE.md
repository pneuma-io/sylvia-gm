# CLASS-SKILL INDEPENDENCE

**At a glance:** CLASS determines progression options and unusual capabilities; SKILLS describe learned competence. No class owns a skill.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - A character may invest in any SKILL subject to the general progression rules; classes do not impose rigid skill lists or class-specific skill caps.
> - A class or specialty may make particular uses of a SKILL unusually powerful, efficient, or tactically flexible without making ordinary competence exclusive to that class.
> - The same SKILL may pair with different APTITUDES depending on the action.
> - Being highly trained in a SKILL does not grant another class's specialty features.
