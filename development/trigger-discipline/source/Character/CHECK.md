# CHECK

**At a glance:** Roll `1d20 + SKILL + relevant APTITUDE + situational modifiers` against DIFFICULTY when the outcome is uncertain and matters.

> [!note]- Full rules
> - Do not roll for trivial, inevitable, impossible, or otherwise unwarranted actions.
> - SKILL should contribute more numerically than APTITUDE so trained competence beats raw talent.
> - SKILLS are independent; the action determines the relevant APTITUDE.
> - Most SKILLS may be attempted untrained. Some technical tasks can require minimum training or proper equipment.
> - DIFFICULTY is generally visible before committing AP/resources unless uncertainty itself is meaningful.
> - Prefer one active roll against a derived defense when one side is clearly acting and another resisting.
> - Repeated attempts are DM discretion.
