# MANUAL CONTROL

**At a glance:** Trade operator attention/AP for much better drone performance: fine movement, aggressive FPV maneuvers, precision aiming, and difficult navigation.

> [!note]- Full rules
> The operator's physical body remains wherever it actually is and can still be threatened while attention is on the drone.
