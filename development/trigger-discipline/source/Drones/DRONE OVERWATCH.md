# DRONE OVERWATCH

**At a glance:** An appropriately equipped autonomous drone can watch a defined area and perform an OVERWATCH-like standing behavior without requiring the operator to pay AP for each already-authorized response.

> [!note]- Full rules
> Autonomous combat performance should generally be worse than skilled MANUAL CONTROL unless a SPECIALTY improves it. Exact targeting and shot rules remain open.
