# DRONE

**At a glance:** A real physical unit with position, sensors, movement, equipment, and vulnerabilities; it is another body in the world, not another free full player turn.

> [!note]- Full rules
> Drones can scout, relay, carry, repair, provide medical support, observe, or fight depending on model. Sensor information is limited to what the drone can actually perceive. A drone is also a hackable NODE. Campaign Setting determines the tech ceiling.
