In this document, **the Licensor** means Daniel Young, operating under the Pneuma IO brand.

The Licensor adopted standard [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](LICENSE) with separate [Sylvia
Additional Permissions](SYLVIA-ADDITIONAL-PERMISSIONS.md) on 16 October 2026 for material the
Licensor is authorised to license, with the stated scope and exclusions. The standard base text
is unmodified. The adopted wording is version 2026-10-16, revision 3. Recording adoption in this
isolated source does not mean an official public release or website deployment has occurred.

## Adopted software scope

The adopted terms cover Sylvia's application, engine, desktop frontend and general authoring
code to the extent the Licensor has the necessary rights. It is source available; its use
restrictions differ from open-source licensing. Check the actual terms rather than assuming
unrestricted commercial reuse.

The adopted instrument permits using covered software to sell independently authored content,
monetise gameplay recordings and streams, lead paid human-GM sessions and operate supporting
infrastructure for the GM's own sessions, and share free projects or integrations with optional
donations that do not purchase access. Separate businesses selling Sylvia hosting, platform or
backend access to other GMs require a separate licensing agreement with the Licensor unless an
independent grant already permits their use. This boundary is settled. The instrument does not
itself permit selling Sylvia or forks, commercial code embedding or commercial automated
services. It adds to, and never subtracts from, the base licence's institutional permissions,
statutory rights or independently applicable earlier grants.

## Preserved content and earlier grants

SRD material retains its applicable Creative Commons terms, source attribution and modification
notices. Dependencies keep their own licences. Severed Lands original contributions retain their
existing MIT grant and its incorporated SRD material retains CC BY 4.0. This migration does not
relicense that Setting. Original Plan, Setting or Ruleset expression without an identified supporting
grant is excluded from any unsupported new grant. Catalogue membership and publisher fields are not
authorship or licensing evidence. No ownership of model output is conferred.

Previously granted MIT rights remain preserved. `MIT-PREVIOUS-GRANT.txt`, distributed with this
status, preserves the prior root licence verbatim as historical evidence (in source it is also
under `licenses/`). The retained source does not establish which prior distributions or
contributions it covered. Rely on existing sufficient grants rather than presuming a change to a
source file revokes them. Authors retain their rights in their own modifications; no assignment,
sublicense or requirement for a new agreement from every contributor is inferred. The instrument
explains direct recipient permission for the Licensor's covered code in permitted modified
versions and the separate treatment of other authors' additions.

Trigger Discipline remains separately licensed. The selected policy allows official acquisition,
local installation, compatible-fork play and loaders, official download links and original
compatible additions. It imposes no official-app-only runtime restriction. Bundling,
preinstallation, mirroring or redistribution of protected material requires the applicable
permission or another sufficient grant.

## Separate branding policy

The software grant does not grant branding or artwork rights. The separate policy concerns
Sylvia GM/Pneuma IO names and logos, official Sylvia artwork and sprites, and recognizable
Sylvia character depictions, including alternate depictions used as another product's character
or branding, only to the extent the relevant rights are actually controlled.

The community permissions cover truthful compatibility statements, required attribution,
official-app and personal use, screenshots, gameplay recordings, reviews and tutorials including
monetised videos, and noncommercial Sylvia fan art. Ordinary contribution forks need no
rebranding merely to contribute. Independently distributed fork products must use their own
branding unless authorised, to the extent controlled rights require it. Merchandise and use of
Sylvia as another product's character require permission where controlled rights require it.

The source supplies no asset-by-asset authority schedule. This status makes no finding that
every image or alternate depiction carries enforceable rights, claims no trademark registration
and creates no copyright in generated material, visual style or generic traits. It subtracts no
independent rights.

Permissions and the preserved prior grant. The desktop installer resource map and both SRD
package payloads carry the corresponding files. Older retained package bytes and saved campaign
pins keep their existing notices; recording adoption does not rewrite them.
