# Saved equipment in SRD Lite 0.3.0

This is Sylvia GM implementation policy approved for the Lite game. Persistent melee/ranged sets, free prepared switching, one combat Action to replace a pair, and retaining worn-shield AC during ranged use are Lite adaptations. They are not represented as verbatim SRD rules. The weapon catalogue, actual inventory, armor, ammunition and turn authorities remain the existing SRD-backed implementations. Off-hand attacks remain explicit, including Light/Nick and its once-per-turn budget.

Migration adds the saved format through the existing atomic package activation path. It preserves story, characters, resources, inventory and actual known held equipment; an unresolved loadout starts unselected. Never rewrite a pinned package in place.
