# SRD Lite ancestry adaptations

These are product adaptations approved for the fixed117 launch roster. Acquisition tiers, use limits and progression are retained. They are not unchanged official ancestry rules. Original authored characters and story remain preserved. Apply the mechanical choices through the ordinary adaptation flow rather than rewriting narrative history.

| Ancestry | Acquired at | Original | Lite spell | Difference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| elf_drow | 1 | dancing_lights | light | A simple magical light replaces the four moving lights. |
| elf_drow | 3 | faerie_fire | bane | A persistent hostile curse retains concentration and debuff play; it penalizes attacks/saves rather than outlining foes. |
| elf_drow | 5 | darkness | blur | Personal visual concealment replaces the darkness area; its duration and affected creatures differ. |
| elf_high | 3 | detect_magic | longstrider | Arcane movement utility leads into the retained Misty Step; magical-aura detection is not granted. |
| elf_wood | 1 | druidcraft | guidance | Nature-informed assistance replaces minor nature signs. |
| elf_wood | 5 | pass_without_trace | enhance_ability | Cat's Grace can aid stealth through Dexterity-check advantage; it does not grant a party-wide +10 or erase tracks. |
| gnome_forest | 1 | minor_illusion | mage_hand | A magical utility hand retains small-scale playful magic; it is not an illusion or concealment effect. |
| gnome_rock | 1 | prestidigitation | light | A magical lamp complements retained Mending and the crafting theme; cleaning and sensory tricks are not granted. |
| tiefling_chthonic | 5 | ray_of_enfeeblement | blindness_deafness | A necromantic impairment retains second-level progression; blindness/deafness differs from weakening Strength attacks. |
| tiefling_infernal | 3 | hellish_rebuke | burning_hands | First-level infernal fire remains; the spell uses an Action and a Cone instead of a retaliatory Reaction. |
| tiefling_infernal | 5 | darkness | blindness_deafness | Second-level vision impairment remains; it affects creatures rather than creating a darkness area. |

Existing per-spell ancestry charges retain their original pool IDs. A spent original charge remains spent; ordinary long-rest recovery restores it. Forest Gnome retains its proficiency-scaled Speak with Animals pool; cantrips remain unlimited. Original policies are kept in the reversible Full archive.
