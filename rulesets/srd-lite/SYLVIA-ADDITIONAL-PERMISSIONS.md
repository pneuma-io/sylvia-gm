The following permissions concern use of the covered software and permitted modified versions.
These permissions also apply when the specified uses generate revenue.

1. **Independently authored content.** You may use the covered software to create, publish,
   distribute and sell campaigns, adventures and other independently authored content.
   This grants software-use permission, not ownership of output or rights in incorporated material.
2. **Recordings and streams.** You may make, publish and monetise gameplay recordings and
   livestreams involving a permitted use of the covered software. Rights in included content,
   artwork, music, branding and performances remain separately applicable.
3. **Human-led GM sessions.** You may charge for sessions that you actively lead as a human GM
   with Sylvia assisting you. You may operate or arrange the supporting infrastructure solely
   for your own actively led games and their participants, and include its cost in your session
   charges. You must remain responsible for leading those games; describing an automated or
   subscription roleplaying service as a human-led game does not bring that service within this

4. **Free community projects.** You may make and share otherwise free personal, community or

   or use and does not purchase paid software, integration or service access. Contribution work

These additional permissions do not authorise selling Sylvia or its forks, commercially
embedding its code in another product, or providing a commercial automated roleplaying service.
They also do not authorise a separate business to sell Sylvia hosting, platform or backend
access to other GMs or their customers. Such a business requires a separate licensing agreement
with the Licensor unless it already has an independent right to that use. A GM's own supporting
infrastructure under section 2.3 is within the human-led session permission; a paid hosting
provider serving other GMs does not acquire that permission merely because its customers lead
human-led games.

These limits describe what this additional grant supplies. They do not restrict a use
independently permitted by the base licence, statute or an applicable earlier or third-party
grant. In particular, the base licence's institutional-use permissions remain intact regardless
of funding, including its specified charitable, educational, public-research, public-safety,
health, environmental and government uses. Nothing in this instrument revokes or narrows rights
already granted under MIT or another licence.
