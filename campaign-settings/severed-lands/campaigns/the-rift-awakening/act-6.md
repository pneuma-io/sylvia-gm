# Act 6: Resolution

**The final confrontation.** Play the operation begun at the Act 5 closing scene to its observable success or ended failure. Apply only the chosen operation's preparation. Surviving opponents intervene only with established motive and access; reconciliation does not create a replacement attack.

## Intended outcomes

**waystation-stabilized.** The chosen operation is Waystation stabilization; alignment matches, the limited feed holds, the pulse is regular, the Margin boundary holds and outside observers confirm calmer surges. Waystation is stabilized and its surviving communities have a future; the world is not a portal home.

**auxiliary-refuge-held.** The chosen operation is the auxiliary refuge; its aligned controlled feed holds the surveyed shelter through a surge, while outside observers confirm that wider instability continues. The survivors have a functioning local refuge while the wider world remains unstable.

**thornwall-arrival.** The chosen evacuation reaches the agreed Thornwall refuge and the receiving party accounts for its named people and actual losses. The evacuees have reached the agreed refuge and begin making a life there.

**operation-ended-in-failure.** The selected operation has actually failed and ended: a Door/auxiliary attempt has its feed disconnected or exhausted and escape or losses established; an evacuation has ended at a stated retreat location or with established losses. An attempt still under way is unfinished. The attempted solution failed. Survivors face the consequences together in the still inhabited world.

## Immediate ending

The immediate ending. Bring the surviving crew or receiving party together on safe ground. They acknowledge the actual result, account for losses and establish shelter and immediate care. Pay outstanding promises or name their remaining owner. Portray the consequences of the player's actual disclosure and leadership choices; do not force a public revelation if it was withheld. Include reactions and celebrations over the next days or weeks when appropriate. Use friendships and romances actually developed without requiring every relationship or emergent thread to conclude. This completes the final act. Record the matching intended ending only after that immediate aftermath. Sylvia then offers continued adventuring or Finish the story. Continuing stays here without a lifetime jump. Only Finish invites the authored after-story, personalized from the actual campaign; OOC can override it.

## Episode ending

All requirements below must hold; alternatives apply only where stated.

1. The selected operation has a concrete result. Waystation success requires matching alignment, a sustained limited feed, a regular pulse, a held Margin boundary and outside confirmation of calmer surges; failure requires the ended attempt's feed disconnected or exhausted and the crew escaped or losses established. Auxiliary success requires aligned controlled feed holding the surveyed local shelter through a surge while outside observers still report instability; failure requires the feed shut down or exhausted and escape or established losses, without claiming a world-saving cure. Evacuation success requires the convoy at the agreed Thornwall refuge and its named people accounted for by the receiving party; failure requires retreat to a stated location or established losses and this evacuation attempt explicitly ended. An ongoing attempt or journey is unfinished.

2. The actual crew or evacuees are accounted for and the immediate response to that result is played, including consequences of the player's disclosure and leadership choices. Outstanding promises are paid or assigned an explicit remaining owner; no relationship quota or universal reconciliation is required.

3. Closing event: the surviving crew or receiving party acknowledges the operation's actual result and losses, and the next safe shelter and immediate care arrangements for those present are established. Play their immediate reactions and any chosen celebration. No extra rescue, replacement boss, lifetime timeskip or epilogue choice is required.

## Optional after-story

Each structured intended ending declares open-world continuation viable and supplies a brief after-story premise. Read that exact premise only when needed. Narrate the longer future after the player chooses Finish the story, using the campaign's actual people and choices. No after-story selection or refusal is an act-ending condition.
