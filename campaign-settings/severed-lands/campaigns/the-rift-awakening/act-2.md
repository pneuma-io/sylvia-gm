# Act 2: The Hunt

**The player has a foothold.** They may join the Returners, work with them independently, or pursue these leads through another alliance. The act is structured around recovering what's needed to understand the dormant Door: Warden artifacts, inscriptions, fragments. Each mission advances the plot and deepens relationships without requiring faction membership.

**Required evidence journeys and an optional political mission:**

1. **The Hollows — The Archivist's Spire.** Kell's Iron offers an expedition to its Warden chamber. The required find is the inward-flow diagram, also repeated on a portable maintenance plate: the lines feed into inhabited ground, not toward an outward destination. Kell's earlier survey includes a rubbing of that same plate and a witnessed sketch of the diagram, available at the expedition's safe staging point. If the shifting stair prevents entry or the player declines that risk, Kell can show these records and identify where they came from. The party can compare their markings with Linden's records or ask the Archivist to interpret them. Additional combat inscriptions and fragment trades are optional; they do not gate the required interpretation.

2. **The Cairnfields — The Spire.** Old Crane has been signaling. His dated measurements show pulses becoming stronger and closer together. They can be copied below the exposed platform without repairing its reflector. The Deep Rift and the Elder Riftbeast's stirring give those measurements visible context. Tallow's notes about a voice near the Deep Rift are an optional lead, not a substitute for Crane's readings.

3. **Optional: Thornwall — The Council.** The player is sent to negotiate with Lady Kael — the council member secretly funding Door research. She needs Returner protection for a trade route. In exchange, she shares intelligence: Lord Voss is pushing a Riftborn registration bill that would cripple Waystation's supply lines. The player has to navigate Thornwall politics — convince Lord Dorn to vote against Voss, or gather leverage on Lord Merrick, or appeal to Lady Trask's ambitions. The bill's outcome affects the rest of the campaign.

**The Radicals escalate.** A mission is sabotaged — not fatally, but dangerously. Equipment goes missing from the armory. The Quartermaster identifies the pattern: someone is stockpiling. The player begins to suspect specific NPCs. The cell is real. The question is who's in it.

**The Door flickers.** Nine seconds this time. The passage stays opaque while pressure eases inside Waystation and nearby lamps settle. Linden's instrument record and the outside technician's account remain available if the party missed the test. Compare these observations with both regional findings at Waystation. The comparison challenges the promise of a route home without revealing the full protected truth. A cell contact then warns of activation at the Hollows fragment chamber after the next supply delivery; the player chooses whether to intervene.

## Episode ending

All of these requirements must be met; alternatives apply only where stated.

1. The Hollows inward-flow diagram or its portable maintenance plate has been obtained and interpreted: the depicted flow goes into inhabited ground rather than toward an outward destination. A copy or supported witness account of this same evidence counts; unrelated lore does not.

2. Crane's dated measurements have been obtained and their result established: pulses are growing stronger and closer together. Repairing the reflector is optional; obtaining these readings is required.

3. At Waystation, both findings have been compared with the nine-second flicker: the passage stayed opaque while local pressure eased and nearby lamps settled. Linden or the outside technician can supply that observed test record.

4. Closing event: the Waystation comparison makes clear that these observations do not support the promised passage home, and the party receives the warning naming the Hollows fragment chamber and the cell's activation planned after the next supply delivery. This is doubt, not the full protected truth. Accepting intervention is the player's choice, not another condition of Act 2 completion.
