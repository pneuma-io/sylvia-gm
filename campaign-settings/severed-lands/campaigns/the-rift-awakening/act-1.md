# Act 1: Arrival

**Starting truth.** At the first narrated moment, the selected party is already present inside an Anchor Clearing in the Verdant Margin; how or when they reached it is not established. Begin from current Campaign State and give the player a concrete external situation for a first choice. The strange ecology and unstable Veil are available ingredients, not a required sequence.

**Early survival pressure.** A separated juvenile Riftwolf can express the world's central pressure: everything here wants to survive. It may appear directly, leave tracks, drive other creatures, threaten someone else, or be delayed or replaced if play finds a better route. It is not present until the GM establishes it. Violence is possible, not compulsory.

**A route toward Waystation.** Stern's Returner patrol is nearby and can provide aid, suspicion, rescue, pursuit, or a lead when that fits events. Waystation is the act's useful hub, not a destination the player must accept immediately. The Margin can reveal Anchor Clearings, Rift pools, and Veil surges along whatever route emerges.

**Waystation possibilities.** Marla, Linden, Pike, and the Quartermaster can introduce the settlement and its surface beliefs: the Rift is a wound, the Doors offer hope of home, and V is searching for an answer. Bring in the people and information that matter to the player's interests instead of presenting a roster.

**V is absent.** V is in the field, tracking a lead. The player hears V's name constantly — from every Returner, every conversation, every reference to the mission. V is a myth before V is a person. The player won't meet V until Act 4.

**Optional side commitments.** An escort to Thornwall, scavenging in the Hollows, a Cairnfields patrol, or a player-created objective can introduce a secondary location and deepen the survival mystery. These jobs can develop relationships and later leads, but cannot replace the missing-consignment main quest. Combat is not required.

**The Radicals appear — subtly.** A supply cache goes missing. A researcher's notes are found in the wrong room. Someone in Waystation is working against the mission. No one knows who. The player is asked to keep their eyes open.

## Episode ending

All of these requirements must be met; alternatives apply only where stated.

1. The party has reached Waystation and learned its public claims: Returners shelter Rift arrivals, seek a route home through the Doors, and follow V's research while V is away. These claims do not establish that return is possible.

2. The party has established that two copper coils and lamp oil were deliberately diverted, identified Ressa's transfer to a Hollows cell, and obtained the receiving chit or her witnessed account of that transfer.

3. That stolen consignment has reached its final disposition: recoverable contents have returned to Waystation, or loss or consumption has been established at the destination and reported. An assigned investigation, a future return agreement or a promise to recover it later remains unfinished.

4. Closing event: the Quartermaster receives the transfer evidence, checks the returned goods or confirmed losses and acknowledges that this consignment's investigation has concluded. Marla receives the same report if the Quartermaster is unavailable. The recipient states Ressa's actual treatment and the restitution owed, or explicitly that none is owed, and these are recorded. A hearing promised for later with that decision still pending leaves this condition unfinished; repayment and further jobs are not act requirements.
