# Environmental Effects — Severed Lands

Rift-touched environments and hazards. Use these when the setting itself becomes a threat — during travel, combat, or exploration in Rift-saturated zones.

---

## Rift Surges

A Rift surge is a sudden spike in Rift energy — violet-black light, crackling static, and the sensation of reality thinning. Surges are unpredictable. They last 1d6 rounds and affect everything in the area.

### Surge Table (roll 1d6 when a surge begins)

| Roll | Effect |
|------|--------|
| 1 | **Veil Bleed:** The Veil thins. All creatures can see into the ethereal for the duration. Phase-stepping creatures gain advantage on attack rolls. Hollowed creatures gain temporary HP equal to their CR. |
| 2 | **Rift Static:** The air fills with crackling energy. At the start of each round, one random creature in the area takes 1d6 Rift damage (2d6 at mid tier, 3d6 at high). Rift-touched creatures are immune. |
| 3 | **Reality Tear:** The ground warps. All movement is difficult terrain. Creatures that end their turn in the affected area must succeed on a DC 12 DEX save or be restrained by warped ground until the surge ends. |
| 4 | **Pool Flash:** Any Rift pools in the area erupt. Creatures within 10 feet of a pool take 2d6 Rift damage (DEX save DC 13 for half). Pool-touched creatures regenerate 1d6 HP per round for the surge's duration. |
| 5 | **Echo Storm:** The Rift replays fragments of past events. All creatures in the area perceive spectral images — battles, deaths, conversations — imposing disadvantage on Perception checks. Hollowed creatures gain truesight (30 ft) for the duration. |
| 6 | **The Hum:** A subsonic vibration that bypasses the ears and resonates in the chest. All non-Riftborn creatures must succeed on a DC 12 CON save each round or be incapacitated by vertigo. Riftborn are immune. Hollowed are empowered — they gain an additional action on their turn. |

### Surge Zones

Some areas are more surge-prone than others:

- **Cairnfields:** 25% chance of a surge during any combat encounter. Surges here are stronger — roll twice on the surge table and apply both.
- **Verdant Margin:** 15% chance. Surges trigger accelerated growth — the area becomes overgrown, creating difficult terrain and concealment for 1d4 hours after the surge ends.
- **The Hollows:** 20% chance. Surges trigger phasing events — the area blinks out of reality for 1d4 rounds. Creatures in the area are shunted to a safe adjacent space unless they succeed on a DC 14 CHA save (Riftborn have advantage).
- **Thornwall/Nexus/Anchor Points:** 5% chance. Surges near settlements are weaker but more feared — roll once with disadvantage (roll twice, take lower effect).

## Rift Pools

Rift pools are concentrations of liquid Rift energy — violet-black, humming, slightly warm. They're environmental features with mechanical weight.

### Pool Properties

- **Regeneration:** Rift-touched creatures (riftbeasts, Hollowed, Riftborn with the Rift-touched condition) regenerate 1d6 HP per round while in contact with a pool.
- **Rift Damage:** Non-Rift-touched creatures that enter a pool or end their turn in contact with one take 1d6 Rift damage (2d6 at mid tier, 3d6 at high). This damage cannot be reduced by resistance unless the resistance is Rift-specific.
- **Progressive Exposure:** Each round of contact forces a DC 12 CON save. On a failure, the creature gains a Rift Exposure point. At 3 points, the creature's eyes show violet flecks (Touched stage). At 6 points, they gain the Rift-touched condition (advantage on Rift-related checks, disadvantage on social checks with non-Riftborn in settlements). At 10 points, roll on the Hollowing table below.
- **Size:** Small pools (puddles) affect 1-2 creatures. Medium pools (ponds) affect up to 6. Large pools (the Deep Rift, major Hollows pools) affect an entire encounter area.

### Pool Hazards

| Pool Type | Additional Effect |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **Dormant:** Still, dark violet. No additional effect. |
| **Active:** Churning, crackling. Creatures within 10 feet take 1 Rift damage at the start of each round. |
| **Spawning:** Violently active, bright violet light. 1d4 Rift Leeches or 1 minor Rift elemental emerges each round until the pool is disrupted. |
| **Warden-touched:** Surrounded by Warden stone. Safer — reduces Rift damage by half. The Wardens knew how to contain these. |

## Hollowing Progression

For characters exposed to too much Rift energy — voluntarily or otherwise. This is a narrative tool, not a punishment.

### The Hollowing Table (roll 1d6 at 10+ exposure points)

| Roll | Stage | Effect |
|------|-------|--------|
| 1-2 | **Touched (deepening):** Eyes fully violet. Passive Perception gains +2 — the character perceives Rift phenomena before they manifest. Disadvantage on CHA checks with Thornwall guards. |
| 3-4 | **Speaker:** Speech occasionally fragments — the DM may substitute a word in the character's dialogue with a Rift-fragment (prophetic, nonsensical, or both). The character gains advantage on History checks related to Warden or pre-Rift topics. |
| 5 | **Pilgrim:** The character feels drawn to the nearest Rift pool. They can sense its location and distance at all times. Each long rest, they must succeed on a DC 12 WIS save or sleepwalk toward it. Reversible with Athenaeum treatment. |
| 6 | **Channeler:** The character can attempt to channel Rift energy as a bonus action — roll 1d20. On 15+, gain one of: phase-step (30 ft teleport), Rift bolt (2d6 Rift damage, 60 ft range), or Veil sight (see invisible/ethereal for 1 round). On 5 or below, lose control — the DM chooses the effect, and it hits an ally or the environment instead. |

### Reversing Hollowing

Hollowing is not necessarily permanent, but reversal is difficult:

- **Athenaeum treatment (Touched/Speaker):** 1 week of supervised de-exposure. Reduces exposure points by 1d4. Cost: significant. Availability: Cassian or the School of Medicine.
- **Warden artifact (Pilgrim):** A specific Warden artifact — the Cleansing Stone — can reset exposure to zero. Only one is known to exist. It's in the Lodge's vault. Castellan Voss won't part with it easily.
- **Channeler:** No known reversal. The character has weaponized the Rift. It's part of them now. The question is whether they can control it.

## Phase-Step Anomalies

Where the Veil is thin, phase-stepping becomes unpredictable.

| Location | Effect on Phase-Step |
|----------|---------------------|
| **Cairnfields (normal):** Phase-step functions as expected. |
| **Cairnfields (near Deep Rift):** Phase-step distance doubled (60 ft instead of 30). After stepping, roll 1d20. On 1, you arrive somewhere else — the DM chooses a location within 60 feet. |
| **The Hollows (stable sector):** Phase-step functions normally. |
| **The Hollows (Breathing Street / deep sectors):** Phase-step may deposit you in a different time — the DM rolls 1d6. On 1-2, you arrive 1 round later. On 3-4, you arrive 1 round earlier. On 5-6, you arrive normally. |
| **Verdant Margin:** Phase-step into a tree — DEX save DC 14 or take 1d6 bludgeoning and arrive prone. The Margin's growth is too fast to predict. |
| **Near active Anchor Door:** Phase-step fails entirely. The Doors stabilize reality. You can't slip through the Veil near them. |

## Rift-Charged Weather

Rift energy affects the Severed Lands' weather patterns. These effects can last for hours or days.

| Weather | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **Violet Storm:** Lightning arcs in violet-black. All Rift pools in the area become Active (if dormant) or Spawning (if active). Rift-touched creatures gain advantage on all attacks. Visibility reduced to 30 feet. |
| **Veil Fog:** Thick, violet-tinged fog. Concealment beyond 10 feet. Riftborn can see normally. Non-Riftborn must succeed on a DC 12 WIS save each hour or become lost. |
| **Surge Wind:** Gusts of Rift-charged air. Ranged attacks have disadvantage. Flying creatures must land or be blown 1d4×10 feet in a random direction each round. |
| **Stillness:** The Rift's energy goes quiet. No surges, no pool activity, no phase-stepping possible. The Veil is momentarily sealed. This is terrifying to Hollowed and practitioners of the Veiled Path — it feels like the world holding its breath. |

## Warden Architecture

Warden-built structures interact with Rift energy differently than natural terrain.

| Structure | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| **Anchor Door (dormant):** Stabilizes reality in a 100-foot radius. No surges, no phasing, no Rift damage. Phase-step impossible. The Door hums faintly. |
| **Anchor Door (dead):** No effect. The stone is cold. The sigils are dark. The ground around it becomes Rift-touched within weeks. |
| **Door Fragment:** Stabilizes reality in a 10-foot radius per fragment. Multiple fragments stack — three fragments in proximity create a stable zone the size of a small room. |
| **Warden Sigil (active):** Repels Rift energy. Rift-touched creatures must succeed on a DC 14 WIS save to approach within 10 feet. Hollowed cannot approach at all. |
| **Warden Sigil (dormant):** No active effect, but can be reactivated by a Warden Knight using Echo Dice or a character making a DC 16 Arcana check. |

## Using Environmental Effects

- **Announce the surge.** "The air goes violet. The humming starts. Everyone feels their teeth vibrate." Let the players react before applying mechanics.
- **Use terrain in combat.** A Rift pool in the encounter area changes tactics — riftbeasts retreat toward it, smart players pull them away. Warden sigils create safe zones. Phase-step anomalies force movement.
- **Hollowing is a story tool, not a punishment.** Characters who choose to engage with Rift energy should feel the consequences — but the consequences should be interesting, not crippling. A Channeler is a character-defining choice, not a death sentence.
- **The environment is the Severed Lands' personality.** It should feel alive, dangerous, and connected to the Rift. Every environmental effect is the Rift reminding the party where they are.
