# History of the Severed Lands

A timeline for DMs. Dates are approximate. The further back you go, the less certain the record. Events marked (C) are contested — scholars, factions, and survivors disagree about what happened.

---

## The Warden Era (??? — ~1,000 years ago)

**The Wardens build the Anchor Doors.**
A civilization of builders, artificers, and warriors constructs seven massive stone archways across the land. The Doors are not just architecture — they are stabilizers, reality-anchors, built with techniques that no surviving culture can replicate. The Wardens leave inscriptions, artifacts, and the Doors themselves. They do not leave an explanation.

**The Wardens vanish. (C)**
Scholars debate the cause. The Athenaeum's dominant theory: the Wardens departed through their own Doors, sealing them behind them. The Anchor Church believes they ascended — transformed by the Doors into something beyond mortal. The Hollowed believe they never left — that they became the Veil itself, watching from the other side. No theory is confirmed. The Wardens' fate is the central mystery of the Severed Lands' history.

**The symbol remains.**
Every Warden site bears the same mark: a circle bisected by a straight line. Its meaning is unknown. Its consistency is absolute. Whatever the Wardens were, they agreed on one thing, and they carved it into everything they built.

---

## The Rift Appears (~500-800 years ago)

**The wound opens.**
The Rift tears through the sky — violet-black, crackling, alive. It is not a portal. It is not a door. It is a wound in reality, and it has been spreading ever since. The exact date of the Rift's appearance is unknown — records from the era are fragmentary, corrupted, or simply gone.

**The Severing.**
Whatever the Severed Lands was connected to before the Rift — a larger world, a network of realities, something else — that connection is severed. The Severed Lands becomes a pocket reality, cut off from its origin, floating in the space the Rift carved out. No one alive remembers what was on the other side.

**The First Riftborn. (C)**
People begin emerging from the Rift — disoriented, amnesiac, changed. They arrive from different worlds, different eras, different realities. Some remember fragments of home. Most remember nothing. The settled population — those who were already here when the Rift opened — reacts with confusion, then fear, then the institutional suspicion that persists to the present day.

---

## The Age of Ruin (~300-500 years ago)

**The Hollows falls.**
A city — the largest pre-Rift settlement whose name is lost — is swallowed by the Rift. The Hollows becomes what it is today: a partially phased ruin where streets blink in and out of reality and the Veil is thin enough to touch. No one knows how many died. The city's original name is forgotten. Only "the Hollows" remains.

**The Cairnfields form.**
Rift surges reshape the western landscape into an expanse of stone pillars and unstable ground. The Anchor Door fragments littering the Cairnfields suggest that multiple Doors were destroyed in this period — whether by the Rift or by something else is unknown.

**The Verdant Margin accelerates.**
A forest region becomes saturated with Rift energy. Growth cycles compress from years to hours. The ecosystem adapts — or is adapted by — the Rift's presence. The Verdant Scaled arrive through the Rift during this period and claim the Margin as their territory.

**The first Hollowed appear.**
People exposed to concentrated Rift energy begin to change — eyes going violet, speech fragmenting, perceptions shifting. The Hollowed are initially feared and persecuted. Over generations, they become an accepted — if distrusted — part of the Severed Lands' population.

---

## The Returner Era (~60 years ago to present)

**V arrives. (~60 years ago)**
A Riftborn emerges from the Rift with memories intact — a rarity. V remembers their home world: green, quiet, gone. V begins studying the Anchor Doors, mapping Rift behavior, and searching for a way back. This search will consume the rest of their life.

**The Returners form. (~40 years ago)**
V gathers other Riftborn who want to return home. What begins as a support network — Riftborn helping Riftborn — becomes an organization. Waystation is established around the dormant Anchor Door in a mountain pass. The Returners begin systematic research into Door reactivation.

**Thornwall rises. (~35 years ago)**
A merchant settlement within an ancient fortress becomes the Severed Lands' commercial hub. The Thornwall Council is formalized, power consolidating among the five merchant houses that still rule today. The council's anti-Riftborn policies begin — initially as cautious regulation, hardening over decades into the institutional barriers of the present.

**Nexus is founded. (~25 years ago)**
A city grows at the crossroads of major trade routes, welcoming Riftborn and settled alike. The Athenaeum is established around a dormant Door fragment — scholars from across the Severed Lands gather to study the Rift, the Wardens, and the Doors. Nexus becomes the cultural and scholarly capital.

**The Anchor Church forms. (~30 years ago)**
A Thornwall blacksmith named Iron touches a dormant Door fragment and experiences what she calls a revelation. She founds the Anchor Church — the belief that the Doors are sacred, the Wardens were prophets, and the Rift is a trial preparing the faithful for something beyond. The Church grows rapidly, especially among Riftborn.

**The Doors begin dying. (~20 years ago to present)**
One by one, the Anchor Doors go dark. Four of the seven known Doors are confirmed destroyed or permanently inactive. Two are unaccounted for — their locations lost. One — the Door beneath Waystation — is dormant but intact. The timing coincides with increased Rift activity. The cause is unknown. The Returners are running out of time.

**The Eternal Battle arrives. (~15 years ago)**
A Rift Raider warband — an entire company of soldiers from a war-world — is pulled through the Rift mid-battle. They never stop fighting. The Raiders claim the Cairnfields as their new warzone. Their presence reshapes the region's politics and makes the western trade routes significantly more dangerous.

**The Stayers emerge. (~10 years ago)**
Riftborn who've given up on the Door begin building permanent communities. The Nexus Riftborn Quarter is established — the first legal, above-ground Riftborn district. Councillor Merrow becomes the first Riftborn to hold formal political office. The tension between Return (the Returners' mission) and Stay (the Stayers' reality) becomes the defining political divide among Riftborn.

**The Rememberers and the Lodge form. (~20-30 years ago)**
Small, scattered traditions — the Oath of Remembrance paladins, the Warden Lodge knights, the Veiled Path monks — emerge independently, each built around a recovered piece of Warden knowledge or a personal oath. They are not institutions. They are lineages: master to student, oath to oath.

---

## Present Day

**The Door flickers.**
The dormant Door beneath Waystation — the last intact Anchor Door in the Severed Lands — has begun flickering. Brief surges of violet light, lasting seconds, leaving scorch marks on the cavern walls. The Returners believe it can be reactivated. They don't know how. They don't know what will happen if they succeed.

**The Rift is spreading.**
Rift surges are increasing in frequency and intensity. The Hollows is becoming less stable. The Verdant Margin is accelerating. The Cairnfields are trembling. Something is changing — and the change is accelerating.

**The Elder stirs.**
Old Crane, the eldest Veiled Path practitioner, has been signaling Waystation for days — three mirrored flashes, the emergency code. From his perch at the Spire in the Cairnfields, he can see the Deep Rift. He's been watching it for decades. He's never signaled before.

**The Radicals are moving.**
A faction within the Returners believes V is moving too slowly. They want to force the Door open — whatever the cost, whatever comes through. They've been stockpiling Warden fragments, recruiting disillusioned Returners, and waiting. Their patience is running out.

**Seven Doors. One remains.**
Four dead. Two missing. One dormant. The window for return — for every Riftborn who still dreams of home — is closing. The campaign begins now.

---

## Using This Timeline

- **The ambiguity is the point.** The Wardens' fate, the Rift's origin, the cause of the Doors' decline — these are mysteries for the DM to answer (or leave open). The timeline provides what the characters could learn through research, not what the DM must decide.
- **Events marked (C) are contested in-world.** An Athenaeum scholar, an Anchor Church cleric, and a Hollowed prophet will give different accounts of how the Wardens vanished. All are plausible. None are confirmed.
- **The "present day" section is the campaign's starting gun.** Everything before it is context. Everything in it is happening now. The flickering Door, the spreading Rift, the stirring Elder, the Radicals' impatience — these are the pressures that will shape the campaign's first session and its last.
