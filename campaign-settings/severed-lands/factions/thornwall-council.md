# The Thornwall Council

**Type:** Oligarchic merchant council
**Leadership:** Five hereditary merchant houses
**Headquarters:** Thornwall Hall
**Influence:** Thornwall and surrounding settlements, major trade routes

## Overview

The Thornwall Council is the governing body of the Severed Lands' largest human settlement — and, by extension, its commercial heart. Five merchant families control trade, law, and defense within Thornwall's walls, and their influence extends to every settlement that depends on Thornwall's caravans.

The council is not elected. It is inherited. Membership passes through family lines, and the five houses that currently hold seats have held them for generations. This stability is both the council's greatest strength and its greatest weakness: it provides consistent governance, but it also entrenches power in families whose priorities haven't changed in decades.

The council's defining tension is its relationship with Riftborn. The official position is "tolerance with caution." In practice, this means Riftborn can enter Thornwall, trade in its markets, and even live in its Warrens — but they are never fully welcome, never fully trusted, and never safe from the next policy shift.

## The Five Houses

### House Voss — Agriculture and Land
The wealthiest house. Voss controls the farmland surrounding Thornwall, the grain supply, and the livestock trade. They're conservative, traditional, and the strongest voice for Riftborn restrictions. Lord Voss, the eldest council member, has pushed for mandatory Riftborn registration and travel restrictions. He genuinely believes Riftborn are a danger to the Severed Lands — not out of malice, but out of a lifetime of associating Riftborn arrivals with Rift surges. He's wrong. He's also powerful.

### House Kael — Textiles and Luxuries
The pragmatists. House Kael deals in dyes, fabrics, and the luxury goods that Thornwall's wealthy use to signal status. Lady Kael is the council's swing vote — she sides with Voss on trade policy and against Voss on Riftborn policy, depending on what serves Kael interests. Kael has been quietly funding Anchor Door research through intermediaries, keeping it hidden from the rest of the council. If this were exposed, it would shatter the council's fragile consensus on Riftborn issues.

### House Dorn — Metals and Construction
The industrialists. House Dorn controls the forges, the foundries, and every major construction project in Thornwall. Lord Dorn doesn't care about Riftborn politics — he cares about who's buying steel. His Forge Quarter employs more Riftborn than any other part of Thornwall, not out of principle but out of practicality: blacksmithing doesn't care where you came from, and good smiths are hard to find. Dorn is the council member most likely to break ranks on Riftborn issues if it serves his economic interests.

### House Merrick — Spices, Alchemy, and Information
The spymasters. House Merrick imports spices and alchemical supplies, but their real business is intelligence. They know everything that happens in Thornwall and most of what happens outside it. Lord Merrick is the council's quietest member — he rarely speaks in sessions, but when he does, everyone listens. Merrick maintains a network of informants that includes merchants, guards, Warrens residents, and at least one Returner. The extent of this network is unknown even to the other council members.

### House Trask — Shipping and Cartage
The newcomers. House Trask was elevated to the council two generations ago after the previous shipping house collapsed, and they still carry the insecurity of the recently promoted. Lady Trask is ambitious, expansionist, and more open to Riftborn trade than the others — she sees an untapped market in the Riftborn Quarter of Nexus and the Warrens of Thornwall. Trask is the council's weakest voice but its most creative one. She's been quietly building alliances with the other houses' younger generation, positioning herself for the day the old guard steps down.

## Council Dynamics

The council meets weekly in Thornwall Hall. Decisions require a majority of three votes. Deadlocks are broken by the eldest council member — currently Lord Voss. The current alignment:

- **Anti-Riftborn bloc:** Voss (ideological). Usually supported by Merrick (pragmatic — he follows Voss because Voss usually wins).
- **Pro-trade bloc:** Trask (expansionist). Usually supported by Dorn (economic).
- **Swing:** Kael. Decides most contested votes. Both sides court her constantly.

The council projects unity to the public. In private, the infighting is constant. Lord Voss is aging. Lady Kael's secret research is a liability. Lord Dorn's Riftborn workforce is a political risk. Lord Merrick's spy network makes everyone nervous. Lady Trask is playing a long game that no one else fully sees.

## Policies

- **Riftborn Registration:** Riftborn entering Thornwall must register with the guard — name, place of origin (if known), purpose of visit. Enforcement varies by guard and by mood. In the Warrens, it's not enforced at all.
- **Trade Regulation:** All goods passing through Thornwall's gates are subject to council tariffs. This is the council's primary revenue source and its primary lever of power.
- **Warrens Non-Interference:** The council does not patrol or govern the Warrens. Officially, this is because the Warrens is "self-governing." Unofficially, it's because policing the Warrens would cost more than the council is willing to spend on Riftborn problems.
- **Returner Relations:** The Returners are tolerated as long as they stay at Waystation. When they come to Thornwall, they're treated as any other Riftborn visitors — registered, watched, and not entirely welcome.

## Secrets

- House Kael's Anchor Door research is funded through a shell trading company that employs Returner-aligned scavengers. If Voss discovered this, he would push for Kael's removal from the council.
- Lord Merrick has a Hollowed informant — someone deep in the Hollows who feeds him information about Rift surges, Door activity, and scavenger movements. The other council members don't know this. The Hollowed doesn't know they're an informant.
- Lady Trask has been meeting with Nexus guild representatives about establishing a direct trade route that bypasses Thornwall's tariffs entirely. If the council discovered this, it would be seen as treason.
