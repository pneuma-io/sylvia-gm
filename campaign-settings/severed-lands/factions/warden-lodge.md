# The Warden Lodge

**Type:** Martial order (Warden Knight fighters)
**Headquarters:** A converted Warden structure at the edge of the Cairnfields
**Territory:** The Lodge proper, plus patrol routes through the Cairnfields
**Size:** ~40 sworn knights, plus squires, smiths, and support staff

## Overview

The Warden Lodge is a martial order dedicated to recovering and preserving Warden combat techniques. They are historians as much as warriors — every technique they learn, every weapon they master, every piece of Warden armor they maintain is a piece of recovered knowledge. The Lodge's motto, carved in Warden script above their gate: "What was lost is not forgotten. What was broken can be remade."

The Lodge occupies a Warden structure at the edge of the Cairnfields — a fortress that predates the Rift, built from the same stone as the Anchor Doors. The Wardens used it as a barracks and training ground. The Lodge uses it for the same purpose. The knights believe that training in Warden halls, using Warden methods, with Warden weapons, brings them closer to understanding the Wardens themselves — not just how they fought, but why.

## The Echo Dice

The Lodge's signature technique is the Echo Dice system — a combat method recovered from Warden inscriptions that allows a knight to bond with a Warden artifact. The bond grants the knight access to combat abilities that no living fighter has independently developed: predictive parries, attacks that strike along timelines the opponent hasn't chosen yet, a sense of where enemies will be before they move there.

Bonding requires a Warden artifact — usually a weapon or piece of armor — and a ritual that takes days of meditation and physical contact. Not every knight succeeds. Those who do describe the artifact as "awake" — aware of them, responsive to their intent, almost conversational. The Lodge believes the Wardens embedded something of themselves in their artifacts. The knights who bond with them are, in a sense, carrying the Wardens into battle.

## Structure

The Lodge is led by a Knight-Commander, elected by the sworn knights and serving for life or until they choose to step down. The current Knight-Commander is **Castellan Voss** — no relation to Thornwall's House Voss, a fact he mentions frequently and with visible irritation.

Below the Knight-Commander:

- **Sworn Knights:** Full members who have completed their training and bonded with a Warden artifact. Sworn knights take the Lodge's oath: to preserve Warden knowledge, to defend Door sites, and to train the next generation.
- **Squires:** Knights-in-training. Squires study Warden combat theory, maintain the Lodge's artifacts, and accompany sworn knights on patrol until they're deemed ready for the bonding ritual.
- **Smiths:** The Lodge's artificers. Not sworn knights, but essential — they maintain the Lodge's artifact collection, forge replicas of Warden weapons for training, and study Warden metallurgy. The head smith, **Iron Mother Hest**, is the only person in the Severed Lands who can repair a damaged Warden artifact. She's also the only person who knows how the Echo Dice system actually works, on a metallurgical level. She's not telling.

## The Code

The Warden Lodge operates on a code recovered from Warden inscriptions — fragmentary, incomplete, but coherent enough to build a tradition around:

- **Guard the Doors.** The Wardens built the Doors for a purpose. That purpose must be preserved, even if it can't yet be fulfilled.
- **Preserve the Knowledge.** Every Warden inscription, every artifact, every technique is a piece of something larger. The Lodge's duty is to find it, protect it, and pass it on.
- **Train the Worthy.** Warden techniques are not for everyone. The Lodge accepts candidates based on demonstrated character, not birth, wealth, or connections. A Riftborn street kid from the Warrens has the same chance as a Thornwall merchant's child.
- **Never Bond to a Living Thing.** The Echo Dice system works on artifacts — objects, weapons, armor. The Wardens' inscriptions contain a warning: the bond was never meant for anything alive. The Lodge doesn't know why. They don't intend to find out.

## Relationship to Other Factions

- **Returners:** Close allies. The Lodge and the Returners share a mission — understanding the Wardens — and their strengths complement each other. The Returners provide research access. The Lodge provides combat capability. Knight-Commander Voss and V have a standing agreement: the Lodge defends Waystation if it's ever attacked.
- **The Athenaeum:** Cordial but competitive. The Athenaeum studies Warden history. The Lodge studies Warden combat. They collaborate on artifact recovery, but each side believes their approach is the more important one.
- **The Rememberers:** Mutual respect. The Lodge sees the Rememberers as kin — another order built around an oath, another tradition that values commitment over institution. A Warden Knight and a Rememberer who meet will often spar, compare oaths, and part with the sense that they're fighting the same war from different angles.

## Hooks

- Iron Mother Hest has discovered something in the artifact vaults — a Warden weapon that's been dormant for centuries. It woke up when a new squire touched it. The squire isn't in the Lodge's records. They arrived three weeks ago with no history and no home world. The weapon knew their name.
- Castellan Voss is dying. The succession vote is in three months. Two candidates have emerged: a traditionalist who wants to preserve the Lodge's current practices, and a reformer who believes the Lodge should be training more knights, faster — the Doors are dying, and the Lodge can't afford its current pace. The vote will determine the Lodge's future.
- An Athenaeum expedition into the Cairnfields found a new Warden structure — a training hall, larger than the Lodge, intact. The Lodge is claiming salvage rights. The Athenaeum is claiming research rights. The debate is about to become a confrontation.
