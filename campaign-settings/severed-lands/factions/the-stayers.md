# The Stayers

**Type:** Riftborn integration movement
**Territory:** Riftborn communities across the Severed Lands, headquarters in the Nexus Riftborn Quarter
**Size:** ~400 active members across multiple settlements

## Overview

The Stayers are Riftborn who've decided that the Severed Lands is home. Not a waiting room, not a waystation — home. They've stopped chasing the Door and started building lives. Permanent communities. Businesses. Families. A future that doesn't depend on V's research or the dormant Door's flickering.

The movement began quietly — Riftborn who'd been in the Severed Lands for years, who'd built relationships and found purpose, who realized they didn't want to leave even if the Door opened. For a long time, these Riftborn kept quiet about it. The Returners' mission was the only thing giving most Riftborn hope. Admitting you'd given up on that mission felt like betrayal.

Then the Nexus Riftborn Quarter was established — the first permanent Riftborn community built by and for people who'd fallen through the Rift. The Stayers had a physical home. They had a name. They weren't hiding anymore.

## Beliefs

The Stayers don't oppose the Returners' mission. Many of them still support it — they just don't need it. Their core argument:

- **The Rift brought us here for a reason.** Whether that reason is cosmic, random, or something in between, it doesn't matter. What matters is that we're here now. We need to live like we're staying.
- **Integration, not assimilation.** Riftborn shouldn't have to hide in the Warrens or pass as settled. They should have legal rights, political representation, and communities of their own. The Stayers push for Riftborn recognition — not as guests, but as residents.
- **The Doors are a choice, not an obligation.** If the dormant Door opens, every Riftborn should have the right to go home. But they should also have the right to stay. The Returners' mission shouldn't be the only future Riftborn are allowed to imagine.

## Leadership

- **Councillor Merrow:** The Stayers' unofficial leader — a Riftborn woman who arrived twenty years ago with no memories and built a trading business from nothing. Merrow sits on the Nexus council as the Riftborn Quarter's representative. She's pragmatic, persuasive, and has spent two decades learning how to make settled politicians take her seriously. She's the reason Thornwall's anti-Riftborn policies haven't spread to Nexus.
- **Gavin Thatch:** The Stayers' organizer in the Thornwall Warrens. Gavin was a Returner for ten years before he decided the Door wasn't worth the wait. Now he builds — housing, infrastructure, the basic systems the Warrens needs to become a community instead of a camp. The Warrens Court tolerates him because his work makes their lives easier. The council ignores him because he's in the Warrens, where they don't look.
- **The Founders' Circle:** A group of Stayers who've established Riftborn communities in the smaller settlements — the farming villages, the waystations, the places the maps barely acknowledge. Each founder built something from nothing. Each one has stories about what it cost.

## Tensions

The Stayers' relationship with the Returners is the defining tension of the Riftborn experience:

- **From the Returners' perspective:** The Stayers have given up. They're settling for exile when the goal should be return. Their integration efforts make the councils comfortable with Riftborn marginalization — why grant rights when the Stayers are building communities anyway?
- **From the Stayers' perspective:** The Returners are chasing a dream that might never materialize. In the meantime, Riftborn are dying in the Hollows, in the Cairnfields, in the Warrens. Someone needs to build the infrastructure for the ones who survive, whether the Door opens or not.
- **Where they agree:** Both sides want Riftborn to be safe. Both sides want the councils to stop treating Riftborn as threats. The disagreement is about priority — return versus integration — and neither side is entirely wrong.

## Hooks

- The Thornwall Council is debating a "Riftborn Quarter" proposal — a legal, above-ground district for Riftborn residents, modeled on Nexus. Lady Trask supports it. Lord Voss vehemently opposes it. The Stayers are lobbying every council member they can reach. The vote is in two weeks.
- Gavin Thatch's construction project in the Warrens hit something — a Warden structure, buried beneath the foundations. It's intact. It's humming. The Warrens Court wants it sealed. The Returners want it excavated. The Stayers just want to keep building their community without the ground opening up beneath them.
- A Stayer community in a small settlement has stopped responding to Guild caravans. No one's heard from them in three weeks. A Returner patrol went to investigate. They haven't come back either.
