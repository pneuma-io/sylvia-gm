# The Returners

**Type:** Refugee network / research organization
**Leader:** V
**Headquarters:** Waystation
**Size:** ~150 active members, plus allies and affiliates

## Overview

The Returners are Riftborn who've banded together around a single goal: reactivate an Anchor Door and return to their home worlds. They're not an army. They're not a government. They're a support network — refugees who share resources, protect each other, and pool their knowledge toward a common purpose.

The Returners were founded by V, the oldest known Riftborn, who arrived in the Severed Lands decades ago with memories of their original world intact — a rarity among Riftborn. V spent those decades studying the Anchor Doors, mapping Rift behavior, and gathering others who wanted the same thing: a way back. Waystation, built around the last known intact Door, is the physical expression of that decades-long effort.

## Leadership: V

V is a figure of near-mythical status among Riftborn — not because of power or authority, but because they've been doing this longer than anyone. V remembers their home world. V has been mapping Door sites since before most current Returners arrived. V is the reason the Returners exist at all.

But V is rarely at Waystation. They spend most of their time in the field — tracking leads on Warden fragments, investigating the two unaccounted-for Doors, following Rift surge patterns that might reveal something about the Doors' decline. The Returners operate on V's trust and V's reputation. When V returns, decisions get made. When V is gone, the senior members keep things running.

V's personality: patient, methodical, carrying the weight of decades. V doesn't inspire through charisma — they inspire through competence. Every Returner knows that V has given their entire life to this mission. That kind of commitment is hard to argue with.

## Structure

The Returners have no formal hierarchy. Seniority is earned through time served and contributions made. The closest thing to leadership positions:

- **Patrol Leaders:** Experienced members who lead scavenging expeditions into the Hollows, Cairnfields, and Verdant Margin. Patrol leaders have authority in the field. In Waystation, they're just another voice at the table.
- **Door Researchers:** Scholars — some Athenaeum-trained, some self-taught — who study the dormant Door and Warden artifacts. They report to V directly when V is present. When V is away, they report to each other, which is exactly as functional as it sounds.
- **Quartermaster:** Manages Waystation's supplies, equipment, and the armory. The current quartermaster is a Riftborn from a world of endless war who doesn't discuss their past but can identify any weapon by weight alone.
- **Signal Watch:** The members who maintain the signal tower, track patrol movements, and keep Waystation connected to the outside world. They're the first to know when something goes wrong.

## Goals

1. **Reactivate the dormant Door.** This is the mission. Everything else serves this goal.
2. **Locate the two missing Doors.** Four of the seven known Doors are dead. Two are unaccounted for — their locations lost to time or Rift surges. Finding them is secondary to reactivating the dormant Door, but still critical.
3. **Recover Warden artifacts.** Every artifact recovered brings the Returners closer to understanding how the Doors work — and how to fix them.
4. **Protect Riftborn.** The Returners may be focused on going home, but they're also the closest thing the Severed Lands has to a Riftborn advocacy group. They shelter new arrivals, defend Riftborn communities, and push back against Thornwall's anti-Riftborn policies when they can.

## Methods

- **Scavenging:** Patrols recover Warden fragments and Door-related artifacts from ruins and wild zones. This is dangerous, essential, and the primary activity of most Returners.
- **Research:** The Door researchers study artifacts, translate Warden inscriptions, and monitor the dormant Door's activity. They collaborate with the Athenaeum when it serves their interests and withhold information when it doesn't.
- **Diplomacy:** The Returners maintain relationships with Thornwall (grudging), Nexus (cordial), the Verdant Scaled (transactional), and individual Hollowed (unpredictable). They're not a political faction — they're a special interest group — but they've learned that politics affects their mission whether they engage with it or not.
- **Trade:** Scavenged artifacts that aren't Door-related are sold to fund operations. The Returners have a standing relationship with House Kael through intermediaries, though neither side acknowledges it publicly.

## Internal Tensions

The Returners are united by a goal, not an ideology. Cracks are forming:

- **The Stayers:** Some Returners have given up on going home. They've built relationships, found purpose, and decided the Severed Lands is where they belong. They stay with the Returners because it's the only community they have, but their hearts aren't in the mission anymore.
- **The Radicals:** A minority faction believes V is moving too slowly. They want to force the dormant Door open — consequences be damned. They argue that every day of caution is a day the Door could go permanently dark. V has refused. The radicals haven't given up.
- **The Weary:** Most Returners fall somewhere in the middle — they want to go home, but they've been waiting for years. The hope that sustains them is fraying. Every Door that goes dark makes it harder to believe the dormant one will ever open.

## Relationship to Other Factions

- **Thornwall Council:** Distant but functional. The council tolerates the Returners because they keep Riftborn organized and mostly out of Thornwall's sight. The Returners tolerate the council because they need supplies. Neither side trusts the other.
- **The Athenaeum:** Complicated. The scholars have knowledge V needs. V has access to Door sites the scholars can't reach. They collaborate when interests align. They hoard information from each other when they don't.
- **The Hollowed:** Unpredictable. Some Hollowed are former Returners who pushed too deep into Rift studies. Some are allies — prophets who share visions that prove useful. Some are dangerous. The Returners approach every Hollowed encounter as a negotiation with an unreliable partner.
- **The Wardens (historical):** The Returners are the closest thing the Severed Lands has to Warden successors — not by lineage, but by purpose. The Wardens built the Doors. The Returners are trying to use them. Whether the Wardens would approve is unknown.
