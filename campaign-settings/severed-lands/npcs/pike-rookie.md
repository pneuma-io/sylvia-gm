# Pike

Pike is a Returner patrol rookie — three months in, already survived one riftbeast encounter, already too confident for his own good. He's young, earnest, and convinced that he's going to be the one who finds the Door fragment that changes everything. The veteran patrol members let him believe it. They were young once too.

Pike's first riftbeast encounter was a riftwolf — a solo juvenile, separated from its pack, more scared than aggressive. Pike killed it with a lucky spear thrust and has been insufferable about it ever since. He hasn't faced a pack yet. He hasn't seen what an advanced riftbeast can do. The veterans are waiting. They hope he learns before he gets someone killed.

## Personality

Eager, brave, and dangerously inexperienced. Pike represents the best and worst of the Returner spirit — the hope that keeps people going, and the overconfidence that gets patrols killed. He'll either grow up fast or not at all.
