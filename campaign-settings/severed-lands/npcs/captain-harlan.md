# Captain Harlan

Captain Harlan commands the Thornwall guard — the men and women who keep order in the market, enforce the council's policies, and very pointedly do not patrol the Warrens. He's been a guard for twenty years, captain for five. He's good at the job. He's not sure he likes it anymore.

Harlan enforces policies he didn't write. The Riftborn registration requirements were Lord Voss's idea. The travel restrictions were Voss's idea. Harlan enforces them because it's his job, not because he believes in them. He's seen Riftborn who are more settled than some of the market's human vendors. He's also seen Rift surges coincide with Riftborn arrivals. He doesn't know what the truth is. His job doesn't require him to know. It requires him to keep order.

Harlan is the council's instrument. He's aware of this. It bothers him more than it used to.

## Personality

Harlan is professional, conflicted, and increasingly uncomfortable with his role. He treats Riftborn registrants with more courtesy than policy requires — a small resistance, but a real one. He's not a reformer. He's not ready to defy orders. But he's listening.
