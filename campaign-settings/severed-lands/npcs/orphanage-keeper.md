# Dame Maris

Dame Maris runs the Warrens orphanage — a converted basement that houses the children of Riftborn who didn't survive. She's been doing this for twenty years. She's raised more children than anyone in the Warrens. She's buried some of them too. She doesn't talk about those ones.

The orphanage is funded by Madame Thistle's protection network — a percentage of the northern quarter's dues goes to food, bedding, and the small comforts Maris insists on: books, toys, a mural on the wall that the children paint and repaint every season. The mural is the only thing in the Warrens that changes because the residents want it to, rather than because the world forces it.

Maris is tired. She's been tired for twenty years. She's not stopping. The children need her. The Warrens needs her. She'll rest when she's dead.

## Personality

Maris is fierce, exhausted, and absolutely immovable. She treats the orphanage like a fortress — the Rift took these children's parents. It will not take anything else from them while she's standing.
