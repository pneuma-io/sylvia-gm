# Drum

Drum is a fence — he buys scavenged goods from independent scavengers who don't want to deal with the Lace Brothers' commission rates. His operation is smaller, quieter, and considerably less legal than the Brothers'. The Court tolerates him because he handles the volume they don't want. The council doesn't know he exists. Drum prefers it that way.

Drum's specialty is items that are too hot for the regular market — artifacts with identifiable origins, goods that "fell off a caravan," information that would start a war if it reached the wrong faction. He doesn't ask where things come from. He doesn't share who he sells to. His operation is built on the principle that the less you know, the less you can be forced to reveal.

## Personality

Drum is nervous, meticulous, and very good at staying invisible. He treats fencing as a risk management profession — every transaction is calculated, every buyer is vetted, every item is stored in a different location. He's been doing this for years. He's never been caught. He intends to keep it that way.
