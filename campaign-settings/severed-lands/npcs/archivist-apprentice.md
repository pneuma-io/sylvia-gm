# Thorn

Thorn is Linden's apprentice — a young Riftborn who arrived two years ago with a scholar's training and a desperate need to feel useful. Linden took them on because they reminded Linden of themself at that age. Thorn has been proving Linden right ever since.

Thorn's specialty is pattern recognition — they've been cataloguing the dormant Door's fluctuations and comparing them to historical records of Door activity. They've found correlations that Linden missed. They're not sure what the correlations mean. They're afraid to present them until they're certain.

Thorn works too hard, sleeps too little, and treats the Door's flickers like personal failures — if they could just understand the pattern, maybe they could predict the next one, maybe they could help. Linden has tried to explain that the Door doesn't work that way. Thorn doesn't quite believe them.

## Personality

Thorn is bright, anxious, and desperately dedicated. They represent the Returners' best quality — the refusal to give up — and its worst — the tendency to treat the Door's silence as a personal failure. They're going to burn out if someone doesn't intervene. Someone should.
