# Maw

Maw leads the Breathless — the Hollows Crew that specializes in scavenging during phasing events. When the Hollows breathes and a street blinks out of reality, Maw is already there, already moving, already reaching for whatever the Rift is about to bring back. She's survived more phasing events than anyone alive. She doesn't talk about how.

Maw is not a large woman, but she occupies space like she is. Other crews give her territory they wouldn't cede to anyone else. The Hollowed leave offerings at her crew's shrines. Something about Maw makes people — and things — treat her as an exception to rules that should be absolute. No one's ever been disrespectful to Maw twice.

The Breathless have a mortality rate that would terrify anyone sane. Maw recruits from the desperate — people who'd rather die rich in the Hollows than live poor in the Warrens. She doesn't promise safety. She promises the best hauls in the business. She delivers.

## Personality

Maw is calm, direct, and unnervingly at ease in the Hollows. She treats phasing events like weather — something to prepare for, not something to fear. She's not reckless. She's just been doing this so long that what looks like insanity to other crews is just Tuesday to her.

## Hook

Maw and the Breathless are overdue — three days past their expected return from a deep run. The other crews are dividing their territory. But something's been coming out of the Hollows at night that leaves tracks matching the Breathless' boots. The tracks don't move like human tracks.
