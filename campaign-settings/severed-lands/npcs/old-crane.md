# Old Crane

Old Crane is the oldest living practitioner of the Veiled Path — a monk who's been living at the Spire, the tallest pillar in the Cairnfields, for longer than anyone can verify. He claims sixty years. He looks older. Some of his stories reference events that happened eighty years ago. When asked about the discrepancy, he says the Veil confuses time. He says it with the kind of casual certainty that's hard to argue with.

Crane no longer trains students. He lives at the Spire in solitude, trading information for supplies with Returner patrols and the occasional brave scavenger. He knows the Cairnfields better than anyone — every pillar, every pool, every riftbeast territory. He's seen things in the Veil that he won't describe. When the Returners signal him and he signals back, it's usually bad news.

Crane's body has been partially transformed by decades of phase-stepping — his movements are slightly out of sync with his physical position, like a video that's a frame behind. His voice echoes even when there's nothing to echo off. He's more Veil than flesh at this point. He seems fine with it.

## Personality

Crane is distant, amused, and evasive when asked direct questions. He speaks in fragments that feel like koans but might just be the Veil's influence on his syntax. He's not hostile — he genuinely seems to enjoy visitors — but he's been alone for so long that conversation is a skill he's half-forgotten. He treats urgency like a mild curiosity. Nothing in the Cairnfields is urgent. Urgency is a surface-world concept.

## Hook

Crane is signaling Waystation — three mirrored flashes, the emergency code. He hasn't signaled in years. The last time he did, a Rift surge reshaped the eastern Cairnfields. Whatever he's seen from the Spire, it's worse.
