# War-Captain Korr

War-Captain Korr leads the Ashen Claw — the largest and most aggressive Rift Raider warband in the Cairnfields. He arrived through the Rift mid-battle, an entire company of soldiers pulled from their war-world into the Severed Lands. He never stopped fighting.

Korr is a strategist, not a brute. His raids are coordinated, his targets are chosen for maximum disruption, and he's been studying Thornwall's guard rotations for months. He knows the Cairnfields better than the Returners do. He knows the trade routes better than the Jackals do. He's been planning something — not just raiding, but something larger. The other warbands are starting to wonder what.

Korr communicates through Rift-tongue, which no outsider speaks, and through the Rift Seekers who occasionally serve as unwilling translators. When he deigns to address outsiders directly, he speaks Common with an accent that sounds like grinding stone. His words are precise. He doesn't waste them.

## Personality

Korr is not cruel, but he is absolute. He believes the Rift brought his people here for a purpose — to continue the Eternal Battle on new ground. He doesn't hate the settled folk or the Returners. He simply categorizes them as combatants, and he treats combatants as targets.

He respects strength. A party that fights well might earn his acknowledgment — not his friendship, but his recognition as worthy opponents. A party that negotiates from a position of strength might earn a temporary truce. A party that shows weakness will be raided.

## Behind the Screen

Korr has information about the missing Anchor Doors — something his scouts found in the deep Cairnfields. He's sent a message to Waystation requesting a meeting with V. What he wants in exchange, and whether he's negotiating in good faith, is up to the DM. He might be a warlord seeking legitimacy. He might be a soldier who's found something that scares even him.
