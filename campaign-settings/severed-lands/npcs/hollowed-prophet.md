# The Prophet of Four Corners

The Prophet has been standing at the Four Corners intersection in the Thornwall market for three years. He doesn't move. He doesn't sleep — or if he does, no one's seen it. He speaks in fragments: names of people who haven't arrived yet, descriptions of Rift surges days before they happen, warnings that make sense only in retrospect.

The guards don't move him. They tried, early on. Every guard who touched him had a personal tragedy within the week — nothing provable, nothing connected, but consistent enough that the guard now has an unofficial policy: the Prophet stays. The market regulars bring him food and water. He doesn't thank them. He just keeps speaking.

The Prophet is the most visible Hollowed in Thornwall. He's also the reason many Thornwall residents are less afraid of the Hollowed than the council wants them to be. He's not threatening. He's just... there. Speaking. Waiting for someone to understand.

## Personality

The Prophet is beyond normal conversation. He responds to questions with fragments that may or may not be answers. He recognizes people who've spoken to him before — his eyes track them — but he doesn't acknowledge them directly. He's not gone. He's just listening to something the rest of Thornwall can't hear.
