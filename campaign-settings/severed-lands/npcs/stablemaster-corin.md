# Corin

Corin runs Thornwall's caravan yard — the stables, the wagon repairs, the organized chaos of a commercial hub's logistics. He's been a stablemaster for twenty-five years. He's forgotten more about horses than most people will ever know. He's also one of the few settled humans who regularly interacts with animals from other worlds — some Riftborn arrived with mounts, and those mounts sometimes bred.

Corin doesn't care about Riftborn politics. He cares about animals. A Riftborn with a sick horse gets the same treatment as a Thornwall merchant with a lame mule. The animals don't know about registration requirements. They just need care. Corin provides it.

He's also the Guild's unofficial veterinarian — caravan animals that get injured on the routes come to Corin. He's saved more Guild livelihoods than the Route Warden. He'd never say so out loud.

## Personality

Corin is gruff, gentle with animals, and allergic to politics. He treats every creature that comes through his yard the same: assess the problem, fix the problem, send them on their way. The world would be simpler if people were more like horses.
