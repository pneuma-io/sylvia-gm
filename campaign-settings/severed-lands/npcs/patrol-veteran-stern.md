# Stern

Stern is a Returner patrol veteran — eight years in the field, more encounters than she's bothered to count, a scar across her jaw from a riftbeast claw that she doesn't talk about. She leads the most experienced patrol at Waystation. Her team has the lowest casualty rate and the highest artifact recovery rate. These statistics are related.

Stern doesn't give speeches. She doesn't inspire. She prepares — equipment checks, route planning, contingency drills. Her patrol members know exactly what to do in every situation because Stern has made them practice every situation. It's not exciting. It's effective.

She's lost patrol members. She remembers every one. Their names are in a notebook she keeps in her kit, separate from her patrol logs. She doesn't show it to anyone. She doesn't need to.

## Personality

Stern is disciplined, unsentimental, and carrying her losses privately. She treats patrol leadership as a sacred responsibility. The patrol comes back. That's the only metric that matters.

## Appearance and artwork

A humanlike adult Riftborn woman in her late forties with dark brown skin, a strong lean build, short salt-and-pepper hair, dark eyes and one healed claw scar across her jaw. Quiet vigilant expression. Practical patched dark olive patrol leathers over a charcoal shirt, plain trousers, sturdy worn boots and a compact belt kit.
