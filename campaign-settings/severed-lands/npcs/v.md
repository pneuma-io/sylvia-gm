# V

V is the oldest known Riftborn — a figure who arrived through the Rift decades ago and has been searching for a way back ever since. Unlike most Riftborn, V retained memories of their original world: a place V describes only as "green" and "quiet" and "gone." Those three words are the most personal information V has ever shared about themself.

V founded the Returners not as an army but as a support network — Riftborn helping Riftborn, pooling knowledge and resources toward a shared goal. Over the decades, the Returners grew into something larger: a research organization, a political force, and the closest thing the Severed Lands has to a Riftborn advocacy group. V didn't plan any of that. V just kept working, and people kept joining.

V is rarely at Waystation. They spend most of their time in the field — tracking leads on Warden fragments, investigating the two unaccounted-for Doors, following Rift surge patterns that might reveal something about the Doors' decline. When V returns, decisions get made. When V is gone, the senior Returners keep things running.

## Personality

V is patient, methodical, and carrying the weight of decades. They don't inspire through charisma — they inspire through competence. Every Returner knows that V has given their entire life to this mission. That kind of commitment is hard to argue with, and V rarely needs to argue.

V is not cold, but they are guarded. They've lost too many people — Returners who died on patrol, Riftborn who gave up and left, friends who became Hollowed and stopped recognizing them. V doesn't form new attachments easily. When they do, it's significant.

V's defining trait is patience bordering on obsession. They've been working on the Door problem for longer than most Returners have been alive. They believe the answer exists — in a Warden inscription they haven't found yet, in a Door fragment they haven't recovered, in a surge pattern they haven't mapped. The belief is not faith. It's the conclusion of decades of evidence. V is not guessing. V is waiting.

## Role in a Campaign

**As ally:** V is the quest-giver, the mentor, the voice of experience. They send the party after Warden fragments, Door leads, and Rift surge data. They provide context, resources, and the occasional warning that goes unheeded until it's too late.

**As antagonist:** V's patience has a dark side. If the party discovers a way to force the Door open — a method V considers too dangerous, too uncertain, too likely to bring something through first — V will oppose them. Not with violence. V doesn't do violence. But with obstruction, with withheld information, with the full weight of the Returners' loyalty. The party wants to force the Door. V has spent decades trying to open it safely. Neither side is wrong. The conflict writes itself.

**As mystery:** V knows more than they share. About their home world. About the Wardens. About what they've seen in the deep Cairnfields and the unaccounted-for Door sites. A campaign that earns V's trust might eventually learn the full story. A campaign that doesn't might never know what V was really looking for.

## Behind the Screen

V's true nature is left deliberately ambiguous. Possible truths include:

- V is exactly what they appear to be: a Riftborn who remembers home and wants to return.
- V was part of the Wardens — not metaphorically, literally. V's "decades" of searching might be centuries. The Wardens' symbol — a circle bisected by a line — appears in V's personal notes.
- V knows why the Doors are dying. They're not trying to open the Door. They're trying to prevent something from coming through.
- V's home world was destroyed by whatever created the Rift. V isn't trying to go home. V is trying to make sure it doesn't happen here.

The truth is for the DM to decide. The ambiguity is the point.
