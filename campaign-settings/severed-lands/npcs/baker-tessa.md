# Tessa

Tessa runs the best bakery in Thornwall. Her bread is the reason the morning market has a line before sunrise. She's been baking since she could reach the oven — her mother ran the bakery before her, and her grandmother before that. The recipes haven't changed in three generations. The quality hasn't either.

Tessa doesn't care about Riftborn politics. She cares about flour prices. The council's grain tariffs — controlled by House Voss — directly affect her margins. She's been quietly vocal about this at market gatherings. "Quietly vocal" in Thornwall means she says things that sound like complaints about the weather but everyone knows are complaints about Lord Voss.

She'll sell to anyone. She'll also slip an extra roll to the Warrens kids who hang around the market at closing time. She doesn't make a thing of it. The rolls just happen to be "leftover."

## Personality

Warm, practical, and politically aware in the way that small business owners have to be. Tessa treats baking as craft and community — the bakery feeds Thornwall, and Thornwall takes care of the bakery. That's how it's always worked. She doesn't see why Riftborn should be any different.
