# Echo

Echo is a bard of the College of Echoes — a subclass that channels fragments of lost worlds through song. Their performances aren't entertainment. They're remembrance. When Echo sings, the audience hears snatches of music from home worlds they can't return to, melodies from places that might not exist anymore, harmonies that feel like memory even to people who remember nothing.

Echo travels between settlements — Waystation, Thornwall, Nexus, the smaller villages that the Guild caravans serve. They perform in taverns and market squares, never charging, never staying long. People who hear Echo's songs sometimes cry without knowing why. Sometimes they remember things they'd forgotten. Sometimes they ask Echo to stop. Echo always stops.

The College of Echoes teaches that music is the only thing that crosses the Rift unchanged. Echo believes this. They're not sure if it's a blessing or a curse.

## Personality

Echo is gentle, melancholy, and carrying the weight of everyone they've performed for. They don't talk much between songs. The music says everything they need to say.
