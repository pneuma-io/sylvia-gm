# Deacon Ash

Deacon Ash was a Thornwall guard for eight years before he found faith. He still moves like one — shoulders back, eyes scanning, hand never far from his weapon. The council considers him a deserter. He considers himself liberated. The distinction matters mostly to his former colleagues, who'd rather not discuss why one of their best guards quit to join a Riftborn church.

Ash leads the Door Wardens — armed congregants who protect Door fragments and Church gatherings. He's the Church's muscle, which is an uncomfortable role for a faith built around Sister Iron's gentle certainty. Ash makes it work by treating protection as service. He's not a soldier defending territory. He's a shepherd guarding a flock. The distinction matters to him.

He's also the Revealers' most likely recruit — the militant faction within the Church that wants to seize Door sites by force. Ash hasn't joined them. He hasn't reported them either. Something's holding him back. He's not sure what.

## Personality

Ash is quiet, watchful, and carrying the tension of a man who used to enforce laws he now considers unjust. He doesn't talk about his guard years. He doesn't talk much at all. When he speaks, it's measured — every word placed carefully, like a footstep in uncertain terrain.
