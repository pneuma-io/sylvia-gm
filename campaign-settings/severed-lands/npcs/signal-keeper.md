# Wren

Wren keeps the signal tower — the highest point at Waystation, where mirrored light is used to communicate with Returner patrols across the Cairnfields. She's been on watch duty for six years. She's seen things from that tower that she doesn't talk about at dinner.

Wren knows the Cairnfields' visual language — the way dust rises before a riftbeast surfaces, the way Rift pools flicker before a surge, the way the light bends wrong when something's phasing. She's spotted more threats than any patrol leader. She's also the reason the Returners know when Old Crane is signaling — she's the one who sees the flashes first.

She's quiet, observant, and spends most of her time alone. The tower is her domain. She prefers it that way.

## Personality

Wren is solitary, watchful, and carries the weight of being the first to know when something goes wrong. She doesn't socialize much. She doesn't need to. The tower is enough.
