# Madame Thistle

Madame Thistle is the Warrens' oldest boss and its closest thing to a legitimate leader. She controls the northern quarter — the densest residential area — and runs protection rackets like an insurance policy. Pay regularly, and problems disappear. Refuse to pay, and you're on your own. Thistle's operation is less a criminal enterprise than a municipal government with a flexible tax structure.

She arrived through the Rift forty years ago — before the Returners existed, before the Warrens was the Warrens, when Thornwall was still deciding whether Riftborn were guests or threats. She's been here longer than most settled families. She's outlasted three council compositions, two Warrens gang wars, and one attempted council crackdown that ended with the guards refusing to enter the northern quarter. The council has quietly approached her more than once about "managing the Riftborn situation." She listens. She doesn't commit.

Thistle is dying. She's been dying for a year — something in her lungs, a Rift-touched condition that the Athenaeum's healers can't identify. She's been grooming a successor, but the Lace Brothers are circling, and the succession fight is going to be bloody.

## Personality

Thistle is tired, amused, and too old to be intimidated. She speaks slowly, as if each word costs her something. She's forgotten more about the Warrens than most people will ever know. She genuinely cares about her territory — not as a source of income, but as a community she's spent forty years building. The protection money funds street repairs, dispute resolution, and the Warrens' unofficial orphanage. "Taxation with representation," she calls it. "Just not the council's representation."

## Hook

Thistle is ready to name her successor — but she wants something first. A Rift pool in the northern quarter, hidden in a basement, cultivated by someone she can't identify. She wants it found and destroyed before she dies. She's offering her endorsement — and her territory's loyalty — to whoever helps.
