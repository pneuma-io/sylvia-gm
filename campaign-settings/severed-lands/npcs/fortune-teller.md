# The Mirror

The Mirror is a fortune-teller in the Crossroads — a Riftborn in the early stages of Hollowing, whose violet-flecked eyes supposedly see fragments of possible futures. Whether she's genuinely prophetic or just very observant is a matter of debate. Her accuracy rate is unsettlingly high. Her prices are reasonable. Her stall always has a line.

The Mirror doesn't claim to see the future. She says she sees "what the Veil is showing today" — a distinction that means something to her and nothing to her customers. Her readings are practical: will the caravan arrive safely, should I take the contract, is my missing relative still alive. The answers aren't always what people want. They're usually what they needed to hear.

The Athenaeum has studied her. The Hollowed consider her barely Touched — a beginner, not a true practitioner. The Mirror doesn't care about either assessment. She's running a business. The Veil provides. She interprets. The customers pay.

## Personality

The Mirror is theatrical, shrewd, and surprisingly honest about her own limitations. She'll tell you when she's guessing. She'll tell you when she's sure. She'll tell you when the answer scares her. The honesty is part of the performance. The performance is part of the truth.
