# The Archivist

The Archivist claims to be the last of her order — a Veil-touched Elf who's been maintaining the Archivist's Spire for longer than anyone can verify. The Spire is a tower in the Hollows that leans at an impossible angle but never falls, its interior filled with pre-Rift books, most too degraded to read, some in languages that don't exist.

She trades information for Warden fragments — a book consulted for a shard of Door stone, a translation provided for a sigil-stone. Her rates are consistent. Her knowledge is vast. Her patience for scholars who ask the wrong questions is nonexistent. She's been known to eject visitors who waste her time. The Spire's front door opens onto a different street each time, so "ejecting" someone is a serious threat.

The Archivist is Hollowed — Speaker stage, violet eyes, speech that fragments when she's tired. She's been in the Hollows for decades, maybe centuries. Time works differently in the Spire. She stopped counting years a long time ago.

## Personality

The Archivist is imperious, precise, and carrying a sadness she doesn't discuss. The Spire is her life's work. The books are her responsibility. She'll be here until the last page crumbles, and she's made peace with that.
