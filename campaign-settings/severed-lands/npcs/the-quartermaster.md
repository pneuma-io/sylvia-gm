# The Quartermaster

The Quartermaster runs Waystation's supplies, equipment, and armory. They're a Riftborn from a world of endless war — they arrived with a soldier's discipline and a logistical mind that's kept the Returners functioning through decades of scarcity. They don't talk about their home world. They don't need to. The way they handle weapons tells you everything.

Every piece of equipment at Waystation passes through the Quartermaster's hands. They know what's in the armory, what's overdue for maintenance, what was checked out and never returned. They're the reason Returner patrols are equipped and fed. They're the reason the signal tower has replacement mirrors. They're the reason Waystation's supplies lasted through the last winter, barely, and they'll be the reason it lasts through the next one, or it won't, and either way the Quartermaster will have done everything possible.

## Personality

The Quartermaster is efficient, unsentimental, and capable of identifying any weapon by weight alone. They treat supplies as a moral responsibility — every ration shorted is a patrol that goes hungry. They don't socialize. They don't need to. The armory is their domain, and they keep it like a temple.

## Hook

Equipment has been going missing from the armory — small items, impossible to trace, but the Quartermaster notices everything. Someone in Waystation is stockpiling. The Quartermaster suspects the Radicals. They're building a case. They need someone to help them prove it — someone who isn't a Returner, who doesn't have loyalties the Quartermaster can't predict.
