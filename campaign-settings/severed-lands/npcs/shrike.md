# Shrike

Shrike leads the Dusk Boys — a Jackal band that specializes in night raids. Quick strikes, minimal violence, maximum confusion. Her band hits caravans that make camp after dark and melts back into the darkness before the guards can organize. They're the most efficient Jackal operation on the routes. They've never killed anyone. They've never needed to.

Shrike was a Hollows scavenger before she realized banditry had a better survival rate. She's not wrong. The Hollows killed four of her crewmates in two years. The routes haven't killed any of her Jackals — she runs her band like she ran her crew, with the same discipline and the same understanding that one mistake means someone doesn't come back.

She's been watching Gareth's unification push with interest. The Red Banner is the largest band, but Shrike has something Gareth doesn't: the respect of every smaller band on the routes. If the moot goes against Gareth, Shrike is the natural alternative. She's not sure she wants the job.

## Personality

Shrike is professional, calculating, and utterly unsentimental about her work. Banditry is a trade, not an identity. She treats it accordingly — fair rates, minimal violence, consistent results. Her raiders respect her. The merchants she hits respect her. That's rarer than it sounds.
