# The Lace Brothers

The Lace Brothers are three Riftborn siblings who run the Warrens' smuggling operation. Thread, Hook, and Needle — names they chose themselves, the origin of which they've never explained. They specialize in contraband the Thornwall Council has banned: unsanctioned Warden fragments, Rift-touched curios, information that would get someone killed if it reached the right ears.

They're the Warrens' primary link to the Hollows Crews — every crew that pulls something valuable out of the ruins eventually does business with the Lace Brothers. They're also the primary fence for the Caravan Jackals, which the Court officially doesn't know about and unofficially profits from.

The Brothers are ambitious, young, and already eyeing Madame Thistle's territory. When Thistle dies — and she is dying — the succession fight will be between the Brothers and Thistle's lieutenants. The Brothers have been preparing. They've been stockpiling funds, building alliances, and making the kind of promises that are very hard to break.

## Personality

The Brothers present a unified front but have distinct personalities: Thread is the negotiator — smooth, charming, always smiling. Hook is the enforcer — quiet, watchful, never more than arm's reach from a weapon. Needle is the logistics mind — the one who knows where everything is, who owes what, whose shipment is late. They finish each other's sentences. It's unsettling.

## Hook

The Brothers have acquired something unusual — a Warden artifact that's fully intact, not fragmentary. They're not sure what it does. They're auctioning it to the highest bidder. The Returners, House Kael, and the Unbound are all bidding. The Brothers are drawing out the auction. They're enjoying the attention.
