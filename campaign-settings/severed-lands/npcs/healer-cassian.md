# Cassian

Cassian is a physician at the Athenaeum's School of Medicine — a Riftborn from a world with medical knowledge centuries ahead of anything the Severed Lands has developed. He arrived through the Rift five years ago with his training intact and his equipment lost. He's been adapting ever since.

He specializes in Rift-touched conditions — the ailments that other healers can't diagnose and won't treat. His clinic sees Hollowed patients, riftbeast attack survivors, and Riftborn whose arrival symptoms don't match any known pattern. His success rate is high. His methods are unconventional. His bedside manner is terrible.

Cassian is direct to the point of rudeness. He doesn't soften diagnoses. He doesn't offer false hope. He tells patients exactly what's wrong, exactly what he can do about it, and exactly what they should expect. Some patients find this refreshing. Others find him insufferable. He doesn't care either way.

## Personality

Brilliant, brusque, and deeply committed to his patients despite his terrible social skills. Cassian treats medicine as a puzzle. The patient is the puzzle. He solves puzzles. Everything else is noise.
