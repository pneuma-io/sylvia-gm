# Old Finch

Old Finch has been in the Severed Lands longer than most Returners have been alive. He arrived through the Rift decades ago — early enough that he remembers when the Returners were just V and a handful of frightened Riftborn sharing a campfire. He left the Returners twenty years ago, not because he gave up on the mission, but because his body gave up on him. His legs don't work like they used to. His hands shake. He can't patrol anymore, can't scavenge, can't contribute the way he wants to.

So he contributes the way he can. Finch holds court in a corner of the Warrens common room, dispensing advice to anyone who asks. He's seen more of the Severed Lands than most active patrol members. He knows the old routes, the pre-phasing geography of the Hollows, the Cairnfields before the Rift Raiders claimed them. His knowledge is decades out of date, but some things don't change — riftbeast behavior, Rift pool patterns, the way the Veil feels before a surge.

## Personality

Finch is cranky, warm, and full of stories. He'll talk for hours if you let him. Most of the stories are true. The ones that aren't are better than the ones that are.
