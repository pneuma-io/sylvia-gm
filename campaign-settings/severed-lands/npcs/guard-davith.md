# Davith

Davith is a Thornwall guard — young, idealistic, and secretly sympathetic to the Radicals. He joined the guard to help people. He's learned that the guard's primary function is to enforce policies he doesn't believe in. The Radicals offered him an alternative: a way to actually help Riftborn, to make a difference that guard duty never would.

Davith hasn't done anything yet. He's been feeding the Radicals minor information — guard rotations, patrol schedules, which council members are meeting with whom. He tells himself it's harmless. He tells himself he's helping. He's not sure he believes either of those things anymore.

The Radicals are asking for more. Davith is scared. He's seen what happens to guards who get caught. He's also seen what happens to Riftborn who don't have anyone on the inside. He doesn't know which side he's on anymore. He's not sure there are sides.

## Personality

Davith is conflicted, frightened, and in over his head. He genuinely wants to help. He's not sure who to trust. The Radicals seem like the only people who actually care. He's starting to wonder if caring and doing the right thing are the same thing.
