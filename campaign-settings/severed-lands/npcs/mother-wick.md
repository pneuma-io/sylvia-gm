# Mother Wick

Mother Wick maintains the Anchor Church's shrine in the Thornwall Warrens — a single basement room, lit by salvaged Door fragment-glow, with seating for twenty and standing room for forty more. It's the only place in Thornwall where Riftborn can worship openly. The council considers the Church a "Riftborn religion" and doesn't interfere. Wick considers their indifference a gift from the Doors.

She's been in the Warrens for twelve years, running the shrine and performing more Riftborn funerals than anyone in the city. She knows every family in the northern quarter. She knows who's missing, who's sick, who's been asking about the Returners, who's been asking about the Radicals. She doesn't share what she knows. The shrine is sanctuary. What's said there stays there.

Wick is small, soft-spoken, and made of something harder than she looks. She's buried children. She's buried parents. She's buried Riftborn who arrived yesterday and didn't survive the week. She writes every name in a book she keeps under the altar. She doesn't call it a ledger. The Rememberers would understand.

## Personality

Wick is gentle, tired, and fiercely protective of her congregation. She doesn't preach fire — her sermons are quiet things, about endurance and community and the promise that home is real. Her faith is practical. The Doors are sacred, but feeding the hungry is holier.

A small middle-aged human-like default with medium olive skin, rounded lined face, dark hazel eyes and greying short black waves under a plain cream headkerchief.
