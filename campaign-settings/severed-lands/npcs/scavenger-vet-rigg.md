# Rigg

Rigg is an independent scavenger — no crew affiliation, no Compact membership, no backup. He's been working the Hollows for a decade and he's still alive, which is either skill or luck or a combination he doesn't examine too closely. He specializes in the smaller finds — Warden fragments embedded in walls, pre-Rift coins, the kind of salvage that doesn't attract crew attention.

Rigg is the person you hire when you need a guide through the shallower parts of the Hollows — the stable sectors, the mapped routes, the places where the breathing is predictable. He won't go deep. He's very clear about that. The deep Hollows is where scavengers go to die. Rigg doesn't intend to die.

He talks to himself while he works. It's a habit he picked up years ago, when the silence of the Hollows started getting to him. The talking helps. It also makes him easy to track, which is why he doesn't go deep.

## Personality

Rigg is cautious, superstitious, and surprisingly cheerful for someone who spends his days in a partially phased ruin. He treats the Hollows like a dangerous coworker — respect it, don't provoke it, and it'll mostly leave you alone. Mostly.
