# The Silent

The Silent is a Rememberer who took a vow of silence — not as penance, but as witness. They believe that if they stop speaking, they can hear the names better. Decades into the vow, they're certain they were right.

The Silent communicates through writing — a small slate and chalk, always at hand. Their handwriting is precise, unhurried. They treat every written response as if it's the only thing they'll say all day. Sometimes it is. Their ledger is written in a script no one can read, but when they recite the names — silently, lips moving, eyes closed — the effect is the same as any other Rememberer's recitation. Something about it feels sacred.

The Silent appears where Riftborn are in danger. They don't announce themselves. They don't stay afterward. They arrive, they help, they write a few names in their ledger, and they leave. No one knows how they know where to go. The Silent doesn't explain.

## Personality

The Silent is patient, watchful, and impossible to read. Their silence isn't cold — they smile, they nod, they express warmth through gesture and expression. But the silence is absolute. They haven't spoken in decades. They don't intend to start now.

## Hook

The Silent has been seen near Thornwall — unusual, they rarely come this far east. They've been watching the council hall. They've been writing names. Someone on the council is in danger. The Silent is waiting.
