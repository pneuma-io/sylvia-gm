# Salt-Ash

Salt-Ash was a scholar of Hollowed consciousness at the Athenaeum before she became her own research subject. She was studying the cognitive effects of Rift exposure when she realized she'd been exposed herself — slow, cumulative, the result of years working with Rift-touched materials without adequate protection. By the time she noticed, her eyes were already violet.

She joined the Unbound rather than retreating into the depths. Salt-Ash is a Speaker — her speech fragments, her perceptions blur between what's real and what the Rift shows her — but her research continues. She takes notes in a script that only other Hollowed can read. The notes are brilliant. They're also the most comprehensive study of Hollowed cognition ever produced. The Athenaeum would pay anything for access. Salt-Ash won't sell.

She's been translating Warden inscriptions that the Athenaeum couldn't crack. The Hollowed see the script differently — the Rift reveals layers of meaning that the unexposed miss. What she's found: a Warden warning, repeated across multiple Door sites. "The last Door must not open." She's not sure if it's a warning that the last Door shouldn't open, or a warning about what happens if it does.

## Personality

Salt-Ash is intense, fragmented, and fighting to stay coherent. The Hollowing is progressive — she knows she's losing language, losing linear thought, losing the ability to distinguish between research and revelation. She's racing her own deterioration, trying to finish her work before the Hollowing finishes her.
