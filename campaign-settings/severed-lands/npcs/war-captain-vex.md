# War-Captain Vex

War-Captain Vex leads the Silent Wing — the Rift Raider warband that specializes in aerial attacks. Her raiders ride tamed Rift Pteranodons, diving from pillar-tops onto caravans and patrols below. The Silent Wing is the most disciplined warband in the Cairnfields, trained to execute complex aerial maneuvers without verbal communication.

Vex hasn't spoken in years. A Rift surge destroyed her voice during a raid on a Returner patrol — not killing her, but burning something out of her throat. She communicates through hand signals and written orders. Her warband has adapted completely. The Silent Wing operates in total silence during raids — no shouted commands, no battle cries, just the shriek of pteranodons and the impact of steel.

Vex is Korr's most reliable ally — and his biggest potential rival. The Ashen Claw is the largest warband, but the Silent Wing is the most feared. If the unification Korr is pushing for fails, Vex is the most likely alternative. She's been patient. She's been watching. She's been waiting for Korr to make a mistake.

## Personality

Vex is silent, observant, and absolutely certain of her own competence. She communicates through action rather than words. Her raiders understand her completely — the hand signals are a full language now, developed over years of silent operations. She treats outsiders with the same silence. It's impossible to negotiate with someone who won't speak.

## Hook

Vex's scouts found something in the deep Cairnfields — a Warden structure, intact, humming with active energy. She's kept it from Korr. She's deciding what to do with it. A Warden weapon in the Silent Wing's hands would shift the balance of power in the Cairnfields permanently.
