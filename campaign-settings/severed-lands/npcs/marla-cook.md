# Marla

Marla is Waystation's cook — a Riftborn from a world where food was currency and a well-prepared meal could settle a blood feud. She arrived through the Rift with nothing but her knife roll and a spice blend that she's never let anyone examine. The blend is running out. She's been experimenting with Severed Lands ingredients, trying to recreate what she can't replace. The results are... mixed. The Returners eat it anyway. Marla's kitchen is the warmest room in Waystation, and no one complains about the cook's experiments when the alternative is cold rations.

Marla treats feeding people as a form of care. She notices who's eating less, who's not showing up to meals, who's been on patrol too many days in a row. She'll slide an extra portion onto a plate without comment. She'll remember how you take your tea.

## Personality

Warm, nosy, and utterly essential. Marla knows everyone's business because everyone talks in her kitchen. She doesn't share what she hears. The kitchen is neutral ground.
