# The Inkwell

The Inkwell was an Athenaeum cartographer expelled for Hoarding — the institution's term for withholding findings. They discovered a map of Warden Door sites that didn't match any known configuration. Instead of sharing it, they disappeared into the Hollows. They've been mapping ever since — not just the Severed Lands, but what lies beneath them.

The Inkwell is Hollowed now — Speaker stage. Their speech is a mix of coherent sentences and Rift-fragments. Their maps are drawn in a hand that shakes when the phasing is close and steadies when it passes. The maps are the most accurate cartography of the Hollows in existence. They're also deeply unsettling. Some of the locations on them don't exist. Yet.

The Inkwell surfaced recently in the Thornwall market, selling maps. They looked terrible — thin, hollow-eyed, hands trembling. They wouldn't discuss what they found in the deep Hollows. They just kept saying the maps were accurate and the price was fair. Someone asked about a route marked in red. The Inkwell said that route didn't go anywhere you'd want to visit. Then they packed up and left.

## Personality

The Inkwell is distracted, brilliant, and increasingly difficult to communicate with. The Hollowing is progressing — they lose track of conversations, respond to things no one else said, stare at empty corners for minutes at a time. But their maps remain flawless. Whatever the Rift is showing them, it's real. The question is whether the Inkwell is mapping the Hollows or something the Hollows is becoming.
