# Old Vesper

Old Vesper runs The Fortress Inn — Thornwall's largest and oldest tavern, built into the original fortress walls. She's been running it for forty years. She's seen council members come and go, policies shift, Riftborn arrive and vanish into the Warrens. She's still here. The inn is still here. Vesper is a constant.

The Fortress Inn is where Thornwall's politics happen informally — council members drink in the back room, merchants negotiate in the booths, and the Warrens Court occasionally sends representatives through the kitchen entrance. Vesper doesn't participate in any of it. She just pours the drinks and remembers everything.

Her memory is legendary. She knows every regular's order, every feud, every unpaid tab. She's been asked to testify at council hearings three times. She's declined three times. The inn is neutral ground. Vesper keeps it that way.

## Personality

Vesper is sharp, observant, and completely uninterested in taking sides. She treats the inn as an institution rather than a business. Institutions outlast politics. Vesper intends to outlast both.

## Appearance and artwork

An elderly human default with cool weathered light skin, a long rectangular face, sharp pale-blue gaze and straight steel-grey hair in a neat low bun. Burgundy, charcoal and oatmeal innkeeping layers, a plain beige half apron and flat boots accompany her composed upright stance. Both hands are empty. This Fortress Inn keeper is separate from Siren Protocol's Vesper and gains no magical memory or rank.
