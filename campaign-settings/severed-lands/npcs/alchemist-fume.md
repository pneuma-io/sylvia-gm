# Fume

Fume sells alchemical supplies in the Crossroads market — reagents and the occasional Rift-touched compound that the Athenaeum would classify as hazardous. He arrived through the Rift with a case of alchemical formulas and the burns to prove he'd tested most of them. His stall smells of sulfur and something sweeter that lingers in the sinuses.

Fume is the go-to for anyone who needs something unstable. Returner patrols buy his Rift-burn salve. Hollows scavengers buy his glow-paste — a compound that emits light for hours without heat, essential for navigating the ruins without attracting riftbeasts. The Athenaeum buys his more exotic compounds through intermediaries. Fume doesn't ask what they're for. He just provides the materials.

For SRD Lite, this stock is narrative setting detail. The stall offers no usable consumables,
and its salves and compounds apply no automatic healing, light or other mechanical effect.
Consumables belong to future SRD Full.

## Personality

Fume is enthusiastic, slightly unhinged, and deeply in love with his craft. He treats alchemy as art — every compound is an expression, every reaction is a conversation. He's blown up his stall three times. He's rebuilt it each time. The Crossroads regulars consider him a local treasure.
