# Henna

Henna is Waystation's healer — a Riftborn from a world where medicine was closer to art than science. She arrived with her training intact and her instruments lost. She's been improvising ever since. Her clinic is a converted storage room with a salvaged operating table, a collection of Margin herbs that Briar supplies, and the accumulated medical knowledge of a dozen Riftborn who've shared what they remember.

Henna treats everything — patrol injuries, Rift exposure symptoms, the common illnesses that spread through a close-quarters settlement every winter. She's delivered three babies since she arrived. She's lost more patients than she's saved. She remembers every name. The Rememberers would understand.

She's also the unofficial therapist for half of Waystation. Patrol members talk to her while she's bandaging them. Researchers talk to her when the Door's silence gets too loud. She doesn't charge for this. She considers it part of the healing.

## Personality

Henna is calm, competent, and carrying the weight of everyone who's died on her table. She treats healing as a privilege rather than a burden. The patients trust her. She's earned it.
