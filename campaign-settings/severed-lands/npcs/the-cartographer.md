# The Cartographer

The Cartographer maintains the Scavenger's Compact most valuable asset — a living map of the Hollows, updated weekly with route changes, phasing patterns, and hazard reports from Compact members. They've been doing this for fifteen years. No one knows their real name. No one knows what they look like under the hood they always wear.

The Cartographer is said to be a former Athenaeum scholar, expelled for reasons they won't discuss. Their cartographic skill is undeniable — the map is the most accurate navigational tool in the Severed Lands, more reliable than anything the Athenaeum or the Returners have produced. The Cartographer doesn't charge for access. The map belongs to the Compact. The Compact belongs to the scavengers.

Recently, the map has been wrong. Routes that should be stable are phasing unpredictably. Landmarks are moving. The Cartographer has been working around the clock to keep up. They're not sleeping. They're barely eating. Something about the Hollows has changed, and the Cartographer is trying to map a city that no longer wants to be mapped.

## Personality

The Cartographer is obsessive, brilliant, and terrified. The map has been their life's work. Watching it become unreliable is like watching a language lose its grammar. They communicate through notes slipped under doors. They never meet anyone in person. Their handwriting has gotten shakier.
