# Anvil

Anvil is Waystation's smith — a settled human who left Thornwall's Forge Quarter after a dispute with House Dorn's foremen. She won't discuss the details. She'll just say she prefers working for people who appreciate the craft, and the Returners appreciate the craft.

Her forge is small — a converted storage room with a salvaged anvil and a bellows that wheezes. She produces functional rather than beautiful work: spearheads, arrow tips, patrol gear repairs. The Quartermaster keeps her supplied with metal. She keeps the patrols equipped. It's a good arrangement.

## Personality

Blunt, skilled, and allergic to politics. Anvil doesn't care about the Returner mission. She cares about the work. The work is honest. Everything else is noise.
