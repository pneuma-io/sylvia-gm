# Lord Silas Merrick

Lord Merrick imports spices and alchemical supplies, but his real business is intelligence. He knows everything that happens in Thornwall and most of what happens outside it. His network includes merchants, guards, Warrens residents, at least one Returner, and a Hollowed informant deep in the Hollows who may not know they're an informant.

Merrick is the council's quietest member — he rarely speaks in sessions. When he does, the room goes still. He's not threatening. He doesn't need to be. Everyone on the council knows that Merrick has files on them — their secrets, their vulnerabilities, the things they'd pay to keep hidden. Merrick has never used any of it. The knowledge itself is the power. Using it would reduce its value.

He's not cruel. He's not kind. He's a collector. Information is the only currency that never depreciates, and Merrick has been collecting for thirty years. He knows about Kael's daughter. He knows about Trask's Nexus negotiations. He knows about Voss's dead son and the unresolved questions around the Rift surge that killed him. He knows more than anyone, and he shares less than anyone, and he sleeps fine at night.

## Personality

Merrick is soft-spoken, patient, and genuinely curious. He asks questions because he wants to know the answers — not to use them, necessarily, but because not knowing bothers him. He treats conversations like puzzles. He treats people the same way.

He's not a villain in waiting. Merrick has never blackmailed anyone, never sold information that would get someone killed (as far as anyone can prove), never used his network for anything other than knowing. But the network exists. The files exist. And Merrick is mortal — someday, someone will inherit that network. The succession question keeps the other council members awake at night.

## Hook

Merrick's Hollowed informant has gone silent — three weeks with no contact. This has never happened before. Merrick is genuinely concerned — not for the informant's safety, but because a silent informant means something is happening in the Hollows that even the Hollowed don't understand. He's willing to share information with anyone who can find out what.
