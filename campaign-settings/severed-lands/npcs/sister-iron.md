# Sister Iron

Sister Iron is the founder of the Anchor Church — the Severed Lands' youngest and fastest-growing faith. She was a Thornwall blacksmith before she touched a dormant Door fragment thirty years ago and experienced what she describes as a revelation. The fragment spoke to her — not in words, but in certainty. The Doors exist to provide a way home. Not just for Riftborn. For everyone.

Iron founded the Church on that certainty. For thirty years, she's preached that the Doors are sacred, the Wardens were prophets, and the Rift is a trial — a crucible preparing the faithful for something on the other side. Her congregation has grown from a handful of Riftborn in a Nexus basement to hundreds across three settlements. She's old now — her hands shake, her voice cracks when she preaches too long. But the certainty is undimmed. If anything, it's stronger.

Iron has never claimed to speak for the Doors. She says she only repeats what they told her. The fact that no one else has heard the Doors speak is, she says, because no one else has listened properly. This claim is either profound or delusional. Iron doesn't care which.

## Personality

Iron is gentle, patient, and utterly immovable. She doesn't argue — she witnesses. She doesn't persuade — she describes what she experienced and lets the listener decide. This makes her nearly impossible to debate. You can't argue with someone's revelation. You can only decide whether you believe them.

She's aware that the Church is growing faster than she can guide it. She's aware that some of her followers — the Revealers — want to be more militant. She's resisted them, but she's old, and she won't be here forever. The question of succession frightens her more than she admits.

## Role in a Campaign

**As ally:** Iron is a source of spiritual guidance, a safe harbor for Riftborn characters, and a moral counterweight to V's secular pragmatism. Where V offers research, Iron offers faith. A campaign can draw on both.

**As antagonist:** If the Revealers seize control of the Church — or if Iron's certainty hardens into fanaticism — the Anchor Church could become an obstacle. A faith that believes the Doors must open at any cost is not so different from the Radicals. Iron would resist that path. She might not succeed.

## Hook

Iron has announced that the Doors will speak again — within the year. She doesn't know to whom or where. She only knows what the fragment told her. Every faction is watching. Some are already positioning to be the recipient. Some are positioning to prevent it.
