# Linden

Linden is one of V's senior Door researchers — a Riftborn from a world of universities and peer review, who's had to adapt to a research environment where "peer review" means "the other researcher in the room." They've been studying the dormant Door for eight years. They know its fluctuations better than they know their own sleep schedule.

Linden is the researcher who documented the Door's first flicker — three seconds of violet light, scorch marks on the cavern walls, and a frequency spike that their instruments are still trying to explain. They've been chasing that spike ever since. They're convinced it's a pattern — the Door communicating something, if they can just decode it.

## Personality

Obsessive, brilliant, and perpetually sleep-deprived. Linden treats the Door like a conversation partner who only speaks in fragments. They've filled seventeen notebooks with observations. They're on their eighteenth.

## Appearance and artwork

A humanlike androgynous adult Riftborn in their late thirties with light olive skin, a slender build, untidy short dark curls, hazel eyes and visible sleep shadows. An absorbed thoughtful expression. A worn moss-gray research coat over a muted plum shirt, charcoal trousers, scuffed ankle boots and a small shoulder satchel. Holds one closed plain notebook.
