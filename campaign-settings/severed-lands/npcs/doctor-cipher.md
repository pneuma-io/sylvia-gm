# Doctor Cipher

Doctor Cipher was the head of the Athenaeum's School of Rift Dynamics before she was expelled for deliberately Hollowing a volunteer to study the progression. She argues that the volunteer consented, that the data was invaluable, that the Athenaeum's ethics are cowardice disguised as caution. She's not wrong about any of those things. She's also not someone you should trust with anything you care about.

Cipher runs the Unbound — the scattered community of expelled scholars and forbidden researchers — from a makeshift laboratory in the Hollows. Her current project is a device she calls the Pool Stabilizer: a Warden-derived mechanism that she claims can control a Rift pool, turning it from a hazard into a controllable energy source. Her previous three prototypes exploded. The fourth is almost ready.

Cipher is brilliant, charismatic, and utterly convinced of her own righteousness. She genuinely believes that the Athenaeum's restrictions are holding back research that could save lives — stabilize Door sites, reverse Hollowing, maybe even close the Rift entirely. She might be right. She might be building a bomb.

## Personality

Cipher treats ethics as an engineering problem. Every moral objection is a constraint to be optimized around, not a line to be respected. She's not evil — she genuinely wants to help people. She just believes that the ends justify any means, and she's very good at convincing others of the same.

She's charming in a way that's hard to resist. She listens. She validates concerns. She explains her reasoning in terms that make perfect sense until you're no longer in the room with her and suddenly the logic doesn't hold. Her former colleagues describe her as "the most dangerous person I've ever respected."

## Hook

Cipher's Pool Stabilizer is ready for a full test. She needs a Rift pool large enough to matter — one of the major pools in the Hollows or the Deep Rift in the Cairnfields. She's recruiting escorts. She's also recruiting subjects to stand near the pool during activation, "in case of unexpected resonance." The pay is generous. The risk is absolute.
