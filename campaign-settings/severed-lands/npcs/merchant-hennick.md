# Hennick

Hennick has been selling general goods in the Thornwall market for thirty years. His stall is unremarkable — canvas awning, wooden counter, the same arrangement of tools and dry goods and miscellaneous salvage that he's been hawking since before the current council composition. He's seen market trends come and go. He's seen Riftborn policy shift from tolerance to suspicion and back again. He's still here. The stall is still here. Hennick is a constant.

He'll sell to anyone — Riftborn, settled, Returners, Jackals (carefully, quietly, through intermediaries). Hennick doesn't judge. Hennick sells. His prices are fair. His opinions are his own business, and he keeps them that way.

## Personality

Hennick is cheerful, unflappable, and aggressively neutral. He's learned that the safest position in Thornwall is "just a merchant." He's very good at being just a merchant. He knows more than he lets on — thirty years of market gossip accumulates — but he won't share it unless you buy something first.
