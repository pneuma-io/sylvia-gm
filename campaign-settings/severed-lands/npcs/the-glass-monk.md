# The Glass Monk

The Glass Monk is what happens when a phase-step goes wrong. They were a promising Veiled Path practitioner, years into their training, when a misjudged step through the Veil left them partially phased — their body permanently caught between reality and the Rift. They're partially transparent now. Their footsteps make no sound. Their voice, when they speak, is barely audible — a whisper at the edge of hearing.

They can't teach anymore. The phase-step technique requires physical contact to demonstrate, and the Glass Monk's touch is... wrong. Cold. Faint. Like touching something through water. Students who've tried to learn from them report nightmares that last for weeks.

The Glass Monk serves as the Veiled Path's living warning: the Veil takes as much as it gives. They've accepted this role with a grace that surprises everyone. They live near the Spire, keeping Old Crane company when Crane wants company and leaving him alone when he doesn't. They speak rarely. When they do, it's worth hearing.

## Personality

The Glass Monk is calm, accepting, and deeply sad. They don't regret their training. They regret the mistake that ended it. They've made peace with being a warning rather than a teacher. It's not the legacy they wanted. It's the one they have.
