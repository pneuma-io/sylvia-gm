# Lace

Lace is a tailor — no relation to the Lace Brothers, a fact she clarifies immediately and with visible irritation. She's been mending clothes in the Thornwall market for twenty years. Her stitching is precise. Her patience for confusion about her name is not.

She's one of the few market vendors who'll take Riftborn commissions without the usual markup or hesitation. Clothes are clothes. Bodies are bodies. Lace has measured every shape that walks through Thornwall's gates, and she's never found one that didn't deserve to be warm.

Her shop is small — two rooms above the market, fabric bolts stacked to the ceiling. She works by candlelight. She hums while she sews. The tune is from a world she's never visited, learned from a Riftborn customer who couldn't remember where it came from. Lace remembers.
