# Brother Ash

Brother Ash is the oldest active Rememberer — a paladin of the Oath of Remembrance who's been protecting Riftborn in the Thornwall Warrens for forty years. His ledger — the book of names every Rememberer carries — is thick as a brick, pages yellowed and soft from decades of handling. He adds names daily. He recites them nightly. He's never missed a night.

Ash protects the newest arrivals — the Riftborn who fell through the Rift yesterday and don't know the Warrens' rules yet. He finds them before the Warrens Court does, gets them fed, gets them oriented, writes their name in his ledger. He doesn't preach. He just shows up. The Warrens knows him by sight — an old man in battered armor, writing in a book, never far from someone who needs him.

## Personality

Ash is gentle, patient, and carrying forty years of names. He's not weighed down by them — the ledger isn't a burden, it's a purpose. He treats every Riftborn he protects as if they're the only one that matters. He's done this thousands of times. It hasn't gotten routine.

## Hook

Ash's ledger has been stolen — taken from his quarters while he was responding to a Warrens disturbance. The thief left a note: "One name for every Warden fragment the Returners have hidden." Ash won't ask the Returners to pay the ransom. He's preparing to get the ledger back alone. He's eighty years old. He won't survive the attempt.
