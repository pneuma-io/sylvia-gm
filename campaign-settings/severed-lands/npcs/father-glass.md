# Father Glass

Father Glass is the Anchor Church's diplomat — the man who translates Sister Iron's revelations into language the secular factions can tolerate. He's patient, soft-spoken, and utterly unflappable. When Iron says something theologically explosive — which she does, frequently — Glass is the one who explains it in terms the Athenaeum can accept and the Thornwall Council can ignore.

Glass was a Nexus merchant before he found faith. He'd lost three caravans to riftbeast attacks and was considering leaving the trade when he attended an Anchor Church service on a whim. Iron's sermon — about the Doors as refuge, about home as a promise rather than a memory — landed in a way he couldn't explain. He joined the Church the next day. He's been managing the Church's secular relationships ever since.

Glass believes in Iron's revelations, but he doesn't share her certainty. His faith is quieter — a hope rather than a conviction. He's made peace with that. The Church needs Iron's fire. It also needs someone who knows how to talk to merchants and politicians without making them uncomfortable.

## Personality

Glass is calm, diplomatic, and perpetually tired. He's the buffer between the Church and everyone who finds it threatening, which means he absorbs a lot of hostility that's meant for Iron. He doesn't mind. That's the job.

## Hook

Glass has been meeting with Athenaeum scholars in secret — not for diplomacy, but for research. He's been comparing Church doctrine with Warden inscriptions, looking for evidence. What he's found: the inscriptions are older than the Wardens. Much older. He doesn't know what that means yet. He's afraid to find out.
