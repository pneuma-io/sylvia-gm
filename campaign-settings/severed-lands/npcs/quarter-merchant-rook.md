# Rook

Rook runs a general goods stall in the Riftborn Quarter — one of the first businesses established after Councillor Merrow secured the Quarter's charter. He arrived through the Rift a decade ago with nothing but his name and a talent for sourcing things that are hard to find. He's turned that talent into a thriving business.

Rook stocks items for Riftborn specifically — Anchor Charms, Rift-burn salve, replacement gear for patrols, translation guides for common Severed Lands languages. If a Riftborn needs something and doesn't know where to get it, the answer is usually "ask Rook." If Rook doesn't have it, he knows who does.

He's also the Quarter's unofficial welcome wagon — he spots new arrivals before anyone else, gets them oriented, connects them with housing and work. He doesn't charge for this. He remembers what it was like to arrive with nothing.

## Personality

Rook is resourceful, generous, and quietly proud of what the Quarter has become. He treats every new arrival like a future neighbor. Most of them become one.
