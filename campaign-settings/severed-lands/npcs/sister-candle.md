# Sister Candle

Sister Candle is a young Rememberer who took the Oath of Remembrance after her entire Riftborn community was wiped out by a riftbeast pack. She was the only survivor. Her ledger starts with their names — all thirty-seven of them, written in a hand that still shakes when she reaches the last few entries.

She's been tracking the pack ever since — through the Verdant Margin, following kill sites and Rift pool patterns. She's studied their behavior. She knows their territory. She knows which one leads, which ones flank, which one hesitates before striking. She's killed three of them so far. She says she'll stop when her ledger is full. She says it's almost full.

Candle is not suicidal, but she's not careful either. The oath doesn't require self-preservation. Her fellow Rememberers are worried about her. Brother Ash has been writing her letters. She hasn't answered the last three.

## Personality

Candle is intense, focused, and very young. She's been a Rememberer for less than two years, but the oath has consumed her completely. She doesn't talk about anything that isn't related to the hunt. She doesn't seem to have anything else.

## Hook

Candle found the pack — denning near an Anchor Clearing in the deep Margin. She's asking for help. She's not asking for mercy. The pack leader is an advanced riftwolf — larger than any she's seen, with phase-step capability and Rift-touched intelligence. She thinks it remembers her. She thinks it's been waiting.
