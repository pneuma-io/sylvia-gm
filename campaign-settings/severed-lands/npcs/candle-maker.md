# Wick

Wick makes candles — tallow, beeswax, the occasional Rift-touched wax that burns violet and smells faintly of ozone. She's the only chandler in Thornwall, and her candles light every council chamber, every temple, and most of the market stalls after dark. She arrived through the Rift fifteen years ago. She doesn't remember her home world. She remembers that she made candles there too.

Wick's shop is warm and smells of honey and smoke. She works in silence, dipping wicks into vats of wax with the rhythm of someone who's been doing it so long it's become meditation. She doesn't talk much. She doesn't need to. The candles speak for her — steady, reliable, lighting the dark.

Her Rift-touched candles are her specialty. They burn longer than normal wax, and some say they ward off minor Rift manifestations. Wick doesn't make that claim. She just makes the candles. What people believe about them is their business.
