# Lord Harren Dorn

Lord Dorn controls the forges, the foundries, and every major construction project in Thornwall. He doesn't care about Riftborn politics. He cares about who's buying steel.

Dorn is the council's most predictable vote — he sides with whoever offers the best economic outcome. When Voss pushes Riftborn restrictions, Dorn asks whether it'll affect his workforce. When Kael argues for trade expansion, Dorn asks about tariff implications. He's not amoral — he simply believes that a merchant's duty is to commerce, not to politics, and he's been a merchant his entire life.

His Forge Quarter employs more Riftborn than any other part of Thornwall. Not out of principle — out of practicality. Blacksmithing doesn't care where you came from, and good smiths are hard to find. Dorn's foremen don't ask about registration papers. They ask about hammer technique. This makes Dorn a quiet ally to Riftborn workers, even if he'd never use the word "ally."

Dorn is the council member most likely to break ranks on Riftborn issues. He respects Voss, but he respects profit more. If Riftborn trade becomes economically essential — or if Riftborn restrictions threaten his workforce — Dorn will vote accordingly. Everyone on the council knows this. Everyone plans around it.

## Hook

Dorn's foremen have been reporting theft — Warden-grade metals disappearing from the foundries, small quantities, impossible to trace. Dorn suspects House Merrick. The truth is worse: someone is forging Warden weapons in the Warrens, and they're getting their materials from Dorn's own supply chain.
