# Orin Thatch

Orin Thatch is the Route Warden of the Caravaneers' Guild — the elected representative of the Severed Lands' independent merchants. He's been running the southern route for twenty years, surviving three Jackal raids, two riftbeast attacks, and one marriage that ended amicably enough that they still trade together.

Thatch is practical to the point of bluntness. He treats every problem as a logistics issue — broken axle, Jackal ambush, political crisis, it's all the same framework: assess the damage, identify the fix, execute. He's suspicious of anyone who's never had to repair a wagon in Rift-touched weather. He's suspicious of people who have, too, but for different reasons.

The Guild is small but essential — they handle the routes the big houses don't want, keeping trade flowing to the settlements Thornwall forgets about. Thatch has kept the Guild alive through sheer stubborn competence. He's retiring at the end of the season. He's not sure the Guild will survive without him.

## Personality

Thatch is gruff, honest, and unexpectedly sentimental about the routes. He's named his wagons. He remembers every Guild member who's died on the routes — thirty-four names, recited from memory at the annual Guild gathering. He doesn't talk about them otherwise. The names are a duty, not a conversation topic.

## Hook

Thatch's retirement is triggering a Guild election — reformer versus traditionalist, expansion versus preservation. He's staying neutral publicly but privately favors the traditionalist. The reformer has been meeting with Lady Trask. If Trask absorbs the Guild into House operations, the independent merchants vanish. Thatch is looking for someone to investigate what the reformer promised Trask — and what Trask promised in return.
