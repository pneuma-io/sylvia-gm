# Tallow

Tallow is a young Veiled Path master — young by the Path's standards, meaning she's been practicing for fifteen years and took her first student two years ago. She's been unusually active recently: training students faster than most masters, visiting settlements, speaking to Returner patrols about something she's sensed in the Veil.

She won't say what she's sensed. She says she's not sure yet. But she's been agitated — the Veil has been "louder," she says. More voices. Closer to the surface. Phase-stepping has become harder — the Veil resists her in ways it never has before. Something is wrong on the other side. She's trying to find out what.

Tallow has asked the Returners for an escort into the Deep Rift — the massive Rift pool at the Cairnfields' center. She says something is waking up down there. She says it's been calling to her. She's not sure if she's strong enough to resist the call. She's going anyway.

## Personality

Tallow is intense, articulate, and frightened in a way she's trying very hard to hide. The Veil is her life's work — her art, her discipline, her way of understanding the world. If the Veil is changing, everything she's built her identity around is changing with it. She needs answers. She's afraid of what the answers might be.
