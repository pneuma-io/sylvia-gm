# Lady Maren Trask

Lady Trask is the Thornwall Council's youngest member and its most ambitious. Her house controls shipping and cartage — the caravan routes, the warehousing, the logistics that keep Thornwall's economy moving. She inherited the seat from her father, who inherited it from his mother, who was elevated to the council two generations ago after the previous shipping house collapsed.

Trask carries the insecurity of the recently promoted. The other houses have held their seats for centuries — Trask has held hers for two generations. She's determined to prove that her family belongs at the table, and she's willing to break convention to do it. She's been quietly meeting with Nexus guild representatives about establishing a direct trade route that bypasses Thornwall's tariffs entirely. She's been courting the Caravaneers' Guild as potential partners. She's been positioning herself as the council's future — the voice of expansion, of new markets, of a Thornwall that doesn't just survive but grows.

She's also the council's most consistent Riftborn ally — not out of principle, but because Riftborn represent an untapped market. The Warrens has its own economy. The Riftborn Quarter in Nexus is thriving. Trask sees money where Voss sees threat, and Trask trusts money.

## Personality

Trask is energetic, direct, and terrible at hiding her ambitions. She's the only council member who seems to enjoy council sessions — the debate, the maneuvering, the slow grind of politics. She treats the other houses as competitors in a game she's determined to win. Voss finds her exhausting. Kael finds her useful. Merrick finds her entertaining.

## Hook

Trask's Nexus negotiations have been discovered — not by the council, but by the Jackals. The Tollmen are demanding a cut of the new route's revenue in exchange for not informing Lord Voss. Trask needs the situation resolved quietly. She's willing to pay — in gold, in trade concessions, in political favors — to anyone who can make the Tollmen go away.
