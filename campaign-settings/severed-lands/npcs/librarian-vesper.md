# Vesper

Vesper is the Athenaeum's head librarian — the keeper of the Severed Lands' largest collection of pre-Rift and Warden texts. She's been at the Athenaeum for thirty years, and she's read more of the collection than anyone alive. She knows which shelves hold the dangerous material. She knows which scholars are asking the wrong questions. She knows more about Warden inscriptions than the School of Warden Studies, but she'd never say so out loud.

Vesper treats knowledge as a responsibility rather than a resource. She'll help anyone who asks a genuine question. She'll quietly redirect anyone who asks the wrong one — the kind of question that suggests they're about to do something dangerous with the answer. She's very good at knowing the difference.

She's also the Athenaeum's unofficial archivist of forbidden research. When a scholar is expelled, their notes are supposed to be confiscated and sealed. Vesper keeps copies. She doesn't share them. She's waiting for the day when the knowledge is needed more than the prohibition matters.

## Personality

Vesper is quiet, precise, and utterly devoted to the collection. She treats books like living things. She treats dangerous books like dangerous animals — respect them, don't provoke them, know when to back away.
