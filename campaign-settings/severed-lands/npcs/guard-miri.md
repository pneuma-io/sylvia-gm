# Miri

Miri is a gate guard — one of the men and women who check everyone entering Thornwall. She's been on gate duty for three years. She's seen every kind of arrival: merchants with caravans, Returners with supply requests, Riftborn with hope and fear in equal measure.

Miri processes Riftborn registrations by the book — name, origin if known, purpose of visit. She asks the required questions. She doesn't ask the ones the book doesn't require. A Riftborn who stammers on the "origin" question gets a moment to collect themselves. A Riftborn who looks like they haven't eaten gets directions to the Warrens. Miri doesn't break policy. She just administers it with the most humanity the policy allows.

Her brother was Riftborn — arrived ten years ago, no memories, died in a Hollows scavenging accident before Miri ever got to know him. She wears his ring on a chain under her uniform. She doesn't talk about it.

## Personality

Quiet, dutiful, and carrying a private grief. Miri does her job. She doesn't make waves. She just makes sure the Gate is slightly less terrifying than it could be.
