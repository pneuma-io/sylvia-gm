# Kestrel

Kestrel runs the Crossroads auction — the weekly event where the most valuable salvage, artifacts, and curiosities find their buyers. She's been an auctioneer for twelve years. Her voice carries across the entire market. Her memory for bids, buyers, and debts is flawless. She's never had to call a bid twice.

The auction attracts everyone — Athenaeum scholars, Returner representatives, House Kael's intermediaries, the occasional Hollows crew captain who wants to see what their competition is selling. Kestrel treats them all the same: the bid is the bid, the buyer is the buyer, and the auction house takes ten percent.

She knows which items are stolen, which are fake, and which are dangerous. She doesn't announce any of this. She just sets the starting price accordingly. A fake goes low. A dangerous item goes high — if someone's going to take a risk, they're going to pay for it.

## Personality

Kestrel is sharp, theatrical, and absolutely in control of her auction floor. She treats every sale like a performance. The performance is always good. The buyers keep coming back.
