# Briar

Briar forages the edge of the Verdant Margin — close enough to find Rift-touched herbs, far enough from the deep growth to make it back by nightfall. She supplies Saffron's clinic, the Athenaeum's medical school, and the occasional alchemist who needs something fresh rather than dried. She's been doing this for eight years. She's only been chased by riftbeasts twice. She considers this a good record.

Briar knows the Margin's edge better than anyone — which plants are safe, which are Rift-touched, which will cure a fever and which will give you one. Her knowledge is self-taught, earned through trial and error and one very bad week involving a misidentified mushroom. She's happy to share what she knows. She's less happy to go deeper than the edge. The deep Margin is the Scaled's territory. Briar respects boundaries.

## Personality

Briar is practical, independent, and completely comfortable in the Margin's borderlands. She treats foraging like a conversation with the forest — the Margin tells you what's available if you listen. Most people don't listen. Briar does.
