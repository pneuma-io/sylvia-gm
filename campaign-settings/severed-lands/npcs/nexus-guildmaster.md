# Guildmaster Vance

Guildmaster Vance represents the Nexus trade guilds — the loose coalition of merchant interests that function as the city's economic government. He's not an elected official. He's not a council member. He's the person the guilds send when they need something from someone who needs something from the guilds. Everyone in Nexus owes Vance a favor. Vance owes favors to no one. That's the math.

Vance has been quietly meeting with Lady Trask about the proposed direct trade route between Nexus and the smaller settlements. The route would bypass Thornwall's tariffs entirely, enriching Nexus at Thornwall's expense. Vance sees this as good business. Trask sees it as political survival. Neither of them is wrong.

Vance is not a villain. He's not a hero. He's a merchant who's very good at his job, and his job is making Nexus richer. Everything else is secondary.

## Personality

Vance is smooth, calculating, and genuinely believes that trade solves more problems than politics. He's not wrong. He's also not entirely right. The trade route would destabilize Thornwall's economy. Vance has factored this into his calculations. It's not a bug. It's a feature.
