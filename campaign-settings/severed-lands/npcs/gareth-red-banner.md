# Gareth of the Red Banner

Gareth was a Thornwall guard captain for twelve years before he was discharged for "excessive ruthlessness" in a bandit crackdown. The council's official finding: he executed prisoners. Gareth's version: he did what needed to be done, and the council was too soft to admit it. He left Thornwall, gathered a band of like-minded outcasts, and applied his military training to the other side of the law.

The Red Banner is the largest Jackal band — thirty riders, well-armed, operating on the eastern route. Gareth runs it like a military unit: disciplined, efficient, and fanatical about the Jackal code. He's executed his own raiders for killing a merchant. The code exists for a reason — dead merchants stop running caravans — and Gareth enforces it with the same ruthlessness that got him discharged.

## Personality

Gareth is a man who believes the world is harsh and that pretending otherwise gets people killed. He's not sadistic — he's pragmatic to the point of brutality. He respects courage and competence. He despises weakness and hypocrisy. The Thornwall Council, in his view, is full of both.

He's a villain in the council's eyes and a folk hero in the Warrens — the guard captain who turned on his masters. Gareth doesn't care about either reputation. He cares about running his band, following the code, and making enough coin to keep his people fed through the winter.

## Hook

Gareth has called a moot — a gathering of all Jackal bands. He wants to unite them into a single organization under his command. Half the bands support him. Half would rather burn the network down than answer to a king. The moot is in three weeks. Whatever happens, the trade routes are about to change.
