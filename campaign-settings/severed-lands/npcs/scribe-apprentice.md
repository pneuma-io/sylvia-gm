# Quill

Quill is an Athenaeum apprentice — a second-generation Riftborn whose parents arrived before she was born. She's never been through the Rift. She's never known any world but the Severed Lands. She's determined to understand the one she was born into.

Quill works in the Athenaeum's scriptorium, copying deteriorating texts before they become unreadable. Her handwriting is precise, her attention obsessive, and her tendency to read the texts she's supposed to be copying has gotten her in trouble more than once. She knows things she shouldn't — fragments of Warden inscriptions, patterns in the Door records that no one else has noticed. She's too junior to bring her findings to anyone important. She's too smart to ignore them.

## Personality

Earnest, curious, and dangerously well-informed. Quill represents the next generation — Riftborn who aren't waiting for a Door to open, who are building their lives here, who want to understand the world they inherited. She's going to be important someday. She doesn't know it yet.
