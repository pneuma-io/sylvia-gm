# Iro

Iro is a Rift Seeker — a descendant of the Rift Raiders who rejected the martial code and sought inner stillness instead. He's been wandering the Cairnfields for fifteen years, mastering the discipline that his ancestors considered cowardice. He's not a coward. He's just decided that the Eternal Battle his people believe in isn't a war. It's the struggle to stay calm in a world that wants you to fight.

Iro occasionally serves as a translator between the Raiders and outsiders — one of the few people who speaks both Rift-tongue and Common, and the only one the Raiders will tolerate. He doesn't enjoy it. Translating for Korr means listening to Korr's plans and being unable to warn anyone. Translating for Vex means standing in silence for hours while she writes orders that will get people killed. The Seeker's discipline is non-interference. The discipline is very hard to maintain.

## Personality

Iro is calm, melancholy, and carrying the weight of his people's choices. He loves the Raiders. They're his family, his heritage, his blood. He also believes they're wrong — about the Eternal Battle, about violence as purpose, about everything. He can't change them. He can barely stand to watch. But he stays, because someone has to speak for them, and he's the only one who can.
