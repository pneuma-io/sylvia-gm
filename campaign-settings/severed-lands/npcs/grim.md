# Grim

Grim controls the tunnels — the warren-passages that connect the Thornwall Warrens to the market district above, the city's underbelly, and (if the rumors are true) the Undercroft beneath Nexus. He doesn't run a gang. He runs a toll system. Pay, and you pass. Don't pay, and you don't get to use his tunnels twice.

Grim is neutral in Warrens Court politics. His tunnels are too valuable for anyone to challenge him — every faction in the Warrens depends on his routes. The Lace Brothers move contraband through his passages. Madame Thistle's protection runners use them to avoid guard patrols. Auntie Salt's informants use them to move unseen. Grim charges everyone the same rate and favors no one.

He's also the Warrens' unofficial cartographer — he knows the tunnels better than anyone, including the ones that predate the Warrens, the ones that were here before Thornwall was built. He doesn't share his maps. He doesn't need to. If you want to go somewhere in the Warrens, you go through Grim, or you don't go.

## Personality

Grim is quiet, professional, and completely uninterested in anything that doesn't involve his tunnels. He treats passage rights like a business — fair rates, consistent enforcement, no exceptions. He's not friendly. He's not hostile. He's infrastructure.
