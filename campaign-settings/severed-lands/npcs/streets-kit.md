# Kit

Kit is twelve years old and knows the Warrens better than anyone except Grim. They're second-generation Riftborn — their parents arrived through the Rift before Kit was born. Kit has never known any world but the Warrens. They've never needed to.

Kit works as an informant for Auntie Salt — running messages, watching doors, reporting who comes and goes. They're paid in food, coin, and the protection that comes from being useful to someone powerful. Kit doesn't think of it as spying. It's just what you do to survive in the Warrens.

Kit knows every Warrens resident by sight. They know who's new, who's sick, who's been asking questions about the Returners, who's been asking questions about the Radicals. They don't understand the politics. They understand that information has value, and they're very good at collecting it.

## Personality

Smart, watchful, and old before their time. Kit doesn't act like a child because the Warrens doesn't let children act like children. They're friendly in a guarded way — they'll talk to anyone, but they're always evaluating. Auntie Salt taught them that.
