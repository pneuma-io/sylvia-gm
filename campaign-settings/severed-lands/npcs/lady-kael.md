# Lady Sera Kael

Lady Kael is the Thornwall Council's swing vote — and its most carefully guarded secret. She controls the textile and luxury goods trade, presenting herself as a pragmatist who votes with whichever side serves Kael interests. Her public persona is unremarkable: a competent merchant, a reliable council member, a woman who keeps her own counsel.

Her private persona is considerably more interesting. Lady Kael has been quietly funding Anchor Door research for years — funnelling gold through a shell trading company to the Returners, the Athenaeum, and independent scholars. She's the reason the Returners have functioning equipment. She's the reason the Athenaeum's Warden Studies school hasn't collapsed from lack of funding. She's the reason three different scavenger expeditions found Door fragments that "happened" to be exactly where her researchers predicted.

Why? Kael's eldest daughter is Riftborn. Arrived seven years ago with no memory, no home world, no explanation. Kael pulled every string to keep it quiet — registered her as a distant relative, hid her in the family estate, lied to the council for seven years. Her daughter wants to go home. Kael wants to give her that chance. Everything else — the research funding, the political maneuvering, the years of deception — serves that single goal.

## Personality

Kael is warm, measured, and impossible to read. She's spent seven years lying to the most powerful people in the Severed Lands, and she's gotten very good at it. She treats every conversation as a negotiation where the other party doesn't know the stakes.

She genuinely likes people — she's not cold or calculating in the way Lord Merrick is. She just has a priority that outweighs everything else, and she's made peace with what that costs. If the council discovered her secret, she'd be disgraced. If her daughter were exposed, she'd be in danger. Kael accepts both risks. The Door research is worth it. Her daughter is worth it.

## Role in a Campaign

**As ally:** Kael is the inside source on the Thornwall Council. She provides funding, intelligence, and political cover for Riftborn-aligned operations. She'll never admit to any of it publicly. A campaign that earns her trust gains access to resources no other Thornwall faction can match.

**As subject of exposure:** Kael's secret is a ticking bomb. If Lord Voss or Lord Merrick discovered it, the council would fracture. A campaign might protect her, expose her, or use the information as leverage. Kael would understand any of those choices. She might even help with the last one, if the exchange served her daughter's interests.

## Hook

Kael's daughter has started remembering. Fragments — a name, a color, a feeling of water. She wants to find her home world. Kael needs information that only the Returners have, and the Returners need funding that only Kael can provide. The arrangement is delicate. Something is about to break it.
