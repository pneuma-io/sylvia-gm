# Joss

Joss is a prospector — one of the independent scavengers who work the Cairnfields without crew affiliation, guild membership, or any backup beyond their own wits. They've been doing this for twelve years. They're still alive. That's statistically remarkable.

Joss specializes in Warden fragments — the smaller pieces that the crews overlook, the shards embedded in pillar walls, the fragments that surface after Rift surges. They're not looking for the big score. They're looking for consistent finds, enough to keep them supplied and moving. They know the Cairnfields' lesser-known routes — the pillar paths the Raiders don't patrol, the pools the riftbeasts have abandoned.

Joss will guide anyone for a price. They'll also leave anyone behind if the situation gets too dangerous. They're upfront about this. "I'm a guide, not a hero" is their standard contract opener. Clients appreciate the honesty. Some of them still hire Joss. Some of them don't come back.

## Personality

Joss is pragmatic, self-interested, and refreshingly honest about both. They don't pretend to be brave. They don't pretend to be noble. They're a prospector trying to make a living in the most dangerous place in the Severed Lands. Everything else is marketing.
