# Soot

Soot is Thornwall's ratcatcher — a profession that sounds humble but involves more knowledge of the city's infrastructure than anyone except Grim. He knows every drain, every basement access, every forgotten passage between the market and the Warrens. He's been doing this job for fifteen years. He's very good at it. The rats are not.

Soot is also the Warrens' unofficial supply line. He carries messages, medicine, and the occasional small package between the surface and the underground. The guards don't search the ratcatcher. No one wants to search the ratcatcher. Soot uses this to his advantage.

He doesn't charge for the deliveries. He just does them. Someone helped him once — a Riftborn in the Warrens, years ago, when he was sick and couldn't work. He's been paying it forward ever since. The Warrens knows him by smell — the rats follow him, and the residents know to step aside.

## Personality

Soot is quiet, grimy, and more connected than anyone realizes. He treats his work as a calling rather than a job. The rats are part of the city. The Warrens is part of the city. Soot takes care of both.

A compact broad mature Human man with medium-deep brown skin, close dark eyes, a broad nose, cropped tight black curls and rough short stubble. Faded taupe rolled-sleeve shirt, charcoal-grey sleeveless work coat, olive trousers, mustard neck cloth and scuffed closed boots. A closed wicker trap is carried in the left hand; a small unmarked supply pouch rests at the right hip. Restrained soot smudges mark the hands and cuffs.
