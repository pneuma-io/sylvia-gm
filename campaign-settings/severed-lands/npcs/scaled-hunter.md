# Reef

Reef is a Verdant Scaled hunter — young by Scaled standards, which means she's been hunting the Margin for twenty years. She's one of the few Scaled who regularly interacts with outsiders, serving as a guide for Returner patrols and the occasional Guild caravan that needs to cross Margin territory.

Reef speaks Common with an accent that makes everything sound like a question. She finds outsiders confusing — their urgency, their impatience, their need to be somewhere by a specific time. The Margin doesn't care about schedules. Reef has learned to translate between Margin-time and outsider-time, but it's clearly an effort.

She guides because Tide-Mother asked her to — the Elders decided that the Scaled needed a bridge to the outside world, and Reef drew the short straw. She doesn't resent it. She's just not sure why it matters. The Margin provides everything the Scaled need. Everything beyond the Margin is someone else's problem.

## Personality

Reef is patient, observant, and bemused by outsiders. She treats escort missions like babysitting — keep the outsiders alive, keep them on the clearings, don't let them touch anything that glows. She's very good at it. She'd rather be hunting.
