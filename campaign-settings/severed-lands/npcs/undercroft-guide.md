# Vale

Vale guides people through the Undercroft — the network of pre-Rift tunnels and catacombs beneath Nexus. They know the routes that connect to the Hollows (officially denied, universally known), the passages the Athenaeum uses for secure storage (officially denied, functionally mapped), and the places where the Undercroft gets... strange.

Vale doesn't ask why you need to go somewhere. They just name a price. The price varies — coin for standard routes, favors for dangerous ones, and "you don't want to know" for the routes that go deep enough to touch the Hollows. No one's ever taken that last option. Vale's expression when they offer it suggests that's wise.

They've been guiding for eight years. They've lost three clients — one to a cave-in, one to something in the dark, one who simply walked away from the group and never came back. Vale still thinks about all three. They don't talk about it.

## Personality

Vale is professional, guarded, and carrying the weight of people they couldn't save. They treat guiding as a responsibility rather than a job. They'll refuse a route if they think the client isn't ready. They've never explained what "ready" means.
