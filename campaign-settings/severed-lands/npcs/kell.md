# Kell

Kell founded Kell's Iron twenty years ago after leaving the Returners. She doesn't talk about why she left. The few people who've asked got a look that made them stop asking. Whatever happened between Kell and V, it's in the past, and Kell keeps it there.

Kell's Iron is the oldest and most respected Hollows Crew — professional scavengers who work the northern Hollows, the most stable sector. They're methodical, disciplined, and famously fair in their dealings with other crews. If two crews have a territory dispute, they bring it to Kell. If someone breaks the crews' code, Kell's the one who decides consequences. She didn't ask for this role. The crews gave it to her because no one else could hold it.

Kell is in her fifties — too old to be running the Hollows daily, not old enough to retire. She's been training a successor for three years. The successor isn't ready. Kell isn't sure anyone can be ready for what the Hollows takes from you.

## Personality

Kell is blunt, fair, and carrying something heavy. She treats scavenging like a profession rather than an adventure — the crews are workers, not thrill-seekers, and she has no patience for anyone who treats the Hollows like a game. She's lost too many people to the breathing. She'll lose more before she's done.

## Hook

Kell's Iron found a sealed Warden chamber beneath the northern sector — sigils active, door intact. They haven't opened it. They're looking for buyers willing to fund the expedition. The Returners, the Athenaeum, and House Kael are all bidding. Kell is taking her time. She's been in the Hollows long enough to know that sealed chambers are sealed for a reason.
