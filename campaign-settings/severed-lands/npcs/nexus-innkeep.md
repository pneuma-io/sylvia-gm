# Palla

Palla runs The Anchor's Rest — the inn nearest the Athenaeum, where visiting scholars argue about research over bad wine and worse breakfast. She's been running the inn for fifteen years. She's heard every academic debate the Severed Lands has to offer. She's forgotten more about Warden studies than most first-year scholars will ever learn, purely through osmosis.

Palla treats her guests like temporary family. She remembers names, dietary restrictions, and which scholars are feuding with which other scholars (important for seating arrangements). She also keeps a locked cabinet of "things guests left behind" — a collection that includes three Warden fragments, a half-translated inscription, and a journal that the Athenaeum would very much like returned. She's waiting for the right moment to bring it up.

## Personality

Palla is warm, shrewd, and absolutely in charge of her establishment. She treats scholars with the same maternal exasperation she'd use on unruly children. The scholars love her. The scholars fear her. The scholars leave generous tips.

## Appearance and artwork

A sturdy mature human default with rosy fair freckled skin, round cheeks, blue eyes and short deep-red curls. Sage, cream, ivory apron and plum practical innkeeping layers with flat boots accompany a warm shrewd expression. Both hands are empty. Her private cabinet remains hidden; the inn's name assigns no religious allegiance or emblem.
