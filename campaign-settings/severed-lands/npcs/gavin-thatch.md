# Gavin Thatch

Gavin Thatch was a Returner for ten years before he decided the Door wasn't worth waiting for. He didn't leave in anger — he just stopped believing that the mission justified putting everything else on hold. He moved to the Warrens and started building. Housing. Infrastructure. The basic systems the Warrens needs to become a community instead of a camp.

Gavin is a builder in the literal sense — he's learned carpentry, masonry, engineering from whoever would teach him. His current project is a water distribution system for the northern quarter, using salvaged Warden piping and a lot of hope. He's been at it for six months. It almost works.

The Warrens Court tolerates him because his work makes their lives easier — running water means fewer disputes at the communal wells. The council ignores him because he's in the Warrens. Gavin prefers it that way. He's not trying to be political. He's trying to prove that Riftborn can build something permanent, with or without the Door.

## Personality

Gavin is practical, optimistic, and completely uninterested in the Returners' debates. He respects V — everyone respects V — but he's made his choice. The Severed Lands is home. Time to act like it. He's not a philosopher. He's a builder with a lot of work to do and not enough hands to do it.
