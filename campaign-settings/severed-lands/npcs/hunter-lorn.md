# Lorn

Lorn hunts riftbeasts for a living. Not for sport, not for vengeance — for money. Every major settlement posts bounties on dangerous riftbeasts, and Lorn collects. They've been hunting for ten years. They've killed more riftbeasts than most patrols. They've got the scars to prove it and the coin to show for it.

Lorn works alone. They've tried partners. The partners died. Lorn doesn't talk about that.

Their knowledge of riftbeast behavior is encyclopedic — they know pack patterns, hunting cycles, pool preferences, the subtle signs that an area is claimed territory. They share this knowledge with Returner patrols when they cross paths. They don't charge for the information. They consider it professional courtesy.

## Personality

Lorn is quiet, solitary, and carrying the weight of every partner they've lost. They treat hunting as a profession rather than a calling — it's dangerous work, but it's honest work, and someone has to do it. They're very good at it. They wish they didn't have to be.
