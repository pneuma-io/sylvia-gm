# Councillor Merrow

Councillor Merrow is the Stayers' unofficial leader — a Riftborn woman who arrived twenty years ago with no memories and built a trading business from nothing. She now sits on the Nexus council as the Riftborn Quarter's elected representative, the first Riftborn to hold a formal political office anywhere in the Severed Lands.

Merrow is pragmatic to her core. She doesn't oppose the Returners' mission — she just believes Riftborn need to build lives whether the Door opens or not. She's spent two decades learning how to make settled politicians take Riftborn concerns seriously: frame arguments in terms of economic benefit, build coalitions across faction lines, never make a demand you can't enforce. She's very good at it.

The Riftborn Quarter exists because Merrow made it exist — negotiating with the Nexus guilds, securing construction rights, building the political scaffolding that turned a camp of displaced people into a permanent community. She's not done. Thornwall is next.

## Personality

Merrow is calm, persistent, and deeply tired. She's been fighting for Riftborn recognition for twenty years, and the fight hasn't gotten easier — she's just gotten better at it. She treats setbacks as data points and victories as leverage. She never raises her voice. She never stops pushing.

## Hook

The Thornwall Council is debating a Riftborn Quarter proposal modeled on Nexus. Merrow is lobbying every council member she can reach. Lord Voss is opposed. Lady Trask is supportive. The vote is in two weeks. Merrow needs allies who can reach the council members she can't — Dorn's economic interests, Kael's secret sympathies, Merrick's information network. She's asking for help.
