# Kale

Kale sells fish. He's been selling fish since he was old enough to hold a scaling knife. His father sold fish before him. His family's stall has been in the Thornwall market for four generations. The fish come from the coastal settlements, brought in by caravan every three days. Kale doesn't ask where the caravans go or what they pass through. He just sells the fish.

Kale's stall is where Thornwall gossips. Everyone buys fish eventually. Everyone talks while they're waiting. Kale hears everything — council politics, Warrens rumors, Returner movements, Jackal sightings — and repeats none of it. He's not keeping secrets. He's just not interested. The fish are enough.
