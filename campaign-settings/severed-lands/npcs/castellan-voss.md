# Castellan Voss

Castellan Voss is the Knight-Commander of the Warden Lodge — no relation to Thornwall's House Voss, a fact he mentions frequently and with visible irritation. He's led the Lodge for fifteen years, training knights in Warden combat techniques and overseeing the Echo Dice bonding rituals that are the Lodge's signature discipline.

Voss is a warrior-historian. He believes that understanding the Wardens means practicing what they practiced — not just studying their inscriptions, but moving like they moved, fighting like they fought, thinking like they thought. His knights learn Warden combat forms alongside Warden history. The two are inseparable. You can't understand a people's fighting style without understanding why they fought.

The Lodge's artifact vault contains the largest collection of bonded Warden weapons in the Severed Lands. Voss has bonded with three of them — a sword, a shield, and a gauntlet that he never removes. He says the Wardens speak through their artifacts, if you listen. He says the sword is the most talkative.

## Personality

Voss is stern, direct, and unexpectedly warm with his knights. He treats the Lodge as family — dysfunctional, argumentative, fiercely loyal family. He knows every knight's name, every squire's background, every smith's specialty. He attends every bonding ritual personally. He writes the condolence letters when a knight falls.

He's also dying. A Rift-touched lung condition, contracted during an expedition into the deep Cairnfields. His healers give him a year, maybe two. He hasn't told the Lodge. He's trying to prepare them for succession without them realizing he's preparing them for succession.

## Hook

Voss's successor must be elected by the sworn knights. Two candidates have emerged: a traditionalist who wants to preserve the Lodge's methods, and a reformer who wants to train more knights faster. The vote is in three months. Voss is trying to stay alive long enough to see it through — and trying not to let either candidate realize how little time he has left.
