# Auntie Salt

Auntie Salt runs the Warrens' information network — a web of barkeeps, merchants, street kids, and domestic servants who feed her everything they see and hear. She's the Warrens Court's intelligence arm, but she's not loyal to the Court. She's loyal to the information trade, and she sells to anyone who can pay.

Salt has no territory and no gang. Her currency is knowledge, and she's the richest person in the Warrens — not in gold, but in leverage. She knows about Kael's daughter. She knows about Trask's Nexus negotiations. She knows which Warrens resident has been cultivating a Rift pool in their basement. She's known that last one for weeks. She hasn't told anyone yet. She's waiting for the right buyer.

Salt's network extends beyond the Warrens — she has informants in Thornwall proper, in the Hollows Crews, and (rumor has it) in the Athenaeum. The extent of her reach is unknown. The accuracy of her information is frighteningly consistent.

## Personality

Salt is cheerful, grandmotherly, and utterly amoral about the information trade. She treats secrets like produce — some are fresh, some are past their sell-by date, all of them have a price. She genuinely likes people. She just likes knowing things about them more. She keeps a pot of tea going at all hours. Visitors are always offered a cup. No one knows if the tea is safe. Everyone drinks it anyway.
