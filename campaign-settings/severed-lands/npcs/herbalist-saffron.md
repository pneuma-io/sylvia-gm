# Saffron

Saffron runs a herbalist stall in the Thornwall market by day and a free clinic in the Warrens by night. She's settled — born in Thornwall, never been through the Rift — but she's spent enough time in the Warrens to know that the council's policies are killing people. She doesn't make a political statement about it. She just keeps showing up with medicine.

Her knowledge of Rift-touched conditions is largely self-taught — the Athenaeum's medical texts don't cover Warrens-level care, and Saffron has had to figure out on her own what helps and what makes things worse. She's made mistakes. She's learned from them. She's saved more lives than Thornwall's official healers.

## Personality

Saffron is tired, compassionate, and quietly furious at the system she works within. She doesn't waste energy on anger. She uses it on patients. Her hands are steady. Her diagnostic eye is sharp. Her supply of Rift-burn salve is always running low.
