# Tide-Mother

Tide-Mother is the oldest Verdant Scaled alive — a reptilian Riftborn who's been hunting the Verdant Margin for over sixty years. Her scales are scarred with Rift-burns that glow faintly in the dark, each one a record of a riftbeast kill. She's killed three Elder Riftbeasts. The youngest Scaled hunters tell stories about her. The stories don't do her justice.

She leads the Scaled not by authority but by consensus. When the Scale-Elders meet at the Heartwood, Tide-Mother speaks rarely. When she does speak, the other Elders stop talking. Her voice is quiet — a rasp, damaged by decades of Rift exposure — but it carries. The Scaled have learned that when Tide-Mother has something to say, it's because she's already considered every angle and reached the only conclusion worth reaching.

## Personality

Tide-Mother is patient in a way that unsettles non-Scaled. She measures time in seasons, not days. A conversation that takes an hour doesn't bother her — she's been in the Margin for sixty years. An hour is nothing. She treats outsiders with caution but not hostility. The Scaled have no interest in the politics of the settled lands, but they're not isolationists. They watch. They wait. They hunt.

She carries a Rift-touched spear that's absorbed the energy of every riftbeast it's killed. The spear hums when riftbeasts are near. It sings when Elder Riftbeasts are near. Tide-Mother holds it loosely, like an old friend.

## Hook

Tide-Mother has requested a meeting with the Returners — something she hasn't done in twenty years. The Margin is changing. The growth is accelerating. Something is feeding the Rift pools, and she doesn't know what. She's willing to grant outsiders access to the deep canopy — something the Scaled have never done — in exchange for answers.
