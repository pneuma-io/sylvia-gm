# Iron Mother Hest

Iron Mother Hest is the only person in the Severed Lands who can repair a damaged Warden artifact. She's also the only person who understands how the Echo Dice bonding system actually works, on a metallurgical level. The Lodge's sworn knights bond with artifacts through ritual and meditation. Hest bonds with them through metallurgy and sixty years of smithing experience. She doesn't need the ritual. She just looks at the metal and knows.

Hest has been with the Lodge longer than any current knight — including Castellan Voss. She was here when the previous Knight-Commander fell. She'll be here when Voss is gone. The Lodge changes around her. Hest stays the same: in the forge, at the anvil, singing Warden-forging songs in a language she's mostly reconstructed from inscriptions on broken blades.

She's gruff, unsentimental, and treats the Lodge's knights like particularly promising apprentices. They call her Iron Mother — a title she earned by being the only person in the Lodge who's never been challenged to a duel. No one wants to fight the woman who maintains their weapons.

## Personality

Hest is direct, profane, and deeply invested in the Lodge's future. She's been forging Warden-replica weapons for forty years. She's still not satisfied with the results — the Wardens' metallurgy was better, and it bothers her that she can't match it. She's close, she says. She's been close for thirty years.
