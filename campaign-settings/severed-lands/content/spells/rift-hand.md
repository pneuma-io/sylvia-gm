---
name: "Rift Hand"
level: 5
school: evocation
casting_time: 1 action
range: 120 feet
components:
- V
- S
- M
duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
classes:
- wizard
concentration: true
material: an eggshell and a snakeskin glove
at_higher_levels: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th level or higher, the clenched fist damage increases by
  2d8 and the grasping hand's damage increases by 2d6 for each slot level above 5th.
---

You create a Large hand of shimmering, translucent force in an unoccupied space that you can see within range. The hand lasts for the spell's duration, and it moves at your command, mimicking the movements of your own hand. When you cast the spell, and as a bonus action on subsequent turns, you can move the hand up to 60 feet and then cause one of the following effects: Clenched Fist (4d8 force), Forceful Hand (push 5 + STR mod × 5 feet), Grasping Hand (grapple), or Interposing Hand (cover against one creature).

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th level or higher, the clenched fist damage increases by 2d8 and the grasping hand's damage increases by 2d6 for each slot level above 5th.
