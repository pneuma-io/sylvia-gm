A burrowing insectoid predator that came through the Rift from an alien world, now established in the Cairnfields. Its carapace has absorbed Rift minerals, giving it a violet sheen, and its acid spray is laced with traces of Veil energy. It tunnels through stone and soil alike, leaving behind unstable ground and occasional Rift-venting.
## Actions
**Bite.** +5 to hit, 10 (2d6 + 3) slashing + 3 (1d6) acid. If Large or smaller, grappled (escape DC 13). Grappled target takes 3 (1d6) acid at start of each of the rift ankheg's turns.
**Acid Spray.** 30-foot line, DC 13 DEX save or 10 (3d6) acid (half on success). Recharges 6.

## Appearance and artwork

A large burrowing insectoid with six strong articulated legs in three pairs, a long segmented low body, a heavy wedge-shaped insect head and two massive opposing mandibles. Dark brown mineralized chitin has an iridescent violet sheen. Compact black compound eyes, short paired antennae and broad digging forelegs. The rear abdomen tapers naturally. No wings, humanoid torso, extra legs, acid spray or prey.
