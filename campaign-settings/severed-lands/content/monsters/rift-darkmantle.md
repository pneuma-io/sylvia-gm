A Rift-touched cave-dweller that clings to ceilings in the tunnels beneath the Cairnfields, its body studded with faintly glowing Rift-nodules. When it drops onto prey, it envelopes them in a sphere of absolute darkness — not mere magical shadow but a pocket of Veil-stuff through which even darkvision cannot penetrate.
## Traits
**Echolocation.** No blindsight while deafened.
**False Appearance.** While motionless and attached to a ceiling, indistinguishable from a stalactite.

## Actions
**Crush.** +5 to hit, 6 (1d6 + 3) bludgeoning. If Small or smaller, grappled (escape DC 13) and the rift darkmantle's Darkness Aura activates.
**Darkness Aura.** 15-foot-radius sphere of magical darkness emanates from the rift darkmantle. Lasts until the rift darkmantle is killed or dismisses it as a bonus action.

## Appearance and artwork

A compact cave creature with a tapered grey-brown rocklike mantle and a webbed skirt of eight short muscular tentacles. Two small dark eyes lie low under the mantle rim. Layered folded hide gives a stalactite-like silhouette; small faint violet Rift nodules have nested mineral interiors. A charcoal-plum membrane joins the tentacle roots. The selected eight-tentacle anatomy remains visible in its reference views; its activated darkness sphere is omitted from identity art.
