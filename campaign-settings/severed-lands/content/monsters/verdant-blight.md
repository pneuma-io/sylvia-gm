A patch of the Verdant Margin that has grown malevolent — not just aggressive but actively parasitic. It feeds on ambient Rift energy and living creatures alike, extending thorned creepers that drain vitality from anything they touch. Even other Verdant creatures give it a wide berth.
## Actions
**Claws.** +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 6 (2d4 + 1) piercing.
**Needles.** Ranged Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, range 30/60 ft., one target. 6 (2d4 + 1) piercing.

## Appearance and artwork

A faceless mobile thorn-creeper default with one knotted trunk, two bundled vine arms ending in three hooked thorn-prongs apiece and two splayed root-leg bundles. A folded deep-green bract crown surrounds a closed woody bud. Moss-brown bark, olive leaves, maroon pods and pale thorn tips frame inward-recurring violet-emerald growth arches.
