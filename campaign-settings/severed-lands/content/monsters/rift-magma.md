Rift energy bonded with molten stone in the volcanic vents beneath the Severed Lands — the most destructive of the elemental forms. It melts through stone fortifications and leaves behind cooling flows of black, glassy Rift-glass that still carry a faint psychic echo of the world the magma once belonged to.
## Traits
**Death Burst.** Explodes in lava. 5-foot radius, DC 11 DEX save or 7 (2d6) fire.
**False Appearance.** Indistinguishable from a small lava formation while motionless.

## Actions
**Claws.** +3 to hit, 3 (1d4 + 1) slashing + 2 (1d4) fire.
**Fire Breath.** 15-foot cone, DC 11 DEX, 7 (2d6) fire. Recharges 6.
