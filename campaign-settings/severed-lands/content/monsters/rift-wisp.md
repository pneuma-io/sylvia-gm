A Rift Echo of lost travelers — those who became separated from their parties and died alone in the wilderness near Rift pools. Wisps appear as floating violet lights that mimic torch or lantern flame, luring the lost deeper into dangerous territory. Following a wisp never leads to safety anywhere in the Severed Lands.
## Traits
**Consume Life.** As a bonus action, the wisp can target one creature within 5 feet with 0 HP. The target must succeed on DC 10 CON save or die. If dies, the wisp regains 10 HP.
**Ephemeral.** The wisp can't wear or carry anything.
**Incorporeal Movement.** Can move through creatures and objects as difficult terrain. Takes 1d10 force damage if ends turn inside an object.
**Variable Illumination.** Sheds bright light in a 5-20 foot radius and dim light for additional 5-20 feet (adjustable as a bonus action).

## Actions
**Shock.** Melee Spell Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., 9 (2d8) lightning.
**Invisibility.** The wisp and its light magically become invisible until it attacks or casts a spell, or until its concentration ends as if concentrating on a spell.

## Appearance and artwork

A tiny bodiless floating violet flame with one curling teardrop silhouette and a pale lilac core. Nested translucent flame-shaped cavities recede into its luminous body. It has no face, limbs, clothing or carried object; its flame-like appearance is not a physical torch or lantern. No individual lost traveler's identity is assigned.
