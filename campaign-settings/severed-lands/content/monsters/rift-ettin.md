A two-headed giant Riftborn whose twin minds have been driven in opposite directions by Rift exposure — one head is murderously violent, the other cunning and manipulative. They argue constantly, sometimes coming to blows over which head controls the body. Both heads remember different versions of their homeworld's fall.
## Traits

**Two Heads.** The rift ettin has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks and on saving throws against being blinded, charmed, deafened, frightened, stunned, and knocked unconscious.
**Wakeful.** When one of the rift ettin's heads is asleep, its other head is awake.

## Actions

**Multiattack.** The rift ettin makes two attacks: one with its battleaxe and one with its morningstar.
**Battleaxe.** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (2d8 + 5) slashing damage.
**Morningstar.** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (2d8 + 5) piercing damage.
