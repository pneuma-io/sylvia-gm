A human exposed to so much Rift energy that their body has swollen and warped into a hulking, nearly mindless brute. The transformation erases the person they were; all that remains is raw physical power and a dim, painful awareness of what they've lost. Hollowed brutes serve as shock troops for Riftborn warlords who have learned to direct their rage.
## Traits

**Brute.** A melee weapon deals one extra die of its damage when the bugbear hits with it (included in the attack).
**Surprise Attack.** If the bugbear surprises a creature and hits it with an attack during the first round of combat, the target takes an extra 7 (2d6) damage from the attack.

## Actions

**Morningstar.** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d8 + 2) piercing damage.
**Javelin.** Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (2d6 + 2) piercing damage.
