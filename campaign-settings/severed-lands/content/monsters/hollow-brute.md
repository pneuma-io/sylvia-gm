A human exposed to so much Rift energy that their body has swollen and warped into a hulking, nearly mindless brute. The transformation erases the person they were; all that remains is raw physical power and a dim, painful awareness of what they've lost. Hollowed brutes serve as shock troops for Riftborn warlords who have learned to direct their rage.
