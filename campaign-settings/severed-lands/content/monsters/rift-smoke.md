A writhing column of dark smoke shot through with violet threads — Rift discharge from a fire that burned something unnatural. It chokes and blinds, and those who breathe it in find their lungs filling with whispers from the other side of the Veil. It is drawn to conflict, feeding on the smoke of burning siege engines and pyres.
## Traits
**Death Burst.** When the mephit dies, it leaves behind a cloud of smoke in a 5-ft. radius sphere. The area is heavily obscured for 1 minute. A wind disperses it.
**False Appearance.** While motionless, the mephit is indistinguishable from an ordinary cloud of smoke.

## Actions
**Claws.** +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 4 (1d4 + 2) slashing.
**Cinder Breath (Recharge 6).** 15-ft. cone. DC 10 Dex save or blinded until end of its next turn.
