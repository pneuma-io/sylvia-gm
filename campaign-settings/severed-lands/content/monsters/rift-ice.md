Where the Rift meets deep cold, it crystallizes into a jagged, moving glacier of violet-frosted ice. Rift-ice elementals are rare outside the high peaks and the coldest reaches of the underdark, but when they descend they leave whole settlements flash-frozen — the inhabitants trapped like insects in amber.
## Traits
**Death Burst.** Explodes in ice shards. 5-foot radius, DC 10 DEX save or 4 (1d8) slashing + 4 (1d8) cold.
**False Appearance.** Indistinguishable from an ordinary shard of ice while motionless.

## Actions
**Claws.** +3 to hit, 3 (1d4 + 1) slashing + 2 (1d4) cold.
**Frost Breath.** 15-foot cone, DC 10 DEX save or 5 (2d4) cold. Recharges 6.
