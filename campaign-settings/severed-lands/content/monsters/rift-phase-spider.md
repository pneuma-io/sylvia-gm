A Rift-touched phase spider whose natural ability to shift between planes has merged with the Veil. It now phases between reality and the Veil boundary rather than the Ethereal Plane, emerging from solid surfaces trailing wisps of Rift-vapor. Its venom induces visions of falling through endless violet light.
## Traits
**Ethereal Jaunt.** As a bonus action, the spider can magically shift from the Material Plane to the Ethereal Plane, or vice versa.
**Spider Climb.** Can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down.

## Actions
**Bite.** +4 to hit, 7 (1d10 + 2) piercing. DC 11 CON save or 18 (4d8) poison (half on success). If poison reduces target to 0 HP, target is stable but poisoned for 1 hour and paralyzed while poisoned.
