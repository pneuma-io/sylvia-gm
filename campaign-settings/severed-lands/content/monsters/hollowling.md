Hollowlings are the smallest and most numerous of the Hollowed — humans warped by Rift exposure until they regressed to child-sized, broken things. They scavenge in chittering packs through the ruins of the Hollows, collecting anything that might once have been valuable, hoarding fragments of lives they can no longer remember living. They are not evil, only shattered — but a pack of Hollowlings cornering a lone traveler is no less deadly for its lack of malice.

## Traits
