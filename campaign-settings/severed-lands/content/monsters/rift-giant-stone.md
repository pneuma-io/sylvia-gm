A Riftborn stone giant whose people found refuge in the mountain caverns after falling through the Rift. They carve elaborate records of their lost homeworld into the living rock — vast galleries of bas-relief depicting cities of impossible geometry. They attack only when their memory-galleries are threatened.
## Traits
**Stone Camouflage.** Advantage on Stealth checks in rocky terrain.
