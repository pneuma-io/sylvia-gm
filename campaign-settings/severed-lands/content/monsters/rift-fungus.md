A pervasive fungal growth that spreads wherever Rift energy has saturated the soil. It carpets the ground in the deeper Hollows, feeding on residual Veil energies and the corpses of creatures caught in Rift events. Its spores induce feverish visions of the worlds that fell through the Rift.
## Traits
**Distress Spores.** When taking damage, all other myconids within 240 feet can sense its pain.
**Sun Sickness.** While in sunlight, disadvantage on ability checks, attacks, and saves. Dies if spends more than 1 hour in sunlight.

## Actions
**Fist.** +2 to hit, 2 (1d4) bludgeoning + 5 (2d4) poison.
**Pacifying Spores.** One creature within 5 feet must succeed on DC 11 CON save or be stunned for 1 minute. Repeat save at end of each turn. Recharges after short or long rest.
**Rapport Spores.** 20-foot radius. Affected creatures can't speak but can communicate telepathically with each other for 1 hour.
