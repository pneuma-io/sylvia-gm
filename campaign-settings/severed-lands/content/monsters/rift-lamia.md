A Rift-touched lamia — a lion-bodied enchantress whose illusory magic has been amplified by Veil exposure. Her lair shimmers with false paradises drawn from the memories of her victims, and those who succumb to her charms find themselves reliving their happiest moments as she drains their life in the desolate margins of the Severed Lands.
## Traits
**Innate Spellcasting.** CHA (DC 13). At will: disguise self, major image. 3/day: charm person, mirror image, scrying, suggestion. 1/day: geas.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Claw + dagger, or claw + intoxifying touch.
**Claw.** +5 to hit, 14 (2d10 + 3) slashing.
**Dagger.** +5 to hit, 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing.
**Intoxicating Touch.** Melee Spell Attack: +5 to hit, one creature. Target cursed for 1 hour, with disadvantage on WIS saves and ability checks.
