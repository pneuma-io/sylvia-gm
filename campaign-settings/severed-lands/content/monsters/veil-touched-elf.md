An elf who spent too long in proximity to the Veil, their innate fey sensitivity making them particularly vulnerable to its pull. Veil-touched elves flicker between reality and the Veil boundary, their forms unstable and their minds crowded with memories not their own. They speak in layered voices and know things no living creature should know.
## Traits
**Fey Ancestry.** Advantage on saves against charmed, and magic can't put to sleep.
**Sunlight Sensitivity.** Disadvantage on attacks and Perception in sunlight.
