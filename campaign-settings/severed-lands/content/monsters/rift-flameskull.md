A Rift Echo of a spellcaster consumed by their own magic — the skull and residual spellfire imprinted together when the caster's final spell backfired near a Rift pool. Flameskulls burn with cold Rift-flame and retain fragments of spellcasting ability, repeating their last magical patterns endlessly through the ruins of the Severed Lands.
## Traits
**Illumination.** Sheds dim light in a 15-foot radius.
**Magic Resistance.** Advantage on saves against spells.
**Rejuvenation.** If destroyed, the rift flameskull regains all its hit points in 1 hour unless holy water is sprinkled on its remains or a dispel magic or remove curse spell is cast on them.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Two fire ray attacks.
**Fire Ray.** Ranged Spell Attack: +5 to hit, range 30 ft., 10 (3d6) fire.
