A haze of hot ash and embers animated by a Rift spark that found fuel in the aftermath of a fire. It drifts across the scarred battlefields and burnt homesteads of the Severed Lands, reigniting anything flammable and leaving a trail of glowing coals that whisper in dead languages.
## Traits
**Death Burst.** When the rift cinder dies, it explodes. Creatures within 10 ft. make DC 11 Dex save, taking 7 (2d6) fire on fail, half on success. Flammable objects ignite.
**Ignited Illumination.** The rift cinder sheds bright light in a 10-ft. radius and dim light 10 ft. further.

## Actions
**Touch.** +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 7 (2d6) fire. If the target is a creature or flammable object, it ignites. Until a creature takes an action to douse, the target takes 3 (1d6) fire at the end of each of its turns.
