A Rift-touched land-shark whose armored plates have fused with crystallized Rift minerals. It tunnels through the unstable ground of the Cairnfields, erupting without warning. Old Returners swear the bulette's dorsal crest patterns match Warden architectural motifs — and whisper that the first bulettes were Warden creations that escaped.
## Traits
**Standing Leap.** Long jump up to 30 feet, high jump up to 15 feet.

## Actions
**Bite.** +7 to hit, 30 (4d12 + 4) piercing.
**Deadly Leap.** If the rift bulette jumps at least 15 feet as part of its movement, it can use this action to land on its feet in a space that contains one or more creatures. Each creature must make DC 16 STR or DEX save (target's choice) or be knocked prone and take 14 (3d6 + 4) bludgeoning + 14 (3d6 + 4) slashing. On success, half damage and not prone.
