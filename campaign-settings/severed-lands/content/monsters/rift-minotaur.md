A Rift-touched minotaur whose labyrinth-sense has been warped by Veil exposure into something far stranger — it no longer navigates physical mazes so much as it perceives the labyrinthine structure of the Veil itself. Rift-minotaurs can find paths through solid stone by following Veil-currents, making them impossible to trap in the Severed Lands.
## Traits
**Charge.** If the rift minotaur moves at least 10 feet straight toward a target and hits with a gore attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 9 (2d8) piercing damage. DC 14 STR save or pushed up to 10 feet and knocked prone.
**Labyrinthine Recall.** Can perfectly recall any path it has traveled.

## Actions
**Greataxe.** +6 to hit, 17 (2d12 + 4) slashing.
**Gore.** +6 to hit, 13 (2d8 + 4) piercing.
