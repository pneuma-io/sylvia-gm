A Rift Echo of violent death — a warrior's final battle imprint given form by the Rift. Unlike most Echoes, wights retain fragments of tactical intelligence from their original selves and can coordinate attacks. They are drawn to the battlefields of the Severed Lands, endlessly refighting conflicts that ended centuries ago.
## Traits

**Sunlight Sensitivity.** While in sunlight, the rift wight has disadvantage on attack rolls.

## Actions

**Multiattack.** The rift wight makes two longsword attacks or two longbow attacks. It can use its Life Drain in place of one longsword attack.
**Life Drain.** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) necrotic damage. The target must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or its hit point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the damage taken. This reduction lasts until the target finishes a long rest. The target dies if this effect reduces its hit point maximum to 0. A humanoid slain by this attack rises 24 hours later as a rift zombie under the rift wight's control.
**Longsword.** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d8 + 2) slashing damage.

## Appearance and artwork

A gaunt mature male-presenting warrior Echo with dry graphite-grey skin, angular face, pale grey eyes, short uneven iron-grey hair and an alert calculating expression. Dark brown studded leather covers faded slate clothing, dark trousers and worn boots. A plain longsword is carried lowered in the right hand; a longbow lies diagonally behind the left shoulder and a quiver behind the right. Fine nested opaque Echo layers at the temples, throat and hands show muted silver-blue recesses without wounds or extra anatomy. No army, rank or personal history is assigned.
