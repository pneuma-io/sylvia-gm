An ancient tree of the Verdant Margin, animated by decades of Rift exposure. Its bark glows faintly violet along the grain, and its sap runs with Rift essence. Unlike true treants, the Rift-treant remembers nothing of the forest it once protected — only a burning drive to spread the Margin's boundaries.
## Traits
**False Appearance.** While motionless, indistinguishable from a normal tree.
**Siege Monster.** Double damage to objects and structures.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Two slam attacks.
**Slam.** +10 to hit, reach 5 ft., 16 (3d6 + 6) bludgeoning.
**Rock.** +10 to hit, range 60/180 ft., 28 (4d10 + 6) bludgeoning.

## Appearance and artwork

An editable active Treant default is one huge brown/grey gnarled broadleaf tree with lush deep green/olive crown, two three-pronged bough arms and two load-bearing root-leg bundles. Its one subtle bark-seam face can close for normal motionless tree disguise. Faint violet grain/sap seams and nested organic wood lamellae add Rift structure, without a forest memory, named species, permanent portal or new ability.
