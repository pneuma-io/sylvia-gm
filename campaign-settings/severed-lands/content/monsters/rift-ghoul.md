A Rift Echo of hunger — formed when someone starved near a Rift pool, their final craving imprinted on the Veil's energy. The ghoul-Echo seeks endlessly to fill a hunger that cannot be satisfied, consuming flesh not for sustenance but because the memory of starvation drives it. Its bite carries the chill of the Rift itself.
## Actions

**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 9 (2d6 + 2) piercing damage.
**Claws.** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (2d4 + 2) slashing damage. If the target is a creature other than an elf or Rift-touched, it must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or be paralyzed for 1 minute. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns.
