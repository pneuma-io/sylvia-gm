A Veil-touched elf who has not only survived the transformation but mastered it. Elites can control their phasing, stepping through the Veil at will to bypass obstacles and avoid attacks. They remember clearly the worlds that have fallen and sometimes serve as unwilling oracles for those desperate enough to seek them out.
## Traits
**Fey Ancestry.** Advantage vs charmed.
**Sunlight Sensitivity.** Disadvantage in sunlight.

**Hand Crossbow.** +7 to hit, 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing. DC 13 CON or poisoned 1 hour.
