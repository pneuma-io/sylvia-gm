A Rift Echo of vengeance — formed when someone was murdered unjustly near a Rift pool, their dying vow of revenge captured and given form. Unlike most Echoes, revenants are focused and purposeful; they seek out their killer with relentless determination. If the killer is already dead, the revenant targets their descendants instead.
## Traits
**Regeneration.** 10 HP at start of turn. If takes fire or radiant damage, this doesn't function at start of its next turn. Revives 24 hours after body destroyed, unless wish or similar magic is used.
**Turn Immunity.** Immune to effects that turn Rift-touched.
**Vengeful Tracker.** Knows the distance and direction to its killer, even across planes.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Two fist attacks.
**Fist.** +7 to hit, 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning. If both fists hit a Large or smaller creature, target grappled (escape DC 14) and must succeed on DC 14 WIS save or be frightened for 1 minute.
