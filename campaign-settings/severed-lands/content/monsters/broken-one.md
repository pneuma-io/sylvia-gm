A humanoid who fell through the Veil and was shattered in the passage — body and mind fractured into overlapping, contradictory pieces. Broken Ones speak in fragments of their old life, sometimes lucid, more often incomprehensible. They are drawn to Anchor Doors, as if seeking a way back through a gate that no longer opens.
## Traits

**Aggressive.** As a bonus action, the orc can move up to its speed toward a hostile creature that it can see.

**Javelin.** Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage.
