A Riftborn entity that hunts not for flesh but for knowledge — it fell from a world of libraries and scholars, and now collects the memories and written records of the Severed Lands with obsessive intensity. Seekers can extract information directly from a victim's mind, leaving them alive but hollowed of everything they knew.
## Traits
**Innate Spellcasting.** WIS (DC 12). 3/day: feather fall, jump, see invisibility, shield. 1/day: plane shift (self only).
