A wretched humanoid that lives in the abandoned Warden tunnels beneath the Severed Lands. Tunnel wretches were once human — miners, delvers, or lost Returners — but generations of isolation and Rift exposure have turned them into pale, long-limbed creatures that shun light. They know the tunnels better than anyone alive and trade that knowledge for fresh meat.

**Sunlight Sensitivity.** Disadvantage on attacks and Perception in sunlight.
**Pack Tactics.** Advantage if ally within 5 feet.
