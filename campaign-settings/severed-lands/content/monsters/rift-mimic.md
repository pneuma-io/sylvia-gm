A Rift-touched shapechanger that has adapted to the Severed Lands by mimicking Warden artifacts and Anchor Door fragments instead of mundane furniture. Rift-mimics are drawn to the residual magic in Warden architecture and often lurk in the form of promising treasure caches — a trap that even experienced Returners fall for.
## Traits
**Shapechanger.** The rift mimic can use its action to polymorph into an object or back. Stats same. Adhesive only in object form.
**Adhesive (Object Form Only).** Adheres to anything touching it. Huge or smaller stuck (escape DC 13). Disadvantage on ability checks to escape.
**False Appearance (Object Form Only).** While motionless, indistinguishable from an ordinary object.
**Grappler.** Advantage on attack rolls against any creature grappled by it.

## Actions
**Pseudopod.** +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 7 (1d8 + 3) bludgeoning. If in object form, target subjected to Adhesive.
**Bite.** +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing plus 4 (1d8) acid.

## Appearance and artwork

An editable activated artifact-mimic default: one squat grey stone cache with dull bronze clamps and stepped arch relief, a single tooth-lined violet mouth at the lid join, and two attached blunt plum pseudopods. Nested offset arch lamellae recede inside the mouth. No eyes, readable script, named artifact or new mechanics; ordinary motionless-object disguise remains.
