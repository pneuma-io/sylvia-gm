A Riftborn troll whose regenerative abilities have been twisted by Rift exposure. Near active Rift pools, a Rift-troll regenerates at terrifying speed — severed limbs crawl back and reattach within seconds. Away from Rift energy it still heals, but slowly and with visible violet scarring. They are most dangerous in the depths of the Hollows.
## Traits

**Keen Smell.** Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.
**Regeneration.** The rift troll regains 10 hit points at the start of its turn. If the rift troll takes acid or fire damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of the rift troll's next turn. The rift troll dies only if it starts its turn with 0 hit points and doesn't regenerate.

## Actions

**Multiattack.** The rift troll makes three attacks: one with its bite and two with its claws.
**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage.
**Claw.** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage.
