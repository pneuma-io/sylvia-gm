A mass of prehensile, Rift-infused vines that lash out from the canopy of the Verdant Margin. It strangles prey and hoists them into the branches, where they are slowly drained. The vines are studded with thorns that weep a paralytic sap — victims remain alive and aware as they are consumed.
## Traits
**False Appearance.** While motionless, the verdant vine is indistinguishable from a tangle of vines.
**Entangling Plants (Recharge 5-6).** Grasping roots/vines in 15-ft. radius. Creatures in area make DC 12 Str save or restrained. Action to break free DC 12 Str check.

## Actions
**Constrict.** +4 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 9 (2d6 + 2) bludgeoning, and Large or smaller target is grappled (escape DC 12). Until grapple ends, target restrained, blight can't constrict another.

## Appearance and artwork

One connected coiled tangle of living dark-green/brown vines with sweeping loops, fine tendrils, olive heart-shaped leaves and backward thorns. Clear amber sap and recurring leaf-vein/tendril folds supply restrained inner depth. Its resting shape remains an ordinary vine tangle, without a face, victim or detached appendage.
