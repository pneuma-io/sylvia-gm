The rarest and most dangerous of Rift elementals — a fusion of multiple elemental forms bound together around a core of pure Veil energy. Weirds are unstable, unpredictable, and often explode into Rift surges when destroyed. Scholars at the Waystation believe each weird contains a fragment of a world's dying scream.
## Traits
**Invisible in Water.** Invisible while fully immersed.
**Water Bound.** Dies if it leaves the water to which it is bound or if that water is destroyed.

## Actions
**Constrict.** +5 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 13 (3d6 + 3) bludgeoning. If Medium or smaller, grappled (escape DC 13) and restrained. Can grapple only one.
