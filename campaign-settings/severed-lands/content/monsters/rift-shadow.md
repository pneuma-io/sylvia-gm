A Rift Echo thin enough to be nearly transparent — a death-memory so faint that only the outline of a person remains. Shadows are drawn to living creatures with strong emotional states, feeding on fear and despair. They are most dangerous in the darkness of the Hollows, where they are nearly invisible until they strike.
## Traits
**Amorphous.** Can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without squeezing.
**rift shadow Stealth.** While in dim light or darkness, can take Hide action as a bonus action.
**Sunlight Weakness.** While in sunlight, disadvantage on attacks, ability checks, and saves.

## Actions
**Strength Drain.** +4 to hit, 9 (2d6 + 2) necrotic. Target's STR score reduced by 1d4. Target dies if STR reaches 0. Reduction lasts until short or long rest. If a non-evil humanoid dies from this, a new rift shadow rises from the corpse 1d4 hours later.
