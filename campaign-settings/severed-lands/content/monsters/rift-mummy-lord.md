A Rift Echo of royal death — the imprint of a ruler whose burial was catastrophic enough to scar the Veil permanently. Mummy lords retain commanding intelligence and marshal lesser Echoes into an undead court. Their tombs are the most dangerous sites in the Severed Lands, rivaling active Anchor Doors in peril.
## Traits
**Magic Resistance.** Advantage on saves against spells.
**Rejuvenation.** If destroyed, regains its body in 24 hours. Heart in canopic jar must be destroyed to prevent.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Rotting Fist + Dreadful Glare.
**Rotting Fist.** +9 to hit, 14 (3d6 + 4) bludgeoning + 21 (6d6) necrotic. DC 16 CON or cursed with rift mummy rot.
**Dreadful Glare.** One creature within 60 feet must succeed on DC 16 WIS save or frightened until end of next turn.
**Spellcasting.** 10th-level cleric (WIS, DC 17, +9). Cantrips: sacred flame, thaumaturgy. 1st (4): command, guiding bolt, shield of faith. 2nd (3): hold person, silence, spiritual weapon. 3rd (3): animate dead, dispel magic. 4th (3): divination, guardian of faith. 5th (2): contagion, insect plague. 6th (1): harm.
