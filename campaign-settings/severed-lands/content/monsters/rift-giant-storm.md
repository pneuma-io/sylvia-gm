A storm giant Riftborn, the mightiest of the giant-kin who fell. Its voice carries the thunder of its lost world's final storm, and lightning arcs violet between its fingers. It remembers being a king among giants and demands homage from all lesser beings — a demand the Returners have learned to take seriously.
## Traits
**Amphibious.** Breathe air and water.
**Innate Spellcasting.** CHA (DC 17). At will: detect magic, feather fall, levitate, light. 3/day: control weather, water breathing.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Two greatsword attacks.
**Greatsword.** +14 to hit, 30 (6d6 + 9) slashing.
**Rock.** +14 to hit, range 60/240 ft., 35 (4d12 + 9) bludgeoning.
**Lightning Strike.** The giant hurls a magical lightning bolt at a point it can see within 500 feet. Each creature within 10 feet of that point must make a DC 17 DEX save, taking 54 (12d8) lightning damage on failure, or half on success.
