A great cat Hollowed by Rift exposure, its natural stealth enhanced to supernatural levels. Rifthollowed tigers can briefly phase through the Veil, reappearing behind their prey. Their stripes pulse with faint violet light when they are about to strike — the last thing many Returners ever see.
## Traits
**Shapechanger.** Can use action to polymorph into a tiger-humanoid hybrid or a tiger, or back.
**Keen Hearing and Smell.** Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks relying on hearing or smell.
**Pounce (Tiger or Hybrid Form Only).** If moves 15+ ft. straight and hits with claw, DC 14 Str save or knocked prone. If prone, can make one bite as a bonus action.

## Actions
**Multiattack (Humanoid or Hybrid Form Only).** Three attacks: one bite and two claws (hybrid) or two scimitar and one bite (hybrid); or two claws and one bite (tiger).
**Bite (Tiger or Hybrid Form Only).** +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 8 (1d10 + 3) piercing. If humanoid, DC 13 Con save or cursed with rifthollowed tiger Rift-touch.
**Claw (Tiger or Hybrid Form Only).** +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 7 (1d8 + 3) slashing.
**Scimitar (Humanoid or Hybrid Form Only).** +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing.
**Longbow (Humanoid or Hybrid Form Only).** +4 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing.

## Appearance and artwork

The selected default is an agile adult tiger-humanoid hybrid with copper-orange fur, black stripes, white cheeks/throat, pale green eyes, rounded ears and a long ringed tail. Two arms with empty clawed hands and two digitigrade legs retain a coherent tiger-like body. Nested stripe contours carry faint violet-opal reflections within the fur. Plain dark wrap-trousers and a woven sash cover the hips; no weapon or individual human biography is assigned. The other authored forms and abilities remain unchanged.
