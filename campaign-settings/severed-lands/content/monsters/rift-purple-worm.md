A Rift-touched purple worm — already one of the most feared predators underground, now enhanced by exposure to deep Rift veins. Its hide is studded with violet crystals, and its swallowing maw is ringed with teeth that carry a residual Rift charge. Rift-purple worms are the apex predators of the deep Cairnfields, feared even by the Wardens' remaining constructs.
## Traits
**Tunneler.** Can burrow through solid rift rock at half its burrow speed, leaving a 10-foot-diameter tunnel.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Bite + tail stinger.
**Bite.** +14 to hit, 22 (3d8 + 9) piercing. If Large or smaller, DC 19 DEX save or swallowed. Swallowed: blinded, restrained, 21 (6d6) acid at start of each worm turn. If worm takes 30+ damage from inside on a single turn, it must make DC 21 CON save or regurgitate all swallowed creatures.
**Tail Stinger.** +14 to hit, 19 (3d6 + 9) piercing. DC 19 CON save or 42 (12d6) poison (half on success).
