A Rift-touched harpy whose lure-song carries the echo of the Veil itself — listeners hear fragments of their own lost memories blended with promises of escape. Harpies roost in the ruins of fallen settlements throughout the Severed Lands, luring survivors and Returners alike to their deaths with songs that feel like home.
## Actions
**Multiattack.** Claw + club.
**Claw.** +3 to hit, 6 (2d4 + 1) slashing.
**Club.** +3 to hit, 3 (1d4 + 1) bludgeoning.
**Luring Song.** The rift harpy sings a magical melody. Every humanoid and giant within 300 feet that can hear must make a DC 11 Wisdom saving throw or be charmed until the song ends. The charmed target must use its movement to approach the rift harpy on each of its turns. A target can repeat the save each time it takes damage. A target that successfully saves is immune for 24 hours.
