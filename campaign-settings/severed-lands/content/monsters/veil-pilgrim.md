A failed pilgrim who attempted to cross the Veil, their body now covered in weeping mouths that speak fragments of lost worlds in a dozen languages — some long dead, some never spoken in this reality. The ground warps and softens around them, as if the boundary between worlds grows thin in their presence.
## Traits
**Aberrant Ground.** The ground in a 10-foot radius around the pilgrim is doughlike difficult terrain. Creatures starting their turn there must succeed on DC 10 STR save or speed becomes 0 until start of their next turn.
**Gibbering.** the pilgrim babbles incoherently. Any creature starting its turn within 20 feet that can hear must succeed on DC 10 WIS save or be unable to take reactions and roll a d8 to determine action on its turn (1-4: nothing, 5-6: attack nearest, 7-8: move randomly).

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Bite + blinding spittle.
**Bites.** +2 to hit, 17 (5d6) piercing. If Medium or smaller, grappled (escape DC 10), speed 0, and restrained. At start of each mouther's turn, grappled target takes 17 (5d6) piercing.
**Blinding Spittle.** Ranged Weapon Attack: range 15/30 ft., one creature. DC 10 DEX save or blinded until end of its next turn.

## Appearance and artwork
