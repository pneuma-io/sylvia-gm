A creature that lurks at the edges of Rift pools, scavenging the remains of creatures that fell through or were dissolved. Riftpond scavengers are twisted amalgams of whatever they have consumed — parts crustacean, parts mammalian, all wrong. They drag themselves onto land to hunt when Rift pool pickings grow scarce.
## Traits
**Amphibious.** Breathes air and water.
**Otherworldly Perception.** Can sense the presence of any creature within 30 feet that is invisible or on the Ethereal Plane.
**Slippery.** Advantage on ability checks and saves made to escape a grapple.
**Sunlight Sensitivity.** Disadvantage in sunlight.

## Actions
**Bite.** +3 to hit, 3 (1d4 + 1) piercing.
**Spear.** +3 to hit, 4 (1d6 + 1) piercing.

## Appearance and artwork

A squat intact crustacean/mammalian amalgam default with one mammalian muzzle beneath a layered blue-grey brow shell, two amber eyes, small fur-edged ears, grey-brown fur and teal chitin. Two arms retain a right spear-gripping hand and left pincer forearm with a strapped driftwood shield. Two bent legs have broad webbed feet; one flattened segmented tail completes the body. Nested shell shelves and fur loops carry restrained purple-green inner depth. No consumed individual, new ability or personal origin is assigned.
