A deliberate Rift Echo — not an imprint of death but a spellcaster who chose to bind their consciousness to the Veil while still alive. Rift-liches are not undead in the traditional sense; they have moved their existence partially into the Rift itself, becoming less physical and more... remembered. They are among the most dangerous entities in the Severed Lands.
## Traits
**Legendary Resistance.** 3/day.
**Rejuvenation.** If destroyed, the rift lich regains its body in 1d10 days near its phylactery.
**Turn Resistance.** Advantage on saves against effects that turn Rift-touched.

## Actions
**Paralyzing Touch.** +12 to hit, 10 (3d6) cold. DC 18 CON save or paralyzed 1 minute.
**Spellcasting.** 18th-level wizard (INT, DC 20, +12). Cantrips — mage hand, prestidigitation, ray of frost. 1st (4) — detect magic, magic missile, shield, thunderwave. 2nd (3) — detect thoughts, invisibility, acid arrow. 3rd (3) — animate dead, counterspell, dispel magic, fireball. 4th (3) — blight, dimension door. 5th (3) — cloudkill, scrying. 6th (1) — disintegrate, globe of invulnerability. 7th (1) — finger of death, plane shift. 8th (1) — dominate monster, power word stun. 9th (1) — power word kill.
