A Rift-touched piercer — a stalactite-like ambush predator that has absorbed calcite infused with Rift minerals. It drops from cavern ceilings with lethal precision, its body shimmering with the same violet luminescence as the mineral veins around dormant Anchor Doors. Miners in the Severed Lands have learned to fear the glow.
## Traits
**False Appearance.** While motionless on the ceiling, indistinguishable from a normal stalactite.
**Spider Climb.** Can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

## Actions
**Drop.** +3 to hit, one target directly below. 14 (3d6 + 4) piercing per 10 ft. fallen, up to 21 (6d6 + 4). Miss: half damage. rift piercer lands prone after, regardless.
