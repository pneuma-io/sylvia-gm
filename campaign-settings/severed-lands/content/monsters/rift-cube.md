A perfect geometric horror — the Rift's energy condensed into a translucent cube of latent dissolution. It drifts through the Hollows and Cairnfields, nearly invisible until prey steps into its space. Unlike natural oozes, the cube's shape never falters, as if held in a mold of crystallized Veil-stuff.
## Traits
**Transparent.** DC 15 Perception check to spot a motionless cube.

## Actions
**Pseudopod.** +4 to hit, 10 (3d6) acid.
**Engulf.** The cube moves up to its speed. While doing so, it can enter Large or smaller creatures' spaces. Creatures must make DC 12 DEX save or be engulfed. Engulfed creature is restrained and takes 10 (3d6) acid at start of each cube's turn. Can hold one Large or up to four Medium or smaller creatures.

## Appearance and artwork

A complete mathematically regular translucent cube of nearly clear pale-violet gelatinous material, with straight perpendicular edges and flat square faces that never slump. Subtle internal Rift haze and delicate violet edge refraction make the volume readable. Empty interior without victims, bones, gear or debris. No eyes, face, mouth, limbs, core crystal, container or metal frame. The complete geometric silhouette is its identifying feature.
