A Rift elemental that has bound itself to a boulder or rock formation, using the stone as its body. The boulder's surface crawls with shifting violet cracks — veins of liquid Rift energy — and it crushes prey with a slow, grinding intelligence born where the Veil runs thin.
## Traits
**False Appearance.** While motionless, indistinguishable from a normal boulder.
**Rolling Charge.** If the rift boulder rolls at least 20 feet straight toward a target and hits with a slam on the same turn, +7 (2d6) bludgeoning. DC 16 STR save or knocked prone.

## Actions
**Slam.** +8 to hit, 12 (2d6 + 5) bludgeoning.
**Animate Boulders.** The rift boulder targets up to two boulders it can see within 60 feet. They sprout limbs and become creatures (AC 17, 10 HP). When created, and as a bonus action, the duhr can command them to move up to 30 feet and attack one target (+8, 12 (2d6 + 5) bludgeoning). Recharges 5-6.
