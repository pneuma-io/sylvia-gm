A Warden creation that even they came to fear — a flesh-construct stitched together from the bodies of Riftborn and animated with a stolen fragment of Veil essence. It was designed to fight the Rift by turning its own nature against it. Now, with no Warden to control it, the horror stitches new parts onto itself from whatever it kills.
## Traits
**Magic Resistance.** Advantage on saves against spells.
**Spell Immunity.** Immune to three spells chosen by its creator (typically fireball, heat metal, lightning bolt).

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Two longsword attacks.
**Longsword.** +6 to hit, 8 (1d8 + 4) slashing.

## Appearance and artwork

An adult-proportioned composite humanoid construct with a bald mature head, dull grey/violet eyes and intact ash-grey/olive skin panels joined by closed healed stitch seams. Two arms and legs retain ordinary five-fingered hands. Dark steel plate and charcoal undercloth accompany a lowered right-hand longsword and left-arm unmarked heater shield. Restrained opaline folds remain beside intact seams; no victim identity is assigned.
