Not an animated treant but a tree that has absorbed so much Rift energy it has become predatory. Its branches creak and reach, its roots drag it slowly across the Margin floor, and it impales prey on sharpened limbs to let their blood feed its soil. The oldest specimens have absorbed the memories of their victims.
## Traits
**False Appearance.** While motionless, indistinguishable from a normal tree.

## Actions
**Slam.** +6 to hit, 14 (3d6 + 4) bludgeoning.

## Appearance and artwork

A mature broad-canopied deciduous tree default with rough grey-brown trunk, deep-green foliage, reaching branches and four main gnarled root supports. Broken wooden branch tips remain natural in shape. Nested bark grain and subtle cool inner shadows preserve ordinary-tree concealment when still. There is no humanoid face or treant body.
