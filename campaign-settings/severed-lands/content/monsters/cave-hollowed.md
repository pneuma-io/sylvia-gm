A Hollowed adapted to the darkness of the underdeep — their eyes have atrophied to pale, sightless orbs, replaced by an uncanny Rift-sense. Cave Hollowed dwell in the tunnels beneath the Cairnfields, emerging only to drag surface prey back into the dark. Their flesh is translucent, and Veil-light pulses visibly through their veins.
## Traits
**Wounded Fury.** While it has 10 hp or fewer, the quaggoth has advantage on attack rolls. It also adds 5 (2d4) damage to any melee attack it makes.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Two claw attacks.
**Claw.** +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing.
