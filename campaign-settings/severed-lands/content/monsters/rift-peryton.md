A Rift-touched peryton whose shadow has been severed from its body by Veil exposure — it now casts the shadow of whatever creature's heart it most recently consumed. Perytons in the Severed Lands hunt humanoids exclusively, as if trying to reclaim a shadow of their own. They stalk the skies above Thornwall on moonless nights.
## Traits
**Dive Attack.** If flying and dives 30+ ft. straight toward a target and hits with a gore attack, deals extra 9 (2d8) damage.
**Flyby.** Doesn't provoke opportunity attacks when flying out of enemy's reach.
**Keen Sight and Smell.** Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks relying on sight or smell.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** One gore and one talons.
**Gore.** +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing.
**Talons.** +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 8 (2d4 + 3) piercing.

## Appearance and artwork

An editable Peryton default: one russet stag-like head with golden eyes, pointed ears and two branched dark antlers on a charcoal/russet avian body, cream throat, two feathered wings, two taloned raptor legs and one feather tail. Nested inward antler grain and secondary-feather arcs carry structural Rift detail. Projected shadow is omitted to avoid inventing the unidentified last prey; the authored severed-shadow rule remains.

This prepared appearance is an editable visual default; the character's authored identity, mechanics and lore above remain authoritative.
