A floating, gas-filled fungal orb native to the deeper reaches of the Hollows. It drifts on Rift currents, releasing clouds of hallucinogenic spores when threatened. Inhaling them gives brief, chaotic visions of worlds falling — skyscrapers tumbling through violet light, alien oceans boiling into steam.
## Traits
**Death Burst.** When drops to 0 HP, explodes. Each creature within 20 feet must succeed on DC 15 CON save or take 10 (3d6) poison damage and become infected with spores. Infected creature takes 3 (1d6) poison at the start of each of its turns and must repeat the save at the end of each turn, ending the infection on a success. An infected creature that dies sprouts 2d4 rift spores from its corpse after 24 hours.
**Eerie Resemblance.** Resembles a beholder. A creature that can see the rift spore and succeeds on a DC 10 INT check knows it is not a true beholder.

## Actions
**Touch.** +0 to hit, 1 poison damage.
