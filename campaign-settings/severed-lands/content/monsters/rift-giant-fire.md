A fire giant Riftborn from a world of volcanic forge-cities. Its body radiates unnatural heat mixed with flickers of Rift energy, and the weapons it forges are quenched not in water but in concentrated Rift pool runoff. It believes itself a master-smith and seeks materials to recreate the lost craft of its dead world.
## Actions

**Multiattack.** The giant makes two greatsword attacks.
**Greatsword.** Melee Weapon Attack: +11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 28 (6d6 + 7) slashing damage.
**Rock.** Ranged Weapon Attack: +11 to hit, range 60/240 ft., one target. Hit: 29 (4d10 + 7) bludgeoning damage.
