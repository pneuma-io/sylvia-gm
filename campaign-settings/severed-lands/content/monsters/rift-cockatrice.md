A Rift-touched hybrid of bird and reptile whose petrifying bite injects crystallized Veil-stuff — a venom that calcifies tissue into Anchor-stone-like material. Cockatrices nest in ruined Warden outposts, as if instinctively drawn to the ancient architecture that mirrors their own unnatural form.
## Actions
**Bite.** +3 to hit, 3 (1d4 + 1) piercing. DC 11 CON save or be petrified. The petrification lasts for 24 hours.

## Appearance and artwork

A small bird-reptile hybrid with one rooster-like head, russet comb and wattles, dark hooked beak, amber eyes, ochre breast feathers and olive-green neck and tail scales. Two scaly taloned legs, two leathery shoulder wings and one long sinuous tail. Dusty plum wing membranes contain nested prismatic Veil lattices; repeated translucent mineral folds run through the neck scales. Pale mineral flecks surround the beak without showing an attack or victim.
