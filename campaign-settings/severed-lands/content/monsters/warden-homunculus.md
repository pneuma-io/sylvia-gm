A tiny, winged construct built by the Wardens as messengers and scouts, formed from copper wire, preserved leather, and a single drop of crystallized Veil-ichor. Homunculi still flit through the Severed Lands carrying messages meant for masters dead ten thousand years. They are docile unless their delivery is interfered with.
## Traits
**Telepathic Bond.** While the warden homunculus is on the same plane as its master, it can magically convey what it senses to its master, and the two can communicate telepathically.

## Actions
**Bite.** +4 to hit, 1 piercing. DC 10 CON save or poisoned for 1 minute. If failed by 5+, unconscious while poisoned.
