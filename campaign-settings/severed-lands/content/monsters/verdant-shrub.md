A seemingly innocuous bush of the Verdant Margin — until it moves. The shrub uproots itself and skitters on twisted root-legs, seeking prey. Its berries are laced with Rift-toxins that induce vivid, madness-tinged hallucinations before paralysis sets in.
## Traits

**False Appearance.** While the shrub remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a normal shrub.

## Appearance and artwork

A compact olive-green berry bush default shown uprooted on four main twisted root-legs. Brown twigs, small oval leaves and dark red berry clusters retain an ordinary shrub appearance when motionless. Fine repeated forks and restrained violet-green depth remain inside shaded growth; there is no face or luminous effect.
