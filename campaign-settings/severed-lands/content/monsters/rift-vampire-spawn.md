A younger, weaker Rift Echo of predation — formed from the death-imprint of someone killed by a rift-vampire. Spawn are bound to the vampire-Echo that created them, acting as extensions of its predatory will. They are less intelligent than their creator but share the same unending hunger.
## Traits
**Regeneration.** Regains 10 HP at start of its turn if it has at least 1 HP and isn't in sunlight or running water. If reduced to 0 HP while unable to regenerate, it dies.
**Spider Climb.** Climb difficult surfaces including upside down.
**rift vampire Weaknesses.** Takes 20 radiant damage when starting turn in sunlight. Takes 20 acid damage when ending turn in running water. Disadvantage on attacks and abilities if presented with a holy symbol by a creature within 5 feet.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Two claw attacks, or claw + bite.
**Claws.** +6 to hit, 8 (2d4 + 3) slashing. Grappled on hit (escape DC 13).
**Bite.** +6 to hit, 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing + 7 (2d6) necrotic. Target's max HP reduced by necrotic damage taken. Dies if max HP reaches 0. Grappled, incapacitated, or restrained target hit automatically.

## Appearance and artwork

A lean adult female-presenting humanoid Echo with ash-brown skin, a narrow heart-shaped face, dark amber eyes and short uneven dark copper hair. A tense mouth shows a small dry fang. A fully opaque charcoal-indigo wrap shirt and worn grey work jacket accompany dark trousers and scuffed boots. Empty hands have sharp dark nails; fine folded skin planes along the forearms and cheek edges carry dull blue-green inner reflections. Wary slightly crouched bearing. No named master, ancestry or formation ritual is assigned.
