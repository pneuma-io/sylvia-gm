A Rift-touched iron-plated bull whose petrifying breath is a concentrated burst of Veil-stuff. Rift-gorgons are most common in the Cairnfields, where their breath transforms victims into standing stones indistinguishable from the natural pillars — a grim addition to the already treacherous landscape.
## Traits
**Trampling Charge.** If moves 20 ft. straight and hits with gore, target must succeed on DC 16 STR save or be knocked prone. Can then make bonus action hooves attack.

## Actions
**Gore.** +8 to hit, 18 (2d12 + 5) piercing.
**Hooves.** +8 to hit, 16 (2d10 + 5) bludgeoning.
**Petrifying Breath.** 30-foot cone, DC 13 CON save. On a failed save, begins to turn to stone and is restrained. Must repeat save at end of its next turn, becoming petrified on a failure or ending on success. Recharges 5-6.
