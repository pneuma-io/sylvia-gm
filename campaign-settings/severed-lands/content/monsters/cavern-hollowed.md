A Hollowed found in the vast natural caverns beneath the Severed Lands, where Rift pools have pooled for centuries. Cavern Hollowed are larger than their surface kin, their bodies bloated with accumulated Rift energy. They worship the pools as sacred and sacrifice living captives to them, believing the Rift will grant them transformation rather than dissolution.
## Traits
**Chameleon Skin.** Advantage on Stealth checks.
**Stench.** Creatures starting turn within 5 feet must succeed on DC 12 CON save or be poisoned until start of their next turn.
**Sunlight Sensitivity.** Disadvantage in sunlight.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Bite + claws, or bite + javelin.
**Bite.** +4 to hit, 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing.
**Claw.** +4 to hit, 4 (1d4 + 2) slashing.
**Javelin.** +4 to hit, range 30/120 ft., 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing.
