A Rift Echo — the death-imprint of an elven woman whose grief was so profound it scarred the Veil itself. When she died near a Rift pool, her final wail was captured in the Rift's energy and now replays endlessly. The Echo's scream is not sound but memory: everyone who hears it relives her moment of loss as if it were their own.
## Traits
**Detect Life.** The rift banshee can magically sense the presence of living creatures up to 5 miles away that aren't Rift-touched or constructs.
**Incorporeal Movement.** Can move through creatures and objects as difficult terrain.

## Actions
**Corrupting Touch.** +4 to hit, 12 (3d6 + 2) necrotic.
**Horrifying Visage.** Each non-Rift-touched within 60 ft. must make DC 13 WIS save or be frightened for 1 minute.
**Wail.** The rift banshee releases a mournful wail. All non-Rift-touched creatures within 30 feet that can hear must make DC 13 CON save. On failure, drop to 0 HP. On success, take 10 (3d6) psychic damage. Recharges on a rest of any kind.
