A brutish Riftborn giant-kin whose ancestors tumbled through the Rift generations ago. Rift-ogres are stronger and crueler than natural ogres, their flesh permanently mottled with patches of violet pigmentation. They gather in loose bands in the badlands south of Thornwall, raiding settlements in numbers.
