The smallest of the Verdant Margin's mobile horrors — a bundle of animated twigs and thorns no larger than a housecat. Individually weak, they travel in swarms that strip flesh from bone in seconds. They are thought to be the Margin's way of recycling dead wood into something useful.
## Traits
**False Appearance.** While motionless, the verdant twig is indistinguishable from a dead shrub.

## Actions
**Claws.** +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 3 (1d4 + 1) piercing.

## Appearance and artwork

One low cat-sized bundle of dry brown twigs and thorns with four crooked branch-legs; the front pair end in three hooked twig points each. A loose central basket-knot, sparse dry leaves and fine offshoots retain dead-shrub concealment at rest. Small nested branch forks carry restrained shaded colour; there is no face or eye.
