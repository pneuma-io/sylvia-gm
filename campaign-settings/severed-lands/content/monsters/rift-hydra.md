A Rift-touched hydra whose regenerative heads have been accelerated by Rift exposure to regenerate almost instantly. Each severed head regrows as two — but the new heads are increasingly warped, developing configurations of eyes and mouths that seem to belong to different creatures entirely, as if the Rift is splicing random life-forms into the hydra.
## Traits
**Multiple Heads.** The rift hydra has five heads. While it has more than one head, advantage on saves against blinded, charmed, deafened, frightened, stunned, and knocked unconscious. Whenever the rift hydra takes 25+ damage in a single turn, one of its heads dies. At the end of its turn, it grows two heads for each head that died since its last turn, unless it has taken fire damage. The rift hydra regains 10 HP per head grown.
**Reactive Heads.** For each head beyond one, the rift hydra gets an extra reaction that can be used only for opportunity attacks.
**Wakeful.** While the rift hydra sleeps, at least one of its heads is awake.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** As many bite attacks as it has heads.
**Bite.** +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., 10 (1d10 + 5) piercing.
