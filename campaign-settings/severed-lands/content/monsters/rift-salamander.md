A serpentine Rift manifestation of heat and hunger, its body a coil of violet-white fire with a core of cooling Anchor-stone. Rift salamanders lair in the geothermal vents beneath the Cairnfields, where the world's natural heat mingles with residual Veil discharge from the deep Rift veins.
## Traits
**Heated Body.** A creature that touches the rift salamander or hits it with a melee attack within 5 feet takes 7 (2d6) fire.
**Heated Weapons.** Any metal melee weapon the rift salamander wields deals an extra 3 (1d6) fire damage.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Spear + tail.
**Spear.** +7 to hit, 11 (2d6 + 4) piercing + 3 (1d6) fire.
**Tail.** +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning + 7 (2d6) fire. Grappled (escape DC 14). Restrained until grapple ends. Can only grapple one creature.
