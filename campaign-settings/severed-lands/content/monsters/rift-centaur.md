A centaur whose herd was caught in a Rift surge that fused them partially with the Veil. Rift-centaurs can briefly phase between physical and Veil-touched states, allowing them to charge through solid obstacles. They are fiercely territorial, guarding the grasslands between Thornwall and the Verdant Margin against all intruders.
## Traits
**Charge.** If moves 30 ft. straight and hits with pike, +10 (3d6) piercing.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Two melee attacks, or pike + hooves.
**Pike.** +6 to hit, reach 10 ft., 9 (1d10 + 4) piercing.
**Hooves.** +6 to hit, 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning.
**Longbow.** +4 to hit, range 150/600 ft., 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing.
