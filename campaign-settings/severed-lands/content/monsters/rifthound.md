A Rift-touched canine that hunts in the margins between settled lands and Rift-scarred wilderness. Rifthounds are not Echoes — they are living creatures warped by Rift exposure, their senses attuned to Veil energy. They can track a creature's Rift-signature for days and are used by Riftborn as hunting beasts and guards.
## Traits
**Shapechanger.** Polymorph into a specific Medium human or a jackal-humanoid hybrid, or back.
**Keen Hearing and Smell.** Advantage on Perception relying on hearing or smell.
**Pack Tactics.** Advantage if ally within 5 feet.

## Actions
**Bite.** +4 to hit, 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing.
**Scimitar.** +4 to hit, 5 (1d6 + 2) slashing.
**Sleep Gaze.** One creature within 30 feet must succeed on DC 10 WIS save or fall unconscious for 10 minutes or until damaged. Immune for 24 hours on success.
