A Rift-touched fungal sentinel that grows in clusters near Anchor Door fragments and dormant Rift pools. When it detects movement or vibration, it emits a piercing psychic shriek that echoes across both physical space and the Veil itself — alerting everything within a mile. Warden constructs seem to ignore them entirely.
## Traits
**False Appearance.** Indistinguishable from an ordinary fungus.

## Actions
**Shriek.** When bright light or a creature is within 30 feet, the rift shrieker emits a piercing shriek audible up to 300 feet. Continues until the disturbance moves out of range and for 1d4 rounds after.
