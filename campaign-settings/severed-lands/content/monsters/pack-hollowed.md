A Hollowed that hunts in coordinated packs, their Rift-twisted minds linked by a shared exposure event. Pack Hollowed were once a single community — a hamlet, a caravan, a patrol — exposed together and transformed into a collective predator. They communicate through a crude psychic bond, coordinating ambushes with terrifying precision.
## Traits
**Rampage.** When the gnoll reduces a creature to 0 HP with a melee attack on its turn, it can take a bonus action to move up to half its speed and make a bite attack.

## Actions
**Bite.** +4 to hit, 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing.
**Spear.** +4 to hit, 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing, or 6 (1d8 + 2) two-handed.
**Longbow.** +3 to hit, range 150/600 ft., 5 (1d8 + 1) piercing.
