A Rift Echo of hatred — the death-imprint of a creature consumed by spite and malice, its final moments so venomous they crystallized in the Veil. Wraiths drain life not to feed but to make others share their death, spreading the cold emptiness at their core to everything they touch. They rise most often from the mass graves at the edges of the Hollows.
## Traits
**Incorporeal Movement.** Move through creatures/objects as difficult terrain.
**Sunlight Sensitivity.** Disadvantage in sunlight.

## Actions
**Life Drain.** +6 to hit, 21 (4d8 + 3) necrotic. DC 14 CON save or max HP reduced by damage until long rest. Humanoid slain rises as rift specter at next dusk under rift wraith's control.
**Create rift specter.** Target a humanoid within 10 feet that died violently and has been dead no more than 1 minute. Its spirit rises as a rift specter under the rift wraith's control.

## Appearance and artwork

A hovering faceless shadow apparition with one narrow head-like mass, two long arm-like extensions ending in five tapering finger wisps each, and a continuous ragged smoke base without physical feet. Nested charcoal and blue-black shadow planes recede within the same connected form, with dim cold-violet depth in chest and forearms.
