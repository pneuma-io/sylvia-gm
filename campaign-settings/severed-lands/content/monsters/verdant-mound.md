A shambling heap of compost and Rift-soaked vegetation that rises from the floor of the Verdant Margin. It is not truly alive in any conventional sense — more like the forest floor learning to walk. It engulfs invaders and breaks them down into more of itself.
## Traits
**Lightning Absorption.** When subjected to lightning damage, takes no damage and regains HP equal to the lightning damage dealt.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Two slam attacks. If both hit a Medium or smaller target, the target is grappled (escape DC 14) and the verdant mound uses its Engulf on it.
**Slam.** +7 to hit, 13 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning.
**Engulf.** The verdant mound engulfs a Medium or smaller creature grappled by it. The engulfed target is blinded, restrained, and unable to breathe, and it must succeed on a DC 14 CON save at the start of each of the mound's turns or take 13 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage. If the mound moves, the engulfed target moves with it. The mound can have only one creature engulfed at a time.

## Appearance and artwork

A faceless broad compost-and-vegetation mound default with two heavy root arms, two short supporting root columns and one continuous hunched mass. Dark earth, moss, leaf litter, roots and fern fans contain nested green-violet growth corridors; a small cream shelf-fungus cluster belongs to the anatomical right shoulder. There is no separate head, eye, mouth, victim or new item. Authored engulf and lightning traits remain unchanged.
