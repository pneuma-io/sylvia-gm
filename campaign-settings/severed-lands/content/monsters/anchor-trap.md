A defensive mechanism installed around Anchor Doors by the Wardens — a construct of interlocking stone rings and rune-plates that activates when unauthorized creatures approach. It doesn't kill; it imprisons, encasing intruders in a shell of magically hardened stone until a Warden overseer could deal with them. There are no Warden overseers left.
## Traits
**Antimagic Susceptibility.** Incapacitated while in an antimagic field. If targeted by dispel magic, must succeed on a Con save against caster's DC or fall unconscious for 1 minute.
**Damage Transfer.** While grappling a creature, takes half damage dealt to it; grappled creature takes the other half.
**False Appearance.** While motionless, indistinguishable from a normal rug.

## Actions
**Smother.** +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one Medium or smaller target. Grappled (escape DC 13). While grappled, target is restrained, blinded, and at risk of suffocating. Rug can smother one at a time.
**Squeeze.** When smothering, bonus action: 10 (2d6 + 3) bludgeoning to grappled creature.
