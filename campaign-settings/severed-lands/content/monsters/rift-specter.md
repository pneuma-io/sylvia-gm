A Rift Echo of betrayal — formed when someone was murdered by those they trusted, their dying shock captured by a nearby Rift pool. Specters are drawn to communities, where they seek out relationships to poison. They cannot be reasoned with; they exist only to replicate the betrayal that created them.
## Traits
**Incorporeal Movement.** Can move through creatures and objects as difficult terrain. Takes 1d10 force if ends turn inside an object.
**Sunlight Sensitivity.** Disadvantage on attacks and Perception in sunlight.

## Actions
**Life Drain.** +4 to hit, 10 (3d6) necrotic. DC 10 CON save or max HP reduced by damage taken until long rest. Dies if max HP reaches 0.
