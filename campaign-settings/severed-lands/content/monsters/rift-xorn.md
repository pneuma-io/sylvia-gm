A Rift-touched xorn — a trilateral elemental predator whose affinity for precious metals has been corrupted by Rift exposure. It now seeks not just gems and metals but crystallized Veil-stuff, which it senses as more precious than any mundane mineral. Xorns burrow through the Cairnfields, creating the unstable sinkholes that give the region its name.
## Traits
**Earth Glide.** Can burrow through nonmagical, unworked earth and stone without disturbing it.
**Stone Camouflage.** Advantage on Stealth checks in rocky terrain.
**Treasure Sense.** Can pinpoint the location of precious metals, stones, and gems within 60 feet.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Three claw attacks and one bite.
**Bite.** +6 to hit, 13 (3d6 + 3) piercing.
**Claw.** +6 to hit, 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing.

## Appearance and artwork

A squat trilateral barrel-bodied stone elemental with three radial arms, three tripod legs and one upward-facing mouth at the top. Broad hands and feet end in blunt stone claws, and each radial side carries one small amber eye. Warm dark basalt and ochre plates contain smoky quartz, copper veins and nested geode shelves with restrained violet-green depth.
