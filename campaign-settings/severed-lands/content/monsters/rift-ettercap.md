A Rift-touched spider-herder whose bond with arachnids has been twisted into a psychic hive-link. Ettercaps in the Severed Lands do not simply tend spiders — they merge with them, their bodies riddled with Rift-warped webbing that pulses in unison with nearby spider swarms.
## Traits
**Spider Climb.** Can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down.
**Web Sense.** While in contact with a web, knows the location of any other creature in contact with the same web.
**Web Walker.** Ignores movement restrictions caused by webbing.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Bite + claws.
**Bite.** +4 to hit, 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing + 4 (1d8) poison. DC 11 CON save for half.
**Claws.** +4 to hit, 7 (2d4 + 2) slashing.
**Web.** Ranged Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, range 30/60 ft., Large or smaller creature. Restrained. DC 11 STR check or deal 5 slashing (AC 10) to escape. Recharges 5-6.
