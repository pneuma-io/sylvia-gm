A Rift-touched worm-like ambush predator that lurks in the ruins of Warden structures. Its natural camouflage has been enhanced by Rift exposure, allowing it to blend with ancient stonework so perfectly that even experienced delvers step within reach. Its tentacles leave wounds that fester with lingering Rift-burn.
## Traits
**Stone Camouflage.** Advantage on Stealth checks in rift rocky terrain.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Beak + tentacles.
**Tentacles.** +4 to hit, 9 (2d6 + 2) slashing.
**Beak.** +4 to hit, 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing.

## Appearance and artwork

A thick eyeless olive-grey worm with one tapering tail and four short muscular tentacles arranged around a central hooked ivory-and-charcoal beak. It has no walking legs or wings. Stone-like skin patches and recessed tile-shaped growths fold recursively along the trunk, suggesting impossible mineral depth within living tissue. Restrained violet and blue-green reflections sit inside those seams; the outer worm silhouette remains coherent.
