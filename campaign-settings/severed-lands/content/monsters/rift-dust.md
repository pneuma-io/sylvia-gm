A choking, sentient dust storm that accumulates where the Rift has ground stone to fine particles over centuries. It is the most patient of the Rift elementals — it settles over ruins for decades, then rises as one when a living creature disturbs its rest. Those who inhale it find it colonizing their lungs with grains of Veil-tainted sand.
## Traits
**Death Burst.** When dies, explodes in a burst of dust. Each creature within 5 feet must succeed on DC 10 CON save or be blinded for 1 minute. Can repeat save at end of each turn.

## Actions
**Claws.** +4 to hit, 4 (1d4 + 2) slashing.
**Blinding Breath.** 15-foot cone, DC 10 DEX save or blinded for 1 minute. Recharges 6.
