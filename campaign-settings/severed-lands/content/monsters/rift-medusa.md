A Rift-touched medusa whose serpentine hair is shot through with writhing Veil-energy instead of mere snakes. Her petrifying gaze does not simply turn flesh to stone — it pulls victims partway into the Veil, leaving them as permanent statues that somehow still scream silently when the Rift surges.
## Traits
**Petrifying Gaze.** When a creature that can see the rift medusa's eyes starts its turn within 30 feet, the rift medusa can force it to make a DC 14 Constitution saving throw if the rift medusa isn't incapacitated and can see the creature. If the saving throw fails by 5 or more, the creature is instantly petrified. Otherwise, if it fails by less than 5, the creature begins to turn to stone and is restrained. The restrained creature must repeat the save at the end of its next turn, becoming petrified on a failure or ending the effect on a success. A creature can avert its eyes, but if it does, it can't see the rift medusa until the start of its next turn.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Snake hair + shortsword, or longbow.
**Snake Hair.** +5 to hit, 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing + 14 (4d6) poison.
**Shortsword.** +5 to hit, 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing.
