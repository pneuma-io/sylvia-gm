A minor construct left behind by the Wardens to watch over a dormant Anchor Door fragment. Unlike greater Warden constructs, these sentinels were designed to observe rather than engage. Centuries of isolation and Rift exposure have degraded their protocols, and many now attack anything that approaches their charge.
## Actions
**Bite.** +1 to hit, 2 (1d6 - 1) piercing.
**Eye Rays.** The sentinel shoots up to two of the following magical eye rays at one or two creatures within 90 feet: 1. Confusion Ray (WIS save or can't take reactions, roll d8 for behavior). 2. Paralyzing Ray (CON save or paralyzed 1 min). 3. Fear Ray (WIS save or frightened 1 min). 4. Wounding Ray (CON save or 16 (3d10) necrotic). Recharges 5-6.
**Create Food and Water.** The sentinel magically creates enough food and water to sustain one Medium creature for 24 hours.

## Appearance and artwork

A Medium hovering orb of weathered dark stone and dull iron plates, with one large amber slit-pupil eye, four smaller amber perimeter eyes, a compact toothed split jaw, narrow pale-blue luminous seams, worn iron braces and age cracks. It has no humanoid limbs or clothing.
