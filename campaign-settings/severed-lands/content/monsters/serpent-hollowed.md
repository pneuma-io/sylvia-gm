A Hollowed whose body has elongated and flattened through prolonged Rift exposure, becoming serpentine. Serpent Hollowed slither through the rubble of the Hollows, constricting prey with coils that shimmer with Veil-light. They retain a cruel, patient intelligence that most other Hollowed lack.
## Traits
**Magic Resistance.** Advantage on saves against spells.
**Innate Spellcasting.** CHA (DC 12). At will: animal friendship (snakes only). 3/day: poison spray. 1/day: suggestion.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Two scimitar attacks.
**Scimitar.** +3 to hit, 4 (1d6 + 1) slashing.
**Shortbow.** +3 to hit, range 80/320 ft., 4 (1d6 + 1) piercing + 7 (2d6) poison.
