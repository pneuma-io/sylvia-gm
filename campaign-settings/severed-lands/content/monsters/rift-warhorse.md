A horse that fell through the Rift from a world of endless cavalry charges and was transformed by the passage. Its coat shimmers with Veil-light, and its hooves leave scorch marks on stone. Rift-warhorses are prized by Riftborn warlords and occasionally captured and broken by Returner cavalry — though the process has broken more riders than horses.
## Actions
**Hooves.** +6 to hit, 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning.

## Appearance and artwork

A powerful intact horse-shaped undead with a deep blue-grey coat, restrained violet-green Veil shimmer, pale silver-grey eyes, dark muzzle, four dark hooves and heavy mane and tail. Rejoining nested branches within the mane, tail and coat grain create subtle impossible folds while preserving one complete horse. Battered unmarked iron barding scraps cover the neck and breast on brown leather straps. No rider, saddle or faction identity is assigned; there are no exposed bones.
