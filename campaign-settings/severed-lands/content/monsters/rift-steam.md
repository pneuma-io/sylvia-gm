A scalding cloud formed where Rift water meets extreme heat — geothermal vents, wildfires, or the aftermath of Warden forge-spells. It scalds flesh and clouds vision, and within its billows one can sometimes glimpse the silhouettes of creatures falling endlessly through the Veil.
## Traits
**Death Burst.** Explodes in steam. 5-foot radius, DC 10 DEX save or 4 (1d8) fire.

## Actions
**Claws.** +2 to hit, 2 (1d4) slashing + 2 (1d4) fire.
**Steam Breath.** 15-foot cone, DC 10 DEX, 4 (1d8) fire. Recharges 6.
