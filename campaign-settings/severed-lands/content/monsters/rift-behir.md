A massive, many-legged serpent that crawled through the Rift from a storm-wracked world. Its lightning breath is tinged with violet Rift-energy and leaves afterimages burned into the retina. Behir lairs are invariably near dormant Anchor Doors, as if the creatures are drawn to the lingering Warden architecture.
## Actions
**Multiattack.** Bite + constrict.
**Bite.** +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., 22 (3d10 + 6) piercing.
**Constrict.** +10 to hit, reach 5 ft., 17 (2d10 + 6) bludgeoning + 17 (5d6) lightning. Large or smaller grappled (escape DC 16). Restrained until grapple ends. Can only constrict one creature at a time.
**Lightning Breath.** 20-foot line, DC 16 DEX, 66 (12d10) lightning (half on success). Recharges 5-6.
**Swallow.** Bite attack against Medium or smaller grappled creature. Swallowed: blinded, restrained, 42 (12d6) acid at start of each rift behir turn. If rift behir takes 30+ damage from inside in a single turn, DC 20 CON save or regurgitate.

## Appearance and artwork

A long muscular storm-blue serpent with six pairs of short bent clawed legs, one horned saurian head and one tapering tail. Pale blue-grey belly plates, two short swept-back horns and narrow lilac eyes. Overlapping scales form nested wave bands with restrained cyan-violet misregistration along selected ridges, suggesting an afterimage within the material rather than a second body. Its mouth holds a faint contained violet gleam. The chosen twelve-leg anatomy is a visual default, not a new gameplay trait.
