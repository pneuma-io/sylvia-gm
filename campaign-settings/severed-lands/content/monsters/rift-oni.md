A shapechanging Riftborn from a world of spirits and hungry gods. The oni fell through the Rift during a great exorcism that went catastrophically wrong, banishing the spirit instead of the demon. It walks the Severed Lands in stolen forms, savoring the fear of mortals who have never known creatures like it.
## Traits
**Innate Spellcasting.** The rift oni's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 13). It can cast: at will — *darkness*, *invisibility*; 1/day — *charm person*, *cone of cold*, *gaseous form*, *sleep*.
**Magic Weapons.** The rift oni's weapon attacks are magical.
**Regeneration.** The rift oni regains 10 hp at the start of its turn if it has at least 1 hp.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** The rift oni makes two attacks, either with its claws or its glaive.
**Claw (rift oni Form Only).** +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 8 (1d8 + 4) slashing.
**Glaive.** +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 15 (2d10 + 4) slashing, or 9 (1d10 + 4) in Small/Medium form.
**Change Shape.** The rift oni magically polymorphs into a Small or Medium humanoid, a Large giant, or back to true form. Stats unchanged except size. Equipment not transformed.
