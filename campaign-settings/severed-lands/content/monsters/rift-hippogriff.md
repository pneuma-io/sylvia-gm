A Rift-touched hippogriff whose flight feathers shimmer with trapped Veil-light. Unlike griffons, hippogriffs can sometimes be tamed — a few Returner cavalry units ride Rift-touched hippogriffs, though the bonding process requires a dangerous Veil-attunement ritual that not all survive.
