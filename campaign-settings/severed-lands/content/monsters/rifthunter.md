A Riftborn humanoid that lives only to hunt — it fell from a world where the hunt was sacred and has turned the Severed Lands into its new hunting ground. Rifthunters stalk the most dangerous prey available, collecting trophies they offer to crude effigies of their dead world's gods at Rift-pool shrines.
## Traits
**Blood Frenzy.** Advantage on melee attacks against creatures without all HP.
**Limited Amphibiousness.** Can breathe air and water, but needs to be submerged at least once every 4 hours to avoid suffocation.
**Shark Telepathy.** Can magically command any shark within 120 feet.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Bite + claws or spear.
**Bite.** +3 to hit, 3 (1d4 + 1) piercing.
**Claws.** +3 to hit, 3 (1d4 + 1) slashing.
**Spear.** +3 to hit, 4 (1d6 + 1) piercing.

## Appearance and artwork

An adult fish-like humanoid default with deep teal/olive scales, wedge-shaped head, amber eyes, neck gills, swept-back dorsal crest and one finned tail. Two arms and legs retain webbed clawed hands and broad feet. Nested fin membranes and shoulder scales create green-violet internal depth. A plain woven waist-wrap and fiber straps accompany a right-hand spear and empty left hand. No named original world, deity or personal identity is assigned.
