A Rift-touched merrow — an aquatic ogre-kin adapted to the Severed Lands' Rift-tainted waterways. Its gills filter Veil energy along with oxygen, giving it a violet luminescence visible even in murky water. Merrows lair near submerged Anchor Doors, guarding them with the same ferocity as their surface-dwelling kin.
## Traits
**Amphibious.** The rift merrow can breathe air and water.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** The rift merrow makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its claws or harpoon.
**Bite.** +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing.
**Claws.** +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 9 (2d4 + 4) slashing.
**Harpoon.** +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. 11 (2d6 + 4) piercing. If thrown, target must make DC 14 Str save or pulled 20 ft. toward rift merrow.
