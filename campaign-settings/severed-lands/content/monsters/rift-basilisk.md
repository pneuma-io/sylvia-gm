A Rift-touched reptile whose petrifying gaze channels the Veil's boundary-locking property — it temporarily pulls victims partway into the Veil, freezing them in stasis. True basilisks were rare in the Severed Lands before the Rift, but the Rift-touched variant has multiplied rapidly throughout the Cairnfields.
## Traits
**Petrifying Gaze.** If a creature starts its turn within 30 feet and both can see each other, the rift basilisk can force a DC 12 CON save. On failure by 5+, instantly petrified. On lesser failure, restrained and repeats save at end of next turn.

## Actions
**Bite.** +5 to hit, 10 (2d6 + 3) piercing + 7 (2d6) poison.

## Appearance and artwork

A squat eight-legged reptile with four pairs of short stout clawed legs, a low continuous lizard torso, one broad blunt head and a heavy tapered tail. Moss-olive and slate scales contrast with a dull ochre underside. Two amber eyes have nested angular gold-violet iris structure beneath a heavy brow. Stepped keratin chevrons along neck and spine give the living growth a recursive rhythm. This is a living reptile; its gaze ability is not represented by an added beam or power.
