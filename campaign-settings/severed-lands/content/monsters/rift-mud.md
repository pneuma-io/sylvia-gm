A sluggish but relentless elemental formed where Rift pools saturate soil after heavy rains. It swallows travelers whole, dragging them into its viscous body where they suffocate in absolute darkness. The mud preserves their bodies perfectly — some Rift-mud elementals contain dozens of perfectly mummified victims suspended in their depths.
## Traits
**Death Burst.** When the mephit dies, it explodes in a burst of sticky mud. Each creature within 5 ft. must succeed on a DC 11 Dexterity saving throw or be restrained. A creature can break free with a DC 11 Strength check as an action.
**False Appearance.** While motionless, the mephit is indistinguishable from an ordinary mound of mud.

## Actions
**Fists.** +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 4 (1d6 + 1) bludgeoning.
**Mud Breath (Recharge 6).** 15-ft. cone. DC 11 Dex save or restrained 1 min. Repeat save each turn.
