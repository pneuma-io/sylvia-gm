The most common Rift-born seepage: raw, violet-tinged protoplasm that pools where the Veil has thinned. It shimmers with faint Rift-light and instinctively flows toward living warmth. Those who have survived its touch describe a sensation of being unmade — their flesh dissolving not just physically, but as if erased from memory.
## Traits
**Amorphous.** Can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch wide without squeezing.
**Corrode Metal.** Any nonmagical weapon made of metal that hits the ooze corrodes, taking a permanent -1 penalty to damage rolls per hit. Destroyed at -5. Nonmagical ammunition made of metal that hits is destroyed after dealing damage. The ooze can eat through 2-inch-thick, nonmagical metal in 1 round.
**False Appearance.** While motionless, indistinguishable from an oily pool or wet rock.

## Actions
**Pseudopod.** +3 to hit, 4 (1d6 + 1) bludgeoning + 7 (2d6) acid. Before dealing damage, nonmagical metal armor worn by the target takes a permanent -1 penalty to its AC. Armor destroyed if penalty reaches -5.
