A Rift-touched rust monster whose metal-consuming ability has been corrupted to also consume the residual magic in Warden alloys. Rift-rust monsters are drawn to ancient Warden sites, where they feed on both the architecture and any equipment unfortunate delvers bring with them. Their antennae glow violet when metal is near.
## Traits
**Iron Scent.** Can pinpoint the location of ferrous metal within 30 feet.
**Rust Metal.** Any nonmagical metal weapon that hits the rift rust monster corrodes, taking a permanent -1 penalty to damage. Destroyed at -5. Nonmagical metal ammunition destroyed. Can eat through 2-inch-thick nonmagical ferrous metal in 1 round.

## Actions
**Bite.** +3 to hit, 5 (1d8 + 1) piercing.
**Antennae.** The rift rust monster corrodes a nonmagical ferrous metal object it can see within 5 feet. If the object isn't being worn or carried, the touch destroys a 1-foot cube of it. If worn or carried, the creature can make a DC 11 DEX save to avoid.
