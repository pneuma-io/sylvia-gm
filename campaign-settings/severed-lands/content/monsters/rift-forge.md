A Rift expression shaped like a dwarf of living bronze and violet flame — not a being from another plane but the Rift's memory of forge-fire and craftsmanship given form. Rift Forges are drawn to Warden ruins, where they obsessively hammer at ancient alloys, trying to recreate something only they remember.
## Traits
**Heated Body.** A creature that touches the rift forge or hits it with a melee attack within 5 feet takes 5 (1d10) fire damage.
**Heated Weapons.** When the rift forge hits with a metal melee weapon, it deals an extra 3 (1d6) fire damage.
**Illumination.** The rift forge sheds bright light in a 10-foot radius and dim light for an additional 10 feet.

## Actions
**Warhammer.** +5 to hit, 7 (1d8 + 3) bludgeoning + 3 (1d6) fire.
