A rat that nested too close to a Rift pool and emerged changed — larger, smarter, carrying a residual Rift charge in every bite. Rifthollowed rats spread through settlements like a plague, their colonies coordinated by a crude hive-mind. Where one is found, a hundred more are lurking in the walls.
## Traits
**Shapechanger.** Can use action to polymorph into a rat-humanoid hybrid or a giant rat, or back. Stats unchanged except size. Equipment not transformed.
**Keen Smell.** Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks relying on smell.

## Actions
**Multiattack (Humanoid or Hybrid Form Only).** Two attacks, only one of which can be a bite.
**Bite (Rat or Hybrid Form Only).** +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing. If humanoid, DC 11 Con save or cursed with rifthollowed rat Rift-touch.
**Shortsword (Humanoid or Hybrid Form Only).** +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing.
**Hand Crossbow (Humanoid or Hybrid Form Only).** +4 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing.

## Appearance and artwork

The selected default shows a lean adult rat-humanoid hybrid with grey-brown fur, lighter muzzle/throat, rounded grey-pink ears, dark brown eyes, fine whiskers and small incisors. Two arms and bent legs retain clawed hands and rat-like feet, with one long bare scaly tail. Cheek, neck and shoulder fur folds into clearly receding nested arches with indigo-green inner planes; a few tail-scale bands repeat the same inward contours. A patched olive waistcoat, plain shirt and dark trousers accompany a lowered right-hand shortsword and a small hand crossbow at the left hip. Equipment remains physical; no former human identity or colony rank is assigned.
