A drifting mass of semi-translucent Rift-stuff that pulses with a sickly violet light, hanging in the air like a malignant jellyfish. It trails tendrils of condensed Veil energy. Scholars at the Waystation theorize it is Rift run-off given buoyancy by trapped elemental air from a world that no longer exists.
## Traits
**Split.** When subjected to lightning or slashing damage, the jelly splits into two with half HP each.
**Spider Climb.** Can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings.

## Actions
**Pseudopod.** +4 to hit, 9 (2d6 + 2) bludgeoning + 3 (1d6) acid.
