A Warden construct programmed to patrol the Cairnfields and eliminate riftbeasts before they can threaten settled lands. It moves on silent stone joints, scanning for Rift signatures with sensors that have not been calibrated since the Wardens fell. Some Field Wardens now mistake Returners for Rift-touched and attack without warning.
## Traits
**False Appearance.** While motionless, indistinguishable from an ordinary inanimate field warden.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Two claw attacks.
**Claw.** +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 6 (2d4 + 1) slashing. DC 11 Wis save or frightened until end of field warden's next turn.
**Terrifying Glare.** One creature within 30 ft. that can see the field warden must make DC 11 Wis save or be frightened 1 minute. Repeat save each turn.
