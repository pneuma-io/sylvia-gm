A cloud giant whose floating citadel fell through the Rift intact, crashing into the mountains east of the Severed Lands. The survivors emerged changed — their innate cloud-magic now tinged with Veil energy, their memories fragmented. They view the Severed Lands with aristocratic contempt, as squatters on what should be theirs.
## Traits
**Keen Smell.** Advantage on Perception relying on smell.
**Innate Spellcasting.** CHA (DC 15). At will: detect magic, fog cloud, light. 3/day: feather fall, fly, misty step, telekinesis. 1/day: control weather, gaseous form.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Two morningstar attacks.
**Morningstar.** +12 to hit, 21 (3d8 + 8) piercing.
**Rock.** +12 to hit, range 60/240 ft., 30 (4d10 + 8) bludgeoning.

## Appearance and artwork

An editable adult Cloud Giant default with slate-grey living skin, a long mature face, silver-grey eyes and ivory curls gathered high then falling behind.
