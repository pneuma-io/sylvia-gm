A cloud giant whose floating citadel fell through the Rift intact, crashing into the mountains east of the Severed Lands. The survivors emerged changed — their innate cloud-magic now tinged with Veil energy, their memories fragmented. They view the Severed Lands with aristocratic contempt, as squatters on what should be theirs.
