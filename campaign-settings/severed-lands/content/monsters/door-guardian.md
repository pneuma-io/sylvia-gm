The greatest of the Warden constructs still active — a colossus built to protect a specific Anchor Door from any who would use or destroy it. Door Guardians were each custom-forged for their assigned Door, their attack patterns reflecting the Warden architect who designed them. They recognize the authority of Warden command-rods, but no such rod has been seen in centuries.
## Traits
**Bound.** The door guardian is magically bound to an amulet. As long as the guardian and its amulet are on the same plane, the wearer can telepathically command it. It knows distance and direction.
**Regeneration.** Regains 10 hp at the start of its turn if it has at least 1 hp.
**Spell Storing.** A spellcaster wearing the amulet can cause the guardian to store one spell of 4th level or lower. The spell has no effect but is stored. When commanded or when a condition is met, the guardian casts it using appropriate slot, spell attack bonus, or save DC. The guardian can store only one spell at a time.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Two fist attacks.
**Fist.** +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning.
**Shield.** When a creature makes an attack against the wearer of the amulet, the guardian grants a +2 bonus to AC if within 5 ft.
