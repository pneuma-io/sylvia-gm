A turbulent column of Rift-saturated water that surges through underground aquifers and emerges without warning. It is not merely water — it carries the memory-taste of oceans from worlds that fell. Drinking from it, even accidentally, floods the mind with alien shorelines and drowning civilizations.
## Traits
**Water Form.** Can enter a hostile creature's space and stop there. Can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without squeezing.
**Freeze.** If the elemental takes cold damage, it partially freezes; its speed is reduced by 20 feet until the end of its next turn.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Two slam attacks.
**Slam.** +7 to hit, 13 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning.
**Whelm.** Each creature in the elemental's space must make a DC 15 STR save. Failure: 13 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning. If Large or smaller, also grappled (escape DC 14). Until grapple ends, target is restrained and unable to breathe unless it can breathe water. If save succeeds, pushed out of elemental's space. Can grapple one Large or up to four Medium or smaller at a time.

## Appearance and artwork

A faceless turbulent azure and turquoise water column rising from a connected dark blue puddle into one broad hollow curling wave crest. Nested transparent water sheets and receding breaking-wave layers create impossible internal depth and abstract shoreline-like refractions. Pale foam traces the folds, with restrained violet-green caustics inside. It has no fixed human or animal face, limbs, weapon or depicted victim.
