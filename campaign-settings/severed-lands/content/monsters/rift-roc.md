A Rift-touched roc — a bird of impossible size whose plumage is streaked with Veil-light. It nests on the highest peaks overlooking the Severed Lands and can blot out the sun with a single wing. Returners tell stories of rocs carrying off entire supply wagons, mistaking them for the giant prey of their forgotten homeworld.
## Traits
**Keen Sight.** Advantage on Perception checks relying on sight.

## Actions

**Talons.** +13 to hit, 23 (4d6 + 9) slashing. Grappled (escape DC 19). Grappled target takes 23 (4d6 + 9) slashing at the start of each rift roc turn.
