A Rift-touched griffon — eagle and lion fused, then further altered by decades of exposure to the Severed Lands. Its feathers carry a faint violet shimmer, and its roar echoes with harmonics that cause vertigo. Rift-griffons nest on the peaks overlooking the Severed Lands, descending to hunt anything larger than a goat.
