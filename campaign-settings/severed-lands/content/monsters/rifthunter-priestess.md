The spiritual leader of a rifthunter band — a shaman whose connection to the Veil allows her to bless hunts with Rift-sight. Priestesses read omens in the patterns of Rift-light on water and invoke the Veil to cloak their hunters from mundane senses. They are the most dangerous of the rifthunters, combining martial prowess with Veil-touched magic.
## Traits
**Blood Frenzy.** Advantage on melee attacks against wounded.
**Limited Amphibiousness.** Must submerge every 4 hours.
**Shark Telepathy.** Commands sharks within 120 feet.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Two melee attacks: one bite and one claw.
**Bite.** +3 to hit, 3 (1d4 + 1) piercing.
**Claws.** +3 to hit, 3 (1d4 + 1) slashing.
