A Rift-touched owlbear — a creature that was already an unnatural hybrid before the Rift and has only become more so. Its feathers and fur are mottled with violet patches, and its screech carries a psychic undertone that disrupts concentration. Rift-owlbears are most common in the forests east of the Verdant Margin.
## Traits

**Keen Sight and Smell.** The rift owlbear has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight or smell.
