A hill giant whose ancestors fell through the Rift from a dying giant-world centuries ago. The Riftborn hill giants make their lairs in the rocky foothills of the Thornwall, raiding outlying farms and ambushing caravans. Generations of Rift exposure have given their skin a faint violet pallor and made them resistant to pain.
