A Rift-touched roper whose stony camouflage has been petrified by Veil exposure into something indistinguishable from Anchor-stone. Its grasping tendrils are barbed with crystallized Rift-shards, and it can remain motionless for decades — some specimens in the Cairnfields may predate the Wardens themselves.
## Traits
**False Appearance.** While motionless, indistinguishable from a normal cave formation, such as a stalagmite.
**Grasping Tendrils.** Can have up to six tendrils at a time. Each tendril can be attacked (AC 20, 10 HP). Destroying a tendril deals no damage. A tendril can also be broken if a creature takes an action and succeeds on a DC 15 Strength check.
**Spider Climb.** Can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Four tendril attacks, uses Reel, and makes one bite.
**Bite.** +7 to hit, 22 (4d8 + 4) piercing.
**Tendril.** +7 to hit, reach 50 ft., one creature. The target is grappled (escape DC 15). Until the grapple ends, the target is restrained and has disadvantage on Strength checks and saves. The rift roper can't use the same tendril on another target.
**Reel.** Pulls each creature grappled by it up to 25 feet straight toward it.
