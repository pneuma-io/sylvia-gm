A Rift Echo of predation — the death-memory of someone drained of life, imprinted with such force that the Echo itself becomes a drainer of life. Rift-vampires do not create spawn through blood; new vampires form when someone dies under a vampire-Echo's life drain near a Rift pool, creating a copy of the predator-imprint in the Veil.
## Traits
**Shapechanger.** Polymorph into Tiny bat, Medium cloud of mist, or back.
**Legendary Resistance.** 3/day.
**Regeneration.** 20 HP at start of turn if has at least 1 HP and isn't in sunlight or running water.
**Spider Climb.** Climb difficult surfaces, including upside down.
**rift vampire Weaknesses.** Takes 20 radiant damage when starting turn in sunlight. Takes 20 acid damage when ending turn in running water. Disadvantage on attacks/abilities if presented with a holy symbol. Can't enter a residence without invitation. Stake through heart destroys if resting in coffin.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Two Unarmed Strike attacks, or one bite and one charm.
**Unarmed Strike.** +9 to hit, 8 (1d8 + 4) bludgeoning. Grappled (escape DC 18) instead of dealing damage.
**Bite.** +9 to hit, 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing + 10 (3d6) necrotic. Target's max HP reduced by necrotic damage taken. Dies if max HP reaches 0. Humanoid slain rises as rift rift vampire spawn at next sunset.
**Charm.** One humanoid within 30 feet must succeed on DC 17 WIS save or charmed for 24 hours. Charmed target is a willing target for the rift vampire's bite.

## Appearance and artwork

A lean adult male-presenting humanoid Echo with pale ash-olive skin, angular face, pale grey eyes and black hair combed back to the nape. A slight predatory smile reveals two upper fangs. An ankle-length charcoal high-collared coat covers a dark burgundy folded tunic, dark trousers and closed boots. Empty long-fingered hands remain visible.
