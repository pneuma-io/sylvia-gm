A Rift-touched shapeshifter that fell through the Veil from a world where identity was currency. Its ability to read thoughts and assume forms has been corrupted — it now copies not just appearances but fragments of personality, sometimes losing itself in the roles it plays. Doppelgangers infiltrate settlements throughout the Severed Lands, replacing key figures for reasons they no longer remember.
## Traits
**Shapechanger.** Use action to polymorph into Small or Medium humanoid, or back.
**Ambusher.** Advantage on attack rolls against any creature it has surprised.
