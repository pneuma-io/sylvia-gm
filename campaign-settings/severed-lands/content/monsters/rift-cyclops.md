A one-eyed Riftborn giant whose single eye sees into the Veil itself. Cyclopes were the first giant-kin to fall through the Rift, and the experience broke something in them — they mutter prophecies in their dead language and see omens in the patterns of Rift-light on water. Some Returners seek them out as oracles, though the price is always steep.
## Traits
**Poor Depth Perception.** The rift cyclops has disadvantage on any attack roll against a target more than 30 ft. away.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** The rift cyclops makes two greatclub attacks.
**Greatclub.** +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. 19 (3d8 + 6) bludgeoning.
**Rock.** +9 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. 28 (4d10 + 6) bludgeoning.
