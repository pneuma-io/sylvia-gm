Not all Hollowed regress to animal instinct. Some remember — fragments of military training, discipline from a home world they can't return to. Hollowed Enforcers organize the broken ones into something resembling units, hold territory in the Hollows, and fight with tactics that feel eerily professional. They're the closest thing the Hollowed have to leaders, and they remember enough of war to make them dangerous.

## Traits

**Martial Advantage.** Once per turn, the hobgoblin can deal an extra 7 (2d6) damage to a creature it hits with a weapon attack if that creature is within 5 feet of an ally of the hobgoblin that isn't incapacitated.
