A Hollowed formed from someone who fell into a Rift pool and was not consumed but transformed. Pond Hollowed are perpetually wet, their flesh spongy and translucent, dripping Rift-water from every pore. They lurk in and around bodies of water throughout the Severed Lands, dragging victims down to share their drowning-transformation.
## Traits
**Amphibious.** Breathes air and water.
**Speak with Frogs and Toads.** Can communicate simple concepts to frogs and toads.
**Standing Leap.** Long jump 20 ft., high jump 10 ft.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Two melee attacks: one bite and one spear.
**Bite.** +3 to hit, 3 (1d4 + 1) piercing.
**Spear.** +3 to hit, 4 (1d6 + 1) piercing.
