Born from a deep, stagnant Rift pool where the dimensional wound's energy has thickened into a corrosive sludge. It slithers across the landscape, dissolving everything in its path and reconstituting its form from the dissolved matter. Older puddings develop a cruel, alien intelligence.
## Traits
**Amorphous.** Can move through 1-inch-wide spaces.
**Corrosive Form.** Creature touching the pudding or hitting it with melee within 5 ft. takes 4 (1d8) acid. Nonmagical metal weapons corrode (-1 damage, destroyed at -5). Nonmagical ammunition destroyed. Can eat through 2-inch nonmagical metal or wood in 1 round.
**Spider Climb.** Climb difficult surfaces.

## Actions
**Pseudopod.** +5 to hit, 6 (1d6 + 3) bludgeoning + 18 (4d8) acid. Nonmagical armor worn takes -1 AC, destroyed at -5.
