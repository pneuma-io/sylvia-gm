A Rift-touched predator that hunts by sensing the Veil-residue in its prey — every living thing in the Severed Lands carries a faint Rift signature, and stalkers can follow that trail across any terrain. They are known to track prey for days, waiting for the perfect moment to strike. Nothing that enters a stalker's hunting ground leaves without its permission.
## Traits
**Invisibility.** The stalker is invisible.
**Faultless Tracker.** Always knows the direction and distance to its quarry, provided both are on the same plane.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Two slam attacks.
**Slam.** +6 to hit, 10 (2d6 + 3) bludgeoning.
