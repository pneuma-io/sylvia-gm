The Elder Riftbeast — a living cataclysm born from the Rift itself, or perhaps the first and oldest thing to fall through it. No one knows its origin; only that when it stirs, even the Rift pools grow still and the Anchor Doors tremble. It is the Severed Lands' ultimate predator, devouring everything in its path with the same indifference as the dimensional wound that birthed it.
## Traits
**Legendary Resistance.** 3/day.
**Magic Resistance.** Advantage on saves against spells.
**Reflective Carapace.** Immune to magic missile and similar line effects. On a ranged spell attack or effect that requires a roll, roll a d6. On a 6, the tarrasque is unaffected and the effect is reflected back at the caster.
**Siege Monster.** Double damage to objects and structures.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Frightful Presence + bite + two claws + horns + tail.
**Bite.** +19 to hit, reach 10 ft., 36 (4d12 + 10) piercing. If the target is a creature, it is grappled (escape DC 20). Grappled creature takes 56 (16d6) acid at the start of each tarrasque turn.
**Claw.** +19 to hit, reach 15 ft., 28 (4d8 + 10) slashing.
**Horns.** +19 to hit, reach 10 ft., 32 (4d10 + 10) piercing.
**Tail.** +19 to hit, reach 20 ft., 24 (4d6 + 10) bludgeoning. DC 20 STR save or knocked prone.
**Frightful Presence.** 120 ft., DC 17 WIS save or frightened 1 minute.
