A boar transformed by Rift exposure into a bristling engine of destruction. Its tusks are veined with crystallized Veil-stuff, and its charge leaves a trail of scorched earth. Rifthollowed boars are indiscriminately aggressive — they attack Riftborn, Returners, and even Anchor Door constructs with equal fury.
## Traits
**Shapechanger.** Can use action to polymorph into a boar-humanoid hybrid or a boar, or back. Stats unchanged except size and speed.
**Charge (Boar or Hybrid Form Only).** If moves 15+ ft. straight and hits with tusks, extra 7 (2d6) slashing. DC 13 Str save or knocked prone.
**Relentless (Recharges after Short or Long Rest).** If it takes 14 damage or less that would reduce it to 0 hp, reduced to 1 hp instead.

## Actions
**Multiattack (Humanoid or Hybrid Form Only).** Two attacks, only one can be a tusks attack.
**Maul (Humanoid or Hybrid Form Only).** +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 10 (2d6 + 3) bludgeoning.
**Tusks (Boar or Hybrid Form Only).** +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 10 (2d6 + 3) slashing. If humanoid, DC 12 Con save or cursed with rifthollowed boar Rift-touch.

## Appearance and artwork

The selected default is a barrel-chested boar-humanoid hybrid with charcoal/russet bristles, long dark snout, amber eyes, two triangular ears and two ivory tusks veined with opalescent violet Veil mineral. Two strong arms, two cloven-hoofed legs and one short curled tail retain a coherent body. Nested chevron bristles recur along the back. Plain wrap-trousers cover the hips; a heavy iron-headed maul is held in the right hand while the left is empty. Other authored forms and abilities are unchanged; no individual human identity is assigned.
