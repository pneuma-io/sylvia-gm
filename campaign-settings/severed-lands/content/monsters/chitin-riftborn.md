A Riftborn creature whose body is encased in violet-tinged chitin plates grown from crystallized Veil-stuff. It is insectile in form but clearly intelligent, tapping its mandibles in rhythmic patterns that some scholars believe is a language. Chitin Riftborn build hive-structures from Rift-fused stone in the outskirts of the Hollows.
## Traits
**Chameleon Carapace.** Advantage on Stealth checks.
**Standing Leap.** Long jump up to 30 feet, high jump up to 15 feet.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Two gythka attacks or two chatkcha attacks.
**Gythka.** +3 to hit, 5 (1d8 + 1) slashing. If holding two-handed, additional 1d8.
**Chatkcha.** Ranged Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, range 30/120 ft., 5 (1d6 + 2) slashing.
