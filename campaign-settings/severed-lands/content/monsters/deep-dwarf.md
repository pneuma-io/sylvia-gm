A dwarf whose clan delved too deep and broke into a sealed Rift chamber buried beneath the Cairnfields. The Rift exposure twisted them — physically into gaunt, pale shadows of dwarvenkind, mentally into obsessive guardians of the chambers they now call home. Deep dwarves forge weapons from Warden alloys they mine from the surrounding ruins.
## Traits
**Deep dwarves Resilience.** Advantage on saves against illusions and against being charmed or paralyzed.
**Sunlight Sensitivity.** Disadvantage on attacks and Perception while in sunlight.

## Actions
**Enlarge.** For 1 minute, the duergar magically increases in size to Large, along with its equipment. While Large, advantage on STR checks and saves, and its weapons deal 1d4 extra damage. Recharges after short or long rest.
**War Pick.** +4 to hit, 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing.
**Javelin.** +4 to hit, range 30/120 ft., 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing.
