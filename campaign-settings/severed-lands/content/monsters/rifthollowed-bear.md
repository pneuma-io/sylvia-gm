A bear Hollowed by concentrated Rift exposure that burned away its animal nature and left something else in its place. Its fur is patchy, revealing violet-veined flesh beneath, and its roar carries a psychic undertone that freezes prey in place. Rifthollowed bears are solitary — too unstable even for others of their kind.
## Traits
**Shapechanger.** Can use action to polymorph into a Large bear-humanoid hybrid or a Large bear, or back. Stats unchanged except size.
**Keen Smell.** Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks relying on smell.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** In bear form: one bite and one claw. In hybrid: two battleaxe and one bite; or one greataxe and one bite.
**Bite (Bear or Hybrid Form Only).** +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 15 (2d10 + 4) piercing. If humanoid and Medium or smaller, DC 14 Con save or cursed with rifthollowed bear Rift-touch.
**Claw (Bear Form Only).** +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 13 (2d8 + 4) slashing.
**Greataxe (Humanoid or Hybrid Form Only).** +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 10 (1d12 + 4) slashing.
**Battleaxe (Humanoid or Hybrid Form Only).** +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. 8 (1d8 + 4) slashing.

## Appearance and artwork

The selected default depicts a Large bear-humanoid hybrid with a broad blunt brown-bear head, small round ears, copper-brown eyes and heavy dark-brown fur. Patchy shoulder, arm and flank fur exposes intact grey-brown skin with fine violet veins and nested inward-folding growth patterns. Two arms and two heavy legs retain broad clawed hands and bear-like feet. Patched canvas trousers cover the hips; the right hand holds a plain greataxe and the left remains empty. This is a hybrid reference, not a new human identity or a replacement for the other authored forms.
