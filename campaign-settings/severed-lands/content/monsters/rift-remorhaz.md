A Rift-touched remorhaz — a polar horror whose internal heat has been fused with Rift energy, making its body temperature a paradox of searing cold and freezing heat simultaneously. Its carapace glows violet-hot, and its mandibles crackle with Veil discharge. Remorhaz lairs are surrounded by fused glass from melted stone and snow.
## Traits
**Heated Body.** A creature that touches the rift remorhaz or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet takes 10 (3d6) fire damage.

## Actions
**Bite.** +11 to hit, reach 10 ft., 40 (6d10 + 7) piercing + 10 (3d6) fire. If Medium or smaller, grappled (escape DC 17). Grappled target takes 21 (6d6) fire at start of each of the rift remorhaz's turns.
**Swallow.** The rift remorhaz makes one bite attack against a Medium or smaller creature it is grappling. If the attack hits, the target is swallowed: blinded, restrained, and takes 21 (6d6) acid + 21 (6d6) fire at start of each rift remorhaz turn. If takes 30+ damage from inside on a single turn, the rift remorhaz must succeed on DC 20 CON save or regurgitate all swallowed creatures.
