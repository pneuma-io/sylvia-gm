A Rift-touched hound that hunts not by scent but by sensing the Rift-signature of living creatures. Its baying can be heard across the Veil boundary, causing disorientation in those sensitive to dimensional instability. Death hounds are favored by rift-vampires and other intelligent Echoes as hunting companions.
## Traits
**Two-Headed.** Advantage on Perception and on saves against blinded, charmed, deafened, frightened, stunned, or knocked unconscious.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** Two bites.
**Bite.** +4 to hit, 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing. DC 12 CON save or 7 (2d6) poison, half on success. If failed by 5+, max HP reduced by damage taken. Lasts until disease cured or long rest.

## Appearance and artwork

A powerful four-legged hound with exactly two separate canine heads on two necks sharing one broad shoulder girdle and one body. Short rough slate-black fur, lean muscular flanks, broad paws, a long lowered tail, four small amber eyes in total and faint violet Rift glimmers beneath a few patches of fur. Both heads are distinct and equally visible, with alert closed jaws and intact skin. No collar, armor, extra legs or additional heads.
