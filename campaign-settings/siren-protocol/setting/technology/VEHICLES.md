# Vehicles

**Status:** Siren Protocol v0.1 authored technology layer  
**Rules authority:** Trigger Discipline `VEHICLES` for uncertain/demanding operation

Vehicles are ordinary parts of 2225 life. Autonomous driving, direct interfaces, sophisticated assistance, dense transport infrastructure, and advanced electric powertrains are common, but Siren Protocol does not currently add a separate tactical vehicle-combat subsystem.

## Rules boundary

Use Trigger Discipline `VEHICLES` when operation is uncertain, demanding, dangerous, contested, or otherwise calls for a CHECK.

For tactical scenes, a vehicle is also a real spatial object: its size, route, passengers, doors, lines of sight, and physical presence matter.

v0.1 does **not** invent:

- universal vehicle HP/RES;
- vehicle armour penetration tables;
- chase points;
- abstract speed bands;
- mounted-weapon action economy;
- ramming damage formulas;
- a second combat turn economy for vehicles.

Campaign Plans may stage chases and vehicle incidents through existing CHECKS, spatial reasoning, hazards, and authored consequences. A dedicated vehicle-combat system can be added later only if it is genuinely needed.

## VECTORLINE Cinder R

**Type:** high-performance electric motorcycle  
**Occupancy:** 2  
**Control:** manual; DriveLink-compatible

The Cinder R is Blaze's default v0.1 vehicle: extremely responsive, compact, fast, and useful for urban extraction routes that larger vehicles cannot exploit.

Its Siren Protocol identity does not itself grant a VEHICLES bonus. Blaze's competence comes from her actual SKILL and circumstances.

## VECTORLINE V9 Courier

**Type:** compact high-performance road vehicle  
**Occupancy:** 4  
**Control:** manual, assisted, autonomous

The V9 is a common premium courier/executive platform balancing dense-city footprint, cargo space, high acceleration, and autonomous route integration.

Autonomy can perform intended driving tasks within its actual sensors, maps, legal permissions, and operating envelope. It is not an all-purpose tactical AI and can still be blocked, hacked through a plausible route, damaged, deceived, or forced into a situation outside its model.

## PALISADE Bastion Carrier

**Type:** armoured emergency/security utility vehicle  
**Occupancy:** 8  
**Control:** manual, assisted  
**Mounted weapon:** none in the v0.1 reference profile

Used for emergency response, protected transport, infrastructure incidents, and private/public security work.

'Armoured' is an important fictional/material property, but v0.1 deliberately does not invent a standalone vehicle damage/armour profile before Trigger Discipline has a vehicle-combat framework or Siren Protocol explicitly extends it.

## TIDEGLASS Skimmer

**Type:** fast coastal response craft  
**Occupancy:** 6  
**Control:** manual and assisted marine operation

Common around Sydney's ports, harbour, Central Coast, and Newcastle/Hunter maritime infrastructure. Skimmers are useful for rescue, customs, security, maintenance, courier work, and coastal pursuit.

The `VEHICLES` SKILL covers demanding operation; marine context does not require a new Class.

## DriveLink

VECTORLINE DriveLink is an interface, not a driving stat booster. It gives a compatible operator a real control route and richer vehicle state feedback. The user still needs VEHICLES competence when the task is difficult, and compromising a DriveLink connection still requires a plausible access route under HACKER rules.

## Autonomous transport and MERIDIAN

MERIDIAN's rail and transport dominance does not mean every road vehicle is owned by MERIDIAN. VECTORLINE and many other manufacturers/operators exist inside the larger transport ecosystem.

Autonomous vehicles are normal enough that a Campaign Plan does not need to roll routine commuting. Checks become relevant when circumstances become uncertain: pursuit, damaged sensors, hostile interference, extreme terrain, emergency manoeuvres, illegal routing, manual override, or similar pressure.

## Future extension gate

Before adding tactical vehicle combat, first identify the campaign problem that ordinary spatial scenes + VEHICLES checks cannot solve. Any extension must define real interaction with AP, geometry, occupants, weapon systems, damage, movement, and replay rather than bolting on a disconnected minigame merely because vehicles exist.
