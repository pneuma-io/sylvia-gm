# Sydney Playable Districts — Phase 3

**Status:** creator-approved location canon  
**Present year:** 2225  
**Scope:** Illawarra Works, Central Coast Ribbon, Newcastle–Hunter Complex

## Purpose

This document completes the first full street-level district map of the Sydney coastal showcase region.

These districts use the established seven-layer jurisdiction stack:

1. constitutional sovereignty;
2. municipal / regional law;
3. land ownership;
4. infrastructure concession;
5. network / identity authority;
6. contracted security;
7. practical street control.

A district is therefore a playable place, not a clean faction territory. Public government, oligarchies, second-tier corporations, unions, community organisations, syndicates, gangs, and infrastructure operators can all hold different kinds of power in the same area.

Recognisable inherited place-names remain normal where useful. New common-use names appear only where two centuries of growth created a distinct urban identity.

# Illawarra Works

Illawarra Works is the southern industrial and fabrication counterweight to central Sydney. It combines an old coastal city, heavy engineering, advanced manufacturing, maritime infrastructure, research, dense housing, union power, and the escarpment chokepoints connecting the coast to inland Australia.

The Illawarra should feel productive and technically sophisticated rather than like a ruined industrial wasteland.

## Wollongong Centre

**Geographic basis:** Wollongong CBD, university precincts, hospital centres, and adjoining urban coast.  
**Default authority:** comparatively strong public municipal/regional law with large institutional and corporate footprints.  
**Major powers:** public government, universities, unions, MORROW, BLUEHAVEN, LATTICE, professional organisations, and local business networks.

Wollongong Centre is the civic, educational, medical, and commercial heart of the Illawarra. The old city became denser and more vertical but retained a strong identity separate from central Sydney.

Public institutions remain unusually visible. Universities and technical colleges feed the fabrication economy, while hospitals and research facilities link the district to MORROW and public medicine. BLUEHAVEN has major housing and utility interests, but local politics and organised labour make unilateral redevelopment difficult.

The district is one of the best places in the megaregion to find engineers, materials specialists, vehicle technicians, medical staff, union organisers, and public-sector professionals in the same social networks.

**Typical play:** industrial investigations, labour politics, research incidents, public corruption, medical cases, technical recruitment, and local-government operations.

## Port Kembla Forge

**Geographic basis:** Port Kembla, harbour industry, shipyards, fabrication campuses, and adjoining heavy-industrial coastline.  
**Default authority:** public port and industrial law crossed by long-term concessions, private industrial property, union agreements, and PALISADE security.  
**Major powers:** MERIDIAN, INLAND, PALISADE, industrial unions, advanced-materials firms, maritime companies, and specialist manufacturers.

Port Kembla Forge is one of Australia's most important advanced-fabrication centres. Ship systems, structural components, pressure vessels, orbital and lunar logistics hardware, advanced alloys, ceramics, composites, heavy vehicles, and industrial machinery are built and serviced here.

Much of the work is automated, but automation increased rather than eliminated the value of highly skilled technicians, engineers, inspectors, and maintenance crews.

Organised labour retains genuine leverage because a technically competent workforce cannot be replaced instantly without shutting down billion-credit production systems.

PALISADE security is common around high-value facilities, but union stewards, public inspectors, and private operators often possess equally important forms of authority.

**Typical play:** sabotage, industrial espionage, heavy tactical action, labour conflict, stolen materials, shipyard incidents, TECH operations, and infrastructure defence.

## Dapto Works Belt

**Geographic basis:** Dapto, southern Wollongong growth areas, logistics corridors, fabrication parks, and dense worker/residential development.  
**Default authority:** public municipal law with large BLUEHAVEN estates, industrial campuses, transport concessions, and union-controlled workplaces.  
**Major powers:** BLUEHAVEN, specialist manufacturers, MERIDIAN, unions, local government, logistics firms, community organisations, gangs, and grey-market suppliers.

Dapto Works Belt is where the Illawarra's industrial economy meets ordinary residential life. Dense housing, schools, retail, fabrication parks, repair yards, logistics depots, and vocational campuses are woven together.

The district is less glamorous than Wollongong Centre and less restricted than Port Kembla. It is one of the region's strongest markets for legal and grey-market tools, vehicle work, industrial cybernetics, custom fabrication, and repair.

Union halls, sports clubs, local businesses, family networks, and neighbourhood organisations remain part of daily life rather than set dressing around factories.

**Typical play:** equipment sourcing, industrial crime, gang disputes, missing workers, labour cases, vehicle modification, smuggling, and community stories.

## Escarpment Lines

**Geographic basis:** the Illawarra Escarpment, rail and road tunnels, transmission routes, water systems, maintenance settlements, and chokepoints linking coast and interior.  
**Default authority:** strong public environmental and infrastructure law crossed by INLAND, MERIDIAN, BLUEHAVEN, and specialist maintenance concessions.  
**Major powers:** INLAND, MERIDIAN, BLUEHAVEN, public infrastructure agencies, emergency services, engineering unions, and maintenance contractors.

The escarpment makes geography politically important. Power, data, freight, water, passengers, and maintenance access must cross a limited number of corridors between the dense coast and inland systems.

The Lines are not one settlement. They are a network of tunnels, service roads, substations, reservoirs, maintenance facilities, emergency stations, older towns, protected land, and highly secured infrastructure.

A failure here can isolate or throttle the entire Illawarra. This makes apparently mundane maintenance contracts strategically important.

**Typical play:** infrastructure sabotage, tunnel incidents, wilderness pursuit, disaster response, convoy protection, engineering emergencies, and covert access between coast and interior.

# Central Coast Ribbon

The Central Coast Ribbon is the connective urban tissue between Greater Sydney and Newcastle. It contains commuter centres, beaches, old towns, logistics hubs, transport concessions, tourism, dense housing, and local communities that spent two centuries resisting absorption into either metropolitan pole.

Its defining political feature is fragmentation rather than one dominant oligarchy.

## Gosford Basin

**Geographic basis:** Gosford CBD, Brisbane Water, waterfront redevelopment, and surrounding urban basin.  
**Default authority:** public regional/municipal law with substantial BLUEHAVEN, LATTICE, and MERIDIAN infrastructure.  
**Major powers:** local government, BLUEHAVEN, MERIDIAN, LATTICE, healthcare providers, business associations, community organisations, and professional networks.

Gosford Basin is the Central Coast's largest civic and commercial centre. It acts as a regional capital in practice even where formal constitutional boundaries remain complicated.

The basin combines dense housing, offices, healthcare, education, regional public agencies, waterfront development, and transport connections. Corporate influence is strong but less overwhelming than in Sydney's Harbour Core.

Local institutions have enough scale to bargain rather than simply accept decisions made in Sydney or Newcastle.

**Typical play:** regional politics, property disputes, public-sector cases, corporate lobbying, missing persons, commercial crime, and inter-jurisdiction investigations.

## Woy Woy Narrows

**Geographic basis:** Woy Woy, Brisbane Water crossings, rail approaches, and constrained coastal transport corridors.  
**Default authority:** public municipal law repeatedly crossed by MERIDIAN transport infrastructure, maritime authority, and private development.  
**Major powers:** MERIDIAN, local government, ferry and maritime operators, community groups, small businesses, unions, smugglers, and local gangs.

Woy Woy Narrows is a geographic chokepoint. Rail, road, water, and dense settlement squeeze through terrain that offers fewer alternatives than the open western city.

MERIDIAN therefore has more leverage here than its raw property holdings suggest. A transport shutdown can alter movement across a large part of the Central Coast.

At street level the district remains recognisably local: waterfront businesses, apartment neighbourhoods, older housing, ferry terminals, workshops, markets, and community institutions.

**Typical play:** transport disputes, maritime pursuit, smuggling, sabotage, local politics, commuter incidents, and infrastructure extortion.

## Tuggerah Exchange

**Geographic basis:** Tuggerah, Wyong transport/commercial interfaces, logistics zones, and large residential development corridors.  
**Default authority:** mixed public law, MERIDIAN concessions, BLUEHAVEN development, logistics property, and LATTICE identity/payment infrastructure.  
**Major powers:** MERIDIAN, BLUEHAVEN, LATTICE, regional government, logistics firms, retail operators, unions, and syndicates.

Tuggerah Exchange grew around transport and logistics long before 2225 and became one of the Ribbon's largest interchange and commercial centres.

MERIDIAN stations and freight systems anchor dense mixed-use development. BLUEHAVEN housing and utilities spread outward from those nodes, while older communities remain between newer precincts.

This creates the classic concessionary-ratchet landscape: public streets and public law remain, but the systems that make the district useful are mostly privately operated.

**Typical play:** freight crime, credential fraud, transport surveillance, corporate disputes, syndicate activity, missing cargo, and commuter-scale emergencies.

## Coastline Freeholds

**Geographic basis:** Terrigal, Avoca, coastal centres, headlands, beaches, and surrounding high-value residential/tourism districts.  
**Default authority:** fragmented municipal, private-property, community, tourism, and environmental jurisdiction.  
**Major powers:** local councils, BLUEHAVEN, wealthy property interests, hospitality operators, environmental groups, community organisations, and maritime businesses.

The Freeholds are a loose common-use label for affluent and semi-affluent coastal centres that retained stronger local identities than the continuous urban corridors inland.

Some areas are high-end BLUEHAVEN developments; others are older communities that successfully constrained corporate redevelopment; others are tourism economies dependent on public beaches and privately operated services.

The result can feel almost utopian on a good day: beaches, clean public space, excellent food, advanced infrastructure, green corridors, and high-quality housing. Access and property politics are the harder edge.

**Typical play:** wealthy crime, local political fights, environmental disputes, social campaigns, maritime incidents, property conflicts, and secluded-estate investigations.

# Newcastle–Hunter Complex

Newcastle–Hunter is the northern counterweight to Sydney rather than a distant suburb. It possesses its own port, civic institutions, industry, research, labour politics, energy interfaces, and corporate rivalries.

Sydney-centred factions are important here, but Newcastle is not governed culturally or practically from the Harbour Core.

## Newcastle Harbour

**Geographic basis:** Newcastle CBD, harbour waterfront, port interfaces, and inner-city redevelopment.  
**Default authority:** strong public municipal/port law crossed by MERIDIAN maritime concessions, private development, union agreements, and PALISADE security.  
**Major powers:** public government, MERIDIAN, port authorities, maritime unions, PALISADE, LATTICE, local business networks, and syndicates.

Newcastle Harbour is simultaneously civic centre, working port, cultural district, commercial core, and heavy-logistics interface.

Unlike Port Botany, the port is physically and politically inseparable from a major independent city. Public institutions and maritime labour therefore have more leverage over corporate operators.

The harbour is one of the places where a MERIDIAN asset can be strategically essential without MERIDIAN being the strongest actor in every surrounding street.

**Typical play:** maritime crime, port politics, public corruption, labour disputes, executive visits, customs cases, and waterfront tactical operations.

## Carrington Freight

**Geographic basis:** Carrington, Kooragang/industrial-port interfaces, freight yards, depots, storage, and maritime logistics systems.  
**Default authority:** port law and public industrial regulation overlaid by freight concessions, private compounds, union workplaces, and security contracts.  
**Major powers:** MERIDIAN, maritime/freight unions, PALISADE, logistics companies, specialist manufacturers, syndicates, and customs authorities.

Carrington Freight is the industrial operating system behind the harbour's public face. Automated yards, bonded storage, maritime freight, bulk-material systems, rail interfaces, repair depots, and machine-managed logistics dominate.

The district is highly networked and physically hazardous. Control of routing data, maintenance access, cargo identity, or union labour can matter more than literal territorial ownership.

**Typical play:** cargo interception, industrial sabotage, smuggling, freight hacking, labour action, heavy-vehicle pursuit, and warehouse raids.

## Hexham Junction

**Geographic basis:** Hexham, lower Hunter crossings, freight rail, high-speed transport interfaces, logistics corridors, and industrial land.  
**Default authority:** heavily mixed public transport law, MERIDIAN concessions, industrial property, and environmental/water regulation.  
**Major powers:** MERIDIAN, INLAND, logistics firms, public transport authorities, unions, and regional infrastructure agencies.

Hexham Junction is the point where Newcastle's coastal systems connect to the Hunter hinterland and the wider east-coast transport network.

High-speed passenger movement, heavy freight, inland energy routes, industrial traffic, and river infrastructure all intersect. It is less urban-glamorous than Newcastle Harbour but more strategically important than many outsiders realise.

**Typical play:** convoy work, transport sabotage, freight pursuit, infrastructure security, regional smuggling, and jurisdiction disputes.

## Callaghan Research Arc

**Geographic basis:** Callaghan, university/research precincts, medical and engineering campuses, technology parks, and surrounding dense redevelopment.  
**Default authority:** public/university law and property mixed with research concessions, corporate laboratories, LATTICE networks, and MORROW medical facilities.  
**Major powers:** universities, public research bodies, MORROW, INLAND, LATTICE, second-tier technology firms, professional associations, and hacker/research communities.

Callaghan Research Arc is Newcastle's major knowledge economy cluster. Advanced materials, medicine, robotics, industrial AI, energy systems, maritime engineering, environmental science, and cybernetics are all represented.

It provides a counterweight to Pyrmont Exchange: less finance/media-oriented, more engineering and applied research.

Corporate money is everywhere, but universities and public research institutions remain independent enough to produce genuine whistleblowers, public-interest research, and ugly intellectual-property wars.

**Typical play:** research theft, academic intrigue, prototype security, HACKER/TECH operations, medical incidents, whistleblowers, and corporate espionage.

## Hunter Conversion Belt

**Geographic basis:** the broader Hunter industrial and energy hinterland, including Maitland-linked logistics, former extractive landscapes, new energy/materials campuses, and service settlements.  
**Default authority:** regional public law, land and environmental regulation, large INLAND concessions, industrial property, and community/land-interest negotiations.  
**Major powers:** INLAND, advanced-materials firms, public authorities, unions, Aboriginal organisations and land interests, environmental groups, logistics operators, and local communities.

The Hunter's old extractive economy did not disappear overnight. Over two centuries it was repurposed, remediated, built over, and politically fought over.

By 2225 the Conversion Belt contains energy storage, grid interfaces, materials processing, recycling, industrial agriculture support, fabrication supply chains, remediation projects, and service settlements built on landscapes with long memories of extraction and redevelopment.

This is one of the strongest places in the showcase region for conflicts over land, infrastructure, jobs, environmental repair, and who benefits from the transition to a supposedly cleaner economy.

**Typical play:** infrastructure conflict, environmental investigations, industrial sabotage, land-rights disputes, union action, rural/industrial crime, and energy-sector operations.

# Full showcase-region result

With Phase 3, all nine Sydney coastal macro-zones now have an initial playable street-level treatment:

- Harbour Core;
- Eastern & Northern Coastal Enclaves;
- Parramatta Civic Spine;
- Western Megacity;
- Western Gate;
- Botany–South Industrial Arc;
- Illawarra Works;
- Central Coast Ribbon;
- Newcastle–Hunter Complex.

The Blue Mountains / Hawkesbury / Nepean edge remains a strategic geographic and infrastructure layer rather than a tenth urban macro-zone.

This first complete district pass is intentionally broad. Later Campaign Plans may subdivide any district further without changing setting canon.

## What remains flexible

The following are still allowed to vary or be expanded without contradicting this map:

- exact formal 2225 administrative boundaries;
- exact property/concession lines;
- block-by-block faction influence;
- local police/security contracts;
- specific gang corners or syndicate routes;
- individual buildings, venues, clinics, arcologies, stations, businesses, and residences;
- core-character homes and affiliations unless separately locked.
