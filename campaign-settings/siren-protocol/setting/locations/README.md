# Siren Protocol Locations

Location-specific human-facing canon lives in this directory.

## Sydney coastal showcase region

The current authored showcase region is the Sydney coastal megaregion of Australia.

Read in this order:

1. [Sydney Coastal Region](SYDNEY_COASTAL_REGION.md) — regional foundation, political economy, oligarchies, and patchwork jurisdiction.
2. [Sydney Macro Geography](SYDNEY_MACRO_GEOGRAPHY.md) — physical/urban macro structure: nine major macro-zones plus the Blue Mountains / Hawkesbury / Nepean edge.
3. [Sydney Playable Districts — Phase 1](SYDNEY_DISTRICTS_PHASE1.md) — thirteen street-level districts across Harbour Core, Parramatta Civic Spine, and Western Gate, plus the canonical jurisdiction-stack design rule.
4. [Sydney Playable Districts — Phase 2](SYDNEY_DISTRICTS_PHASE2.md) — fourteen districts across Western Megacity, Botany–South Industrial Arc, and the Eastern & Northern Coastal Enclaves.
5. [Sydney Playable Districts — Phase 3](SYDNEY_DISTRICTS_PHASE3.md) — thirteen districts across Illawarra Works, Central Coast Ribbon, and Newcastle–Hunter Complex, completing the first playable pass across all nine macro-zones.

The macro-zone names are development/geographic descriptors unless separately promoted as formal 2225 administrative names. District names may be inherited place-names, common-use names, or evolved urban descriptors and do not automatically define formal governmental boundaries.

Every location may combine public law, land ownership, infrastructure concessions, network/identity authority, contracted security, and practical street control.

The initial full regional district layer is now complete. Later development may subdivide districts into neighbourhoods, sites, venues, buildings, routes, and encounter-scale locations without replacing this structure.
