# Sydney Showcase Completion Status

**Status:** first complete playable showcase layer  
**Present year:** 2225

The Sydney coastal showcase now has:

- 9 macro-zones plus the Blue Mountains / Hawkesbury / Nepean infrastructure edge;
- 40 initial playable districts across three district phases;
- a seven-layer jurisdiction model for resolving overlapping authority;
- 6 principal Australian corporate oligarchies;
- 8 recurring second-tier corporations;
- 5 named public/civic institutions;
- 5 named labour/professional powers;
- 5 named community/advocacy powers;
- 7 named criminal, hacker, or grey-market powers.

This means the showcase is structurally ready for Campaign Plans and for later drill-down into individual neighbourhoods, venues, buildings, routes, NPC institutions, and encounters.

It does **not** lock the final official 2225 regional name, exact constitutional borders, street-by-street ownership, oligarchy executives/cross-holdings, or core-character affiliations.
