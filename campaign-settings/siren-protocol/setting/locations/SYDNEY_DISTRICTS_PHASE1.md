# Sydney Playable Districts — Phase 1

**Status:** creator-approved location canon  
**Present year:** 2225  
**Scope:** Harbour Core, Parramatta Civic Spine, and Western Gate  
**Relationship:** subdivision of the macro-zones defined in [Sydney Macro Geography](SYDNEY_MACRO_GEOGRAPHY.md)

## Purpose

This document establishes the first street-level playable districts in the Sydney coastal showcase region.

The districts are not clean faction territories or necessarily formal 2225 administrative units. A district name may be an inherited place-name, common contemporary name, planning descriptor, or locally dominant name. Formal jurisdiction may overlap the district rather than match it.

The approved first pass contains **thirteen districts**: four in the Harbour Core, four in the Parramatta Civic Spine, and five in Western Gate.

## District-design rule: the jurisdiction stack

A Sydney location can contain several simultaneous layers of authority:

1. **constitutional sovereignty** — the surviving or successor public state with ultimate formal legal claim;
2. **municipal / regional law** — local public government, courts, police, regulation, and services;
3. **land ownership** — whoever owns the building, parcel, campus, development, or facility;
4. **infrastructure concession** — a corporation or other body with rights over rail, utilities, airports, ports, hospitals, networks, or other systems;
5. **network / identity authority** — systems such as LATTICE that authenticate identity, access, payment, credentials, and trust;
6. **contracted security** — PALISADE, public police, specialist security, or another body with enforcement rights inside a defined space;
7. **practical street control** — the actor that can actually enforce decisions locally, including government, a corporation, union, community organisation, syndicate, or gang.

Do not reduce a district to statements such as "this is a MERIDIAN district" when several layers are relevant. A public street can sit above a MERIDIAN station, inside a BLUEHAVEN development, use LATTICE identity systems, employ PALISADE security, and border a neighbourhood where a local gang or community organisation has stronger practical control than any of them.

## Naming rule

Recognisable inherited Sydney names remain in ordinary use unless there is a specific reason for a new name. Sydney Harbour, Parramatta, Westmead, Granville, Wollongong, Newcastle, and similar names do not need to disappear merely because two centuries have passed.

New names are appropriate for genuinely new urban structures, merged districts, corporate developments, or evolved colloquial areas. The district names below are canonical common-use / development names, but they do not automatically define formal governmental boundaries.

# Harbour Core

The Harbour Core is the place outsiders most often mistake for "Sydney" as a whole: spectacular, wealthy, vertical, heavily networked, and heavily secured. Even here, no one corporation or public authority owns the whole system.

## The Quays

**Geographic basis:** Circular Quay, The Rocks, and the eastern CBD harbourfront.  
**Default authority:** predominantly public streets and civic law surrounded by private property and infrastructure concessions.  
**Major powers:** surviving Sydney civic institutions, LATTICE, PALISADE, wealthy property interests, diplomatic offices, and major corporate tenants.

The Quays preserve unusually visible layers of old Sydney. Heritage stone, civic buildings, harbour infrastructure, and historic streets survive underneath and between 2225 towers, elevated walkways, ferry systems, commercial complexes, and vertical development.

Government deliberately remains visible here. Police, courts, civic offices, public demonstrations, diplomatic activity, and public ceremony all matter. Being seen in the Quays still has political value.

That visibility does not make the district uniformly public. A person can cross a public plaza, enter a privately governed tower, descend into concession-controlled transport infrastructure, and encounter different security and access rules within minutes.

**Typical play:** diplomacy, corporate meetings, court matters, executive protection, protests, investigations, political scandals, and high-profile violence.

## Barangaroo Crown

**Geographic basis:** Barangaroo and the western CBD waterfront.  
**Default authority:** heavily chartered private property embedded within nominally public Sydney.  
**Major powers:** LATTICE is especially strong; all six principal oligarchies maintain important facilities or interests.

Barangaroo Crown is the extreme corporate face of the Harbour Core. Two centuries of redevelopment produced a dense vertical corporate city of headquarters, luxury housing, private clubs, hotels, financial centres, skybridges, controlled gardens, and managed waterfront space.

Senior executives and wealthy residents can spend days moving between towers, transport, dining, recreation, medical facilities, and private meeting spaces without meaningfully entering uncontrolled public space.

The district is genuinely beautiful: excellent air management, landscaped public-looking spaces, harbour views, exceptional food, reliable infrastructure, and extremely low violent street crime.

It is also one of the most intensely authenticated places in Australia. Buildings, networks, transport, payments, private security, and service systems constantly know who is present and what access they possess.

**Typical play:** corporate intrigue, infiltration, executive work, high-value theft, financial investigation, social operations, and protected-target extraction.

## Pyrmont Exchange

**Geographic basis:** Pyrmont, Ultimo, and the western Darling Harbour area.  
**Default authority:** intensely mixed public, institutional, commercial, and network jurisdiction.  
**Major powers:** LATTICE, universities and research institutions, second-tier technology firms, media organisations, cultural institutions, and hacker communities.

Pyrmont Exchange is one of Sydney's primary data, media, education, research, and network-exchange districts. Old industrial redevelopment evolved into dense university infrastructure, technology firms, exchange facilities, studios, entertainment spaces, research institutes, independent businesses, and residential towers.

Public roads can run beneath privately owned buildings whose internal network infrastructure is effectively sovereign. Legitimate cybersecurity researchers, corporate HACKERs, public-interest technologists, independent specialists, grey-market operators, and outright criminals can operate within blocks of one another.

The district is less polished than Barangaroo Crown and considerably more porous. It is one of the best places in the Harbour Core to disappear into legitimate technical activity without leaving civilisation behind.

**Typical play:** hacking, data theft, surveillance, journalism, research, whistleblowers, corporate espionage, and digital forensics.

## Northspan

**Geographic basis:** North Sydney, Milsons Point, and the northern harbour bridge approaches.  
**Default authority:** public municipal structures interwoven with corporate development and infrastructure concessions.  
**Major powers:** LATTICE, BLUEHAVEN, professional organisations, wealthy residents, and multiple second-tier corporations.

Northspan grew around the value of being physically adjacent to the Harbour Core without being absorbed entirely into its most restrictive corporate developments. Corporate towers, finance, professional services, high-end housing, and commercial infrastructure extend northward, but the district remains politically more mixed than Barangaroo Crown.

The Harbour Bridge approaches demonstrate the jurisdiction stack particularly well. Heritage and public infrastructure coexist with privately operated systems, corporate maintenance contracts, transport concessions, security zones, network infrastructure, and commercial development.

Northspan is wealthy but less ceremonially corporate than the western CBD waterfront. It is a natural home for lawyers, consultants, financiers, technical professionals, senior public servants, and executives who want proximity to the Core without living inside a corporate campus.

**Typical play:** finance, corporate work, white-collar investigation, surveillance, access operations into the Harbour Core, and high-end residential incidents.

# Parramatta Civic Spine

The Parramatta Civic Spine is deliberately different from the Harbour Core. Sydney's long westward population shift made Parramatta too important to remain secondary, while surviving public institutions increasingly concentrated there to avoid becoming permanent tenants of Harbour corporate property.

It is the closest thing the megaregion has to a public-government capital.

## Parramatta Commons

**Geographic basis:** Parramatta CBD and river centre.  
**Default authority:** strongly public.  
**Major powers:** public government first; LATTICE and BLUEHAVEN second; unions, legal organisations, universities, and advocacy bodies remain significant.

Parramatta Commons is the institutional heart of surviving metropolitan government. Courts, agencies, regulators, elected bodies, public archives, public housing authorities, civic organisations, and public-service institutions cluster here.

Corporations own buildings, provide services, finance projects, and operate infrastructure, but direct private jurisdiction is considerably weaker than in the Harbour Core.

A corporate executive accustomed to private enclaves can genuinely be subpoenaed, arrested, regulated, or overruled here. That does not mean Parramatta is free of corruption or corporate influence. It means there is still an actual public state apparatus capable of fighting back and sometimes winning.

**Typical play:** legal cases, public contracts, political operations, corruption investigations, regulatory disputes, public-sector intrigue, and criminal investigations.

## Westmead Health Quarter

**Geographic basis:** Westmead medical precinct and surrounding redevelopment.  
**Default authority:** mixed public-health, university, and MORROW institutional jurisdiction.  
**Major powers:** MORROW, public health agencies, universities, specialist medical organisations, and professional bodies.

Westmead Health Quarter is one of the largest medical and reproductive-health clusters in the southern hemisphere. Hospitals, fertility centres, trauma facilities, cybernetic surgery and rehabilitation, genetic archives, teaching institutions, research facilities, and public health agencies form a vast biomedical district.

MORROW is enormously powerful here but does not possess uncontested control. Public hospitals, universities, professional organisations, and government health infrastructure remain large enough to force genuine competition.

That rivalry is politically intense and often beneficial to patients: public and corporate systems compete over outcomes, talent, regulation, funding, research, and trust.

**Typical play:** medical emergencies, genetic cases, reproductive crime, stolen records, hospital security, cybernetic treatment, research incidents, and disputes over medical or registry evidence.

## Olympic Ward

**Geographic basis:** Sydney Olympic Park and surrounding river redevelopment.  
**Default authority:** predominantly public / regional, with extensive temporary and event-specific private concessions.  
**Major powers:** public agencies, entertainment and sporting operators, PALISADE contractors, unions, community organisations, media, and event promoters.

The old Olympic precinct evolved into one of Sydney's major public gathering, sport, entertainment, exhibition, esports, convention, festival, and political-rally zones. Arenas, parks, dense housing, cultural facilities, markets, temporary installations, and mass-transit infrastructure coexist at enormous scale.

It remains one of the few places where hundreds of thousands of people can gather without entering a corporate arcology.

That makes Olympic Ward commercially valuable and politically dangerous. Demonstrations, election rallies, union actions, sporting events, celebrity appearances, conventions, and public ceremonies all compete for the same spaces.

**Typical play:** mass events, VIP protection, protests, sporting intrigue, crowd incidents, political demonstrations, public attacks, and large-scale emergency response.

## Granville Junction

**Geographic basis:** Granville, Clyde, Auburn, and adjacent transport / industrial interfaces.  
**Default authority:** public municipal jurisdiction repeatedly crossed by MERIDIAN infrastructure and private industrial property.  
**Major powers:** MERIDIAN, transport unions, local businesses, municipal authorities, logistics operators, and organised crime.

Granville Junction demonstrates how quickly Parramatta's relatively strong public authority becomes complicated at infrastructure boundaries. Rail, freight, workshops, dense housing, light industry, warehouses, and multiple transport systems intersect in a compressed area.

A pursuit can begin on a public road, continue through a MERIDIAN-controlled station, cross a corporate freight easement, enter a privately maintained tunnel, and end inside a union-controlled maintenance facility without leaving the broader neighbourhood.

Granville's unions possess practical leverage because the district keeps essential systems moving. Organised crime is influential in specific contractors, yards, and logistics chains without openly governing the district.

**Typical play:** pursuits, freight crime, labour disputes, smuggling, transport sabotage, raids, and tactical operations.

# Western Gate

Western Gate is where the political theory of concessionary oligarchy becomes physically obvious. It is a vast airport, transport, freight, logistics, residential, and infrastructure system created by generations of public planning and private expansion.

## Bradfield Gate

**Geographic basis:** the future Bradfield / Badgerys Creek urban centre.  
**Default authority:** extreme patchwork of public streets, corporate development charters, airport zones, MERIDIAN transport jurisdiction, and PALISADE-secured facilities.  
**Major powers:** MERIDIAN first; PALISADE, BLUEHAVEN, and LATTICE also strongly entrenched.

The planned aerotropolis eventually became real and then grew far beyond its original conception. Bradfield Gate is now a high-density transport city organised around the western airport, interstate high-speed rail, logistics systems, corporate campuses, mixed-use towers, hotels, housing, and infrastructure interchange.

It is the clearest visual demonstration of concessionary oligarchy in Australia.

No government ever formally "gave MERIDIAN Bradfield." Governments approved one additional station, parcel, logistics zone, maintenance agreement, security concession, development right, and transport extension at a time until the company's functional control became structural.

**Typical play:** transit, corporate operations, airport crime, smuggling, security work, pursuit, infiltration, transport incidents, and jurisdiction disputes.

## Kingsford International

**Geographic basis:** Western Sydney Airport and its surrounding airport city.  
**Default authority:** public border and customs authority overlaid with private airport, MERIDIAN, commercial, and security jurisdiction.  
**Major powers:** MERIDIAN, PALISADE, public customs and border agencies, LATTICE, airlines, freight operators, and specialist contractors.

Kingsford International is effectively a city within the city. Runways, passenger terminals, freight terminals, hotels, customs zones, retail, commercial districts, maintenance facilities, bonded storage, logistics systems, and secure transport infrastructure cover an enormous area.

Jurisdiction can change inside the same building depending on whether an incident concerns immigration, customs, passenger conduct, corporate property, aviation safety, transport security, criminal law, or a private concession.

The airport is simultaneously one of Australia's most tightly controlled spaces and one of its busiest interfaces with the outside world.

**Typical play:** international arrivals, contraband, protected persons, fugitive pursuit, corporate freight, extractions, customs investigations, and security incidents.

## The Interchange

**Geographic basis:** the main convergence of interstate high-speed rail, airport rail, freight systems, and autonomous road transport in Western Gate.  
**Default authority:** overwhelmingly MERIDIAN operational jurisdiction with retained statutory powers for public authorities.  
**Major powers:** MERIDIAN, PALISADE, LATTICE, public transport regulators, and transport unions.

The Interchange is no longer meaningfully a single railway station. It is a vertical transport district handling hundreds of thousands of passengers and vast volumes of freight every day.

It is the most visible expression of the century-old east-coast transport concession. MERIDIAN does not legally own the concept of travelling between Sydney, Brisbane, and Melbourne; it simply owns or controls most of the infrastructure that makes doing so fast and convenient.

Station access, passenger identity, payment, baggage systems, security, retail, freight transfer, hotels, maintenance, and network services all create different operational boundaries inside the same complex.

**Typical play:** transit, surveillance, escapes, interdiction, hostage situations, smuggling, pursuit, and contact meetings.

## Freightlands

**Geographic basis:** the western airport logistics belt extending toward established industrial Western Sydney.  
**Default authority:** corporate concessions and municipal industrial zoning mixed across large private compounds, roads, freight easements, and public infrastructure.  
**Major powers:** MERIDIAN, PALISADE, transport and warehouse unions, logistics corporations, syndicates, and gangs.

Freightlands is a huge belt of warehouses, autonomous freight depots, bonded storage, fabrication plants, logistics campuses, maintenance yards, vehicle depots, container systems, and service businesses.

It is less polished than Bradfield Gate and considerably more physically dangerous. Heavy machinery, autonomous freight, restricted compounds, hazardous materials, industrial cybernetics, and 24-hour logistics define daily life.

Organised crime does not need to govern Freightlands openly. Control of a maintenance contractor, one union local, several warehouse supervisors, a credential broker, or a freight-routing exploit can move enormous quantities of illegal material through legitimate systems.

**Typical play:** raids, cargo theft, arms trafficking, industrial espionage, gang activity, missing shipments, sabotage, and warehouse firefights.

## South Creek Belt

**Geographic basis:** the urban and infrastructure corridor following South Creek and surrounding western development.  
**Default authority:** mostly public municipal law with very large BLUEHAVEN property, water, utility, and service footprints.  
**Major powers:** BLUEHAVEN, local government, community organisations, neighbourhood businesses, schools, local institutions, and gangs.

South Creek Belt is where Western Gate becomes an ordinary home rather than an airport-industrial landscape. Huge numbers of people live in towers, mid-rise blocks, mixed-use neighbourhoods, schools, shopping districts, parks, sports facilities, clinics, restaurants, and locally run businesses stretching for kilometres.

BLUEHAVEN has enormous influence through housing, flood control, water infrastructure, cooling, district utilities, maintenance, and redevelopment. It does not administer every street and PALISADE does not stand on every corner.

Daily life matters here. People complain about rent, commute to work, argue with local councils, run restaurants, raise children, join clubs, form relationships, organise tenants, commit ordinary crimes, and live in the shadow of infrastructure compounds capable of supplying water or utilities to millions.

**Typical play:** ordinary crime, community stories, gang conflicts, missing persons, BLUEHAVEN disputes, neighbourhood investigations, protection, and local politics.

# Phase-1 design summary

These districts establish three different expressions of Sydney's political system:

- **Harbour Core:** dense corporate concentration inside and around highly visible public institutions;
- **Parramatta Civic Spine:** surviving public authority strong enough to challenge oligarchies directly;
- **Western Gate:** concessionary infrastructure where public and private authority overlap so deeply that boundaries themselves become gameplay-relevant.

Future district passes should preserve this variety rather than making every macro-zone a repetition of the same corporate downtown.

- exact formal 2225 governmental boundaries for these districts;
- exact parcel-by-parcel or building-by-building jurisdiction;
- named gangs, syndicates, unions, municipal agencies, advocacy organisations, or second-tier corporations active in each district;
- exact corporate cross-holdings or concession documents;
- detailed landmarks within each district;
- which core Siren Protocol characters live, work, or operate in these districts;
- districts for the remaining Sydney macro-zones.
