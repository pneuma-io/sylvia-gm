## Role identity

Velvet is a **celebrity performer, media personality, and public-attention broker**. She represents the `INFLUENCER` fantasy through reputation, audience mobilisation, narrative framing, public trust, social access, sponsorship, interviews, events, and the ability to make an issue impossible to ignore.

She is not Siren with a larger audience. Velvet's power is public and scalable; Siren's HUMINT power is intimate, private, and deniable.

## Personality

Velvet is warm, highly perceptive, playful, disciplined, and much harder to manipulate than people expect from somebody whose job requires appearing emotionally available to millions of strangers.

She genuinely enjoys performance and attention. Her public persona is not fake; it is a selected part of a real person, repeated at industrial scale.

Velvet becomes cold when somebody treats admiration as consent, fame as ownership, or audience access as personal entitlement. She is generous to staff and brutal about professional standards.

## Background

Velvet became famous through live performance and network media rather than through inherited corporate status. NORTHLIGHT Media & Entertainment distributed and amplified her rise, but she negotiated aggressively to keep her name, likeness, audience channels, and production identity from becoming wholly corporate property.

By 2225 she is large enough to be a political actor without holding office. A statement from Velvet can move consumer sentiment, make an obscure court case internationally visible, fill Olympic Ward, or turn a corporate PR problem into a week-long market event.

That reach creates constant pressure from sponsors, politicians, corporations, advocacy groups, fans, and criminals who all want access to the same attention economy.

## Affiliations

- **Current:** independent performer/brand with major distribution and production contracts through NORTHLIGHT.
- **Professional ecosystem:** event operators, fashion houses, publicists, entertainment unions, LATTICE media/payment systems, Olympic Ward venues.
- **Occasional cause work:** public-health campaigns, anti-trafficking events, civil-liberties fundraising, and other causes selected by her rather than assigned as permanent faction loyalty.
- **Typical operating areas:** Bondi Shelf, Harbour Core, Olympic Ward, major public venues across the megaregion.

## Established relationships

- **Siren:** friendly rival and occasional collaborator. Velvet can move a crowd; Siren can move the five people in the private room that actually decide what happens next.
- **Pixie:** Velvet has quietly used Pixie for physical security testing and stalker-countermeasure work where a conventional visible security team would only prove that the obvious entrance was protected.

No romantic or sexual relationship is implied by either tie.

## Equipment identity

Velvet's working equipment includes secure communications, identity/privacy tools, stage and broadcast interfaces, personal audio systems, bodyguard coordination, and fashion designed to carry modern technology without losing the visual language of couture.

She may carry discreet self-defence equipment appropriate to the situation, but she is not a combat class disguised as a singer.

## Use in play

Velvet works naturally as:

- a pre-made companion in social, political, celebrity, or public-facing campaigns;
- a client whose appearance turns a private operation into a public event;
- an ally able to make evidence visible when institutions would prefer silence;
- a target of stalking, blackmail, kidnapping, corporate pressure, or reputation warfare;
- a public rival who can defeat a plan without firing a shot.

Her central tension is **attention versus ownership**: her influence exists because millions feel connected to her, while the most dangerous people in her life are often those who believe connection gives them a claim.
