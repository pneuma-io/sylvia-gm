## Role identity

Blaze is a **high-risk courier, extraction driver, and competitive racer**. She represents the mobility/`VEHICLES` identity by making transport itself part of the tactical plan: route choice, pursuit, escape, vehicle control, difficult terrain, moving pickups, emergency driving, and getting a team somewhere before the opposition can make 'somewhere' inaccessible.

She is not the TECH of the cast. Blaze knows machines by feel, maintenance, and use under pressure; Nova is the systems engineer.

## Personality

Blaze is energetic, competitive, direct, and almost offensively cheerful when a plan becomes dangerous. She likes motion, hates waiting rooms, and thinks most arguments improve if everybody has somewhere useful to be.

She has a high risk tolerance but is not suicidal. What looks like recklessness is usually confidence built from repetition. She gets angry when passengers confuse 'fast' with 'careless' and interfere with a vehicle because they are scared.

She is generous with friends, terrible at pretending she is not excited, and deeply suspicious of anyone who wants permanent control over where she can go.

## Background

Blaze trained as a professional test rider and vehicle operator for VECTORLINE Mobility, running prototype road systems, autonomous-assist packages, high-performance chassis, and hostile-condition validation.

She left corporate employment after realising that being paid to find a machine's limits was gradually becoming being paid to make corporate telemetry decide her limits for her.

Now she works independently across legal courier contracts, emergency transport, specialist extraction, vehicle testing, racing, and occasional Night Market work. She has enough VECTORLINE contacts to get parts and enough history with them to know exactly which doors are closed.

Her reputation is strongest in Western Gate and the industrial south, where transport infrastructure creates both enormous opportunity and enormous surveillance.

- **Nova:** technical collaborator. Nova improves vehicles, drones, and control systems; Blaze tests whether the improvements survive speed, bad surfaces, gunfire, weather, and human panic.

No romantic or sexual relationship is implied by either tie.

## Equipment identity

Blaze's signature equipment is a heavily customised high-performance motorcycle plus access to other vehicles appropriate to the job. She uses a visor/HUD, secure navigation, route tools, protective riding gear, and practical sidearms rather than turning every vehicle into a weapons platform.

Her leg augmentations support her mobility identity visually, but their exact mechanical effect is not yet fixed.

Trigger Discipline currently handles uncertain/demanding vehicle operation through `VEHICLES`; tactical vehicle combat remains outside the base Ruleset unless Siren Protocol explicitly extends it.

## Use in play

Blaze works naturally as:

- a pre-made companion for mobile, urban, courier, or extraction-heavy campaigns;
- the driver hired for a route corporate systems say should be impossible;
- a former VECTORLINE insider with strong opinions about mobility monopolies;
- a racer, contact, or rival around the Night Market and Western Gate;
- the person who turns 'we need to leave now' into an actual plan.

Her central tension is **freedom versus infrastructure**: almost every road, vehicle, payment, identity system, charging point, or tunnel in 2225 makes movement easier by making it more legible to somebody else.
