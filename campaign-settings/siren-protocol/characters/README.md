# Siren Protocol Core Cast

**Status:** creator-approved character-foundation canon  
**Present year:** 2225

The eleven core women are canonical pre-made NPC/companion designs. They are **not** a mandatory party, not automatically one social group, and not automatically romantic or sexual partners of any player character.

Their early development focused on visual identity and the Trigger Discipline role fantasy each one helped represent. The authored foundation pass gives them enough biography, personality, affiliations, and setting placement to be usable in play without turning those design anchors into complete mechanical builds.

## Visual authority

The canonical visual source is the locked `Siren Protocol_Locked_Character_Visual_Canon_v1` registry and its eleven `*_LOCKED.png` images.

**Generated text inside those images is non-canonical.** Visual appearance may be used as authority; labels, biographies, affiliations, statistics, quotes, logos, equipment names, and other rendered writing may not be imported unless separately authored.

## Role-contrast rule

Keep the pairings distinct:

- **Vesper vs Sable:** Vesper is the adaptable combat riflewoman who solves a live firefight; Sable is the patient observer who shapes the fight before the first shot.
- **Riot vs Kora:** Riot opens the hard point; Kora holds the line and gets people through it alive.
- **Pixie vs Hex:** Pixie physically gets inside; Hex digitally gets inside.
- **Velvet vs Siren:** Velvet moves crowds and public narratives; Siren moves individuals through trust, secrets, and leverage.
- **Blaze vs Nova:** Blaze makes machines move under pressure; Nova makes machines and systems do the right thing in the first place.
- **Solace:** Solace keeps bodies alive when every other role goes wrong.

## Established core-cast ties

These relationships are canon, but they do **not** imply one eleven-person team.

- **Vesper and Kora:** professional respect from joint protective-response work. They trust one another under fire while disagreeing about whether institutions deserve loyalty.
- **Vesper and Sable:** friendly professional rivalry between fast live-fire judgement and slower evidence/overwatch certainty.
- **Riot and Blaze:** close friends and frequent trouble-partners. Blaze gets Riot out; Riot gets Blaze in.
- **Riot and Pixie:** occasional infiltration partners; Pixie prefers the unnoticed route and Riot handles the route that stops being subtle.
- **Solace and Hex:** Solace has treated Hex after serious interface overuse. Hex trusts her with medical access she gives almost nobody else.
- **Solace and Kora:** recurring emergency-response partners; Kora creates the safe space and Solace keeps the casualty alive inside it.
- **Pixie and Hex:** close operational friends; physical infiltration and digital intrusion make them an unusually effective two-person team.
- **Velvet and Siren:** friendly rivals who understand that public attention and private information are different currencies. They trade introductions and occasionally compete for the same room.
- **Velvet and Pixie:** occasional security-testing relationship around backstage access, stalker resistance, and routes conventional visible security misses.
- **Nova and Blaze:** technical collaborators. Nova improves machines; Blaze subjects those improvements to conditions Nova would never choose in a laboratory.
- **Nova and Hex:** occasional technical collaborators and philosophical irritants over control, auditability, and who gets to understand a system.
- **Sable and Siren:** recurring professional relationship. Siren supplies human intelligence; Sable verifies what survives contact with evidence.

Any romance, attraction, sexual relationship, household structure, jealousy, or harem connection involving these characters is **Campaign Plan content unless separately authored later**.

## Incomplete mechanical templates

The first mechanical implementation profile is documented in [Playable Builds v0.1](PLAYABLE_BUILDS.md) and machine-readable under [`../implementation/`](../implementation/). Complete starting resources and progression remain pending as recorded in [SOURCE.md](../SOURCE.md).

Those Level 5 builds and loadouts are **implementation defaults rather than immutable character biography**. Campaign Plans may rebuild them, use another level, change equipment, or change imported side while preserving the authored character identity.

Two profiles intentionally distinguish fiction anchor from runtime Class:

- Blaze remains the VEHICLES/mobility character but uses GHOST/EVADER in the v0.1 runtime build because Trigger Discipline has no dedicated driver Class.
- Siren remains the social/HUMINT alternate but uses INFLUENCER/HANDLER in the v0.1 runtime build.

This is an application of Trigger Discipline's class-skill independence, not a rewrite of either character.

## Mechanical boundary

The role associations and authored biographies do not silently establish permanent `SKILLS`, `APTITUDES`, `SPECIALTY` choices, HP, RES, AP, armour values, weapon statistics, equipment costs, or progression.

The v0.1 playable builds make explicit choices for one authored profile using current Trigger Discipline rules. Future balance or Ruleset changes may revise those implementation choices without retconning the fiction.
