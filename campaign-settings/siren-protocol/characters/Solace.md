## Role identity

Solace is a **field trauma physician and emergency medic**. She expresses the `CORPSMAN` fantasy through stabilisation under fire, triage, trauma care, casualty movement, medical judgement under uncertainty, and the ability to keep a tactical team functioning when bodies stop cooperating with the plan.

She is a physician first, not a magical healer. Siren Protocol medicine can be extremely advanced, but it remains medicine.

## Personality

Solace is calm, clinically blunt, private, and compassionate in action more than language. She will tell somebody exactly how bad an injury is and then stay beside them until the danger has passed.

She dislikes euphemism around pain, coercion, reproductive care, and bodily autonomy. To her, accurate information is kinder than false reassurance.

She has a dry sense of humour that usually arrives when everybody else is finally safe enough to appreciate it.

## Background

Solace trained inside the Sydney Public Health Network and became a trauma specialist accustomed to mixed-jurisdiction emergencies: public streets, corporate facilities, transport systems, protests, industrial accidents, and incidents where five institutions are arguing over authority while one patient is bleeding.

She remains part of SPHN and the Australian Clinical Workers Association, but she has also built a quiet reputation for treating people whose legal, criminal, registry, gang, or employment situation makes official care frightening.

That does not mean she operates a secret criminal hospital. It means she sometimes treats first and lets the paperwork become tomorrow's problem.

The Free Status Network occasionally refers clients to her when a naturally born man needs medical care without having every detail of his private life turned into a corporate or family access point.

## Established relationships

- **Hex:** Solace has treated Hex after serious interface overuse and neural stress. Hex grants her medical access and candour she gives almost nobody else.
- **Kora:** recurring emergency-response partner. Kora creates space for medicine to happen; Solace is one of the few people who can order Kora to sit down and be treated without turning it into a debate.

No romantic or sexual relationship is implied by either tie.

## Equipment identity

Solace carries a modular trauma kit, injectors, diagnostic interfaces, sterile tools, compact emergency drugs, wound-management supplies, and field equipment designed to bridge the gap between the scene and a proper hospital.

Her visible augmentations and medical interface hardware support her professional identity, but their exact mechanical benefits remain undefined until Siren Protocol's augmentation/equipment layer is authored against current Trigger Discipline rules.

## Use in play

Solace works naturally as:

- a pre-made companion for any campaign where injury and tactical consequence matter;
- an SPHN physician pulled into a corporate or criminal dispute because of who the patient is;
- a medical witness whose records matter to an investigation;
- a source of ethical conflict around reporting, privacy, registry obligations, or employer access;
- the NPC who can tell the party what survival will cost without turning advanced medicine into a reset button.

Her central tension is **care versus systems**: she believes institutions can save lives at scale, while knowing those same institutions can turn a vulnerable person's body into data, leverage, or evidence.
