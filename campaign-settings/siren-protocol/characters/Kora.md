## Role identity

Kora is an **armoured emergency and protective-response commander**. She represents the `BULWARK` fantasy through protection, positional control, casualty extraction, disciplined close-range defence, leadership under pressure, and being the person who remains functional when the team needs somewhere safe to stand.

She is not Riot with more armour. Riot destroys the obstacle; Kora makes sure the team survives the consequences.

## Personality

Kora is steady, authoritative, practical, and deeply uncomfortable with promises made casually. If she says she will get somebody out, she treats that as an operational fact until reality proves otherwise.

She has a protective streak that can feel maternal without being soft or patronising. Kora does not believe people need to be helpless before they deserve protection.

Her weakness is responsibility. She can absorb more obligation than one person can reasonably carry and then become angry with herself rather than admit the load is impossible.

## Background

Kora spent the first major part of her career in PALISADE critical-response and protective operations, where she learned both the genuine value and the political danger of private coercive power.

She later moved into the Sydney Coastal Coordination Authority, becoming a field commander for cross-jurisdiction incidents where public police, corporate security, transport authorities, emergency services, unions, and infrastructure operators all need one operational plan before the legal arguments are finished.

The move was not a moral conversion from 'bad corporation' to 'good government.' Kora still works with PALISADE constantly and still believes its best teams are excellent. She simply wanted her primary duty during a regional emergency to be the people inside the incident rather than the client contract surrounding it.

## Affiliations

- **Primary:** Sydney Coastal Coordination Authority (`SCCA`) field response.
- **Former:** PALISADE critical-response / protective operations.
- **Frequent partners:** SPHN, public emergency services, PALISADE, ETWF, infrastructure operators, municipal authorities.
- **Typical operating areas:** Parramatta Civic Spine, Western Gate, critical infrastructure sites, major disasters and cross-boundary incidents region-wide.

## Established relationships

- **Vesper:** professional respect from joint protective-response work. Kora trusts Vesper's judgement under fire; Vesper trusts Kora to keep a team coherent when everybody else's authority is colliding.
- **Solace:** recurring emergency-response partner. Kora gives Solace room to work; Solace is one of the few people who can order Kora out of the line when Kora herself is the casualty.

No romantic or sexual relationship is implied by either tie.

## Equipment identity

Kora uses heavy personal protection, reinforced cybernetic/armoured systems, secure command communications, practical sidearms, protective tools, and equipment focused on keeping people alive and maintaining a defensible position.

Her armour and augmentations are visually canonical, but exact protection values, damage mitigation, strength effects, and other mechanical benefits remain undefined until authored through current Trigger Discipline/Siren Protocol equipment rules.

## Use in play

Kora works naturally as:

- a pre-made companion for tactical, protection, disaster, or public-response campaigns;
- an SCCA commander who pulls independent PCs into an incident because every formal agency is politically compromised in a different direction;
- a former PALISADE operator forced to negotiate with the corporation she still respects;
- the person holding a corridor, vehicle, clinic, station, or evacuation route while everyone else solves the larger problem;
- an authority figure whose competence makes disobeying her meaningfully harder than simply disliking authority.

Her central tension is **duty versus burden**: Kora believes somebody has to stand between danger and the people who cannot stop it, and has not fully accepted that 'somebody' cannot always be her.
