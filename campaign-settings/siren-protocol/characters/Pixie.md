## Role identity

Pixie is a **physical infiltrator, scout, courier, and security-bypass specialist**. She represents the `GHOST` fantasy through access, concealment, route-finding, climbing/crawling service spaces, defeating physical security, placing or recovering devices, moving unnoticed through crowds, and being where the team needs somebody before anyone realises a team exists.

She is not the cast's HACKER. Pixie can use technical tools, but Hex owns the deep network-intrusion role.

## Personality

Pixie is curious, playful, talkative, and visibly delighted by systems that insist they are impossible to enter. She treats locked doors as puzzles and security theatre as a personal invitation.

She is also far more cautious than her presentation suggests. Pixie obsesses over exits, timing windows, guard habits, maintenance schedules, and which innocent person will get blamed if a job goes wrong.

Her fastest route to anger is being treated like a mascot, a child, or disposable bait because she is small and cheerful.

## Background

Pixie learned the megaregion from its service spaces: rail maintenance passages, building plant rooms, old utility routes, freight handling, event infrastructure, university campuses, and the forgotten seams where one concession ends and another begins.

She began as a legitimate courier and technical runner, then discovered that being very good at getting things into places naturally creates a market for getting things out.

Today she works a mixture of security testing, discreet courier work, recovery, physical penetration testing, surveillance placement, grey-market jobs, and the occasional outright theft when she thinks the target deserves it.

Null Choir cells sometimes use Pixie when a network operation needs somebody physically inside the secure room. She does not consider herself a Choir member and refuses ideological ownership of her work.

- **Riot:** occasional partner. Pixie prefers the door nobody noticed; Riot is the contingency plan for the door that notices back.

No romantic or sexual relationship is implied by these ties.

## Equipment identity

Pixie favours compact bypass tools, climbing/entry gear, concealment-friendly weapons, portable sensors, lock/credential tools, micro-cameras, compact communications, and small support drones or devices.

Her tech should enable infiltration rather than replace Hex's HACKER identity. Do not silently give her universal electronic access because she carries a deck-like device in the visual reference.

## Use in play

Pixie works naturally as:

- a pre-made companion for stealth and urban infiltration campaigns;
- the person who can physically reach a network Hex cannot touch remotely;
- a courier carrying something whose existence matters more than its size;
- a security tester who discovers the client's real problem is internal rather than physical;
- a cheerful thief who has already been inside the room the party is still planning to enter.

Her central tension is **invisibility versus recognition**. Pixie survives by being underestimated and overlooked, while personally hating the assumption that being easy to miss means being unimportant.
