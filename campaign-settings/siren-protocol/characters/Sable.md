## Role identity

Sable is an **independent investigator, tracker, and deliberate overwatch marksman**. She represents the `SHARPSHOOTER` fantasy through observation, target identification, concealment, patience, range, evidence, and choosing the moment when one precise shot matters more than a magazine of good shooting.

She is not Vesper with a longer rifle. Vesper excels inside a live firefight; Sable's advantage is making the fight happen on terms she has already studied.

## Personality

Sable is quiet without being shy, intensely observant, sceptical of easy explanations, and more comfortable asking one useful question than ten conversational ones.

She does not fetishise mystery. She likes evidence because evidence lets people stop guessing.

Her weakness is fixation. Once Sable sees a pattern, she can become too invested in proving that the pattern is real. She therefore keeps people around who are willing to tell her when she has started fitting facts to a conclusion instead of the other way around.

Her humour is dry and often arrives late enough that people are not sure whether she was joking.

## Background

Sable began with the Coastal Customs & Freight Bureau, where long-range observation, patient surveillance, freight investigation, and targeted tactical intervention often mattered more than dramatic raids.

She developed a reputation for cases that crossed too many systems at once: public customs authority, MERIDIAN logistics, LATTICE identity, private warehouses, port unions, syndicate subcontractors, and corporate legal teams all claiming that some other layer held the answer.

She eventually left permanent public service after a politically sensitive investigation was narrowed to the point that the official case no longer matched the evidence she had collected. She did not become anti-government; she became unwilling to let one employer decide which facts were professionally convenient.

Now she works independently for public bodies, lawyers, journalists, insurers, advocacy groups, and private clients when the case interests her and the terms preserve evidentiary control.

## Established relationships

- **Vesper:** friendly professional rivalry. Sable thinks Vesper is too comfortable making fast decisions inside noise; Vesper thinks Sable sometimes waits for a certainty the world will never provide.
- **Siren:** recurring professional relationship. Siren supplies human intelligence; Sable verifies what survives contact with evidence.

No romantic or sexual relationship is implied by either tie.

## Equipment identity

Sable works with a precision rifle when the job requires it, a practical sidearm, high-quality optics/sensors, surveillance tools, concealment gear, evidence capture, and an ocular augmentation integrated into her visual identity.

Her cyber-eye does not grant supernatural perception. Its exact capabilities and any mechanical bonuses remain undefined until authored through Siren Protocol equipment/augmentation rules.

## Use in play

Sable works naturally as:

- a pre-made companion for investigation, surveillance, and precision-combat campaigns;
- an investigator following a case the PCs accidentally entered;
- a rival hunter competing for the same person, evidence, or objective;
- a former public officer who still knows how customs and freight systems actually work;
- the NPC who notices that three apparently unrelated incidents share one impossible detail.

Her central tension is **truth versus closure**. Sable wants the answer badly enough to be useful and dangerously close to wanting it badly enough to see one where there is not.
