## Role identity

Vesper is an **independent precision-response shooter and executive-protection contractor**. She is the core cast's clearest expression of the `RIFLEMAN` fantasy: fast target discrimination, disciplined fire, weapon handling under pressure, movement with a team, and the ability to solve a chaotic live gunfight without needing ideal sniper conditions.

She is not primarily an assassin and should not be confused with Sable's patient `SHARPSHOOTER` niche.

## Personality

Vesper is composed, dry, demanding, and difficult to fluster. She dislikes theatrical aggression and treats competence as a form of courtesy: know your weapon, know your exits, and do not make somebody else pay for your lack of preparation.

Her apparent coldness is mostly economy. She is unexpectedly patient with frightened civilians, inexperienced clients, and people who admit what they do not know. What she has little patience for is arrogance backed by somebody else's risk.

She rarely raises her voice. When angry, she becomes more precise.

## Background

Vesper built her reputation inside PALISADE protective-response work, where she learned to operate across corporate, public, and mixed jurisdictions without assuming that the uniform standing beside her had the same legal authority she did.

She eventually left permanent corporate service rather than continue accepting a system in which contract priority could decide whose life counted first during a crisis. The break was professional rather than revolutionary: she still respects good PALISADE operators, still takes PALISADE-adjacent work, and still believes private security can save lives.

Independence lets her choose the client and the terms. It also means she has no institution automatically standing behind her when a contract becomes politically inconvenient.

## Affiliations

- **Current:** independent licensed security and protective-response contractor.
- **Former:** PALISADE field operations.
- **Frequent clients/partners:** executive offices, public investigators, SCCA emergency teams, specialist legal firms, and occasionally the Free Status Network when a protection case requires serious armed capability.
- **Typical operating areas:** Harbour Core, Northspan, Parramatta Civic Spine, Western Gate, and wherever a protected person needs to move.

## Established relationships

- **Kora:** professional respect from joint response work. Vesper trusts Kora to hold a line; Kora trusts Vesper to make the shot that keeps that line from collapsing. Their recurring argument is whether flawed institutions can still deserve loyalty.
- **Sable:** friendly professional rivalry. Vesper thinks Sable waits too long for certainty; Sable thinks Vesper is too comfortable making decisions inside noise.

No romantic or sexual relationship is implied by either tie.

## Equipment identity

Vesper normally works around a full-size service rifle or compact precision-capable rifle, a sidearm, high-quality optics, secure communications, and discreet protective equipment that fits beneath or integrates with her formal silhouette.

Her visible arm augmentation is canonical character technology, but its exact game statistics and capabilities are not yet fixed.

Do not assign weapon damage, armour values, implant bonuses, or hidden SPECIALTY benefits without using the current Trigger Discipline rules and explicit Siren Protocol equipment canon.

## Use in play

Vesper works naturally as:

- a pre-made companion for tactical or protection-heavy campaigns;
- a professional rival to a combat-focused PC;
- security hired by somebody the PCs need to reach;
- an escort responsible for a rare male, witness, executive, activist, or researcher;
- a contractor forced to decide whether the job and the right thing are still the same thing.

Her central tension is **control versus conscience**: she built her life around being the person who can make the correct decision under fire, while knowing that powerful clients often try to define 'correct' before the shooting starts.
