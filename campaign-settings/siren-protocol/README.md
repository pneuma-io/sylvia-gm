# Siren Protocol

> **Setting name:** Wan selected **Siren Protocol** on 2026-09-23. Harem Run is the former working title; historical source and internal IDs retain that name for provenance.

Siren Protocol is an early-development **future-Earth cyberpunk Campaign Setting** for the [Trigger Discipline](https://github.com/pneuma-io/trigger-discipline) TTRPG ruleset. It is not a standalone Ruleset.

`Trigger Discipline Ruleset > Siren Protocol Campaign Setting > individual Campaign Plans`

Trigger Discipline owns generic mechanics and can support other eras, including present-day play. Siren Protocol selects a specific future expression of that Ruleset.

## Present era

The Siren Protocol present is **2225**.

Earth's geography remains recognisable, but political borders and state structures have changed substantially over two centuries. Present-day countries may survive, merge, fragment, federalise, corporatise, or be replaced by successor states. Siren Protocol does not require a one-to-one extrapolation of modern politics.

Civilisation remains overwhelmingly Earth-based. Satellites, orbital stations, and limited lunar industrial/research infrastructure exist, but Siren Protocol is not an interplanetary setting and does not assume off-world civilian civilisation comparable to Earth.

See [World Architecture](docs/WORLD_ARCHITECTURE.md) for the setting-wide geography, registry, reproductive-industry, orbital, and showcase-region rules.

## Setting at a glance

Siren Protocol takes place roughly two centuries after **the Severance**, a reproductive catastrophe that reduced the natural birth ratio to approximately **100,000 women for every 1 man**. Humanity survived by industrialising assisted conception around archived male genetic material, and modern society is not living in permanent demographic collapse.

Naturally born men still exist and are registered from birth. Their rarity creates enormous social value, legal protection, romantic opportunity, political attention, and vulnerability to control. The setting does not treat male rarity as automatic kingship: a man may be protected, manipulated, claimed, criminally confined, or genuinely free depending on the power they can exercise around them.

Female civilisation has had generations to adapt. Female/female relationships and adult sexual expression are mainstream; male-centred polyamory is common without becoming compulsory; jealousy, monogamy, exclusivity, and individual preference still exist. Reproductive policy and romance are separate systems.

Power is multipolar. Governments, megacorporations, corporate city-states, gangs, syndicates, wealthy private interests, local authorities, unions, advocacy bodies, and autonomous enclaves overlap. Technology is advanced but recognisable: firearms, cybernetics, neural interfaces, hacking, drones, AI, networks, advanced medicine, shields, vehicles, biotechnology, and modest orbital infrastructure all exist within the grounded assumptions of Trigger Discipline.

## Showcase region: Sydney coast

The authored showcase is the **Sydney coastal megaregion of Australia**, built from the modern Sydney basin and adjoining east-coast urban corridor through the Illawarra, Central Coast, and Newcastle–Hunter region.

Australia's 2225 power grew from **desert-scale solar generation** and enormous **data-centre / compute infrastructure**. Public authority eroded gradually through more than a century of concessions, privatisation, public-private dependency, lobbying, corruption, and infrastructure ratchets. Government still legislates, taxes, polices, runs courts and services, but major corporate blocs control enough energy, compute, networks, logistics, housing, medicine, reproductive infrastructure, transport, and security to function as near-peer powers.

Sydney is a **patchwork of both** public government and private jurisdiction. A person can move from a municipal street into a privately governed station, travel through corporate transport space, return to a public interchange, and emerge into a neighbourhood where a union, cooperative, community group, syndicate, or gang holds stronger practical local power.

The first full playable district layer covers all nine macro-zones through three district documents:

- [Phase 1](setting/locations/SYDNEY_DISTRICTS_PHASE1.md) — Harbour Core, Parramatta Civic Spine, Western Gate;
- [Phase 2](setting/locations/SYDNEY_DISTRICTS_PHASE2.md) — Western Megacity, Botany–South Industrial Arc, Eastern & Northern Coastal Enclaves;
- [Phase 3](setting/locations/SYDNEY_DISTRICTS_PHASE3.md) — Illawarra Works, Central Coast Ribbon, Newcastle–Hunter Complex.

The Blue Mountains / Hawkesbury / Nepean edge remains a strategic geographic and infrastructure layer rather than a tenth urban macro-zone.

## Corporate and local power

Australia's six principal oligarchic blocs are:

- **INLAND Systems Group** — energy, desert solar, compute, and AI infrastructure;
- **Meridian Transit & Development** — transport, freight, stations, ports, and transport-led development;
- **Morrow Genomics & Health** — medicine, reproductive infrastructure, genetics, cybernetics, and pharmaceuticals;
- **Lattice Civic Systems** — networks, finance, payments, insurance, identity, and credentials;
- **Palisade Defence & Security** — private security, defence, drones, intelligence, and critical-infrastructure protection;
- **Bluehaven Urban Systems** — water, housing, property, utilities, urban services, and coastal resilience.

They overlap heavily and depend on one another. None is an omnipotent corporate state, and each provides services people genuinely value as well as possessing dangerous structural power.

The showcase layer also includes eight recurring second-tier corporations — **ASTER, IRONLACE, TIDEGLASS, CALYX, VECTORLINE, EMBERLINE, NORTHLIGHT, and VERDIGRIS** — plus named public institutions, unions, community organisations, advocacy groups, syndicates, gangs, hacker collectives, and grey-market networks.

See [Australian Corporate Oligarchies](setting/factions/AUSTRALIAN_OLIGARCHIES.md), [Australian Second-Tier Corporations](setting/factions/AUSTRALIAN_SECOND_TIER_CORPORATIONS.md), and [Sydney Local Powers](setting/factions/SYDNEY_LOCAL_POWERS.md).

## Campaign freedom

Siren Protocol has **no default player character, no gender requirement, no mandatory harem, and no mandatory campaign premise**.

Combat, relationships, romance, sexual content, investigation, crime, tactical play, and social play can each be foregrounded, backgrounded, or omitted by the Campaign Plan. The setting creates possibilities; the Campaign Plan decides which ones matter.

## Canon

Human-facing setting guidance lives in the [Campaign Setting Guide](docs/CAMPAIGN_SETTING_GUIDE.md), [Setting Constitution](docs/SETTING_CONSTITUTION.md), [World Architecture](docs/WORLD_ARCHITECTURE.md), and focused setting files. Machine-facing canon lives in [`canon.json`](canon/canon.json) and [`canon.yaml`](canon/canon.yaml). The [Agent and Canon Specification](docs/AGENT_AND_CANON_SPECIFICATION.md) defines how agents must use those sources.

Canon authority, from highest to lowest, is:

1. Current creator instruction
2. Campaign Plan explicit overrides
3. Siren Protocol structured canon (`canon.json` / `canon.yaml`) and Agent & Canon Specification
4. Trigger Discipline Ruleset for generic mechanics
5. Siren Protocol human-facing canon documents
6. Approved examples
7. Agent improvisation

A contradiction is an editorial defect, not permission to choose whichever source is convenient.

## Core cast

The canonical core designs are **Vesper, Riot, Solace, Blaze, Sable, Velvet, Pixie, Hex, Kora, Siren, and Nova**. Early design was visual- and role-first; the first authored character-foundation pass now gives every member a fictional role identity, personality foundation, Sydney/Australian affiliation context, and a limited set of non-romantic ties to other cast members.

Their Trigger Discipline anchors remain Vesper/RIFLEMAN, Riot/BREACHER, Solace/CORPSMAN, Blaze/VEHICLES-mobility, Sable/SHARPSHOOTER, Velvet/INFLUENCER, Pixie/GHOST, Hex/HACKER, Kora/BULWARK, Siren/social-HUMINT alternate, and Nova/TECH.

They remain pre-made NPCs/companions rather than a mandatory party. They are not automatically one harem, do not all need to know one player character, and have no setting-wide mandatory romance or sexual relationships. Those elements remain Campaign Plan choices unless separately authored later.

Read [Core Cast](characters/README.md) and the individual files under [`characters/`](characters/).
