# Agent and Canon Specification

## Purpose

This document tells authors and agents how to find, apply, and extend Siren Protocol canon without inventing missing material.

## Authority order

Use the following order, from highest to lowest:

1. Current creator instruction
2. Campaign Plan explicit overrides
3. Siren Protocol structured canon (`canon.json` / `canon.yaml`) and this Agent & Canon Specification
4. Trigger Discipline Ruleset for generic mechanics
5. Siren Protocol human-facing canon documents
6. Approved examples
7. Agent improvisation

A contradiction is an editorial defect, not permission to choose whichever source is convenient.

## Core invariants

Agents must preserve the following unless a higher-authority source explicitly overrides them:

- Siren Protocol is future-Earth cyberpunk.
- The canonical present year is **2225**.
- Trigger Discipline itself remains era-flexible and may support present-day play.
- Earth's geography is recognisable, but two centuries of political change mean present-day borders and governments are not automatically preserved.
- Siren Protocol has satellites, orbital stations, and limited lunar industrial/research infrastructure, but no default interplanetary civilisation comparable to Earth.
- The Severance occurred roughly two centuries before 2225 and reduced natural male births to about 1 per 100,000 female births.
- The Severance's true cause is deliberately undefined and does not require a setting-wide answer.
- Humanity adapted successfully to the demographic change.
- Biological pregnancy remains the default; archived male genetic material supports civilisation-scale assisted conception.
- Reproductive infrastructure is a major civilisational industry.
- Every naturally born male is registered by a relevant jurisdiction and owes one setting-wide baseline genetic contribution.
- There is no single global male registry. Jurisdictional registries interoperate through treaties, standards, cryptographic identity, medical agreements, and genetic-bank protocols.
- Registry status is not equivalent to freedom, ownership, captivity, or class.
- Protected, Claimed, and Free are broad social descriptions of practical autonomy, not universal legal castes.
- Male rarity creates both privilege and vulnerability.
- Male-centred polyamory is socially common without becoming psychologically compulsory.
- Female/female relationships and adult sexual expression are mainstream cultural facts.
- Governments, successor states, megacorporations, corporate city-states, gangs, syndicates, private wealth, local authorities, unions, advocacy bodies, and autonomous enclaves share power; no world government or single hidden ruler exists.
- Technology is advanced but recognisable future cyberpunk.
- Connectivity creates capability and vulnerability.
- Siren Protocol has no default PC and no PC-gender requirement.
- Romance, adult sexual content, and harem structures are optional Campaign Plan elements.
- Siren Protocol has no mandatory Campaign Plan premise, duration, party structure, or activity loop.
- The eleven core character designs are canonical and visually locked, but they are not a mandatory single ensemble.
- Early core-character development was visual- and role-first; the first authored foundation pass now establishes fictional role identity, personality foundation, affiliation/background context, setting placement, and limited non-romantic ties for all eleven.
- Core-cast romance, attraction, sexual relationships, household structure, jealousy, and harem connections remain Campaign Plan content unless separately authored later.
- The showcase region is the **Sydney coastal megaregion of Australia**; it is not a mandatory starting location.
- Australia built strategic power around desert-scale solar generation and data-centre/compute infrastructure.
- Australian authority gradually shifted away from the older Canberra-centred federal/state hierarchy toward competing corporate oligarchies layered over surviving public government.
- The erosion of public authority happened through a century-scale ratchet of incremental concessions, privatisation, public-private dependency, lobbying, corruption, short-term political incentives, and repeated extension of existing private control rather than one coup.
- Sydney is a patchwork of public territory under corporate dependency, formally private jurisdictions, and neighbourhoods where smaller actors may hold stronger practical local power.
- Government remains real in Australia, but corporations are the dominant practical power centres and compete alongside smaller interest groups, unions, communities, syndicates, and gangs.
- Australia's six principal oligarchic blocs are **INLAND Systems Group**, **Meridian Transit & Development**, **Morrow Genomics & Health**, **Lattice Civic Systems**, **Palisade Defence & Security**, and **Bluehaven Urban Systems**.
- These six are overlapping conglomerates with primary industrial spines rather than clean single-sector monopolies.
- Each principal oligarchy provides genuinely valuable services as well as possessing dangerous structural power; do not portray them as interchangeable evil megacorps.
- The first playable district layer covers all nine Sydney coastal macro-zones. Districts are overlapping authority spaces, not clean faction territories.
- The canonical district jurisdiction stack is: constitutional sovereignty; municipal/regional law; land ownership; infrastructure concession; network/identity authority; contracted security; practical street control.
- Named second-tier corporate powers include **ASTER, IRONLACE, TIDEGLASS, CALYX, VECTORLINE, EMBERLINE, NORTHLIGHT, and VERDIGRIS**.
- Named Sydney public/civic institutions include **SCCA, PCS, SPHN, ARIO, and CCFB**.
- Named labour/professional powers include **ETWF, IFU, MFG, ACWA, and GCEU**.
- Named community/advocacy powers include the **Fairfield Compact, South Creek Assembly, Coastal Country Accord, Open Civic League, and Free Status Network**.
- Named criminal/grey/hacker powers include **Redline Syndicate, Saltline, Brick Saints, Glasshouse, Null Choir, Black Kites, and the Night Market**.

## Ruleset boundary

Trigger Discipline is authoritative for generic mechanics. Siren Protocol may select, interpret, reskin, constrain, or explicitly extend those mechanics but must not silently redefine them.

The year 2225, future technology ceiling, orbital infrastructure, registry systems, reproductive industry, Australian political structure, Sydney showcase region, named corporations, named factions, named equipment, character biographies, local politics, and world-specific availability are Siren Protocol Campaign Setting decisions rather than Trigger Discipline defaults.

Setting or character facts do not silently create bonuses, penalties, immunities, social modifiers, hacking permissions, extra actions, vehicle-combat rules, reproductive mechanics, cybernetic powers, or other game rules. A mechanical consequence must come from Trigger Discipline or be written completely as a Siren Protocol extension.

## Geography and politics generation

When generating a region:

- preserve recognisable physical geography;
- do not assume modern political borders survive unchanged;
- allow surviving states, successor states, federations, city-states, autonomous zones, corporate jurisdictions, and contested territory;
- do not create a world government by convenience;
- distinguish nominal legal sovereignty from effective street-level control;
- give ordinary residents lives and institutions beyond corporate conflict.

### Australia

Do not turn Australia into either a fully intact modern federation with cosmetic cyberpunk corporations or a stateless corporate free-for-all.

The canonical direction is gradual institutional drift: government still legislates, taxes, polices, runs courts and services, while major corporate oligarchies dominate because they control essential energy, compute, network, logistics, housing, medical, reproductive, financial, security, and transport infrastructure.

The six principal corporate blocs are:

- **INLAND Systems Group (`INLAND`)** — primary spine: desert solar, storage, transmission, hyperscale compute, and AI infrastructure.
- **Meridian Transit & Development (`MERIDIAN`)** — primary spine: high-speed transport, freight, stations, ports, and transport-led development; it controls the principal Sydney-Brisbane-Melbourne high-speed trunk.
- **Morrow Genomics & Health (`MORROW`)** — primary spine: hospitals, reproductive medicine, genetics, cybernetics, pharmaceuticals, and registry-adjacent medical infrastructure.
- **Lattice Civic Systems (`LATTICE`)** — primary spine: networks, finance, payments, insurance, identity, credentials, and cross-jurisdiction trust infrastructure.
- **Palisade Defence & Security (`PALISADE`)** — primary spine: private security, defence systems, drones, intelligence, emergency response, and critical-infrastructure protection.
- **Bluehaven Urban Systems (`BLUEHAVEN`)** — primary spine: water, housing, property, municipal services, utilities, and coastal resilience.

They overlap heavily. Cross-holdings, debt, public pension ownership, joint ventures, shared infrastructure, and executives moving between corporate and public institutions are normal. Two oligarchies can be partners in one project and rivals in another.

Canberra remains an important constitutional/administrative/judicial/diplomatic centre but is not the single effective command point of the country.

### Sydney coastal region

The showcase region grows from Greater Sydney through the Illawarra/Wollongong corridor, Central Coast, and Newcastle/Hunter complex.

The official 2225 regional name and exact administrative borders are still open. Do not invent them as settled canon.

The region's default political texture is **some government, primarily competing corporate oligarchies, plus smaller interest groups, unions, communities, syndicates, and gangs**.

No single corporation owns Sydney. Government is not decorative. District-level effective authority may differ substantially based on infrastructure ownership, contracts, local law, public institutions, private security, organised crime, labour power, or community legitimacy.

The six oligarchies are unevenly entrenched rather than separated into clean territories:

- INLAND is strongest around energy/compute ingress and exchange infrastructure.
- MERIDIAN dominates transport ribbons, stations, freight corridors, and station-centred development.
- MORROW dominates major medical, reproductive, genetic, and cybernetic precincts.
- LATTICE dominates finance, identity, network exchange, and cross-jurisdiction trust systems.
- PALISADE dominates high-security corporate property, critical infrastructure, defence, and private-policing contracts.
- BLUEHAVEN dominates housing, water, district services, utilities, and redevelopment zones.

Treat these as concentrations of influence rather than territorial monopolies.

### Sydney district handling

Use the three district documents as the initial playable map. They establish common-use district identities and geographic basis but do not imply that every boundary is a formal government border.

For any specific scene, resolve authority through the seven-layer jurisdiction stack rather than by asking which faction 'owns the district.'

Block-level detail may be authored by Campaign Plans when the setting has not specified it. A Campaign Plan may create a new venue, street, building, neighbourhood subdivision, gang corner, security contract, or local dispute so long as it does not contradict the established district identity and higher canon.

## Local-power handling

Sydney's smaller institutions should have independent motives.

Do not treat unions, community groups, public institutions, gangs, or second-tier companies as automatic proxies of an oligarchy.

The same organisation may collaborate with one corporate bloc, fight another, accept funding from a third, and remain independent.

Public institutions are also not automatically virtuous. Corruption, incompetence, genuine public service, factionalism, reform, and institutional self-interest can coexist.

Criminal and hacker groups should not be written as omnipotent shadow governments. Their power comes from specific routes, contacts, skills, local legitimacy, compromised systems, and violence rather than universal control.

## Orbital generation

Satellites, orbital stations, and limited lunar facilities may appear as ordinary advanced infrastructure.

Do not escalate this into a space-opera civilisation without explicit creator or Campaign Plan direction. Major extraterrestrial population centres, Mars civilisation, and off-world societies comparable to Earth are not default canon.

## Male registry handling

Do not invent one global master registry.

Each jurisdiction may implement the setting-wide registration and genetic-contribution baseline differently. Cross-jurisdiction interactions should use interoperable identity, medical, genetic-bank, treaty, and verification systems.

ARIO is an interoperability body, not a master registry.

When describing a man's situation, distinguish registry visibility from actual autonomy. Protected, Claimed, and Free describe practical circumstances, not universal legal labels.

## Reproductive-industry handling

Treat reproductive infrastructure as a pervasive strategic sector comparable in social weight to healthcare, banking, energy, or telecommunications.

Suitable institutions may control archives, clinics, genetic replication, lineage verification, security, privacy, insurance, cross-border recognition, or public health. Criminal markets may target the same infrastructure.

Do not make every reproductive institution sinister merely because the setting is cyberpunk. Genuine public services, ethical clinics, predatory corporations, community providers, corrupt authorities, and criminal actors can coexist.

MORROW is a major private actor in this space but does not own the entire Australian reproductive system. SPHN and CALYX are additional established actors.

The core cast is **Vesper, Riot, Solace, Blaze, Sable, Velvet, Pixie, Hex, Kora, Siren, and Nova**.

The early design anchors remain:

- Vesper — `RIFLEMAN`
- Riot — `BREACHER`
- Solace — `CORPSMAN`
- Blaze — mobility / `VEHICLES`
- Sable — `SHARPSHOOTER`
- Velvet — `INFLUENCER`
- Pixie — `GHOST`
- Hex — `HACKER`
- Kora — `BULWARK`
- Siren — social / HUMINT alternate

Those anchors do **not** by themselves establish mechanics. The authored character files now establish the fictional foundations that were previously open.

- **Vesper:** independent precision-response shooter and executive-protection contractor; PALISADE alumna.
- **Riot:** independent forced-entry and close-assault specialist with Night Market and Brick Saints connections.
- **Solace:** SPHN field trauma physician/emergency medic; ACWA member; FSN referral relationship.
- **Blaze:** independent high-risk courier/extraction driver/racer; VECTORLINE alumna.
- **Sable:** independent investigator/tracker/overwatch marksman; CCFB alumna.
- **Velvet:** independent celebrity performer/media personality/public-attention broker with NORTHLIGHT distribution.
- **Pixie:** adult small-stature physical infiltrator/scout/courier/security-bypass specialist with Night Market and selected Null Choir contacts.
- **Hex:** independent HACKER and identity-systems specialist with loose Null Choir history; her occult-looking interface language is technology, not magic.
- **Kora:** SCCA armoured emergency/protective-response commander; PALISADE alumna.
- **Siren:** independent owner/operator of **Blue Hour**, a Harbour Core club/private meeting venue; dealmaker and HUMINT broker.
- **Nova:** independent drone engineer/robotics/tactical-systems specialist; former EMBERLINE executive engineering and estranged founder-family shareholder scion.

### Role separation

Preserve the following distinctions:

- Vesper handles the live rifle fight; Sable shapes the engagement through patience, investigation, and overwatch.
- Riot opens the hard point; Kora holds and protects the line.
- Pixie gets physically inside; Hex gets digitally inside.
- Velvet moves public attention; Siren moves people through private trust, secrets, and leverage.
- Blaze operates vehicles under pressure; Nova engineers and coordinates machines/systems.
- Solace keeps casualties alive and preserves the team's medical continuity.

### Established cast ties

The following are canonical non-romantic foundations, not proof of one eleven-person team:

- Vesper ↔ Kora: professional respect from joint response work.
- Vesper ↔ Sable: friendly professional rivalry.
- Riot ↔ Blaze: close friends and frequent trouble-partners.
- Riot ↔ Pixie: occasional infiltration partners.
- Solace ↔ Hex: trusted medical relationship after serious interface overuse.
- Solace ↔ Kora: recurring emergency-response partners.
- Blaze ↔ Nova: technical collaborators.
- Velvet ↔ Siren: friendly rivals / occasional collaborators.
- Velvet ↔ Pixie: occasional quiet security-testing relationship.
- Pixie ↔ Hex: close operational friends.
- Hex ↔ Nova: occasional technical collaborators and philosophical irritants.
- Sable ↔ Siren: recurring HUMINT/evidence professional relationship.

Do not invent romance, attraction, sex, jealousy, marriage, household structure, or harem membership among the cast as setting-wide canon. A Campaign Plan may freely choose those elements when compatible with its content boundaries.

### Mechanical restraint

Character files may describe equipment **identity** and visible augmentation, but those descriptions do not automatically establish damage, armour values, AP economy, cybernetic bonuses, drone actions, social modifiers, hacking permissions, or SPECIALTY progression.

Final playable builds must be authored against the current Trigger Discipline rules and explicit Siren Protocol equipment/augmentation/extension canon.

## Generated visual references

> **AI-generated embedded text is NON-CANONICAL.**

The locked visual-reference set establishes appearance: face, hair, body type, outfit language, cybernetics/accessories visible in the approved art, palette, and overall silhouette.

Generated text within those images does **not** establish biography, affiliation, stats, equipment names, signage, logos, quotes, organisations, relationships, or mechanical capability. Where an authored character file differs from generated image text, the authored file controls the fiction while the locked image controls visual appearance.

## Deliberately open information

Do not silently invent the following into setting-wide canon:

- exact date of the Severance;
- final official 2225 name and exact administrative boundaries of the Sydney coastal megaregion;
- exact Australian successor constitutional structure and the final names/statuses of surviving state/federal governments;
- individual CEOs, boards, dynasties, and executive factions within the six principal oligarchies;
- exact corporate cross-holdings, ownership percentages, and block-by-block property maps;
- exact statutory powers and reporting lines of public institutions where not already stated;
- exact neighbourhood/street-level jurisdiction boundaries below the established district layer;
- named major powers outside the currently developed Australian/Sydney showcase material unless separately authored;
- final Trigger Discipline builds, exact equipment/augmentation statistics, and deeper biography for the core cast beyond the individual authored foundations;
- any core-cast romantic, sexual, household, jealousy, or harem relationships not separately authored.
