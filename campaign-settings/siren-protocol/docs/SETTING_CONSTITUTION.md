# Siren Protocol — Setting Constitution

**Status:** creator-approved setting-wide canon  
**Present year:** 2225  
**Ruleset:** Trigger Discipline  
**Title status:** Siren Protocol, selected by Wan on 2026-09-23

## Purpose

This document records the setting-wide truths Siren Protocol development must preserve. Detailed physical/institutional architecture lives in [WORLD_ARCHITECTURE.md](WORLD_ARCHITECTURE.md); Sydney material lives under [`setting/`](../setting/); core-cast foundations live under [`characters/`](../characters/).

## Setting name

Wan selected **Siren Protocol** as the Setting's name on 2026-09-23. The former Harem Run title and
stable internal IDs remain historical provenance. The new name does not itself establish an
in-world organisation, protocol or story premise.

## Ruleset era versus setting era

Trigger Discipline is era-flexible and may support present-day or other grounded tactical settings. Siren Protocol itself is specifically futuristic. The **canonical present year is 2225**.

## Core truths

1. **Siren Protocol is a future-Earth cyberpunk setting.** Earth remains the primary social and political stage.
2. **The present year is 2225.** This is Campaign Setting canon, not a Trigger Discipline Ruleset rule.
3. **Earth's geography remains recognisable while politics have changed.** Present-day states may survive, merge, fragment, federalise, corporatise, or be replaced by successor states.
4. **Humanity has modest orbital and lunar infrastructure.** Satellites, orbital stations, and limited lunar industry/research exist, but Siren Protocol is not an interplanetary civilisation setting.
5. **The Severance happened roughly two centuries before the present.** Its exact date remains open.
6. **Natural births are overwhelmingly female.** The approximate natural ratio is 100,000 women to 1 man.
7. **Naturally born men still exist.** They are not exclusively laboratory creations or synthetic replacements.
8. **The cause of the Severance is deliberately undefined.** The setting does not require one canonical answer; Campaign Plans may choose one if useful.
9. **Humanity adapted successfully.** Siren Protocol is not a permanent demographic-collapse emergency.
10. **Biological pregnancy remains normal.** Artificial wombs are not the default reproductive solution.
11. **Archived male genetic material supports civilisation-scale assisted conception.** Male genetic material can be replicated at enormous scale.
12. **Reproductive infrastructure is a major civilisational industry.** Archives, clinics, lineage verification, fertility medicine, security, privacy, insurance, law, and black markets are structurally important.
13. **Naturally born men are registered from birth.** The setting-wide baseline is one legally required genetic contribution intended to preserve male genetic diversity.
14. **There is no single global male registry.** Registration is jurisdictional and interoperates through treaties, identity standards, genetic-bank protocols, and cross-border recognition.
15. **Registry status is not freedom status.** Registration does not itself mean ownership, confinement, state custody, wealth, or safety.
16. **Protected, Claimed, and Free are social descriptions, not universal legal castes.** They describe practical male autonomy.
17. **Male rarity creates privilege and vulnerability at the same time.** Formal protection can coexist with coercion, criminal capture, manipulation, dependency, or political exploitation.
18. **Male-centred polyamory is common but not compulsory.** The demographic imbalance changes relationship norms without erasing individual psychology.
19. **Reproduction and romance are separate systems.** Genetic contribution, fertility treatment, legal duty, partnership, marriage, sex, affection, and harem membership are not automatically the same thing.
20. **Female civilisation has undergone a major sexual-cultural shift.** Female/female relationships, adult casual relationships, glamorous fashion, and overt sexual expression are ordinary without implying vulnerability or incompetence.
21. **Power is multipolar and local.** Governments, corporations, gangs, syndicates, city-states, private wealth, unions, advocacy bodies, and local institutions overlap; no world government or single secret megacorporation rules everything.
22. **Technology is advanced but recognisable.** Siren Protocol uses grounded cyberpunk technology rather than magical hand-waving.
23. **Connectivity creates capability and vulnerability.** A useful network, implant, drone, AI, interface, or remote system is also an attack surface.
24. **There is no default player character.** Siren Protocol does not prescribe one canonical protagonist or PC gender.
25. **Romance, sex, and harem structures are optional.** A Campaign Plan may foreground them, background them, or omit them entirely.
26. **There is no mandatory campaign premise.** Campaign Plans determine activity loop, scale, tone, cast, duration, and content emphasis.
27. **The eleven core women are canonical designs, not a mandatory party.** Their presence and relationship to any player character remain Campaign Plan choices unless later canon says otherwise.
28. **The core cast began as visual/role-first designs and now has an authored foundation layer.** Role identity, personality foundation, affiliation/background context, Sydney/Australian placement, and limited non-romantic cast ties are established; final Trigger Discipline builds, exact equipment statistics, deeper biography, and romance/sexual/harem relationships remain open.
29. **The showcase region is the Sydney coastal megaregion of Australia.** It exists as a authored vertical slice, not as a mandatory default starting location.
30. **Australia built major future power around solar energy and compute.** Desert-scale solar generation, storage, transmission, and enormous data-centre/compute infrastructure became strategic foundations of Australian wealth.
31. **Australian political power shifted gradually away from the old Canberra-centred hierarchy.** Government survived, but corporate oligarchies became the dominant practical power centres.
32. **Australian power remains contested rather than unified.** Corporations compete with surviving government institutions and with smaller groups including unions, communities, syndicates, gangs, and other local powers.
33. **Australian corporate power emerged through an infrastructure ratchet.** More than a century of small concessions, privatisation, outsourcing, lobbying, corruption, and public-private dependency accumulated into structural corporate sovereignty rather than one coup.
34. **Sydney is a patchwork of both public and private jurisdiction.** Public law, private concessions, property rights, service contracts, and local practical control overlap at street level.
35. **Australia has six principal corporate oligarchies.** They are INLAND Systems Group, Meridian Transit & Development, Morrow Genomics & Health, Lattice Civic Systems, Palisade Defence & Security, and Bluehaven Urban Systems.
36. **The six oligarchies are overlapping conglomerates, not clean monopolies.** Each has a primary industrial spine, extensive adjacent holdings and dependencies, and can be partner, creditor, customer, shareholder, and rival to the others at the same time.
37. **The oligarchies provide real public value as well as structural danger.** Their services genuinely improve lives; their threat comes from concentrated dependency and power rather than universal cartoon villainy.
38. **The Sydney showcase has an initial complete playable district layer across all nine macro-zones.** Districts are common-use/playable geographic units, not automatically formal administrative boundaries.
39. **Authority in Sydney resolves through a seven-layer jurisdiction stack.** Constitutional sovereignty, municipal/regional law, land ownership, infrastructure concession, network/identity authority, contracted security, and practical street control can all differ in the same location.
40. **Sydney's local political ecology is named and plural.** Public institutions, second-tier corporations, labour federations, community organisations, advocacy groups, syndicates, gangs, hacker collectives, and grey-market networks possess independent interests rather than existing only as proxies for the six oligarchies.

## The Severance

The Severance is the civilisation-defining reproductive catastrophe that reshaped natural human birth outcomes. It is commonly described as roughly two centuries before 2225. The exact date remains open, and the setting intentionally does **not** define the ultimate cause.

Public explanations, scientific theories, conspiracies, religious claims, and Campaign Plan answers may differ without forcing one setting-wide truth.

## Reproduction and registry

Pregnancy normally occurs in a biological human body. Archived male genetic material can be replicated and used for assisted conception at enormous scale. The assisted-conception process is approximately seven days; natural conception is likewise treated as a week-scale fertility process between consenting adult partners. These are setting facts, not automatic Trigger Discipline procedures.

Every naturally born male is registered by a relevant jurisdiction. The baseline is one legally required genetic contribution, closer in social logic to compulsory civic service than permanent reproductive ownership. Once completed, the baseline reproductive obligation is complete.

Registries are **jurisdiction-specific rather than one global database**. Cross-border systems use treaties, identity standards, cryptographic verification, medical interoperability, genetic-bank protocols, and lineage records. ARIO is an established Australian interoperability body; it is not a master registry.

## Protected, Claimed, and Free

- **Protected:** genuinely protected inside a family, civic, corporate, community, or other managed structure; protection can also become paternalistic or restrictive.
- **Claimed:** effectively controlled through coercion, criminal capture, contract, dependency, guardianship, manipulation, wealth, isolation, or institutional power.
- **Free:** retains genuine autonomy and enough competence, allies, weapons, money, mobility, reputation, legal leverage, or institutional support to resist control.

These are broad social labels, not universal legal statuses.

A recurring social dynamic is: **the freer a man is, the more partners he can genuinely choose; the more desirable he becomes, the harder remaining free can become.**

## Relationships and sexual culture

Male-centred polyamory and harem-like households are socially ordinary but never mandatory. Monogamy, jealousy, exclusivity, disinterest, bisexuality, lesbian relationships, casual relationships, and individual preference all remain real.

Female/female relationships are fully normalised. Adult sexual expression is broadly mainstream. Glamorous or provocative female presentation does not automatically imply availability, weakness, unseriousness, or professional incompetence.

Campaign Plans decide whether relationship and sexual material matters at all.

## Companion robots

Male-presenting companion and sex robots are common consumer technology, ranging from cheap mechanical products to sophisticated luxury conversational companions. They remain recognisably artificial. Cybernetically augmented people remain biological humans.

## Earth and political power

Earth's geography remains recognisable, but its political order is a two-century successor landscape. There is no world government and no single megacorporation secretly responsible for every important event.

Modest orbital and lunar infrastructure exists, but Earth remains overwhelmingly dominant in population, politics, culture, and campaign relevance.

## Australia and Sydney

Australia's future rise is built around **desert-scale solar generation** and enormous **data-centre / compute infrastructure**. The old federal/state model centred politically on Canberra does not simply vanish; practical authority slowly migrates toward private owners/operators through more than a century of incremental concessions.

By 2225, government still legislates, taxes, polices, runs courts and services, regulates, and participates in diplomacy. It is no longer the uncontested apex of Australian power. Major corporate oligarchies dominate the practical system while competing with one another, surviving public institutions, and smaller powers.

The Sydney coastal megaregion stretches through Greater Sydney, the Illawarra/Wollongong corridor, Central Coast, and Newcastle–Hunter complex. Its first playable layer comprises 40 districts across nine macro-zones, resolved locally through the seven-layer jurisdiction stack.

Principal oligarchies are INLAND, MERIDIAN, MORROW, LATTICE, PALISADE, and BLUEHAVEN. Recurring second-tier corporations are ASTER, IRONLACE, TIDEGLASS, CALYX, VECTORLINE, EMBERLINE, NORTHLIGHT, and VERDIGRIS. Named public/labour/community/criminal layers are defined under [`setting/factions/`](../setting/factions/).

## Reproductive industry

Reproductive infrastructure is one of the world's largest strategic industries. It includes genetic archives, fertility clinics, genetic replication, quality control, lineage verification, chain-of-custody security, privacy law, insurance, public health, corporate medicine, black markets, parentage disputes, inheritance disputes, and cross-jurisdiction recognition.

MORROW is a major corporate actor in this sector, not the sole reproductive institution. SPHN and CALYX provide additional established public/private alternatives.

## Technology baseline

Expected technologies include real firearms, realistic ammunition and reload logic, cover-centric gunfights, cybernetic augmentation, neural interfaces, local and wide-area networks, wired systems, removable HUDs, hacking, drones, advanced medicine, barriers and shields, AI, vehicles, biotechnology, satellites, orbital stations, and limited lunar industry/research.

Advanced AI does not make HACKERs obsolete. Drones do not make human operators irrelevant. Cybernetics do not silently become magic.

The core technology principle remains: **Connectivity creates capability and vulnerability.**
